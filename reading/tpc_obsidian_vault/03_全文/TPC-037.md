## Abstract

The preceding product–slope analysis reduces an orbit–sliced prime–Möbius energy estimate to a weighted coherent multiplicative spectrum. Three possible obstructions remain entangled there: zero residue coordinates, nonconstant complementary weights, and the proper coordinate faces of a three–modulus completion. We separate them exactly.

At the endpoint $$Q=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad  T=X^{193/500+o(1)},$$ choose a prime $q\asymp J$ above the physical orbit support. The part of either orbit slice on which $q\mid m_\alpha j+h$ satisfies $$\boxed{  V_{L,q\mid N}+V_{R,q\mid N}  \ll X^{o(1)}(1+Q/q)Q^2  \ll X^{o(1)}Q^3/J.}$$ Thus every physical $s=0$ or $u=0$ residue face may be removed before finite–field completion at exactly the inherited gate scale. The same holds for any fixed number of orbit–scale primes.

On the regular part, the raw ultra residue vector $G$ has $G(0)=0$. Its punctured centering $$\beta=\frac1{q-1}\sum_{U\ne0}G(U),\qquad  g(0)=0,\qquad g(U)=G(U)-\beta\quad(U\ne0)$$ is both additively centered and still vanishes at zero. If $f=F-q^{-1}\sum_DF(D)$, then for $a,s\ne0$ $$\mathcal C_{a,s}(F,G)  =\mathcal C_{a,s}(f,g)-\beta f(-h/a)+(q-1)\bar F\beta.$$ Hence the centered core has zero complete-$s$ trace, while every centering correction is explicit and rank one in the complementary $s$-coordinate. For the actual dyadic ultra weight, the standard zero–free–region estimate gives $$|\beta|\ll_A (U/q)(\log X)^{-A}.$$ Complete residue cycles of the complementary interval therefore cancel exactly in the core; only its incomplete boundary, the mean corrections, and alias weights remain.

We next diagonalize that nonconstant boundary. If $c=w*_\times g$, then the two–character transform is a shifted Mellin transform $$C_\chi=\sum_{x\in\mathbb F_q^\times}c(x)\overline{\chi(x-h)}.$$ The nonprincipal Jacobi matrix $K_{\chi,\psi}=J(\psi,\bar\chi)$ satisfies $$\boxed{K^*K=KK^*=(q-1)^2I-q\,\mathbf1\mathbf1^*.}$$ Its singular values are $q-1$, with multiplicity $q-3$, and $1$, with multiplicity one. Equivalently, $$\sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}|C_\chi|^2  =\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}  |\widehat w(\psi)\widehat g(\psi)|^2  -\frac q{(q-1)^2}  \left|\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}  \widehat w(\psi)\widehat g(\psi)\psi(h)\right|^2.$$ Thus the Jacobi layer is near–isometric, not an automatic source of cancellation. Nevertheless, for an interval weight $w$, Pólya–Vinogradov saves the full uniform Cauchy factor in the unweighted character average. A sharp punctured character example shows that an arbitrary nonconstant coherent weight may still saturate that factor.

Finally, we give an exact calculus for all eight character faces of three pairwise coprime orbit–scale primes. The faces are orthogonal over one CRT period. Their squared masses, by codimension, are $$J^{-3},\qquad J^{-2},\qquad J^{-1},\qquad 1.$$ The coherent union $P$ of the seven proper faces obeys $$\|P\|_\infty\ll J^{-1},\qquad  \|P\|_2^2\asymp J^{-1},\qquad  \|P\|_1\asymp1.$$ Grouping a reciprocal dyadic block first by the integer determinant $F=su-N$ removes the apparent incomplete–box boundary: every face has total absolute mass at most $X^{o(1)}X/J^3$, while $$\sum_{s,u}|P(su-N)|^2  \ll X^{o(1)}X/J^4=o(1).$$ The full face, however, remains $1-O(J^{-1})$ on every exact equality and retains the terminal Möbius layer. Closing the orbit gate still requires either absorption of the exposed punctured-centering mean corrections and principal outer boundary together with actual nonprincipal spectral overlap, or a target-coupled, coefficient-weighted shifted–fiber Bessel theorem for the unsplit raw physical output. We prove neither route, and claim no parity breach, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, or twin–prime result.

## Zero coordinates are removed on the physical equality

###### ref-9958360ba239b4d7

###### ref-0453d533f399da13

###### ref-862ecf6ed66b2829

Choose a prime $q\asymp J$ larger than the positive orbit support and between the physical $d$- and $\ell$-scales. On the part of the actual packet satisfying $$q\mid m_\alpha j+h,  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-singular-target}$$ a fixed orbit time selects only $O(1+Q/q)$ row labels, whereas a fixed row label occurs at no more than one orbit time. This elementary two-sided incidence, combined with the inherited row $\ell^2$-mass, gives $$\boxed{  V_{L,\rm sing(q)}+V_{R,\rm sing(q)}  \ll X^{o(1)}(1+Q/q)Q^2  \ll X^{o(1)}\frac{Q^3}{J}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-zero-face-bound}$$ If the ultra divisor is opened as $$su=m_\alpha j+h,  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-physical-equality}$$ then, because $q$ is prime, the event in [\[eq:tpc37-intro-singular-target\]](TPC-037.md#ref-9958360ba239b4d7) is precisely the union of the physical faces $s=0\pmod q$ and $u=0\pmod q$. Thus the zero coordinates are absorbed before replacing equality by congruence. The argument extends to any fixed number of orbit-scale primes.

This result does not discard the terminal factorization. On the regular support $q\nmid m_\alpha j+h$, the point $s=1$, $u=m_\alpha j+h$ remains present and both coordinates are units.

## Punctured centering isolates the complete trace

###### ref-8d90e44ae508cea4

###### ref-bb6c12bf70d5be46

###### ref-8deb394b90deb660

###### ref-1d9f3dfcdbbd0a10

After physical zero-face excision, the raw ultra residue vector $G$ satisfies $G(0)=0$. Ordinary additive centering would generally create a nonzero value at the excluded residue. We instead define $$\beta=\frac1{q-1}\sum_{U\ne0}G(U),\qquad  g(0)=0,\qquad g(U)=G(U)-\beta\quad(U\ne0).  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-punctured-centering}$$ If $f=F-q^{-1}\sum_D F(D)$, then for $a,s\ne0$ the raw affine correlation satisfies the exact identity $$\mathcal C_{a,s}(F,G)  =\mathcal C_{a,s}(f,g)  -\beta f(-h/a)+(q-1)\overline F\,\beta.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-punctured-identity}$$ Every correction is explicit and rank one in the complementary $s$-coordinate. The punctured-centered core has the exact complete-cycle cancellation $$\sum_{s\in\mathbb F_q^\times}R_s(a)=0.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-complete-cycle}$$ For the actual dyadic ultra coefficient, a standard one-Möbius estimate gives, for every fixed $A>0$, $$|\beta|\ll_A\frac Uq(\log X)^{-A}.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-beta-bound}$$ Hence all complete residue cycles in the complementary interval vanish from the centered core; the incomplete boundary, the displayed mean corrections, and alias weights remain. The logarithmic saving in [\[eq:tpc37-intro-beta-bound\]](TPC-037.md#ref-1d9f3dfcdbbd0a10) is not promoted here to a fixed power saving.

## The two-character layer is a Jacobi near-isometry

###### ref-1f2f69366b2acbff

###### ref-96e13abf4897c3c3

###### ref-3ffb46fc62ac1e76

###### ref-0bb4657a702fbfbd

Let $w$ be the nonconstant complementary-factor weight and let $c=w*_\times g$ on $\mathbb F_q^\times$. The coherent transform can be written as $$C_\chi=\sum_{x\in\mathbb F_q^\times}  c(x)\overline{\chi(x-h)}.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-shifted-Mellin}$$ Taking a second Mellin transform produces the nonprincipal Jacobi matrix $$K_{\chi,\psi}=J(\psi,\bar\chi),  \qquad \chi,\psi\ne\mathbf1.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-Jacobi-matrix}$$ We compute its Gram matrix exactly: $$\boxed{  K^*K=KK^*=(q-1)^2I-q\,\mathbf1\mathbf1^*.}  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-Jacobi-Gram}$$ Thus its singular values are $q-1$, with multiplicity $q-3$, and $1$, with multiplicity one. The Jacobi layer is therefore a scaled near-isometry rather than a source of free square-root cancellation. The same calculation gives the exact energy identity $$\begin{aligned}  \sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}|C_\chi|^2  ={}&\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}  |\widehat w(\psi)\widehat g(\psi)|^2 \notag\\  &-\frac q{(q-1)^2}  \left|\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}  \widehat w(\psi)\widehat g(\psi)\psi(h)\right|^2.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-Jacobi-energy}\end{aligned}$$

When $w$ is the residue-count weight of an interval, Pólya–Vinogradov bounds its nonprincipal Mellin coefficients and saves one full factor $q$ relative to uniform Cauchy in the unweighted character average. That conclusion is deliberately not transferred to the outer physical coefficient direction: a punctured single-character example still attains the coherent Cauchy factor. For the nonprincipal outer spectrum, a sufficient hypothesis is a Mellin-flatness estimate for the physical convolution $b*_\times f$, or an equivalent bound for the resulting Möbius Hadamard energy. The principal outer boundary remains an explicit separate term. Neither that boundary estimate nor the nonprincipal hypothesis is proved.

## Three-modulus proper faces localize, but the full face remains

###### ref-e2558adbbec6fb9e

###### ref-b3595df5388e78d2

###### ref-d2410a1daeb30fe3

For three pairwise coprime primes $q_i\asymp J$, we decompose the CRT delta into its eight principal/nonprincipal coordinate faces. They are orthogonal over one CRT period, with squared masses $$J^{-3},\qquad J^{-2},\qquad J^{-1},\qquad 1  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-face-scales}$$ according to the number of nonprincipal coordinates. More strongly, the coherent sum $P$ of all seven proper faces satisfies $$\|P\|_\infty\ll J^{-1},\qquad  \|P\|_2^2\asymp J^{-1},\qquad  \|P\|_1\asymp1.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-proper-norms}$$ Grouping first by the integer determinant $F=su-N$ removes an apparent incomplete-box boundary and preserves these exact period norms up to the critical multiplicity $$1+\frac X{q_1q_2q_3}  =X^{1/400+o(1)}.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-critical-periods}$$ This is a fiberwise statement. It does not commute automatically with the coherent row and divisor sums in the physical energy.

###### ref-7f0305764ee29468

On the genuine equality $F=0$, the proper union has amplitude $O(J^{-1})$. Therefore its contribution closes directly from the sign-blind baseline: $$V_{{\rm eq},{\rm proper}}  \ll X^{o(1)}J^{-2}Q^3J  =X^{o(1)}\frac{Q^3}{J}.  \label{eq:tpc37-intro-proper-equality-closure}$$ The full three-coordinate face has amplitude $1-O(J^{-1})$ on the same equality and retains the terminal Möbius layer. An artificial bounded coefficient can also align with the $\ell^1$, rather than the $\ell^2$, size of the proper face. Consequently the periodic quadratic calculation is not a coefficient-free proof of [\[eq:tpc37-intro-orbit-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-9da79f33e99fa08e).

## Claim boundary and organization

The unconditional advances are the physical absorption [\[eq:tpc37-intro-zero-face-bound\]](TPC-037.md#ref-0453d533f399da13), the exact punctured-centering and Jacobi identities, the orthogonal CRT face calculus, determinant-first periodization, and proper-face closure on the exact equality. The remaining alternatives are either separate absorption of the exposed punctured-centering mean corrections and principal outer boundary together with an actual nonprincipal Mellin-overlap theorem, or a target-coupled, coefficient-weighted shifted-fiber Bessel estimate for the unsplit raw physical output. We establish neither route. In particular, this paper proves no estimate for the full regular coherent face, no affine Chowla theorem, no parity breach, no positivity or prime-pair main term, no Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, and no twin-prime theorem.

fixes the physical object and the normalizations. removes the physical zero coordinates. proves the punctured identities and complete-cycle cancellation. gives the exact weighted Jacobi spectrum. develops the three-modulus face calculus and determinant-first reassembly. For a prime $q\nmid h$, the corresponding complete product–slope model is defined on $G_q=\mathbb F_q^\times$ by $$\begin{aligned}  R_s(a)  &:=\sum_{D\in\mathbb F_q}  f(D)g\!\left(s^{-1}(aD+h)\right),  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-Rsa}\\  (Tb)(j,s)  &:=\sum_{\ell\in G_q}b(\ell)R_s(\ell j),  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-Tb}\\  S_w(j)&:=\sum_{s\in G_q}w(s)(Tb)(j,s).  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-Sw}\end{aligned}$$ With the unnormalized multiplicative transforms $$\widehat b(\chi)=\sum_{x\in G_q}b(x)\overline{\chi(x)},  \qquad  \widehat R_s(\chi)=\sum_{a\in G_q}R_s(a)\overline{\chi(a)},  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-transforms}$$ TPC-36 gives the exact coherent identity $$\boxed{  \mathcal G_q(b,f,g;w)  :=\sum_{j\in G_q}|S_w(j)|^2  =\frac1{q-1}\sum_\chi  |\widehat b(\bar\chi)|^2  \left|\sum_{s\in G_q}w(s)\widehat R_s(\chi)\right|^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-coherent-gate}$$ The right side is the actual finite-model coherent functional, not an upper bound. The square-function spectrum $\sum_{j,s}|(Tb)(j,s)|^2$ controls an average over independent $s$’s; it does not control [\[eq:tpc37-interface-coherent-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-4189313da84a770f) without a possible factor $q-1$. TPC-36 proves that this factor is sharp for arbitrary centered data.

## Endpoint and alias ledger

###### ref-bef6061142deaa25

###### ref-949cfc20fdea7a2d

###### ref-e302c25ee10a97ec

###### ref-39f979bc22bf0bcf

At the endpoint used throughout this paper, $$Q=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad  T=X^{193/500+o(1)},  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-endpoint}$$ and the source scales satisfy $D=o(J)$, $J=o(L)$, and $QJ=X^{1+o(1)}$. The identities relevant to the present ledger are $$\begin{aligned}  \frac Q{J^2}  &=X^{1/400+o(1)},  &\frac X{J^3}  &=X^{1/400+o(1)},  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-critical-identities}\\  \frac T J  &=X^{107/2000+o(1)},  &\frac QT  &=X^{563/2000+o(1)}.  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-secondary-identities}\end{aligned}$$ One modulus $q\asymp J$ leaves $O(1+X/q)=O(Q)$ possible integer determinants in a block. Three pairwise coprime orbit-scale moduli leave $$O\!\left(1+\frac X{J^3}\right)  =X^{1/400+o(1)}  \label{eq:tpc37-interface-three-modulus-aliases}$$ joint aliases. This count matches the allowance above the diagonal, but it does not show that principal, proper-coordinate, or full-coordinate faces reassemble with the required sign.

The logical division used below is therefore strict:

1.  the physical equality is used first to remove residue-zero coordinates;

2.  punctured centering and two-character analysis concern the regular complete finite model;

3.  the three-modulus face calculus controls exact periodic masses and determinant multiplicities; and

4.  any transfer of those quadratic masses through the coherent physical sums requires an additional coefficient-specific theorem.

None of the inherited inputs asserts that theorem. In particular, the setup supplies no affine Möbius cancellation uniform in a moving shift, no suppression of the full CRT face, and no parity-breaking estimate.

# Physical excision of the zero coordinate faces

The complete product–slope spectrum is naturally formulated on $\mathbb F_q^\times$. Before completing any integer variable modulo $q$, we remove the physical row–time pairs on which a complementary factor can be zero modulo $q$. This order avoids confusing a coordinate face of a modular relaxation with cancellation in the Möbius coefficient.

## The inherited orbit slice

We use only the following properties of the physical packet. The left and right row sets have $O(Q)$ elements. ## One auxiliary prime

###### ref-39efac21a7e2a9df

Split the ultra increment by the target residue: $$\begin{aligned}  C_m^{\rm sing(q)}(j)  &:=\mathbf 1_{q\mid mj+h}C_m(j),  \label{eq:tpc37-singular-increment}\\  C_m^{\rm reg(q)}(j)  &:=\mathbf 1_{q\nmid mj+h}C_m(j).  \label{eq:tpc37-regular-increment}\end{aligned}$$ Let $Y^{L,\rm sing(q)}$, $Y^{L,\rm reg(q)}$, and their energies be defined from [\[eq:tpc37-left-slice\]](TPC-037.md#ref-fdbe53d83efe57fa) by using the corresponding increment. Define the right versions symmetrically.

> **Theorem: Physical absorption of one zero-coordinate face** \[thm:tpc37-one-prime-face\] Under [\[eq:tpc37-row-label\]](TPC-037.md#ref-963d334e0f592fa3)–[\[eq:tpc37-unit-coordinate-range\]](TPC-037.md#ref-84b391a2b7fc999e), $$\boxed{  V_{L,\rm sing(q)}+V_{R,\rm sing(q)}  \ll X^{o(1)}\left(1+\frac Qq\right)Q^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-one-prime-face-bound}$$ Consequently, at $q\asymp J$, $$\boxed{  V_{L,\rm sing(q)}+V_{R,\rm sing(q)}  \ll X^{o(1)}\frac{Q^3}{J}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-one-prime-gate}$$

###### ref-3e334c2709d865ac

###### ref-8d0267e2532b42da

###### ref-fffc956865db6bda

###### ref-a3f8d00bdf494e5c

> **Proof** For fixed $j$, let $$\mathcal A_q(j)=\{\alpha:q\mid m_\alpha j+h\}.  \label{eq:tpc37-row-residue-set}$$ If $q\mid j$, this set is empty because $q\nmid h$. Otherwise $m_\alpha$ lies in one residue class modulo $q$. The labels are distinct and lie in an interval of length $O(Q)$, so $$|\mathcal A_q(j)|\ll 1+\frac Qq.  \label{eq:tpc37-fixed-time-row-count}$$ Conversely, for fixed $\alpha$, the congruence $m_\alpha j\equiv-h\pmod q$ selects one class of $j\pmod q$. The orbit interval has diameter smaller than $q$, and hence $$\#\{j\in\mathcal J:\alpha\in\mathcal A_q(j)\}\le1.  \label{eq:tpc37-fixed-row-time-count}$$
>
> For one fixed opposite row $\gamma$, Cauchy–Schwarz, [\[eq:tpc37-ultra-envelope\]](TPC-037.md#ref-7ea45effdaf1db0a), and [\[eq:tpc37-fixed-time-row-count\]](TPC-037.md#ref-8d0267e2532b42da) give $$\begin{aligned}  \sum_j|Y_{\gamma,j}^{L,\rm sing(q)}|^2  &\ll X^{o(1)}\left(1+\frac Qq\right)  \sum_j\sum_{\alpha\in\mathcal A_q(j)}  |\gamma_\alpha^{(1)}|^2 \notag\\  &\le X^{o(1)}\left(1+\frac Qq\right)  \sum_\alpha|\gamma_\alpha^{(1)}|^2 \notag\\  &\ll X^{o(1)}\left(1+\frac Qq\right)Q.  \label{eq:tpc37-one-column-singular-energy}\end{aligned}$$ There are $O(Q)$ opposite rows. Finally, $Q/q\to\infty$ and $q\asymp J$, proving [\[eq:tpc37-one-prime-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-9493bfb506fe18b9).

The terminology in the theorem is literal. On opening an ultra divisor, write $$su=N_\alpha(j).  \label{eq:tpc37-complementary-factorization}$$ For a prime $q$, $$q\mid N_\alpha(j)  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  q\mid s\ \text{or}\ q\mid u.  \label{eq:tpc37-zero-face-equivalence}$$ Thus [\[thm:tpc37-one-prime-face\]](TPC-037.md#ref-65af02e6388afb85) absorbs simultaneously the $s=0$ and $u=0$ residue faces of the physical equality. ## A fixed finite family of moduli

The preceding argument is stable under the fixed number of primes used in a CRT realization. For every fixed $r$, the prime number theorem supplies $r$ distinct primes in the same fixed dilate of $J$ for all sufficiently large $X$; the scale separation in [\[eq:tpc37-source-order\]](TPC-037.md#ref-571ae2a8d7d167a5) then gives [\[eq:tpc37-unit-coordinate-range\]](TPC-037.md#ref-84b391a2b7fc999e) for each of them.

> **Corollary: Fixed finite face excision** \[cor:tpc37-finite-prime-faces\] Let $q_1,\ldots,q_r\asymp J$ be distinct primes satisfying [\[eq:tpc37-unit-coordinate-range\]](TPC-037.md#ref-84b391a2b7fc999e), where $r$ is fixed independently of $X$, and put $P=\prod_{i=1}^{r}q_i$. # Punctured centering of the regular ultra coefficient

After [3](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-037.md#src-4297d5889d4f0416), the physical equality is restricted to $q\nmid N_\alpha(j)$. Hence both factors in $su=N_\alpha(j)$ are nonzero modulo $q$. We now center the completed ultra coefficient without reintroducing an artificial value at the zero residue.

## An exact asymmetric centering identity

###### ref-3f67d3d1a6827d0d

###### ref-ee1511f74aebbe79

###### ref-063600fe55e0ae26

###### ref-01f090e53af82736

###### ref-bb886e5a749c6b1a

Let $q>2$ be prime, put $G_q=\mathbb F_q^\times$, and fix $h\in G_q$. Let $F,G:\mathbb F_q\to\mathbb C$, where $$G(0)=0.  \label{eq:tpc37-raw-g-zero}$$ The function $F$ is the raw residue aggregation of a $d$-weight, and $G$ is the raw aggregation of the regular ultra weight. Define the ordinary additive centering of $F$ by $$\overline F=\frac1q\sum_{D\in\mathbb F_q}F(D),  \qquad f(D)=F(D)-\overline F.  \label{eq:tpc37-f-centering}$$ For $G$, instead put $$\beta=\frac1{q-1}\sum_{U\in G_q}G(U),  \label{eq:tpc37-punctured-mean}$$ and define its punctured centering by $$g(0)=0,  \qquad  g(U)=G(U)-\beta\quad(U\in G_q).  \label{eq:tpc37-punctured-g}$$ Then $$\sum_D f(D)=\sum_U g(U)=0,  \qquad g(0)=0.  \label{eq:tpc37-punctured-properties}$$

###### ref-55693383f4dcfe2b

For $a,s\in G_q$, write $$\mathcal C_{a,s}(A,B)  :=\sum_{D\in\mathbb F_q}  A(D)B\!\left(s^{-1}(aD+h)\right).  \label{eq:tpc37-affine-correlation}$$

###### ref-64d4d4a4cfd5268b

> **Proposition: Punctured-centering identity** \[prop:tpc37-punctured-identity\] For every $a,s\in G_q$, $$\boxed{  \mathcal C_{a,s}(F,G)  =\mathcal C_{a,s}(f,g)  -\beta f(-h/a)  +(q-1)\overline F\,\beta.}  \label{eq:tpc37-punctured-identity}$$

###### ref-7f5c9b44ef53a70d

###### ref-f3664435c457b173

###### ref-f8578ecde74d2d12

> **Proof** On $\mathbb F_q$, $$F=f+\overline F,  \qquad  G=g+\beta\mathbf 1_{G_q}.  \label{eq:tpc37-punctured-decompositions}$$ The affine map $D\mapsto s^{-1}(aD+h)$ is a permutation, so the constant part of $F$ contributes $$\overline F\sum_U G(U)=(q-1)\overline F\,\beta.  \label{eq:tpc37-F-mean-contribution}$$ For the other correction, the indicator $\mathbf 1_{G_q}(s^{-1}(aD+h))$ deletes exactly $D_0=-h/a$. Since $f$ is centered, $$\sum_D f(D)\mathbf 1_{aD+h\ne0}  =-f(D_0).  \label{eq:tpc37-deleted-residue}$$ Combining the two identities proves [\[eq:tpc37-punctured-identity\]](TPC-037.md#ref-64d4d4a4cfd5268b).

###### ref-1176686d59561863

###### ref-a1576be44699fe8c

###### ref-2cea0194ef766499

###### ref-2ea29d968d7c7d84

The identity extends directly to the product–slope topology. Define $$\begin{aligned}  R_s^{\rm raw}(a)&:=\mathcal C_{a,s}(F,G),  &R_s(a)&:=\mathcal C_{a,s}(f,g),  \label{eq:tpc37-raw-centered-R}\\  (T^{\rm raw}b)(j,s)&:=\sum_{\ell\in G_q}b(\ell)R_s^{\rm raw}(\ell j),  &(Tb)(j,s)&:=\sum_{\ell\in G_q}b(\ell)R_s(\ell j).  \label{eq:tpc37-raw-centered-T}\end{aligned}$$ Put $$B_0=\sum_{\ell\in G_q}b(\ell),  \qquad  H_f(j)=\sum_{\ell\in G_q}  b(\ell)f\!\left(-\frac h{\ell j}\right).  \label{eq:tpc37-rank-one-data}$$ Then [\[prop:tpc37-punctured-identity\]](TPC-037.md#ref-9ad2495658621ecf) gives the pointwise formula $$\boxed{  (T^{\rm raw}b)(j,s)  =(Tb)(j,s)-\beta H_f(j)  +(q-1)\overline F\,\beta B_0.}  \label{eq:tpc37-product-punctured-identity}$$ For an arbitrary weight $w:G_q\to\mathbb C$, with $W_0=\sum_s w(s)$, $$\begin{aligned}  \sum_s w(s)(T^{\rm raw}b)(j,s)  ={}&\sum_s w(s)(Tb)(j,s) \notag\\  &+W_0\{-\beta H_f(j)  +(q-1)\overline F\,\beta B_0\}.  \label{eq:tpc37-weighted-punctured-identity}\end{aligned}$$ Thus every centering correction is explicit and contains the single scalar $\beta$. No smallness assumption on $\overline F$ is used in this algebraic statement.

## The actual Möbius mean

###### ref-c63f1867a7d53aac

###### ref-19809af554099b52

###### ref-620850b0e7591851

On a reciprocal dyadic block, choose the canonical allocation in which the ultra factor remains $$a_U(u)=-\mu(u)\log u\,\Phi_U(u),  \label{eq:tpc37-clean-ultra-weight}$$ where $\Phi_U$ is the indicator of $\{u:U<u\le2U,\ T<u\le U_0\}$, or a bounded smooth/BV cutoff with controlled seminorms on that interval. On the regular physical support one may insert $\mathbf 1_{q\nmid u}$ exactly. The raw residue aggregation is therefore $$G_U(r)=  \sum_{\substack{u\equiv r\pmod q\\q\nmid u}}  -\mu(u)\log u\,\Phi_U(u),  \qquad r\in\mathbb F_q,  \label{eq:tpc37-regular-ultra-periodization}$$ and indeed $G_U(0)=0$. Its punctured mean is $$\beta_U  =-\frac1{q-1}  \sum_{\substack{u\ge1\\q\nmid u}}  \mu(u)\log u\,\Phi_U(u).  \label{eq:tpc37-physical-beta}$$

> **Proposition: Uniform one-Möbius bound for the punctured mean** \[prop:tpc37-beta-bound\] Assume $q\asymp J$, $U\ge T$, and [\[eq:tpc37-endpoint-scales\]](TPC-037.md#ref-3bbd111afa481d90), and assume that the cutoff family in [\[eq:tpc37-clean-ultra-weight\]](TPC-037.md#ref-c63f1867a7d53aac) has uniformly bounded smooth or BV seminorms. For every fixed $A>0$, $$\boxed{  |\beta_U|  \ll_{A,\Phi}\frac Uq(\log X)^{-A}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-beta-bound}$$ The estimate is uniform over the dyadic ultra blocks.

> **Proof** Let $$M(x)=\sum_{n\le x}\mu(n),  \qquad  M_q(x)=\sum_{\substack{n\le x\\(n,q)=1}}\mu(n).  \label{eq:tpc37-Mertens-functions}$$ Separating the multiples of the prime $q$ gives the exact recurrence $$M_q(x)=\sum_{r\ge0}M(x/q^r),  \label{eq:tpc37-coprime-Mertens-recurrence}$$ where the sum terminates. The standard zero-free-region form of the prime number theorem [`\citep[Chapters~5 and~18]{IwaniecKowalski2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-037.md#cite-49dfea87eaada156) gives, for every fixed $B>0$, $$M(y)\ll_B y(\log y)^{-B}  \label{eq:tpc37-standard-Mertens-bound}$$ when $y$ is large. In [\[eq:tpc37-coprime-Mertens-recurrence\]](TPC-037.md#ref-c6c68608feca7923), the terms after the first have total trivial size $O(x/q)$, which is smaller than $x(\log X)^{-B}$ because $q=X^{133/400+o(1)}$. Hence, uniformly for $x\asymp U$, $$M_q(x)\ll_B U(\log X)^{-B}.  \label{eq:tpc37-uniform-coprime-Mertens}$$ Partial summation, with $B$ chosen beyond $A$ and the fixed seminorm loss of $\Phi_U$, yields $$\sum_{q\nmid u}\mu(u)\log u\,\Phi_U(u)  \ll_{A,\Phi}U(\log X)^{-A}.  \label{eq:tpc37-weighted-one-Mobius}$$ Division by $q-1\asymp q$ proves [\[eq:tpc37-beta-bound\]](TPC-037.md#ref-9357725428fe25e4).

The mean must be extracted while the ultra weight still has the clean form [\[eq:tpc37-clean-ultra-weight\]](TPC-037.md#ref-c63f1867a7d53aac).

## Exact cancellation of complete residue cycles

The punctured-centered core has no complete complementary-factor trace.

> **Proposition: Complete-cycle annihilation** \[prop:tpc37-complete-cycle-annihilation\] For every $a,j\in G_q$, $$\sum_{s\in G_q}R_s(a)=0,  \qquad  \sum_{s\in G_q}(Tb)(j,s)=0.  \label{eq:tpc37-complete-cycle-annihilation}$$

> **Proof** Fix $a$ and put $D_0=-h/a$. The term $D=D_0$ vanishes because $g(0)=0$. For $D\ne D_0$, as $s$ runs over $G_q$, the value $s^{-1}(aD+h)$ runs once over $G_q$. Its total contribution is zero because $\sum_{U\in G_q}g(U)=0$. This proves the first identity; the second follows by summing in $\ell$.

For an integer interval $\mathcal S$, define its nonzero residue lift count by $$W_{\mathcal S}(r)  :=\#\{s\in\mathcal S:s\equiv r\pmod q\},  \qquad r\in G_q.  \label{eq:tpc37-s-lift-count}$$ Writing $|\mathcal S|=kq+r_0$, one has $$W_{\mathcal S}(r)=k+\rho_{\mathcal S}(r),  \qquad  \rho_{\mathcal S}(r)\in\{0,1\},  \qquad  |\operatorname{supp}\rho_{\mathcal S}|<q.  \label{eq:tpc37-cycle-boundary-decomposition}$$ Consequently, $$\sum_{r\in G_q}W_{\mathcal S}(r)(Tb)(j,r)  =\sum_{r\in G_q}\rho_{\mathcal S}(r)(Tb)(j,r).  \label{eq:tpc37-complete-cycles-removed}$$ For the complementary interval $|\mathcal S_U|\asymp X/U$, the number of removed complete cycles satisfies $$k_U\ll1+\frac X{Uq}\asymp1+\frac Q U.  \label{eq:tpc37-complete-cycle-count}$$ It is $\asymp Q/U$ whenever the complementary interval contains at least a fixed positive number of complete residue cycles. In particular, $$k_T=X^{563/2000+o(1)},  \label{eq:tpc37-low-ultra-cycle-count}$$ whereas $k_U=0$ once $U$ exceeds a sufficiently large fixed multiple of $Q$.

Equation [\[eq:tpc37-complete-cycles-removed\]](TPC-037.md#ref-6ee4e3399808ef84) concerns the core residue lift count. An alias selector or another nonconstant reassembly weight belongs to $\rho$-type weighted data and is not annihilated by this proposition.

## Comparison with ordinary additive centering

###### ref-95e939a9549aa75b

###### ref-a17eb108cb8630d8

###### ref-fcfe9c8905f4f66d

###### ref-3a8391dc927ce66c

If one instead uses $$\overline G=\frac1q\sum_U G(U)=\frac{q-1}{q}\beta,  \qquad  g^{\rm add}=G-\overline G,  \label{eq:tpc37-ordinary-g-centering}$$ then $$g^{\rm add}(0)=-\frac{q-1}{q}\beta  \label{eq:tpc37-ordinary-centered-zero}$$ despite the raw identity $G(0)=0$. In fact, $$g^{\rm add}  =g+\frac\beta q-\beta\mathbf 1_{\{0\}}.  \label{eq:tpc37-two-centerings-comparison}$$ The complete-trace formula for ordinary centering becomes $$\sum_{s\in G_q}  \mathcal C_{a,s}(f,g^{\rm add})  =-(q-1)\beta f(-h/a).  \label{eq:tpc37-ordinary-complete-trace}$$ Thus the ordinary value $g^{\rm add}(0)$ is a centering boundary, not a physical coefficient at an allowed zero residue. The punctured identity [\[eq:tpc37-punctured-identity\]](TPC-037.md#ref-64d4d4a4cfd5268b) records the same quantity as an explicit correction that is rank one in the complementary $s$-coordinate while keeping the core value $g(0)=0$. It is an exact reorganization, not an additional cancellation theorem.

The scope is therefore precise. The physical zero coordinate faces are handled by [\[thm:tpc37-one-prime-face\]](TPC-037.md#ref-65af02e6388afb85); punctured centering removes the artificial zero value and complete constant cycles; and [\[prop:tpc37-beta-bound\]](TPC-037.md#ref-a5333fdf4d4a568f) controls the exposed clean Möbius mean. The remaining nonconstant weighted core, the CRT aliases, and the terminal $s=1$ layer are not estimated here. In particular, this section does not prove the full coefficient gate.

# Punctured two–character dispersion

The complete complementary-factor trace from TPC-36 vanishes after the right coefficient is centered on the punctured line $\mathbb F_q^\times$. This does not by itself estimate an incomplete or weighted complementary-factor sum. In this section we compute that weighted sum exactly. The resulting Jacobi matrix is, apart from one rank-one direction, a scaled unitary matrix. Thus the Jacobi expansion does not manufacture cancellation: it transfers a concrete Hadamard energy between two multiplicative-character coordinates.

## Conventions and the weighted transform

Let $q>2$ be prime, put $$G:=\mathbb F_q^\times,  \qquad n:=|G|=q-1,  \label{eq:tpc37-G-n}$$ and write $\widehat G$ for the group of multiplicative characters of $G$. Every character, including the principal character $\mathbf 1$, is extended to $\mathbb F_q$ by the value zero at zero. For $v:G\to\mathbb C$, use the unnormalized Mellin transform $$\widehat v(\psi)  :=\sum_{x\in G}v(x)\overline{\psi(x)},  \qquad  v(x)=\frac1n\sum_{\psi\in\widehat G}  \widehat v(\psi)\psi(x).  \label{eq:tpc37-Mellin-conventions}$$ Thus $$\sum_{x\in G}|v(x)|^2  =\frac1n\sum_{\psi\in\widehat G}  |\widehat v(\psi)|^2.  \label{eq:tpc37-Mellin-Parseval}$$

> **Theorem: Weighted two–character formula** \[thm:tpc37-two-character-formula\] For every $\chi\in\widehat G$, define $$C_\chi  :=\frac{\overline{\chi(-h)}}n  \sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}  W_\psi G_\psi\psi(h)J(\psi,\overline\chi).  \label{eq:tpc37-Cchi-Jacobi}$$ Equivalently, $$C_\chi  =\sum_{s,U\in G}w(s)g(U)  \overline{\chi(sU-h)}.  \label{eq:tpc37-Cchi-direct}$$ Then $$\boxed{  H_\chi=F_\chi C_\chi  \quad(\chi\ne\mathbf1),  \qquad  H_{\mathbf1}=qF_{\mathbf1}C_{\mathbf1}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-Hchi-split}$$ Moreover, $$\boxed{  C_{\mathbf1}  =-\frac1n\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}  W_\psi G_\psi\psi(h).}  \label{eq:tpc37-principal-C}$$ Thus punctured centering removes the principal $\psi$-coordinate, but an incomplete weight can still leave a principal $\chi$-coordinate.

## The Jacobi matrix is almost unitary

The generic size $q^{1/2}$ of a Jacobi sum is misleading if the $\psi$-sum is kept coherently. Exact orthogonality determines the whole matrix.

###### ref-74bfbef262c87d1a

###### ref-f27b26dcb21f9c12

> **Lemma: Full Jacobi orthogonality** \[lem:tpc37-full-Jacobi-orthogonality\] For $\psi,\phi\in\widehat G$, $$\sum_{\chi\in\widehat G}  J(\psi,\overline\chi)  \overline{J(\phi,\overline\chi)}  =n\bigl(n\mathbf1_{\{\psi=\phi\}}-1\bigr).  \label{eq:tpc37-full-Jacobi-orthogonality}$$

> **Proof** Expand both Jacobi sums and use character orthogonality in $\chi$. Only pairs with $1-x=1-y\ne0$, hence $x=y\ne1$, survive. Therefore the left side is $$n\sum_{\substack{x\in G\\x\ne1}}  \psi(x)\overline{\phi(x)}.$$ The last sum is $n-1$ when $\psi=\phi$, and $-1$ otherwise, which proves [\[eq:tpc37-full-Jacobi-orthogonality\]](TPC-037.md#ref-f27b26dcb21f9c12).

> **Theorem: Rank-one defect and exact singular values** \[thm:tpc37-Jacobi-near-unitary\] With $\mathbf 1$ now denoting the all-ones vector on $\mathscr X$, $$\boxed{  K^*K=KK^*=n^2I-q\,\mathbf1\mathbf1^*.}  \label{eq:tpc37-K-Gram}$$ Moreover, $$K\mathbf1=\mathbf1,  \qquad K^*\mathbf1=\mathbf1.  \label{eq:tpc37-K-flat-vector}$$ Consequently the singular values of $K$ are $$n\quad\text{with multiplicity }q-3,  \qquad  1\quad\text{with multiplicity }1.  \label{eq:tpc37-K-singular-values}$$ For $q=3$, only the final singular value is present.

> **Proof** Restrict [\[eq:tpc37-full-Jacobi-orthogonality\]](TPC-037.md#ref-f27b26dcb21f9c12) to $\psi,\phi\in\mathscr X$, and remove the term $\chi=\mathbf1$. Thus the diagonal entries of $K^*K$ are $n(n-1)-1=n^2-q$, while the off-diagonal entries are $-n-1=-q$. This proves the first identity in [\[eq:tpc37-K-Gram\]](TPC-037.md#ref-55a7debda43b39a0). The second follows either by the symmetric calculation in the other character variable or from the row and column sums below.
>

The same argument in $\chi$ gives each restricted column sum one, proving [\[eq:tpc37-K-flat-vector\]](TPC-037.md#ref-5d89baffc2f850b2). On the line spanned by $\mathbf1$, the squared singular value is $$n^2-q(q-2)=1.$$ On its orthogonal complement, [\[eq:tpc37-K-Gram\]](TPC-037.md#ref-55a7debda43b39a0) is $n^2I$. This proves [\[eq:tpc37-K-singular-values\]](TPC-037.md#ref-bafce12467162b62).

## Multiplicative convolution and shifted Parseval

###### ref-facc6ffcec20cf49

###### ref-5589e263c5f60c4a

There is a simpler physical-space interpretation of [\[eq:tpc37-Jacobi-energy\]](TPC-037.md#ref-fe2e56317d17b1f4). Define the multiplicative convolution on $G$ by $$c(x):=(w*_{\times}g)(x)  :=\sum_{s\in G}w(s)g(x/s),  \qquad x\in G,  \label{eq:tpc37-multiplicative-convolution}$$ and extend $c$ to zero by $c(0)=0$. Then $$\widehat c(\psi)=W_\psi G_\psi,  \qquad  \sum_{x\in G}c(x)=0.  \label{eq:tpc37-c-transform-centered}$$

> **Theorem: Shifted multiplicative Parseval** \[thm:tpc37-shifted-Parseval\] For every $\chi\in\widehat G$, $$\boxed{  C_\chi=\sum_{x\in G}c(x)\overline{\chi(x-h)}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-Cchi-shifted-c}$$ In particular, $$\begin{aligned}  C_{\mathbf1}&=-c(h),  \label{eq:tpc37-Cprincipal-ch}\\  \sum_{\chi\in\widehat G}|C_\chi|^2  &=n\bigl(\|c\|_2^2-|c(h)|^2\bigr),  \label{eq:tpc37-all-character-Parseval}\\  \sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}|C_\chi|^2  &=\boxed{n\|c\|_2^2-q|c(h)|^2}.  \label{eq:tpc37-nonprincipal-Parseval}\end{aligned}$$ Equivalently, if $$E_{w,g}:=\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}|W_\psi G_\psi|^2,  \label{eq:tpc37-Hadamard-energy}$$ then $$\boxed{  \frac{E_{w,g}}{n^2}  \le \sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}|C_\chi|^2  \le E_{w,g}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-Hadamard-two-sided}$$ The lower constant is sharp for every $q>2$; the upper constant is sharp for $q>3$.

###### ref-1940a89081bb41eb

> **Proof** Regroup the defining sum according to $x=sU$: $$\sum_{s,U\in G}w(s)g(U)\overline{\chi(sU-h)}  =\sum_{x\in G}c(x)\overline{\chi(x-h)}.$$ This is [\[eq:tpc37-Cchi-shifted-c\]](TPC-037.md#ref-e82d2588d563c129). The principal character deletes the single value $x=h$; since $\sum_xc(x)=0$, this gives [\[eq:tpc37-Cprincipal-ch\]](TPC-037.md#ref-a1b4976952195448). Apply Mellin Parseval to the function $y\mapsto c(y+h)$ on $G$. Its squared norm is $\|c\|_2^2-|c(h)|^2$, because $c(0)=0$. This proves [\[eq:tpc37-all-character-Parseval\]](TPC-037.md#ref-6736e35f7bb28559); subtracting $|C_{\mathbf1}|^2$ proves [\[eq:tpc37-nonprincipal-Parseval\]](TPC-037.md#ref-81d8ece0e1d60e79).
>
> By [\[eq:tpc37-c-transform-centered\]](TPC-037.md#ref-5589e263c5f60c4a) and Mellin Parseval, $$E_{w,g}=n\|c\|_2^2.  \label{eq:tpc37-E-c-norm}$$ The upper bound in [\[eq:tpc37-Hadamard-two-sided\]](TPC-037.md#ref-1da5e3a740aefec1) follows at once. For the lower bound, zero-sum Cauchy gives $$|c(h)|^2  \le\frac{n-1}{n}\|c\|_2^2.$$ Insert this estimate into [\[eq:tpc37-nonprincipal-Parseval\]](TPC-037.md#ref-81d8ece0e1d60e79) and use [\[eq:tpc37-E-c-norm\]](TPC-037.md#ref-1940a89081bb41eb). Equality in the lower bound occurs when $c$ is constant off $h$ and its value at $h$ is the negative of the sum of the other $n-1$ values. For $q>3$, a nonzero centered $c$ with $c(h)=0$ gives equality in the upper bound. Both examples belong to the present class by taking $w=\mathbf1_{\{1\}}$ and $g=c$.

The ratio between the two sides of [\[eq:tpc37-Hadamard-two-sided\]](TPC-037.md#ref-1da5e3a740aefec1) is not a technical defect. It is the condition number of deleting one translated coordinate from a zero-sum vector. Away from that one direction, the two-character Jacobi map is an isometry after division by $q-1$.

## Interval weights and one full average saving

###### ref-adb733bab3d905aa

###### ref-4791fcb3f5162f49

###### ref-e500e22c385002f5

Let $I$ be an interval of integers, and let its nonzero residue multiplicity be $$w_I(r):=\#\{s\in I:s\equiv r\pmod q\},  \qquad r\in G.  \label{eq:tpc37-interval-residue-weight}$$ For $\psi\ne\mathbf1$, complete periods cancel and $$W_{I,\psi}  =\sum_{\substack{s\in I\\q\nmid s}}  \overline{\psi(s)}.  \label{eq:tpc37-interval-character-sum}$$ The classical Pólya–Vinogradov inequality [`\citep[Chapter~9]{MontgomeryVaughan2007}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-037.md#cite-3c91c7e57f203d4b) therefore gives, uniformly in the position and length of $I$, $$B_I:=\max_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}|W_{I,\psi}|  \ll q^{1/2}\log q.  \label{eq:tpc37-PV}$$

###### ref-c7bb9300297ae96a

###### ref-3a0cd8a71952f163

> **Corollary: Interval-weight average** \[cor:tpc37-interval-average\] For every punctured-centered $g$, $$\boxed{  \frac1n\sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}|C_\chi|^2  \le B_I^2\|g\|_2^2  \ll q(\log q)^2\|g\|_2^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-interval-average-bound}$$ More generally, $B_I$ in the first bound may be replaced by any available bound for the nonprincipal Mellin transforms of the actual weight.

> **Proof** By [\[eq:tpc37-Hadamard-energy\]](TPC-037.md#ref-7a9112a4faa9a27c), $$E_{w_I,g}  \le B_I^2\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}|G_\psi|^2  =nB_I^2\|g\|_2^2.$$ Use the upper bound in [\[eq:tpc37-Hadamard-two-sided\]](TPC-037.md#ref-1da5e3a740aefec1) and divide by $n$.

A pointwise Cauchy estimate in the incomplete $s$-variable can lose $q$ in energy after the interval is reduced to a nonconstant remainder modulo $q$. The averaged estimate [\[eq:tpc37-interval-average-bound\]](TPC-037.md#ref-3a0cd8a71952f163) removes that full factor, up to logarithms. It is only an unweighted average in $\chi$; transferring it to the physical coefficient direction requires spectral information about the outer row coefficient.

For $b:G\to\mathbb C$, define the product–slope operator $$(Tb)(j,s):=\sum_{\ell\in G}b(\ell)R_s(\ell j),  \qquad j,s\in G,  \label{eq:tpc37-product-slope-operator}$$ and put $$a(t):=(b*_{\times}f)(t)  :=\sum_{\ell\in G}b(\ell)f(t/\ell).  \label{eq:tpc37-outer-convolution}$$ Its transform with the opposite conjugation is $$\widetilde a(\chi)  :=\sum_{t\in G}a(t)\chi(t)  =\widehat b(\overline\chi)F_\chi.  \label{eq:tpc37-a-transform}$$ Put $$B_0:=\sum_{\ell\in G}b(\ell),  \qquad  r_0:=B_0f(0)c(h).  \label{eq:tpc37-principal-outer-boundary}$$ Since $f$ is centered on $\mathbb F_q$, $$\sum_{t\in G}a(t)=-B_0f(0).  \label{eq:tpc37-a-principal-coordinate}$$ The weighted physical output is therefore $$S_h(j):=\sum_{s\in G}w(s)(Tb)(j,s)  =r_0+\sum_{t\in G}a(t)c(tj+h).  \label{eq:tpc37-affine-core}$$ The first term is the $D=0$ contribution. > **Theorem: Outer spectrum with its principal boundary** \[thm:tpc37-Mellin-flatness-sufficient\] Under [\[eq:tpc37-punctured-centering\]](TPC-037.md#ref-6cb522873bb84f38), $$\boxed{  \sum_{j\in G}|S_h(j)|^2  =\frac{q^2}{n}|r_0|^2  +\frac1n\sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}   |\widetilde a(\chi)|^2|C_\chi|^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-outer-principal-decomposition}$$ If $a\ne0$, then $$\boxed{  \sum_{j\in G}|S_h(j)|^2  \le\frac{q^2}{n}|r_0|^2  +\mathfrak K_{\rm Mell}(a)  \left(\max_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}|W_\psi|^2\right)  \|a\|_2^2\|g\|_2^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-flatness-closure}$$ In particular, for an interval weight, $$\sum_{j\in G}|S_h(j)|^2  \ll q|r_0|^2  +\mathfrak K_{\rm Mell}(a)  q(\log q)^2\|a\|_2^2\|g\|_2^2.  \label{eq:tpc37-flatness-interval}$$ Thus a bound $\mathfrak K_{\rm Mell}(a)=X^{o(1)}$ reaches the complete projective diagonal scale for the nonprincipal outer spectrum, up to soft factors, without a pointwise estimate for every twisted Möbius sum. > **Proof** The term $D=0$ in the definition of $R_s$ gives $r_0$. On $D\in G$, put $t=\ell D$; this proves [\[eq:tpc37-affine-core\]](TPC-037.md#ref-6548f6ac200dec96). The flatness hypothesis is sufficient for the nonprincipal part and is not asserted for the physical row coefficient. The saturation example in TPC-36 used a nonzero value at the residue zero. Puncturing removes that particular example, but not the abstract two-character obstruction.

> **Proposition: Single-character punctured witness** \[prop:tpc37-punctured-character-witness\] Let $q>3$, let $\eta\ne\mathbf1$, and set $$g(0)=0,  \qquad g(U)=\eta(U)\quad(U\in G).  \label{eq:tpc37-character-g}$$ Then $g$ is punctured-centered and $$G_\psi=n\mathbf1_{\{\psi=\eta\}}.  \label{eq:tpc37-character-transform}$$ For every weight $w$, $$\boxed{  C_\chi  =\overline{\chi(-h)}W_\eta\eta(h)  J(\eta,\overline\chi).}  \label{eq:tpc37-character-C}$$ Consequently $$|C_\chi|=  \begin{cases}  |W_\eta|,&\chi=\mathbf1\text{ or }\chi=\eta,\\  q^{1/2}|W_\eta|,    &\chi\ne\mathbf1,\eta.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc37-character-C-sizes}$$ If $w=\mathbf1_I$ for a subset $I\subset G$ of size $r$, then $$\sum_{\eta\ne\mathbf1}|W_\eta|^2=r(n-r).  \label{eq:tpc37-subset-W-energy}$$ Thus, if both $r$ and $n-r$ are $\gg q$, there is a nonprincipal $\eta$ with $|W_\eta|\gg q^{1/2}$, and the generic outputs in [\[eq:tpc37-character-C-sizes\]](TPC-037.md#ref-a0f5045273e74685) have size $\gg q$.

> **Proof** Character orthogonality gives [\[eq:tpc37-character-transform\]](TPC-037.md#ref-6f35f123a924659a); inserting it into [\[eq:tpc37-Cchi-Jacobi\]](TPC-037.md#ref-3cfdd2f17298c995) proves [\[eq:tpc37-character-C\]](TPC-037.md#ref-3d34feb7f1c8d956). The exceptional and generic Jacobi evaluations give [\[eq:tpc37-character-C-sizes\]](TPC-037.md#ref-a0f5045273e74685). Finally, Mellin Parseval gives $$\sum_{\eta\in\widehat G}|W_\eta|^2=nr,  \qquad |W_{\mathbf1}|^2=r^2,$$ which proves [\[eq:tpc37-subset-W-energy\]](TPC-037.md#ref-871ab3e9c2e7100d) and the final assertion.

This witness is not a model for the actual Möbius coefficient. It proves only that punctured centering and interval support, without coefficient-specific information, cannot imply a uniformly small weighted coherent spectrum.

## The actual Möbius Hadamard energy

###### ref-47ae850781804965

The physical zero coordinate is first removed by [\[thm:tpc37-one-prime-face\]](TPC-037.md#ref-65af02e6388afb85); the raw-to-punctured rank-one correction and its clean Möbius mean are then handled by \[[prop:tpc37-punctured-identity](TPC-037.md#ref-9ad2495658621ecf),[prop:tpc37-beta-bound](TPC-037.md#ref-a5333fdf4d4a568f)\]. We analyze only the remaining punctured core. For one physical dyadic block, let $$a_u=-\mu(u)\log u\,\vartheta(u/U),  \qquad  \widetilde g(r)=  \sum_{\substack{u\in\mathcal U\\u\equiv r\!\!\pmod q}}a_u.  \label{eq:tpc37-physical-g-residues}$$ After punctured centering, every $\psi\ne\mathbf1$ satisfies the exact identity $$\boxed{  G_\psi  =\sum_{\substack{u\in\mathcal U\\(u,q)=1}}  -\mu(u)\log u\,\vartheta(u/U)\overline{\psi(u)}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-physical-Mobius-twist}$$ Punctured centering changes only the principal transform; it supplies no pointwise estimate for [\[eq:tpc37-physical-Mobius-twist\]](TPC-037.md#ref-47ae850781804965). It does, however, give a completely unconditional rms envelope. Put $$\nu_{\mathcal U,q}^*  :=\max_{r\in G}  \#\{u\in\mathcal U:u\equiv r\pmod q\}.  \label{eq:tpc37-u-residue-multiplicity}$$ Orthogonal projection onto the punctured zero-sum subspace and Cauchy–Schwarz in each residue class give $$\|g\|_2^2  \le \nu_{\mathcal U,q}^*  \sum_{u\in\mathcal U}|a_u|^2.  \label{eq:tpc37-physical-g-L2}$$ For an interval $\mathcal U$, $\nu_{\mathcal U,q}^*\le1+|\mathcal U|/q$. Hence [\[cor:tpc37-interval-average\]](TPC-037.md#ref-c7bb9300297ae96a) yields $$\boxed{  \frac1n\sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}|C_\chi|^2  \ll q(\log q)^2  \left(1+\frac{|\mathcal U|}{q}\right)  \sum_{u\in\mathcal U}|a_u|^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-physical-rms-envelope}$$ This bound uses no Möbius cancellation and is correspondingly only an envelope; the weighted outer spectrum can still select an exceptional output character.

###### ref-ff60d9ad8a1f67d6

###### ref-7ec880302eac1cd2

###### ref-cc58336b46a53885

For integer weights $\omega_s$ on a complementary interval, the exact Hadamard energy is therefore $$\begin{aligned}  E_{\mathcal I,\mu}(q)  :={}&\sum_{\psi\ne\mathbf1}  \left|   \sum_{\substack{s\in\mathcal I\\(s,q)=1}}   \omega_s\overline{\psi(s)}  \right|^2  \notag\\  &\hspace{4em}\times  \left|   \sum_{\substack{u\in\mathcal U\\(u,q)=1}}   -\mu(u)\log u\,\vartheta(u/U)   \overline{\psi(u)}  \right|^2.  \label{eq:tpc37-physical-Hadamard-energy}\end{aligned}$$ Equivalently, if $$c_q(x):=  \sum_{\substack{s\in\mathcal I,\,u\in\mathcal U\\  su\equiv x\!\!\pmod q}}  \omega_s a_u,  \qquad x\in G,  \label{eq:tpc37-residue-convolution}$$ and $\overline c_q=n^{-1}\sum_xc_q(x)$, character orthogonality gives $$\boxed{  E_{\mathcal I,\mu}(q)  =n\sum_{x\in G}|c_q(x)-\overline c_q|^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-residue-variance}$$ Thus Route B asks for a residue-variance or spectral-overlap theorem for the actual coefficient, rather than for a formal saving from the Jacobi kernel.

###### ref-151ab8fc91145c37

On an unrestricted divisor convolution, $$\sum_{u\mid N}-\mu(u)\log u=\Lambda(N).  \label{eq:tpc37-Mobius-von-Mangoldt}$$ On the physical target range, the restrictions $T<u\le U_0$ give precisely the ultra increment used in the inherited orbit slice. After reduction modulo $q$, [\[eq:tpc37-residue-variance\]](TPC-037.md#ref-cc58336b46a53885) also contains the integer aliases folded into each residue. Controlling this variance at the required scale is consequently arithmetic information about the truncated von-Mangoldt coefficient; it is not a consequence of puncturing, Pólya–Vinogradov, or Jacobi orthogonality alone.

We make no claim here that the physical outer convolution satisfies $\mathfrak K_{\rm Mell}(a)=X^{o(1)}$, that [\[eq:tpc37-physical-Hadamard-energy\]](TPC-037.md#ref-ff60d9ad8a1f67d6) has the diagonal-size bound, or that the principal and residue-zero corrections are negligible on every reassembly layer. The results above neither breach the parity barrier nor prove a twin-prime correlation estimate. They identify the exact two-character transform, remove one spurious uniform Cauchy loss under a clear flatness hypothesis, and isolate the coefficient-specific theorem still required.

# Three-modulus face calculus and determinant-first reassembly

After the physical zero-coordinate excision of [3](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-037.md#src-4297d5889d4f0416), the target $N$ and both factors in $su=N$ are units modulo each auxiliary prime. This section keeps three orbit-scale primes separate and computes all eight multiplicative character faces exactly. The calculation has two consequences. First, the seven proper-coordinate faces have one extra power of $J^{-1}$ in quadratic mass. Second, grouping the completed Cartesian block by the integer determinant $F=su-N$, rather than completing $s$ or $u$ separately, removes the apparent incomplete-box boundary loss. Neither statement is a bound for the physical coherent row energy; the precise remaining Bessel-type input is isolated at the end.

## The eight faces on the correct finite-field domain

Let $q_1,q_2,q_3>2$ be distinct primes, put $$n_i=q_i-1,  \qquad p_i=\frac1{n_i},  \qquad M=q_1q_2q_3,  \label{eq:tpc37-three-modulus-data}$$ and fix an integer $N$ satisfying $$(N,M)=1.  \label{eq:tpc37-N-unit}$$ For $F\in\mathbb Z/q_i\mathbb Z$, define $$\begin{aligned}  d_i(F)&:=\mathbf 1_{F=0},  \label{eq:tpc37-coordinate-delta}\\  e_i(F)&:=\mathbf 1_{F\ne-N},  \label{eq:tpc37-coordinate-unit-mask}\\  m_i(F)&:=p_i e_i(F),  \qquad  c_i(F):=d_i(F)-m_i(F).  \label{eq:tpc37-principal-centered-coordinate}\end{aligned}$$ The exclusion $F=-N$ in $e_i$ is essential. Indeed, if $F=su-N$, then $F=-N\pmod {q_i}$ is exactly the residue on which $su=0\pmod {q_i}$, so every multiplicative character extended by zero must vanish there.

The orthogonality relation on $G_i$ gives $$\begin{aligned}  d_i(F)  &=\frac1{n_i}\sum_{\chi_i}\chi_i(x_i(F)),  \label{eq:tpc37-all-character-coordinate}\\  m_i(F)  &=\frac1{n_i}\mathbf 1_{G_i}(x_i(F)),  \label{eq:tpc37-principal-character-coordinate}\\  c_i(F)  &=\frac1{n_i}\sum_{\chi_i\ne\mathbf 1}  \chi_i(x_i(F)).  \label{eq:tpc37-nonprincipal-character-coordinate}\end{aligned}$$ Thus $m_i$ is the principal multiplicative face and $c_i$ is the aggregate nonprincipal face. In particular, $$d_i=m_i+c_i  \label{eq:tpc37-one-coordinate-face-split}$$ holds also at $x_i=0$, where all three terms vanish.

> **Theorem: Orthogonal face calculus** \[thm:tpc37-orthogonal-face-calculus\] The eight functions $K_S$, $S\subseteq[3]$, are pairwise orthogonal in $\ell^2(\mathbb Z/M\mathbb Z)$. For every $S\subseteq[3]$, $$\begin{aligned}  \boxed{  \|K_S\|_2^2  =\prod_{i\in S}(1-p_i)   \prod_{i\notin S}p_i,}  \label{eq:tpc37-face-l2}\\  \boxed{  \|K_S\|_1  =2^{|S|}\prod_{i\in S}(1-p_i).}  \label{eq:tpc37-face-l1}\end{aligned}$$ Moreover, at the joint residue $F=0\pmod M$, $$\boxed{  K_S(0)=\|K_S\|_2^2.}  \label{eq:tpc37-face-joint-value}$$ Consequently the squared norms of the empty, singleton, double, and full faces have respective scales $$J^{-3},\qquad J^{-2},\qquad J^{-1},\qquad 1  \label{eq:tpc37-face-codimension-scales}$$ when $q_i\asymp J$.

> **Proof** In one coordinate, $$\sum_{F\bmod q_i}c_i(F)=0,  \qquad  \langle c_i,m_i\rangle=0.  \label{eq:tpc37-one-coordinate-orthogonality}$$ There are $n_i$ residues on which $e_i=1$. The scale table follows from $p_i\asymp J^{-1}$.

## Determinant-first periodization

###### ref-7a28fd6d286e7618

###### ref-8d67d360753edda5

The finite calculation becomes useful on the physical reciprocal blocks only after choosing the correct integer variable. Fix $0<N\ll X$ satisfying [\[eq:tpc37-N-unit\]](TPC-037.md#ref-c309545ff1e13672), and let $\mathcal R\subset\mathbb N^2$ be any set of pairs $(s,u)$ such that $$|su-N|\le B,  \qquad B\ll X.  \label{eq:tpc37-determinant-range}$$ TPC-36 supplies this hypothesis on every reciprocal dyadic block. Put $$F(s,u)=su-N.  \label{eq:tpc37-integer-determinant}$$

###### ref-806378d68fef6a48

###### ref-f70b7dc7e5102e6a

> **Theorem: Determinant-first periodization** \[thm:tpc37-determinant-periodization\] Let $H$ be any of the eight faces $K_S$, the full face $C$, or the proper union $P$. For $r\in\{1,2\}$ and every fixed $\varepsilon>0$, $$\boxed{  \sum_{(s,u)\in\mathcal R}|H(F(s,u))|^r  \ll_{\varepsilon}  X^{\varepsilon}  \left(1+\frac BM\right)  \sum_{F\bmod M}|H(F)|^r.}  \label{eq:tpc37-determinant-periodization-bound}$$ The same estimate holds after inserting unary weights of pointwise size $X^{o(1)}$. It is uniform in the shape of the reciprocal rectangle.

###### ref-6c818ba796a5464d

###### ref-cb610a666b163ada

###### ref-16eb71a9514dda88

> **Proof** For a fixed integer $F$, every pair counted on the left satisfies $$su=N+F.  \label{eq:tpc37-fixed-determinant-factorization}$$ When $N+F>0$, the number of positive representations is at most $\tau(N+F)\ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon$; when $N+F\le0$, there are none. Therefore $$\sum_{(s,u)\in\mathcal R}|H(F(s,u))|^r  \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon  \sum_{|F|\le B}|H(F)|^r.  \label{eq:tpc37-divisor-multiplicity-reduction}$$ The function $H$ is periodic modulo $M$. Partitioning $[-B,B]\cap\mathbb Z$ into complete residue blocks and at most two partial blocks gives $$\sum_{|F|\le B}|H(F)|^r  \ll\left(1+\frac BM\right)  \sum_{F\bmod M}|H(F)|^r.  \label{eq:tpc37-period-block-bound}$$ Combining the last two displays proves the theorem. Pointwise-soft unary weights are absorbed into $X^\varepsilon$.

The theorem is the reason to group by $F$ before completing either $s$ or $u$. A one-variable residue count produces an artificial boundary such as $\min\{S,U\}/J$; the determinant fiber has only divisor-bounded multiplicity and sees the exact $M$-periodic norm.

> **Corollary: Endpoint periodization ledger** \[cor:tpc37-periodization-ledger\] Assume $q_i\asymp J$, $B\ll X$, and put $$\mathcal K_X:=1+\frac X{J^3}.  \label{eq:tpc37-critical-period-count}$$ Then every individual face and the proper union satisfy $$\sum_{(s,u)\in\mathcal R}|K_S(F(s,u))|  +\sum_{(s,u)\in\mathcal R}|P(F(s,u))|  \ll X^{o(1)}\mathcal K_X.  \label{eq:tpc37-all-face-l1-periodization}$$ In quadratic mass, $$\begin{aligned}  \sum_{(s,u)\in\mathcal R}|P(F(s,u))|^2  &\ll X^{o(1)}\frac{\mathcal K_X}{J},  \label{eq:tpc37-proper-l2-periodization}\\  \sum_{(s,u)\in\mathcal R}|C(F(s,u))|^2  &\ll X^{o(1)}\mathcal K_X.  \label{eq:tpc37-full-l2-periodization}\end{aligned}$$ At the endpoint [\[eq:tpc37-endpoint-scales\]](TPC-037.md#ref-3bbd111afa481d90), $$\begin{aligned}  \mathcal K_X  &=\frac X{J^3}X^{o(1)}  =\frac Q{J^2}X^{o(1)}  =X^{1/400+o(1)},  \label{eq:tpc37-critical-period-endpoint}\\  \frac{\mathcal K_X}{J}  &=\frac X{J^4}X^{o(1)}  =X^{-132/400+o(1)}.  \label{eq:tpc37-proper-period-decay}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** Apply [\[thm:tpc37-determinant-periodization\]](TPC-037.md#ref-806378d68fef6a48) with the exact norms in \[[thm:tpc37-orthogonal-face-calculus](TPC-037.md#ref-e2d02529b2cb050f),[thm:tpc37-proper-union-norms](TPC-037.md#ref-126141039b1d8bd7)\]. Since every face has $O(1)$ one-period $\ell^1$ norm, the first bound follows. The proper and full $\ell^2$ bounds use respectively $1-A\asymp J^{-1}$ and $A\asymp1$. The endpoint identities follow from $QJ=X^{1+o(1)}$ and $J=X^{133/400+o(1)}$.

###### ref-1a1d7266481cb7ed

> **Remark: Quadratic sparsity is not physical closure** \[rem:tpc37-quadratic-not-physical\] Equation [\[eq:tpc37-proper-period-decay\]](TPC-037.md#ref-c7fbec18c846ece2) is an entrywise/fiberwise quadratic statement. The physical orbit slice sums in $s,u$, the input rows, and the determinant shifts before taking its square. No inequality in this section transfers [\[eq:tpc37-proper-period-decay\]](TPC-037.md#ref-c7fbec18c846ece2) through those coherent sums. In particular, it is not a proof of the inherited coefficient gate.

## Aliases, proper-coordinate ghosts, and the terminal fiber

###### ref-644c228131d4a4c7

###### ref-12c1cff109c6641f

For every integer $k$, the joint alias $F=kM$ has the same face values as $F=0$: $$C(kM)=A,  \qquad  P(kM)=1-A.  \label{eq:tpc37-joint-alias-values}$$ Thus the $O(X/M)$ joint aliases have respective quadratic masses $$O\!\left(\frac XM\right)  \quad\text{and}\quad  O\!\left(\frac X{MJ^2}\right)  \label{eq:tpc37-joint-alias-mass-ledger}$$ on the full and proper faces. The larger proper total in [\[eq:tpc37-proper-l2-periodization\]](TPC-037.md#ref-fb560d89968b5e8d) comes from nonjoint residues where $P=-C$.

###### ref-d7d046c41c164bef

###### ref-d09c1143005f26cc

###### ref-c1ffcc8f32a6f6ae

###### ref-9f96b66e5d01aed9

This cancellation is not optional. For example, work in a terminal-size interval of $u$’s of length $\asymp X$, set $s=1$, and suppose $(N,M)=1$. On the family $$u=N+kq_1q_2,  \qquad  q_3\nmid k,  \qquad  q_3\nmid N+kq_1q_2,  \label{eq:tpc37-two-hit-ghost-family}$$ the first two congruences hold, the third fails, and all three ratio coordinates are nonzero. There are $$\asymp\frac X{q_1q_2}\asymp\frac X{J^2}  \label{eq:tpc37-two-hit-count}$$ such points. At each one, $$C(F)=-(1-p_1)(1-p_2)p_3,  \qquad P(F)=-C(F),  \label{eq:tpc37-two-hit-cancellation}$$ so the full face alone has total absolute mass $$\asymp\frac X{J^2}\frac1J  =\frac X{J^3}.  \label{eq:tpc37-two-hit-critical-mass}$$ The original joint CRT delta is zero on the whole family. Dropping the proper faces before their cancellation with $C$ would therefore create a false alias family at exactly the critical three-modulus scale.

###### ref-1e72b659b45b59e6

###### ref-2daee073c097fc08

###### ref-7328a62518320d6f

On the genuine integer equality $F=0$, the situation is different. Every proper divisor layer and the terminal layer $s=1,u=N$ acquire the same scalar face values $$P(0)=1-A\ll J^{-1},  \qquad C(0)=A=1-O(J^{-1}).  \label{eq:tpc37-exact-equality-face-values}$$ Let $V_{\rm eq}^{\rm abs}$ denote the sign-blind physical energy of the regular exact-equality kernel. The inherited envelopes give $$V_{\rm eq}^{\rm abs}\ll X^{o(1)}Q^3J.  \label{eq:tpc37-equality-absolute-baseline}$$ The exact scalar relation in [\[eq:tpc37-exact-equality-face-values\]](TPC-037.md#ref-1e72b659b45b59e6) therefore yields $$\boxed{  V_{{\rm eq},{\rm proper}}  \ll X^{o(1)}J^{-2}Q^3J  =X^{o(1)}\frac{Q^3}{J}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-equality-proper-closure}$$ This closes all proper faces on the actual equality without using a sign estimate. By contrast, the full face is $A$ times the same vector and retains it with asymptotic coefficient one. In particular, the fused terminal Möbius correlation is not weakened by coordinate centering.

###### ref-1e63229d30864d82

> **Corollary: Full-face centering obstruction** \[cor:tpc37-full-face-obstruction\] No uniform power saving for vectors supported on $F=0$ can follow from three-coordinate multiplicative centering alone. More precisely, on that subspace the full-face operator is scalar multiplication by $$A=1-O(J^{-1}).  \label{eq:tpc37-full-face-scalar}$$ This is a method obstruction for the CRT-centering route, not a lower bound for the physical Möbius correlation.

## A sharp coherent no-go and the next gate

###### ref-4db416ac9cb288fb

The $L^2$ saving for $P$ cannot be upgraded uniformly over all bounded unary coefficients. Retain $q_i\asymp J$ and the endpoint scales. In the same terminal geometry, take $s=1$, let $I$ be an interval of $u$’s of length $B\asymp X$, and assume $B/M\to\infty$. Define the bounded unary weight $$g_N(u)=  \begin{cases}   \overline{P(u-N)}/|P(u-N)|,&P(u-N)\ne0,\\   0,&P(u-N)=0.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc37-coherent-saturating-weight}$$

###### ref-a5a5bd13c89c378d

###### ref-aca81cbe46c76bf0

###### ref-39cf1066e0ea4f81

###### ref-2d38c2b261c33785

> **Proposition: Sharp quadratic-to-coherent separation** \[prop:tpc37-coherent-face-no-go\] For the preceding weight, $$\begin{aligned}  \left|\sum_{u\in I}g_N(u)P(u-N)\right|  &\asymp\frac BM,  \label{eq:tpc37-coherent-saturation-l1}\\  \sum_{u\in I}|P(u-N)|^2  &\asymp\frac B{MJ}.  \label{eq:tpc37-coherent-saturation-l2}\end{aligned}$$ At the endpoint $B\asymp X$, the ratio of the first quantity to the square root of the second is $$\boxed{  \asymp\sqrt{\mathcal K_X J}  =\sqrt{\frac X{J^2}}  =X^{67/400+o(1)}.}  \label{eq:tpc37-coherent-upgrade-loss}$$ Thus no estimate uniform over all bounded unary coefficients can upgrade the displayed periodic $L^2$ mass to the required coherent scale with only an $X^{o(1)}$ loss.

> **Proof** The definition of $g_N$ makes the first left side equal to $\sum_{u\in I}|P(u-N)|$. Decompose $I-N$ into complete $M$-periods and at most two partial periods. The exact one-period norms [\[eq:tpc37-proper-l1\]](TPC-037.md#ref-7540ec06440bf37c)–[\[eq:tpc37-proper-l2\]](TPC-037.md#ref-272d426f102cff12), together with $B/M\to\infty$, give [\[eq:tpc37-coherent-saturation-l1\]](TPC-037.md#ref-aca81cbe46c76bf0)– [\[eq:tpc37-coherent-saturation-l2\]](TPC-037.md#ref-39cf1066e0ea4f81). Finally use $\mathcal K_X=X/J^3$ and the endpoint value of $J$.

The saturating $g_N$ is an artificial periodic coefficient. The proposition does not assert that the physical weight $-\mu(u)\log u$ has this alignment. It proves that avoiding it requires coefficient-specific arithmetic information.

###### ref-29f9c847ecc70da3

To state the remaining target correctly, the dependence on the moving physical target must be retained. Restore it in the notation by writing $C_N(F)$ and $P_N(F)$. The one-period norms are independent of the unit $N\bmod M$. Therefore, directly from periodicity and the exact norms—without using divisor multiplicity—one has, uniformly for $(N,M)=1$, $$\begin{aligned}  \sum_{|F|\ll X}|C_N(F)|^2  &\ll\mathcal K_X,  \notag\\  \sum_{|F|\ll X}|P_N(F)|^2  &\ll\frac{\mathcal K_X}{J}.  \label{eq:tpc37-direct-face-period-ledger}\end{aligned}$$

###### ref-6fc94004224c7b61

Let $\mathcal N_{\rm reg}$ denote the targets occurring in one physical packet after the zero-face excision, so every $N\in\mathcal N_{\rm reg}$ satisfies $(N,M)=1$. Let $$\mathscr H_{\rm phys}  :=\ell^2\{(L,\gamma,j)\}    \oplus\ell^2\{(R,\alpha,j)\}  \label{eq:tpc37-physical-output-Hilbert-space}$$ be the direct sum of the left and right packet output spaces, with the indices restricted to the retained packet. This notation is necessary: the coordinate faces depend on $N\bmod M$ and cannot be pulled out as a single scalar depending only on $F$.

# Endpoint ledger and the relocated coherent-face gates

The three routes above answer different questions and must not be merged into a single cancellation claim. This section records their exact logical output at the endpoint and isolates two sufficient, but presently unproved, continuations.

## What has been removed from the regular problem

###### ref-7a89fd6d7bcda33e

Let $$\mathfrak G_X:=X^{o(1)}\frac{Q^3}{J}  \label{eq:tpc37-target-gate}$$ denote the inherited target scale. The reductions proved above have the following consequences.

1.  By \[[thm:tpc37-one-prime-face](TPC-037.md#ref-65af02e6388afb85),[cor:tpc37-finite-prime-faces](TPC-037.md#ref-0f13810fdd2408f8)\], every physical row–time pair on which one of the chosen CRT primes divides the target contributes $O(\mathfrak G_X)$. Hence the modular analysis may begin with both complementary coordinates in the unit groups.

2.  By [\[prop:tpc37-punctured-identity\]](TPC-037.md#ref-9ad2495658621ecf), ordinary additive centering can be replaced by punctured centering without hiding a zero-residue coefficient. The difference is an explicit term that is rank one in the complementary $s$-coordinate and contains the clean scalar $\beta_U$; complete constant residue cycles vanish by [\[prop:tpc37-complete-cycle-annihilation\]](TPC-037.md#ref-2186201bde6365a6).

3.  By \[[thm:tpc37-Jacobi-near-unitary](TPC-037.md#ref-53fb2c36c3ffb6af),[thm:tpc37-shifted-Parseval](TPC-037.md#ref-f29daf3ea1e8d8a3)\], the nonconstant complementary weight is represented exactly by a Hadamard product of two Mellin spectra. The Jacobi transform has only a rank-one defect and therefore supplies no generic cancellation. For an interval, Pólya–Vinogradov removes one uniform Cauchy factor only in the unweighted character average.

4.  By \[[thm:tpc37-orthogonal-face-calculus](TPC-037.md#ref-e2d02529b2cb050f),[thm:tpc37-proper-union-norms](TPC-037.md#ref-126141039b1d8bd7)\], the seven proper coordinate faces of three moduli form one explicit kernel $P$, with one-period squared mass $\asymp J^{-1}$. Determinant-first grouping gives the fiberwise bound [\[eq:tpc37-proper-l2-periodization\]](TPC-037.md#ref-fb560d89968b5e8d), and on the exact equality the proper-face energy is $O(\mathfrak G_X)$ by [\[eq:tpc37-equality-proper-closure\]](TPC-037.md#ref-7328a62518320d6f).

None of these statements estimates the full regular output after all row, orbit, dyadic, and alias sums are restored. In particular, the fiberwise $L^2$ estimate for $P$ is not a license to apply Cauchy through a coherent sum; [\[prop:tpc37-coherent-face-no-go\]](TPC-037.md#ref-a5a5bd13c89c378d) quantifies the resulting polynomial loss for arbitrary bounded coefficients.

## The two surviving formulations

The unsuppressed equality face and the coherent off-equality alias reassembly can be attacked in two nonexclusive forms.

#### Outer spectral overlap.

###### ref-257d293ed19da958

For one completed product–slope block, put $B_0=\sum_\ell b(\ell)$, $c=w*_\times g$, and $r_0=B_0f(0)c(h)$. The exact arithmetic object is $$\mathfrak S_q^{\rm full}(a,w,g)  :=\frac{q^2}{q-1}|r_0|^2  +\frac1{q-1}\sum_{\chi\ne\mathbf1}  |\widetilde a(\chi)|^2|C_\chi(w,g)|^2,  \label{eq:tpc37-outer-overlap-gate}$$ where $a=b*_{\times}f$ is the actual outer row convolution and $$C_\chi(w,g)=  \sum_{x\in\mathbb F_q^\times}(w*_{\times}g)(x)  \overline{\chi(x-h)}.$$ bounds the nonprincipal sum if the actual $a$ obeys $\mathfrak K_{\rm Mell}(a)=X^{o(1)}$; it leaves $r_0$ explicit. That pointwise flatness hypothesis is stronger than necessary: an aggregate estimate for the nonprincipal overlap, averaged over the physical layers, would suffice, provided the principal $r_0$-term is also controlled. Neither input is proved here.

#### Shifted determinant fibers.

###### ref-d85159d2b153c455

Keeping the integer determinant $F$ visible gives the alternative target-coupled Hilbert-space input [\[eq:tpc37-shift-fiber-bessel-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-535ae3cbd4d02ad6). Its normalization is not arbitrary: the direct period masses [\[eq:tpc37-direct-face-period-ledger\]](TPC-037.md#ref-29f9c847ecc70da3) imply $$\begin{aligned}  \text{full face:}\quad &Q^2J\,\mathcal K_X=Q^3/J,  \notag\\  \text{proper union:}\quad  &Q^2J\,\mathcal K_X/J=Q^3/J^2.  \label{eq:tpc37-two-fiber-ledgers}\end{aligned}$$ Thus the target-coupled, coefficient-weighted Bessel input at the stated normalization would close the full face and overclose the proper union. The moving shift $h_F=h+F$, and the dependence of the face on $N\bmod M$, prevent one from importing a fixed-$h$ incidence estimate without a new uniformity argument.

These are alternative sufficient inputs for the same desired physical output; they are not asserted to be equivalent term by term. The first asks for avoidance of large Mellin channels after completion; the second asks for almost-orthogonality between integer determinant fibers before completion. Failure of a pointwise flatness bound therefore does not rule out a weighted-overlap or fiberwise theorem.

## A route tree with valid fallback results

The next layer has three mathematically distinct outcomes.

Positive overlap theorem.

:   Prove the required aggregate nonprincipal overlap bound using the actual row and truncated Möbius coefficients, and separately absorb both the exposed $\beta_U$-mean corrections and the principal $r_0$-term in [\[eq:tpc37-outer-overlap-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-257d293ed19da958). This would close the completed finite-field coherent core; integer aliases and all normalization constants would still have to be checked in the physical reassembly.

Positive shifted-fiber theorem.

:   Prove the target-coupled input [\[eq:tpc37-shift-fiber-bessel-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-535ae3cbd4d02ad6) uniformly for the moving shifts and actual coefficients. This route directly absorbs the three-modulus alias count at its critical $X^{1/400+o(1)}$ scale.

Scoped obstruction theorem.

:   Exhibit, within a precisely specified coefficient class, unavoidable concentration in the exceptional Mellin direction or unavoidable alignment between shifted fibers. Such a theorem would rule out that proof mechanism at the claimed normalization, not establish a lower bound for a prime-pair correlation.

The present paper advances the program regardless of which branch is viable: zero faces, artificial centering values, constant residue cycles, and proper equality faces are no longer mixed into the unsuppressed equality-face question. The surviving gates are coefficient-specific and have two exact norm formulations.

## Relation to the parity boundary

The sharp examples in \[[prop:tpc37-punctured-character-witness](TPC-037.md#ref-71f2a989d4ba7d8c),[prop:tpc37-coherent-face-no-go](TPC-037.md#ref-a5a5bd13c89c378d)\] show that centering, support restrictions, and periodic $L^2$ sparsity alone do not force the needed coherent cancellation. This is consistent with the classical parity obstruction for sieve methods [`\citep{HeathBrown1982Parity,IwaniecKowalski2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-037.md#cite-bd33f7374ef1e88f); [\[IwaniecKowalski2004\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-037.md#cite-49dfea87eaada156). It is not a new formulation or resolution of that obstruction. A future positive theorem would have to use arithmetic information in the actual Möbius-derived coefficient beyond the formal finite-field identities proved here.

## Unconditional conclusions

Subject only to the explicit inherited packet hypotheses in [2](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-037.md#src-19dc2313dc0f0988), this paper proves:

1.  physical absorption of one, or any fixed finite number of, zero-coordinate faces at $X^{o(1)}Q^3/J$;

2.  the exact punctured-centering identities and corrections that are rank one in the complementary $s$-coordinate, together with complete-cycle annihilation;

3.  the standard superlogarithmic estimate for the exposed clean Möbius mean;

4.  the weighted two-character formula, the exact rank-one Jacobi Gram defect, and the shifted multiplicative Parseval identities;

5.  the interval-weight average bound and the exact outer decomposition into an explicit principal boundary plus a nonprincipal spectrum, with a sufficient flatness criterion for the latter;

6.  a punctured sharp example showing that abstract centered data can retain the full coherent character scale;

7.  the orthogonal three-modulus face calculus and exact norms of the seven-face proper union;

8.  determinant-first $\ell^1$ and $\ell^2$ periodization;

9.  proper-face closure on the exact equality, the full-face scalar obstruction, and the sharp quadratic-to-coherent separation for arbitrary bounded unary weights.

The finite-field flatness theorem is an implication, not a verification of its hypothesis for the physical row coefficient. Similarly, [\[eq:tpc37-shift-fiber-bessel-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-535ae3cbd4d02ad6) is displayed as a sufficient next input and is not among the proved estimates.

## What is not proved

Neither the manuscript nor the certificate proves:

1.  the regular or full physical coefficient gate $V_L+V_R\ll X^{o(1)}Q^3/J$;

2.  $\mathfrak K_{\rm Mell}(a)=X^{o(1)}$ for the actual outer convolution, the required aggregate nonprincipal spectral-overlap bound, or physical absorption of the exposed $\beta_U$-mean corrections and principal $r_0$-term;

3.  a diagonal-size estimate for the physical Möbius Hadamard energy [\[eq:tpc37-physical-Hadamard-energy\]](TPC-037.md#ref-ff60d9ad8a1f67d6);

4.  the target-coupled, coefficient-weighted shifted-fiber Bessel input [\[eq:tpc37-shift-fiber-bessel-gate\]](TPC-037.md#ref-535ae3cbd4d02ad6);

5.  uniform moving-shift incidence control sufficient to reassemble all aliases;

6.  a fixed-power saving from the clean Möbius mean;

7.  an affine Chowla theorem or a new Möbius-disjointness theorem;

8.  a breach of sieve parity or positivity;

9.  a Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair asymptotic; or

10. infinitely many twin primes.

The precise advance is a reduction theorem. It closes the physical zero faces and the proper faces on the exact equality, and diagonalizes the nonconstant one-modulus boundary. The full nonzero coordinate face is the unique unsuppressed face on the exact equality. Off the equality, coherent reassembly of both the full face and the proper union remains a coefficient-specific problem.
