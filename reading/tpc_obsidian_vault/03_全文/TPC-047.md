## Abstract

The large-prime determinant sector in the twin-prime-correlation (TPC) sequence has a smooth orbit variable but an output vector whose orbit coordinate is not uniformly band limited. This is the first of four transfer gates left by the preceding shift-spectral analysis. We act on the literal residual output coordinate: after zero extension in the physical orbit variable, let $\Pi_\Omega$ be the fiberwise orthogonal Fourier projection onto the half-width-$\Omega$ comb around $H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z/\mathbb Z$, leaving the opposite row and the physical residual label $s=d(m)r$ unchanged. For a progression-restricted orbit profile with Fourier bandwidth $\Sigma$, we prove that the projected completed determinant packet has the exact shift-spectral gap $$\frac{\Omega+\Sigma/J}{M_-}<\|\alpha\|_{\mathbb T}  <\frac{H_0^{-1}-\Omega-\Sigma/J}{M_+}.$$ The theorem is Hilbert valued and permits arbitrary arithmetic coefficients on the prime, cofactor, and source variables. We then apply a canonical comb cutoff to the complete vector-valued actual TPC source–mask profile; the proved projective representation is used only to estimate its complement. The discarded tails have super-polynomially small leakage, so the projected frozen shift family built from the literal physical $h_0$-coefficient field inherits rapid decay throughout a slightly shrunken gap. At the inherited TPC endpoint, an orbit band with time–bandwidth size $X^{1/400+o(1)}$ retains the nonempty range $$X^{-399/400+o(1)}<\|\alpha\|_{\mathbb T}  <X^{-267/400+o(1)}.$$ The projection is contractive, commutes with the residual Möbius unitary, and has exact physical-window trace $2H_0N\Omega$ per scalar non-orbit fiber. It is injective on every nonzero finitely supported orbit vector; however, an explicit finite-difference family shows that no uniform fraction of the residual norm must lie in this band. Thus an actual-residual-output sector with growing time–bandwidth trace crosses the orbit-frequency gate, while the high-frequency complement, atomic normalization, alias unfolding, and fixed-shift localization remain open. At the physical intercept this is the actual-coefficient residual synthesis, not the native affine physical Gram face. The shift family outside that intercept is frozen-coefficient, and no fixed-shift TPC closure, parity breach, or twin-prime conclusion is claimed.

# Introduction

###### ref-99e92ec4b4062180

The preceding large-prime descent turns one part of the TPC equality column into the determinant equation $$pr-mj=h_0,  \label{eq:tpc47-opening-determinant}$$ with the prime variable $p$, a Möbius-weighted cofactor $r$, and an actual source row $m$. Projective reassembly separates the physical source–mask coefficient into those arithmetic variables and a smooth orbit profile, up to a fixed progression in $j$ [`\citep{WangTPC36,WangTPC45}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-7286aa6023fa493f); [\[WangTPC45\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-1802a7e8047e595c). TPC–46 then proves an exact mesoscopic shift gap when the Hilbert vector attached to $(p,r,m)$ is independent of $j$ [`\citep{WangTPC46}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-2f37865d719a8eb5). In the actual residual output, the literal label $s=d(m)r$ remains fixed for fixed $(m,r)$, but the non-orbit coefficient vector over opposite rows varies with $j$; its coordinates are $$(\text{opposite row},j,s),\qquad s=d(m)r.  \label{eq:tpc47-actual-output-coordinate}$$ TPC–47 records that dependence by adjoining the literal $e_j$ factor and projecting it. Before projection, an arbitrary dual orbit field can restore the entire spectrum.

###### ref-4b57154232299cb1

This paper does not assume that this coupling is smooth. It applies a bounded projection to the coupling itself. The physical orbit coordinate is embedded by zero extension into $\ell^2(\mathbb Z)$. In every fixed opposite-row and residual-label fiber, we retain the full residue-comb Fourier band $$\mathcal B_\Omega^{(H_0)}  :=\{\beta\in\mathbb T:  \operatorname{dist}(\beta,H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z)\le\Omega\}.  \label{eq:tpc47-low-orbit-band}$$ This defines one canonical sector of the actual output Hilbert space; it is not the choice of one term in a signed projective expansion. The source mask, the Möbius cofactor, and the residual label remain literal.

## The positive theorem

###### ref-f8d98f228f1789ab

###### ref-ac30db9d74dcdd81

The abstract kernel underlying the result is transparent. Let $M_-\le m\le M_+$, let $H_0$ be fixed, and consider one layer $$F_h=\sum_{p,r,m}\sum_{\substack{j\in\mathbb Z\\j\equiv a\!\!\pmod{H_0}}}  A_{p,r,m}\phi(j/J)v_{p,r,m}\otimes e_j  \mathbf 1_{pr-mj=h},  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-layer}$$ where $v_{p,r,m}$ is independent of $j$. If $\operatorname{supp}\widehat\phi\subset[-\Sigma,\Sigma]$, then the orbit Fourier variable $\beta$ changes the Poisson kernel from $m\alpha$ to $m\alpha-\beta$. The exact nonresonance interval is therefore $$\boxed{  \frac{\Omega+\Sigma/J}{M_-}  <\|\alpha\|_{\mathbb T}<  \frac{H_0^{-1}-\Omega-\Sigma/J}{M_+}.}  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-gap}$$ On this interval the shift Fourier transform of $\Pi_\Omega F$ vanishes identically. The statement is vector valued, uses no cancellation in $p,r,m$, and is uniform over their coefficients.

###### ref-3ba5cb244b8832fc

The actual orbit profiles are smooth rather than band limited. We use a fixed smooth cutoff in continuous Fourier space, $$\phi=\phi_{\le\Sigma}+\phi_{>\Sigma},  \qquad  \operatorname{supp}\widehat{\phi_{\le\Sigma}}  \subset[-\Sigma,\Sigma].  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-lowpass}$$ Every Schwartz seminorm of the remainder is smaller than an arbitrary negative power of $\Sigma$, provided a sufficiently high but fixed orbit seminorm of $\phi$ is used. TPC–36 controls the total signed projective mass with every fixed orbit seminorm. Hence the tails in [\[eq:tpc47-intro-lowpass\]](TPC-047.md#ref-3ba5cb244b8832fc) can be summed over the *entire* actual projective representation. No layer is selected and no arithmetic coefficient is smoothed.

At the endpoint $$M_\pm=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J=X^{133/400+o(1)},  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-endpoint-scales}$$ take $$\Omega=X^{1/400+o(1)}/J,  \qquad \Sigma=X^{1/400+o(1)}.  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-endpoint-bands}$$ After a harmless $X^\epsilon$ shrink at both ends, [\[eq:tpc47-intro-gap\]](TPC-047.md#ref-ac30db9d74dcdd81) becomes $$\boxed{  X^{-399/400+\epsilon}  \le\|\alpha\|_{\mathbb T}\le  X^{-267/400-\epsilon}.}  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-endpoint-gap}$$ The projected frozen shift family built from the literal physical $h_0$-coefficient field has leakage $O_{A,\epsilon}(X^{-A})$ on this interval relative to any of the declared polynomial coefficient envelopes. Thus the orbit-frequency gate is crossed on a growing sector whose time–bandwidth size is the same $X^{1/400+o(1)}$ scale as the endpoint TPC allowance.

## What the projection does and does not retain

###### ref-f17331ae0f20e97e

On one fixed opposite-row and residual-label fiber, let $E_I$ restrict the orbit coordinate to a physical interval of $N\asymp J$ integers. The band projection satisfies $$\|\Pi_\Omega\|=1,  \qquad  \|\Pi_\Omega E_I\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2  =\operatorname{tr}(E_I^*\Pi_\Omega E_I)=2H_0N\Omega.  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-shannon-number}$$ Thus $2H_0N\Omega\asymp X^{1/400+o(1)}$ is an exact trace identity, not an informal count of Fourier modes. Moreover, a nonzero finite-orbit vector cannot have zero projection onto an open frequency band: its Fourier transform is a nonzero trigonometric polynomial. The projected sector is therefore genuinely nonempty whenever the physical residual packet is nonzero.

###### ref-c21f1b4332f1943b

There is nevertheless no useful uniform lower bound for the retained mass. Explicit finite-difference vectors $y_n$, supported inside a physical orbit window, satisfy $$\frac{\|\Pi_\Omega y_n\|_2}{\|y_n\|_2}  \ll n^{1/4}\Omega^{1/2}(C_{H_0}\Omega)^n.  \label{eq:tpc47-intro-small-mass}$$ Consequently, the theorem controls a canonical actual-residual-output sector but does not control the orthogonal complement or the norm of the full equality column. This is a sharp distinction between a spectral transfer theorem and a fixed-shift TPC estimate.

## Claim ledger

The results are organized as follows.

1.  identifies the physical large-prime packet and its frozen-coefficient shift extension.

2.  constructs the literal fiberwise orbit projector, proves its exact trace, and records the commuting residual structure.

3.  proves [\[eq:tpc47-intro-gap\]](TPC-047.md#ref-ac30db9d74dcdd81), including the fixed progression.

4.  gives the low-pass/tail decomposition and quantitative leakage bounds.

5.  transfers those bounds to the full actual source–mask coefficient packet.

6.  proves the rational endpoint ledger.

7.  proves nonvanishing but disproves a uniform retained-mass fraction.

8.  separates the remaining gates, and [10](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-047.md#src-798f1d03fa7208a6) records the exact finite certificate and claim boundary.

Only the column $h=h_0$ in the frozen family is the inherited physical equality column. The other shifts keep its coefficient field fixed in order to expose the spectrum. We do not identify them with TPC packets reconstructed at new physical shifts. We also do not deduce atomic normalization, alias unfolding, a deterministic fixed-shift Möbius estimate, a parity breach, or a prime-pair asymptotic.

# The inherited physical-coefficient sector

We record the exact object to which the orbit projection is applied. The purpose is to prevent a low-frequency statement about a model coefficient from being substituted for the actual source–mask field.

## Exact projective layers of the full coefficient

TPC–36 proves the pointwise actual-multiplier representation, and TPC–46 inserts the large-prime and Mellin separations. Their combined interface gives the following statement [`\citep{WangTPC36,WangTPC46}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-7286aa6023fa493f); [\[WangTPC46\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-2f37865d719a8eb5).

> **Proposition: Inherited actual-coefficient reassembly** \[prop:tpc47-inherited-reassembly\] For each fixed integer $B\ge0$, the full frozen left family is a Bochner signed superposition of layers $$\mathcal F_h^\omega  =\sum_{p,r,m}\sum_{\substack{j\in\mathbb Z\\                       j\equiv a_\omega\!\!\pmod{H_0}}}  A_\omega(p,r,m)\phi_\omega(j/J)  v_{\omega,p,r,m}\otimes e_j\,  \mathbf 1_{pr-mj=h},  \label{eq:tpc47-projective-layer}$$ where
>
> 1.  the prime restriction and terminal Mellin factor are retained in $A_\omega$;
>
> 2.  $\mu(r)$ is retained in $A_\omega$, while the literal source and mask coefficient is retained in its $m$-factor;
>
> 3.  $v_{\omega,p,r,m}\in\mathcal V_L$ is independent of $j$ and is supported on coordinates $(\gamma,d(m)r)$; and
>
> 4.  after multiplication by one common fixed compactly supported cutoff that equals one on the physical normalized orbit range, $\phi_\omega\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$, with $$\int \mathfrak a_\omega        \|\phi_\omega\|_{C^B}\,d|\nu|(\omega)        \ll_{B,h_0}X^{o(1)}.        \label{eq:tpc47-projective-seminorm-mass}$$
>
> Here $\mathfrak a_\omega$ is the product of the declared sup-norm weights in the arithmetic and opposite-row factors. More strongly, the construction is coefficientwise before the terminal sum: for every supported $m,r$, $$\begin{aligned}  &\sum_{\gamma\in\mathcal O_L}  \gamma_m^{(1)}\mathfrak A_{m,\gamma}^{\rm act}(j)  e_{\gamma,d(m)r}  \notag\\  &\quad={}  \sum_{a\bmod H_0}\mathbf 1_{j\equiv a\!\!\pmod{H_0}}  \int c_{\omega,a}(m)\phi_{\omega,a}(j/J)  q_{\omega,a}^{m,r}\,d\nu_a(\omega),  \label{eq:tpc47-coefficientwise-reassembly}\end{aligned}$$ where $$q_{\omega,a}^{m,r}  =\sum_{\gamma\in\mathcal O_L}q_{\omega,a}(\gamma)  e_{\gamma,d(m)r},  \label{eq:tpc47-coefficientwise-output-vector}$$ and the integral of $\|c_{\omega,a}\|_\infty \|q_{\omega,a}\|_\infty \|\phi_{\omega,a}\|_{C^B}$ is $X^{o(1)}$. In particular, $$\mathcal F_h^{\rm fr,L}=\int\mathcal F_h^\omega\,d\nu(\omega)  \label{eq:tpc47-full-layer-integral}$$ exactly, with total signed projective mass $X^{o(1)}$ at every fixed orbit differentiability order.

> **Proof** The global row-mask theorem of TPC–36 separates the source row, the opposite row, and a smooth orbit profile after splitting the fixed residue cells. Its fixed-column refinement may additionally split $m=\ell d$, but no such split is needed here. Multiplication by the source coefficient preserves $X^{o(1)}$ projective mass. Mellin inversion separates $\Lambda(pr)$, while the large-prime descent inserts $\mu(r)$ and the literal residual coordinate $d(m)r$. Grouping the opposite-row factor into $v_{\omega,p,r,m}$ gives [\[eq:tpc47-projective-layer\]](TPC-047.md#ref-e37c962303f9ce92). The inherited orbit factors are smooth on a fixed normalized interval. A common cutoff equal to one there makes them compactly supported without changing a physical coefficient and preserves every fixed seminorm estimate. These are exactly the claims in [\[eq:tpc47-projective-seminorm-mass\]](TPC-047.md#ref-999498dbf965aa98) and [\[eq:tpc47-coefficientwise-reassembly\]](TPC-047.md#ref-cc09e90a8c78346b). Multiplication by the terminal factors and summation over $(p,r,m)$ then gives [\[eq:tpc47-full-layer-integral\]](TPC-047.md#ref-a3be8af8d596e0cf).

The signed integral in [\[eq:tpc47-full-layer-integral\]](TPC-047.md#ref-a3be8af8d596e0cf) must remain intact. Selecting a favorable $\omega$ would not define a bounded projection of the physical multiplier. The orbit projection below is instead a single orthogonal operator applied after the complete sum has been reassembled.

# The literal fiberwise orbit projection

###### ref-bc937b9f059ef2bf

###### ref-dde070672591c3da

Let $\mathcal V$ be any Hilbert space carrying all non-orbit output labels, and put $$\widetilde\mathcal H=\mathcal V\widehat\otimes\ell^2(\mathbb Z).  \label{eq:tpc47-abstract-output-space}$$ The second tensor factor is the integer orbit coordinate after zero extension. The unitary map $$\mathscr F_j:\ell^2(\mathbb Z)\longrightarrow L^2(\mathbb T),  \qquad  (\mathscr F_jy)(\beta)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}y_j\mathrm e(-j\beta),  \label{eq:tpc47-orbit-Fourier-transform}$$ is first defined on finitely supported sequences and then extended by Plancherel.

## The residue-comb band

###### ref-95dbe154da7aebd9

###### ref-ea431ca0a3d1f7fe

Fix $H_0\in\mathbb N$ and $0<\Omega<1/(2H_0)$. The fixed progression selector has Fourier branches at $H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z/\mathbb Z$, so the natural low band is the complete residue comb $$\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}  :=\left\{\beta\in\mathbb T:  \operatorname{dist}(\beta,H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z)\le\Omega\right\}.  \label{eq:tpc47-frequency-comb}$$ It is the disjoint union of $H_0$ arcs and has measure $$|\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}|=2H_0\Omega.  \label{eq:tpc47-comb-measure}$$

###### ref-3fe456fe2563edee

###### ref-87035815850bfdd2

> **Definition: Fiberwise orbit-comb projector** \[def:tpc47-orbit-projector\] On $\widetilde\mathcal H$, define $$\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}  :=I_{\mathcal V}\otimes  \mathscr F_j^{-1}M_{\mathbf 1_{\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}}}\mathscr F_j.  \label{eq:tpc47-projector-definition}$$ The same projection is applied in every opposite-row and residual-label fiber.

###### ref-0744cab3e4415e64

###### ref-c1e0715c843e18fb

###### ref-31431a901ee6b930

> **Proposition: Orthogonality and commutation** \[prop:tpc47-projector-structure\] The operator $\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$ is an orthogonal projection of norm one. If $S$ is any bounded operator on $\mathcal V$, then $$(S\otimes I)\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}  =\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}(S\otimes I).  \label{eq:tpc47-projector-commutation}$$ In particular, it commutes with opposite-row coordinate projections, residual-label projections, and the residual Möbius unitary $$(Uy)_{\gamma,s,j}=\mu(s)y_{\gamma,s,j}  \label{eq:tpc47-residual-unitary}$$ on the supported squarefree residual space.

> **Proof** Multiplication by the indicator of $\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$ is self-adjoint and idempotent. Unitarity of $\mathscr F_j$ proves the first assertion. The two sides of [\[eq:tpc47-projector-commutation\]](TPC-047.md#ref-c1e0715c843e18fb) act on separate tensor factors.

This construction retains the literal product label $s=d(m)r$. Projecting after first merging distinct residual labels would be a different operation and would lose the TPC–45 residual geometry.

## Zero extension and physical compression

###### ref-fe526ac0eaab345c

###### ref-06d2cafb925c0e53

Let $I\subset\mathbb Z$ be a physical orbit interval of $N$ integers, and let $$E_I:\mathcal V\widehat\otimes\ell^2(I)\longrightarrow\widetilde\mathcal H  \label{eq:tpc47-zero-extension}$$ be zero extension. It is an isometry. The range of $\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}E_I$ is the projected sector used below. The compression $$E_I^*\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}E_I  \label{eq:tpc47-compressed-projector}$$ is a positive contraction on the finite-orbit-window space, but it is generally not idempotent: the orthogonal projection creates orbit tails outside $I$. Thus $\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$ is a literal projection in the canonical enlarged space, not a coordinatewise deletion inside the finite window.

###### ref-11b1e48797bdce88

For one scalar fiber, the convolution kernel of the projector is $$\begin{aligned}  \kappa_{\Omega,H_0}(n)  &:=\int_{\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}}\mathrm e(n\beta)\,d\beta  \notag\\  &=\left(\sum_{\tau=0}^{H_0-1}\mathrm e(n\tau/H_0)\right)  \begin{cases}  2\Omega,&n=0,\\[3pt]  \displaystyle\frac{\sin(2\pi\Omega n)}{\pi n},&n\ne0.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc47-comb-sinc-kernel}\end{aligned}$$ In particular, it vanishes unless $H_0\mid n$, apart from the diagonal convention already contained in the formula.

###### ref-b1f08eb922b455c4

###### ref-e41409c2368e0fe6

> **Theorem: Exact physical-window trace** \[thm:tpc47-time-bandwidth-trace\] On one scalar orbit fiber, $$\boxed{  \operatorname{tr}  \bigl(E_I^*\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}E_I\bigr)  =\|\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}E_I\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2  =2H_0N\Omega.}  \label{eq:tpc47-time-bandwidth-trace}$$ For a finite-dimensional non-orbit space of dimension $d$, the trace is $2dH_0N\Omega$.

> **Proof** The diagonal matrix coefficient is the measure of the band, $$\langle\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}e_j,e_j\rangle  =2H_0\Omega.$$ Summing over $j\in I$ gives the trace. Orthogonality gives $$\|\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}E_I\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2  =\operatorname{tr}  (E_I^*(\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)})^2E_I),$$ which is the same quantity. Tensoring with a finite-dimensional identity repeats the trace in every non-orbit coordinate.

The number $2H_0N\Omega$ is an exact time–bandwidth trace. The range of $\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$ is infinite dimensional, so this number is not its rank.

## Interaction with the shift transform

###### ref-bbb1522c74331a44

###### ref-62625eedb3f118fb

###### ref-964c5994ffd5b8c4

For a finitely supported family $(F_h)_h\subset\widetilde\mathcal H$, boundedness gives $$\sum_h\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F_h\mathrm e(-h\alpha)  =\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}\sum_hF_h\mathrm e(-h\alpha).  \label{eq:tpc47-projector-shift-commutation}$$ If $$F_h=\sum_{p,r,m,j}A_{p,r,m}\phi(j/J)  v_{p,r,m}\otimes e_j\,\mathbf 1_{pr-mj=h},  \label{eq:tpc47-projector-model-packet}$$ then, in the orbit Fourier representation, $$\begin{aligned}  &(I_{\mathcal V}\otimes\mathscr F_j)  \widehat{\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F}(\alpha)(\beta)  \notag\\  &\quad={}  \mathbf 1_{\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}}(\beta)  \sum_{p,r,m}A_{p,r,m}v_{p,r,m}\mathrm e(-pr\alpha)  \sum_j\phi(j/J)\mathrm e\bigl(j(m\alpha-\beta)\bigr).  \label{eq:tpc47-projected-orbit-kernel-preview}\end{aligned}$$ The replacement of $m\alpha$ by $m\alpha-\beta$ is the transfer mechanism.

###### ref-50be92a179aceaa5

###### ref-32c36bacb5c124c7

> **Remark: A forbidden commutation** \[rem:tpc47-no-multiplier-commutation\] In general $$\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}M_{\phi(j/J)}  \ne M_{\phi(j/J)}\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}.  \label{eq:tpc47-forbidden-commutation}$$ We apply the projector after the complete coefficient has been multiplied and analyze the resulting convolution. No orbit profile is commuted through the projector.

# Exact orbit-band to shift-spectrum transfer

We first isolate the support theorem. No primality, Möbius cancellation, or source-mask estimate is used in this section.

###### ref-9fa9a1b5c7186187

Let $0<M_-\le M_+$, let $m$ range over finitely many integers in $[M_-,M_+]$, and let $\mathcal V$ be a Hilbert space. For one residue class $a\pmod{H_0}$, define $$F_h=\sum_{p,r,m}\sum_{\substack{j\in\mathbb Z\\                     j\equiv a\!\!\pmod{H_0}}}  A_{p,r,m}\phi(j/J)v_{p,r,m}\otimes e_j  \mathbf 1_{pr-mj=h}.  \label{eq:tpc47-bandlimited-packet}$$ The arithmetic supports are finite, $v_{p,r,m}\in\mathcal V$ is independent of $j$, and $\phi\in\mathscr S(\mathbb R)$.

## The two-frequency kernel

###### ref-c8198c27a8c27591

For $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb T$, set $$K_{m,a}(\alpha,\beta)  :=\sum_{\substack{j\in\mathbb Z\\j\equiv a\!\!\pmod{H_0}}}  \phi(j/J)\mathrm e\bigl(j(m\alpha-\beta)\bigr).  \label{eq:tpc47-two-frequency-kernel}$$

###### ref-63dfcfe1dcca90e3

###### ref-735654a91e879ebf

###### ref-c8b7b311f4b913d0

###### ref-5d170431e75e1c10

> **Lemma: Progression Poisson formula** \[lem:tpc47-progression-Poisson\] For every $\alpha,\beta\in\mathbb T$, $$K_{m,a}(\alpha,\beta)  =\frac{J}{H_0}\sum_{q\in\mathbb Z}  \mathrm e(aq/H_0)  \widehat\phi\!\left(  \frac{J}{H_0}\bigl(q-H_0(m\alpha-\beta)\bigr)  \right).  \label{eq:tpc47-progression-Poisson}$$ If $$\operatorname{supp}\widehat\phi\subset[-\Sigma,\Sigma],  \label{eq:tpc47-profile-bandlimit}$$ then $$K_{m,a}(\alpha,\beta)\ne0  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad  \operatorname{dist}  \bigl(m\alpha-\beta,H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z\bigr)  \le\frac{\Sigma}{J}.  \label{eq:tpc47-kernel-support-condition}$$

> **Proof** Write $j=a+H_0t$ and apply Poisson summation in $t$. This gives [\[eq:tpc47-progression-Poisson\]](TPC-047.md#ref-735654a91e879ebf). Under [\[eq:tpc47-profile-bandlimit\]](TPC-047.md#ref-c8b7b311f4b913d0), a nonzero summand requires $$\left|q-H_0(m\alpha-\beta)\right|  \le H_0\Sigma/J,$$ which is equivalent to [\[eq:tpc47-kernel-support-condition\]](TPC-047.md#ref-5d170431e75e1c10).

###### ref-0d490b4fb6e9eec3

The shift and orbit Fourier transforms of the projected packet are therefore $$\begin{aligned}  &(I_{\mathcal V}\otimes\mathscr F_j)  \widehat{\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F}(\alpha)(\beta)  \notag\\  &\quad={}  \mathbf 1_{\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}}(\beta)  \sum_{p,r,m}A_{p,r,m}v_{p,r,m}  \mathrm e(-pr\alpha)K_{m,a}(\alpha,\beta).  \label{eq:tpc47-exact-modal-factorization}\end{aligned}$$

## The exact projected gap

###### ref-c7d1aa6ee16dfc0d

Put $$W:=\Omega+\frac{\Sigma}{J}.  \label{eq:tpc47-total-bandwidth}$$

###### ref-28463fd9002b79fd

###### ref-789ef2e278be0f35

###### ref-033ad44da2363135

###### ref-84dc7ee0def78eb7

###### ref-d96040f667493074

> **Theorem: Exact orbit-band transfer** \[thm:tpc47-exact-transfer\] Assume [\[eq:tpc47-profile-bandlimit\]](TPC-047.md#ref-c8b7b311f4b913d0) and $$0<W<\frac1{2H_0}.  \label{eq:tpc47-band-separation}$$ Then $$\boxed{  \widehat{\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F}(\alpha)=0}  \label{eq:tpc47-exact-projected-vanishing}$$ whenever $$\boxed{  \frac{W}{M_-}  <\|\alpha\|_{\mathbb T}<  \frac{H_0^{-1}-W}{M_+}.}  \label{eq:tpc47-exact-projected-gap}$$ The assertion is uniform in the coefficients and Hilbert vectors in [\[eq:tpc47-bandlimited-packet\]](TPC-047.md#ref-9fa9a1b5c7186187). The assertion is vacuous unless $$\frac{W}{M_-}<\frac{H_0^{-1}-W}{M_+}.   \label{eq:tpc47-nonempty-exact-gap}$$

###### ref-512e4715ab89b06b

###### ref-9a87d0caf46fa5a1

> **Proof** Let $t=\|\alpha\|_{\mathbb T}$ and choose a representative with $|\alpha|=t$. For every supported $m$, $$W<mt<H_0^{-1}-W.  \label{eq:tpc47-malpha-interior}$$ If $\beta\in\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$, choose $q/H_0$ with $|\beta-q/H_0|\le\Omega$. The finite set $H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z/\mathbb Z$ is a subgroup of $\mathbb T$, so $$\begin{aligned}  \operatorname{dist}  (m\alpha-\beta,H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z)  &\ge  \operatorname{dist}(m\alpha,H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z)-\Omega  \notag\\  &>W-\Omega=\Sigma/J.  \label{eq:tpc47-band-distance-lower}\end{aligned}$$ Here [\[eq:tpc47-malpha-interior\]](TPC-047.md#ref-512e4715ab89b06b) places $m\alpha$ strictly between the two nearest subgroup points $0$ and $\operatorname{sgn}(\alpha)/H_0$. Thus [\[eq:tpc47-kernel-support-condition\]](TPC-047.md#ref-5d170431e75e1c10) fails for every $m$ and almost every $\beta$ retained by the projector. Formula [\[eq:tpc47-exact-modal-factorization\]](TPC-047.md#ref-0d490b4fb6e9eec3) is zero in $L^2(\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)};\mathcal V)$, proving the theorem.

###### ref-ea9292d10fd470e3

> **Corollary: Signed projective superpositions** \[cor:tpc47-projective-exact-gap\] Let $F=\int F^\omega\,d\nu(\omega)$, where every layer has the form [\[eq:tpc47-bandlimited-packet\]](TPC-047.md#ref-9fa9a1b5c7186187), the same $M_\pm,H_0,J$, and Fourier bandwidth at most $\Sigma$. If the integral is absolutely convergent in $\ell^1(\mathbb Z;\widetilde\mathcal H)$, then [\[eq:tpc47-exact-projected-vanishing\]](TPC-047.md#ref-033ad44da2363135) holds for the full signed superposition on [\[eq:tpc47-exact-projected-gap\]](TPC-047.md#ref-84dc7ee0def78eb7).

> **Proof** The projected transform of each layer is zero on the same interval. Boundedness of the projector and absolute convergence permit the Bochner integral to pass through both Fourier transforms.

###### ref-e469891adb55921e

###### ref-0641b643e37c91f9

###### ref-aa2b26fce0066bc8

> **Corollary: Vector-valued coefficient band** \[cor:tpc47-vector-coefficient-band\] For each $(p,r,m)$, let $g_{p,r,m}\in\ell^2(\mathbb Z;\mathcal V)$ satisfy $$\operatorname{supp}\mathscr F_jg_{p,r,m}  \subset\mathcal B_{\Sigma/J}^{(H_0)}.  \label{eq:tpc47-vector-profile-support}$$ Define $$F_h=\sum_{p,r,m,j}A_{p,r,m}g_{p,r,m}(j)\otimes e_j  \mathbf 1_{pr-mj=h}.  \label{eq:tpc47-vector-profile-packet}$$ Then [\[eq:tpc47-exact-projected-vanishing\]](TPC-047.md#ref-033ad44da2363135) holds on [\[eq:tpc47-exact-projected-gap\]](TPC-047.md#ref-84dc7ee0def78eb7), with $W=\Omega+\Sigma/J$. The shift transform is understood in $L^2(\mathbb T;\widetilde\mathcal H)$; because the arithmetic support is finite, the modal expression also converges in $\widetilde\mathcal H$ for each $\alpha$.

> **Proof** In the orbit Fourier representation, the $(p,r,m)$-term at output frequency $\beta$ is $\widehat g_{p,r,m}(\beta-m\alpha)$. It can meet $\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$ only if $$d_{H_0}(m\alpha)  \le\Omega+\Sigma/J.$$ The interval [\[eq:tpc47-exact-projected-gap\]](TPC-047.md#ref-84dc7ee0def78eb7) excludes this for every supported $m$, exactly as in the proof of [\[thm:tpc47-exact-transfer\]](TPC-047.md#ref-28463fd9002b79fd).

###### ref-515ae73d8e3686c9

> **Remark: Amplitude, not atomic energy** \[rem:tpc47-amplitude-not-energy\] The vanishing concerns the Hilbert-valued amplitude $h\mapsto\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F_h$. It does not imply a gap for $h\mapsto\|F_h\|^2$, because squaring convolves shift frequencies. Nor does it compare the synthesis at one shift with the atomic diagonal at that shift.

###### ref-ab8ecc10bd862311

> **Remark: Why the whole residue comb is kept** \[rem:tpc47-residue-comb\] The selector $\mathbf 1_{j\equiv a\pmod{H_0}}$ has all $H_0$ Fourier branches. Projecting only near zero would define a smaller valid sector, but it would suppress the other inherited residue modes. The comb [\[eq:tpc47-frequency-comb\]](TPC-047.md#ref-95dbe154da7aebd9) retains them all and changes the time–bandwidth trace only by the fixed factor $H_0$.

# Smooth profiles: rapid leakage and a fixed core

The actual orbit profiles are smooth, not exactly band limited. We give two complementary formulations: a direct projected Poisson bound and an exact low-band core with a quantified remainder.

## Compact-support provenance

The required endpoint regularity is stronger than smooth values on a finite set. A hard cutoff in $j$ would create Dirichlet tails.

###### ref-d5975fc216ad3429

> **Lemma: Inherited common orbit support** \[lem:tpc47-common-orbit-support\] The orbit profiles in [\[prop:tpc47-inherited-reassembly\]](TPC-047.md#ref-4379c9dea9d893c3) may be chosen in one fixed compact subset $K\subset\mathbb R$, independently of $X$ and of the projective parameter. For every fixed $B$, the signed projective mass of their $C^B(K)$ norms is $X^{o(1)}$.

> **Proof** The provenance theorem of TPC–25 places every row-dependent smooth orbit product, in the normalized variable $t=j/J$, in a fixed bounded subset of $C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$, and its Mellin reassembly has integrable polynomial seminorms [`\citep{WangTPC25}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-39e8dc14c55a2411). TPC–36 imports that residue/Mellin representation and proves that multiplication by the actual static mask does not change the orbit seminorm mass [`\citep{WangTPC36}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-7286aa6023fa493f). The large-prime and residual rewrites in TPC–45 and TPC–46 change arithmetic and Hilbert labels but do not modify the orbit profile. This proves the statement.

This lemma is the point at which the physical smooth localization is used. Without it, arbitrary-power leakage below would be false.

## Direct smooth decay

###### ref-8499c948097445cf

For $\theta\in\mathbb T$, write $$d_{H_0}(\theta)  :=\operatorname{dist}(\theta,H_0^{-1}\mathbb Z).  \label{eq:tpc47-subgroup-distance}$$

###### ref-f37d03f3c855e82c

###### ref-e5997a342cef50c1

> **Lemma: Projected progression profile** \[lem:tpc47-projected-smooth-profile\] Let $\phi\in C_c^\infty(K)$. For every integer $B\ge1$, $$\begin{aligned}  &\left\|  \Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}  \left(  \mathbf 1_{j\equiv a\!\!\pmod{H_0}}  \phi(j/J)\mathrm e(mj\alpha)  \right)  \right\|_{\ell^2(\mathbb Z)}  \notag\\  &\qquad\ll_{B,H_0,K}  (\Omega J^2)^{1/2}  \left(1+J\bigl(d_{H_0}(m\alpha)-\Omega\bigr)_+\right)^{-B}  \|\phi\|_{C^{B+2}}.  \label{eq:tpc47-projected-smooth-bound}\end{aligned}$$ Equivalently, if $\Omega=\Lambda/J$, the leading factor is $O_{H_0}((\Lambda J)^{1/2})$.

###### ref-5bde34bb0ec43fb4

> **Proof** In the orbit Fourier representation the squared norm is the integral, over $\beta\in\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$, of the squared modulus of $K_{m,a}(\alpha,\beta)$ from [\[eq:tpc47-two-frequency-kernel\]](TPC-047.md#ref-c8198c27a8c27591). Repeated integration by parts gives $$|\widehat\phi(\xi)|  \ll_{B,K}(1+|\xi|)^{-B-2}\|\phi\|_{C^{B+2}}.$$ Insert this in [\[eq:tpc47-progression-Poisson\]](TPC-047.md#ref-735654a91e879ebf) and sum the integer translates. Uniformly in $\alpha,\beta$, $$|K_{m,a}(\alpha,\beta)|  \ll_{B,H_0,K}  J\left(1+Jd_{H_0}(m\alpha-\beta)\right)^{-B}  \|\phi\|_{C^{B+2}}.  \label{eq:tpc47-smooth-kernel-pointwise}$$ For $\beta$ in the comb band, $$d_{H_0}(m\alpha-\beta)  \ge\bigl(d_{H_0}(m\alpha)-\Omega\bigr)_+.$$ Integrating [\[eq:tpc47-smooth-kernel-pointwise\]](TPC-047.md#ref-5bde34bb0ec43fb4) over a set of measure $2H_0\Omega$ proves [\[eq:tpc47-projected-smooth-bound\]](TPC-047.md#ref-e5997a342cef50c1).

###### ref-2f76cad362a98dc2

For the packet [\[eq:tpc47-bandlimited-packet\]](TPC-047.md#ref-9fa9a1b5c7186187), define the coefficient envelope $$\mathcal M_1(F):=\sum_{p,r,m}|A_{p,r,m}|\|v_{p,r,m}\|_{\mathcal V}.  \label{eq:tpc47-l1-envelope}$$

###### ref-87cbdbd0a2453615

###### ref-d7c4af2ae759501e

> **Corollary: Direct smooth packet bound** \[cor:tpc47-direct-smooth-packet\] Under the hypotheses of [\[lem:tpc47-projected-smooth-profile\]](TPC-047.md#ref-f37d03f3c855e82c), $$\begin{aligned}  \|\widehat{\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F}(\alpha)\|  &\ll_{B,H_0,K}  (\Omega J^2)^{1/2}\mathcal M_1(F)\|\phi\|_{C^{B+2}}  \notag\\  &\quad\times  \max_{M_-\le m\le M_+}  \left(1+J(d_{H_0}(m\alpha)-\Omega)_+\right)^{-B}.  \label{eq:tpc47-direct-smooth-packet-bound}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** Use the modal factorization [\[eq:tpc47-exact-modal-factorization\]](TPC-047.md#ref-0d490b4fb6e9eec3), the triangle inequality in $L^2(\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)};\mathcal V)$, and [\[lem:tpc47-projected-smooth-profile\]](TPC-047.md#ref-f37d03f3c855e82c).

## A fixed band-limited core

###### ref-832a8b9bbdbf2ad9

###### ref-fa91932efebf9ac4

Choose once and for all $\chi\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R)$ satisfying $$0\le\chi\le1,  \qquad  \chi(\xi)=1\ \ (|\xi|\le1/2),  \qquad  \chi(\xi)=0\ \ (|\xi|\ge1).  \label{eq:tpc47-frequency-cutoff}$$ For $J\ge1$ and $\Sigma\ge2$, define $$\begin{aligned}  \widehat{\phi_{\le\Sigma}}(\xi)  &:=\chi(\xi/\Sigma)\widehat\phi(\xi),  &\phi_{>\Sigma}&:=\phi-\phi_{\le\Sigma}.  \label{eq:tpc47-profile-low-high-split}\end{aligned}$$ The low component has outer Fourier half-width exactly at most $\Sigma$, so [\[thm:tpc47-exact-transfer\]](TPC-047.md#ref-28463fd9002b79fd) applies with $W=\Omega+\Sigma/J$.

###### ref-6c42ac8bad8702d9

###### ref-6526b5f9aa9ecba6

> **Lemma: Sampled high-frequency tail** \[lem:tpc47-sampled-tail\] Assume $J\ge1$. For every integer $B\ge2$, uniformly over $\phi\in C_c^\infty(K)$, $$\left(  \sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}|\phi_{>\Sigma}(j/J)|^2  \right)^{1/2}  \ll_{B,K}  J^{1/2}\Sigma^{1/2-B}\|\phi\|_{C^{B+2}}.  \label{eq:tpc47-sampled-tail-bound}$$ The same estimate holds after restriction to any progression modulo $H_0$.

###### ref-f2eef574dd7115a7

###### ref-2778770adc69c033

> **Proof** Repeated integration by parts gives $|\widehat\phi(\xi)|\ll_{B,K} (1+|\xi|)^{-B}\|\phi\|_{C^B}$. Plancherel and the support of $1-\chi(\xi/\Sigma)$ therefore give $$\|\phi_{>\Sigma}\|_{L^2}+  \|\phi_{>\Sigma}'\|_{L^2}  \ll_{B,K}\Sigma^{1/2-B}\|\phi\|_{C^{B+2}}.  \label{eq:tpc47-continuous-tail-Sobolev}$$ For every $g\in H^1(\mathbb R)$, the elementary sampling inequality on the intervals $[j/J,(j+1)/J]$ is $$\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}|g(j/J)|^2  \ll J\bigl(\|g\|_{L^2}^2+\|g'\|_{L^2}^2\bigr).  \label{eq:tpc47-sampling-Sobolev}$$ Apply this to $g=\phi_{>\Sigma}$ and use [\[eq:tpc47-continuous-tail-Sobolev\]](TPC-047.md#ref-f2eef574dd7115a7). A progression is a subsequence, so its sum is no larger.

###### ref-ff4ed5708647a36d

###### ref-c4ddeb1197be3a3d

###### ref-46b14b60ea629a69

###### ref-91ce137886316cf1

> **Theorem: Exact core plus controlled leakage** \[thm:tpc47-core-tail-transfer\] Let $F=F_{\le\Sigma}+F_{>\Sigma}$ be obtained from [\[eq:tpc47-bandlimited-packet\]](TPC-047.md#ref-9fa9a1b5c7186187) by applying [\[eq:tpc47-profile-low-high-split\]](TPC-047.md#ref-fa91932efebf9ac4). On the exact gap $$\frac{\Omega+\Sigma/J}{M_-}  <\|\alpha\|_{\mathbb T}<  \frac{H_0^{-1}-\Omega-\Sigma/J}{M_+},  \label{eq:tpc47-core-exact-gap}$$ one has $$\begin{aligned}  \widehat{\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F_{\le\Sigma}}(\alpha)&=0,  \label{eq:tpc47-core-zero}\\  \|\widehat{\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}F}(\alpha)\|  &\le  C_{B,H_0,K}J^{1/2}\Sigma^{1/2-B}  \mathcal M_1(F)\|\phi\|_{C^{B+2}}.  \label{eq:tpc47-tail-leakage-envelope}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** The first identity is [\[thm:tpc47-exact-transfer\]](TPC-047.md#ref-28463fd9002b79fd). Hence the full projected transform equals the projected transform of the high component. Modulation by $\mathrm e(mj\alpha)$ is unitary on $\ell^2(\mathbb Z)$, and the projector is contractive. Apply the triangle inequality over $(p,r,m)$, followed by [\[lem:tpc47-sampled-tail\]](TPC-047.md#ref-6c42ac8bad8702d9).

The tail estimate is deliberately expressed relative to the declared envelope $\mathcal M_1(F)$. It contains no unproved cancellation among the arithmetic coefficients.

# Transfer to the full actual-coefficient residual packet

We apply the output projector $\Pi_\Omega^{(H_0)}$ to the fully reassembled coefficient field. The auxiliary coefficient projector $Q_\Sigma^{(H_0)}$ below supplies only a proof decomposition, while the projective representation is used only to prove regularity; neither defines the projected output sector.

## A representation-independent coefficient cutoff

###### ref-f18eb3bba9158428

###### ref-5fd0fc726e64ae96

For a supported source row $m=\ell d(m)$ and cofactor $r$, define the left vector profile $$G_{m,r}^{L}(j)  :=\sum_{\gamma\in\mathcal O_L}  \gamma_m^{(1)}  \mathfrak A_{m,\gamma}^{\rm act}(j)  e_{\gamma,d(m)r}  \in\mathcal V_L.  \label{eq:tpc47-full-vector-profile}$$ It is zero outside the physical orbit support. Then the frozen family from [2](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-047.md#src-3578f6bd5bcaf9a0) is $$\mathcal F_h^{\rm fr,L}  =\sum_{p,r,m,j}  \mu(r)\Lambda(pr)G_{m,r}^{L}(j)\otimes e_j  \mathbf 1_{pr-mj=h}.  \label{eq:tpc47-full-packet-vector-profile}$$

###### ref-77a58b8c2fb5b8d7

###### ref-059961bd5a32f562

###### ref-b60e1f425f2b906b

Let $$Q_\Sigma^{(H_0)}  :=\mathscr F_j^{-1}  M_{\mathbf 1_{\mathcal B_{\Sigma/J}^{(H_0)}}}\mathscr F_j  \label{eq:tpc47-coefficient-lowpass}$$ act on the $j$-variable of every $\mathcal V_L$-valued profile. Put $$G_{m,r}^{\rm lo}:=Q_\Sigma^{(H_0)}G_{m,r}^{L},  \qquad  G_{m,r}^{\rm hi}:=(I-Q_\Sigma^{(H_0)})G_{m,r}^{L}.  \label{eq:tpc47-full-profile-split}$$ This is a canonical orthogonal split of the full actual coefficient. It is independent of any choice of projective representation. Because $Q_\Sigma^{(H_0)}$ is a sharp Fourier projection, $G_{m,r}^{\rm lo}$ can have sinc tails and need not belong to $\ell^1(\mathbb Z;\mathcal V_L)$. All low/high frozen families below are therefore defined in $\ell^2$, and their shift transforms use the unitary partial Fourier transform. # The retained-mass frontier

The projected sector is not the zero subspace on a physical orbit window. Nevertheless, the projector can retain an arbitrarily small fraction of an ambient finite-orbit vector. No such smallness is asserted for the actual arithmetic packet. Both operator-theoretic statements are exact.

## Qualitative injectivity on every finite window

###### ref-69e6032211a4c037

###### ref-33ff8bd745391d57

###### ref-44e75f4ffa6a7048

> **Theorem: Finite-support injectivity** \[thm:tpc47-finite-support-injectivity\] Let $0<\Omega<1/(2H_0)$. If $y\in\mathcal V\widehat\otimes\ell^2(\mathbb Z)$ is finitely supported in the orbit coordinate, then $$\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}y=0  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=0.  \label{eq:tpc47-finite-injectivity}$$ Equivalently, for every finite interval $I$, the compression $$E_I^*\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}E_I  \label{eq:tpc47-positive-compression}$$ is strictly positive definite.

> **Proof** Pair $y$ with an arbitrary vector in $\mathcal V$. Its orbit Fourier transform is a Laurent polynomial in $z=\mathrm e(-\beta)$. If the projection is zero, this polynomial vanishes almost everywhere on every open arc in $\mathcal B_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$, hence identically. Every scalar pairing is zero, so all coefficients of $y$ vanish. Applying this to $E_Iy$ proves strict positivity of the compression.

Thus a nonzero finitely supported residual packet always has a nonzero projected component. This is only a qualitative statement; the smallest eigenvalue of [\[eq:tpc47-positive-compression\]](TPC-047.md#ref-44e75f4ffa6a7048) is not bounded below uniformly in the window length.

## Consequences for the TPC gate

The two theorems above establish the exact logical status of the sector:

1.  it is nonzero on every nonzero finite-orbit vector, hence is not vacuous if the finite physical residual packet is nonzero;

2.  on ambient finite-orbit vectors it need not retain a fixed, logarithmic, or even polynomially bounded fraction of the norm; no corresponding smallness is asserted for the actual arithmetic packet; and

3.  the trace identity [\[eq:tpc47-time-bandwidth-trace\]](TPC-047.md#ref-e41409c2368e0fe6) is an average spectral-size statement, not a lower bound for one arithmetic vector.

For example, $\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}e_0$ is the sinc-comb sequence [\[eq:tpc47-comb-sinc-kernel\]](TPC-047.md#ref-11b1e48797bdce88); it has nonzero coordinates away from zero although $e_0$ does not. Only the global Hilbert contraction $$\|\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}y\|_2\le\|y\|_2  \label{eq:tpc47-global-contraction}$$ is automatic.

# Boundaries and the next gate

The positive result is a transfer theorem for one canonical family of orbit bands. This section records every identification that would be needed before it could enter a fixed-shift prime-pair argument.

## Five distinctions that remain active

#### Residual synthesis versus the native affine face.

At $h_0$, the vector analyzed here is the actual-coefficient negative large-prime residual synthesis $-\sum_pUx_p$. TPC–45 obtains this object after averaging the lower prime signs. Its primewise norms agree with the corresponding native vectors, but its cross inner products do not generally agree with those of the physical affine face [`\citep{WangTPC45}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-1802a7e8047e595c). The grouping statement in [\[prop:tpc47-grouped-component\]](TPC-047.md#ref-95ac16d9f83cf9f8) recovers only the large-prime component and pays the exact residual-fiber norm.

#### Frozen shifts versus reconstructed physical shifts.

Only $h=h_0$ has inherited physical provenance. The shift transform uses every integer $h$ after freezing the $h_0$ coefficient field. For a newly reconstructed physical shift, the row set, residue factor, mask, and source residues may change. No growing-window uniformity theorem is asserted.

#### Amplitude spectrum versus atomic normalization.

Theorems [\[thm:tpc47-exact-transfer\]](TPC-047.md#ref-28463fd9002b79fd) and [\[thm:tpc47-actual-transfer\]](TPC-047.md#ref-b1115fb8cc9cc388) concern the Fourier transform of a Hilbert amplitude. Fourier inversion at one $h_0$ integrates all frequencies, including the uncontrolled complement. Moreover, the TPC denominator is the atomic diagonal at $h_0$, not the Cartesian energy over all determinant shifts. TPC–46 proves that these normalizations may differ by a polynomial factor [`\citep{WangTPC46}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-047.md#cite-2f37865d719a8eb5).

#### Consecutive completion versus sparse aliases.

The actual TPC completion samples determinant intercepts separated by the triple-prime modulus. Sparse sampling folds the completed shift spectrum. The present orbit projection neither unfolds those aliases nor proves that the equality-alias residual $d(m)r$ remains literal at nonzero aliases; there the squarefree kernel can replace the product.

#### One band family versus the full orbit space.

The complement $I-\Pi_{\Omega}^{(H_0)}$ is uncontrolled. By [\[thm:tpc47-no-mass-lower-bound\]](TPC-047.md#ref-8f2714d695f92691), qualitative injectivity of the low sector supplies no quantitative reconstruction. Summing separate pointwise bounds over the $X^{33/100+o(1)}$ comparable bands in [\[eq:tpc47-cover-count\]](TPC-047.md#ref-5a9b22c7e902ffd3) would introduce a prohibitive support factor unless orthogonality is retained through the later operations.

## What has genuinely changed

Before this paper, the orbit-output coupling was an arbitrary $j$-dependent Hilbert vector, so the TPC–46 spectral theorem could not be applied to any declared portion of the actual residual field. Now there is a representation-independent norm-one operator, applied to every output row and residual label, for which

1.  the full actual coefficient reassembles with its source mask and Möbius roles intact;

2.  a growing $X^{1/400+o(1)}$ time–bandwidth sector has super-polynomial leakage on the interval [\[eq:tpc47-final-endpoint-gap\]](TPC-047.md#ref-d8ee14dfe0c0e4c9); and

3.  the exact operator-theoretic obstruction to uniform recovery is proved, rather than hidden in a phrase such as “smooth output.”

This crosses Door A of TPC–46 on one bounded projected residual sector. It does not cross the other three doors.

## A reversible continuation

The next paper may pursue any of the following logically separate tasks.

1.  Partition the entire orbit circle into translated comb bands and prove a two-parameter resonance-tube square-function estimate that preserves orthogonality while the bands are reassembled. This avoids asking one low band to contain a fixed fraction of the vector.

2.  Use the literal TPC source/mask phases to prove a coefficient-specific spectral concentration or entropy bound. It must beat the arbitrary-vector counterexample in [\[thm:tpc47-no-mass-lower-bound\]](TPC-047.md#ref-8f2714d695f92691) by using arithmetic data.

3.  Combine orbit-band projection with the exact residual grouping map and prove that one additional cofactor range has acceptable grouping multiplicity at atomic normalization.

4.  Construct a family of actual masks or residual packets that saturates the high-frequency or grouping frontier. This would give a sharp negative boundary without asserting failure of every possible TPC continuation.

Door A is the most direct continuation: the projectors are orthogonal before reassembly, and the bandwise resonance condition is explicit. The missing theorem is a square-function or large-sieve estimate that survives determinant dilation, residual grouping, and fixed-shift localization. No such estimate is assumed here.

## Prohibited conclusions

Nothing in this paper proves or implies

1.  deterministic cancellation of Möbius coefficients at the prescribed physical shift;

2.  an atomic-normalized Bessel bound for the complete equality column;

3.  a uniform family of actual TPC packets over growing shifts;

4.  a parity-barrier breach, the Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair asymptotic, or the twin-prime conjecture.

The advance is sectorial: the canonical low core has an exact gap, while the projected frozen family built from the literal $h_0$-coefficient field has arbitrary-power leakage there. Its possible quantitative thinness is also proved.

## Analytic claim boundary

The following are unconditional theorems of the text:

1.  the orthogonal comb projector, its commutation rules, and the exact trace [\[eq:tpc47-time-bandwidth-trace\]](TPC-047.md#ref-e41409c2368e0fe6);

2.  exact projected vanishing for a band-limited progression profile;

3.  canonical low/high splitting of the complete actual coefficient and the leakage estimate [\[eq:tpc47-actual-leakage\]](TPC-047.md#ref-66228a065b490778) for the projected frozen family built from the literal physical $h_0$-coefficient field;

4.  the endpoint sector [\[eq:tpc47-final-endpoint-gap\]](TPC-047.md#ref-d8ee14dfe0c0e4c9);

5.  qualitative injectivity on finite orbit windows and the sharp absence of a uniform retained-mass bound; and

6.  the exact grouping norm in [\[eq:tpc47-grouping-norm\]](TPC-047.md#ref-1f0f6f96289bd6a6).

The certificate does not test an asymptotic Möbius cancellation statement. Neither the certificate nor the paper proves control of the high-frequency complement, atomic normalization at $h_0$, sparse alias unfolding, uniform local-shift provenance, a parity breach, the Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair asymptotic, or the twin-prime conjecture.
