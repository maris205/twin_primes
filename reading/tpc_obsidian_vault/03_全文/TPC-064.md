## Abstract

TPC–63 separates a literal full incidence from its pair projection and shows that a labeled multiscale completion is an isometry only after the completed incidence has been chosen. It leaves open a prior arrow: does a pair-populated or multiscale repair preserve the true pre-terminal parent, and what terminal lower constant remains after any added parent mass is charged?

We answer that finite-scale representation question at one fixed nonzero shift. On blockwise full incidences we define the literal parent mass $P_m$, terminal-support mass $H_m$, and shaped terminal energy $T_m$. The least parent-complete pair relation is the projection of the literal pre-terminal active incidence. Such a relation is also terminal-atomically faithful if and only if its terminal-active rectangle equals the literal terminal incidence. When the test fails no parent-complete terminal-faithful pair-rectangular lift exists, and our canonical fallback retains the literal active hypergraph; when it passes, the exact excess parent mass is $\Delta_m$. This yields a canonical hybrid repair and $$\widetilde P_m=P_m+\Delta_m,  \qquad  \mathfrak I_X=  \begin{cases}   +\infty,&\exists m:\ P_m=0<\widetilde P_m,\\   \displaystyle\max_{m:P_m>0}\frac{\widetilde P_m}{P_m},       &\text{otherwise},  \end{cases}$$ for a nonzero literal parent. The literal-active-hypergraph baseline has exactly zero parent inflation. A logarithmic number of block labels does not control $\mathfrak I_X$: arbitrary polynomial inflation can occur in one block.

True terminal activation factors, on positive rows, as $$\frac{T_m}{\widetilde P_m}  =\frac{H_m}{P_m}\,   \frac{T_m}{H_m}\,   \frac{P_m}{\widetilde P_m}.$$ We give zero-safe conventions, sharp uniform row minima, exact distinguished weighted averages, and the optimal transfer $\tau_{\rm rep}\ge\tau_{\rm hyp}/\mathfrak I_X$. For a coefficient vector satisfying a cap and an occupied-capacity lower bound, the threshold data $g_m=T_m/\widetilde P_m\ge\eta$ on a repaired-parent fraction $\delta_\eta$ give the sharp certificate $$\frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  \ge  \eta\left(1-\frac{1-\delta_\eta}{\kappa_X}\right)_+.$$ The certified right-hand side is positive exactly when $\kappa_X>1-\delta_\eta$.

Finally, we state a denominator-compatible chain and charge parent inflation once inside the effective terminal loss before applying the strict $1/400$ endpoint test. No favorable bound for $T_m/P_m$, $\delta_\eta$, $\kappa_X$, physical label erasure, or a signed fixed-shift kernel is proved. The result is an L0/L1 activation-interface theorem, not a parity estimate, a prime-pair lower bound, or a twin-prime result.

# Introduction

The fixed-shift program now has two distinct notions of “completion.” TPC–63 completes the logical provenance interface: a pair relation may be rectangularized only after its literal full incidence has passed the appropriate parent, terminal, or grouped-output test [`\citep{WangTPC63}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-822675dc51b7acfd). It also proves that retaining dyadic cofactor scales as orthogonal labels costs only $X^{o(1)}$ at the raw-energy level. Neither statement says that the chosen completed space has the same parent denominator as the coefficientwise physical field of TPC–47 [`\citep{WangTPC47}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-0276fa4f583cdc92).

That denominator matters. Suppose a rectangle adds coordinates which are terminal-null but carry nonzero pre-terminal coefficient. Its terminal numerator is unchanged, while its parent form is larger. Parseval on the enlarged space is exact but answers the wrong comparison unless the extra parent mass is recorded. Conversely, replacing the parent by the terminal-active incidence deletes the very alignment defect which the activation theorem is meant to estimate. The present paper isolates this missing arrow before any represented-Gram or signed-cancellation argument is attempted.

For each dyadic block $k$, let $\mathcal U_{X,k}$ be the literal full incidence and let $$\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}  =\{u:c_u\ne0\},  \qquad  \mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}  =\{u:t_h(u)c_u\ne0\}.$$ The literal row masses are $$\begin{aligned}  P_m&=\sum_{k}\sum_{\substack{u\in        \mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}\\m(u)=m}}|c_u|^2,  \label{eq:tpc64-intro-parent-mass}\\  H_m&=\sum_{k}\sum_{\substack{u\in        \mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}\\m(u)=m}}|c_u|^2,  \qquad  T_m=\sum_{k}\sum_{\substack{u\in        \mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}\\m(u)=m}}|t_h(u)c_u|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-intro-terminal-masses}\end{aligned}$$ All three are fixed before the free row coefficient is chosen. In the physical specialization, $t_h$ contains one inherited cofactor sign and the literal smooth terminal multiplier. It is not normalized to a probability.

###### ref-7c71fbbe16ced368

The first result is an exact existence theorem. Put $$I_{\mathrm{pre},k}  :=\operatorname{pr}_{m,r}    \mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}.$$ A pair relation which retains every literal parent atom and introduces no terminal-active spurious cross exists exactly when $$\operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})  =\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}.  \label{eq:tpc64-intro-faithful-test}$$ When it exists, $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$ is the unique least relation. Its parent is uninflated exactly when its pre-terminal rectangle is already the literal active incidence. Blocks which fail [\[eq:tpc64-intro-faithful-test\]](TPC-064.md#ref-7c71fbbe16ced368) admit no parent-complete faithful pair-rectangular lift. Our canonical fallback retains their literal active incidence, giving a pair-good/hypergraph hybrid.

###### ref-658f2ca070edc5bc

The second result measures the cost of every populated repair. With $\Delta_m$ the structural, $z$-independent squared mass of new terminal-null parent coordinates, $$\widetilde P_m=P_m+\Delta_m.  \label{eq:tpc64-intro-inflated-parent}$$ The optimal full-row comparison is the largest row inflation; a new-only row makes the comparison infinite. The literal-active-hypergraph realization has $\Delta_m=0$ identically. This distinguishes parent inflation from the Mellin-frame conditioning of TPC–51, the capacity saturation of TPC–54, and the physical label-erasure loss of TPC–62 [`\citep{WangTPC51,WangTPC54,WangTPC62}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-09a584963bbbf4f9); [\[WangTPC54\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-6f1a8ec95b6b34b5); [\[WangTPC62\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-7e6a7d2f67f67940).

###### ref-bfabf65e3df0b12b

The third result gives the correct activation quantity. On rows with all denominators positive, $$\frac{T_m}{\widetilde P_m}  =\underbrace{\frac{H_m}{P_m}}_{\text{terminal-support alignment}}   \underbrace{\frac{T_m}{H_m}}_{\text{literal multiplier intensity}}   \underbrace{\frac{P_m}{\widetilde P_m}}_{\text{inverse inflation}}.  \label{eq:tpc64-intro-three-factor}$$ The paper extends this identity through all zero rows, proves the sharp uniform and distinguished minimax laws, and shows that the loss $\mathfrak I_X^{-1}$ in passing from the literal hypergraph parent to the repaired parent cannot be improved from representation data alone.

###### ref-4bbb0b8f68d3d82a

There remains an honest distinguished alternative inherited from the sharp energy-selection interface of TPC–54. If good rows $g_m=T_m/\widetilde P_m\ge\eta$ occupy a structural repaired-parent fraction $\delta_\eta$, while a coefficient vector occupies at least a fraction $\kappa_X$ of the available capped energy, then $$\frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  \ge  \eta\left(1-\frac{1-\delta_\eta}{\kappa_X}\right)_+.  \label{eq:tpc64-intro-capacity}$$ We prove that this bound and the threshold $\kappa_X>1-\delta_\eta$ are sharp from the stated data. The theorem does not assert that either input is favorable on the physical vector.

The compatible endpoint chain uses the same carrier, row vector, raw norm, and uniform or distinguished scope throughout TPC–47, TPC–51, TPC–54, TPC–59, TPC–62, and TPC–63 [`\citep{WangTPC47,WangTPC51,WangTPC54,WangTPC59,WangTPC62,WangTPC63}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-0276fa4f583cdc92); [\[WangTPC51\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-09a584963bbbf4f9); [\[WangTPC54\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-6f1a8ec95b6b34b5); [\[WangTPC59\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-4f045ca3a3f537f2); [\[WangTPC62\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-7e6a7d2f67f67940); [\[WangTPC63\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-064.md#cite-822675dc51b7acfd). If a literal terminal estimate is first proved relative to $P$, parent inflation is charged once in its effective loss. If a direct estimate is proved relative to $\widetilde P$, it is not charged again. Equality at the inherited endpoint $1/400$ is a stop, not a success.

#### Main results.

The paper proves the following finite-scale statements.

1.  Exact literal parent, terminal-support, and shaped terminal forms, with block disintegration and raw atomic normalization.

2.  A necessary and sufficient parent-complete/terminal-faithful pair-lift criterion, its unique least relation, and the exact zero-inflation test.

3.  A canonical hybrid repair and an exact literal-active-hypergraph zero-inflation baseline.

4.  Exact parent-inflation identities, the optimal domination constant, and sharp arbitrary-inflation and zero-row countermodels.

5.  Zero-safe three-factor activation, uniform row minima, distinguished weighted averages, and sharp inflation-corrected transfer.

6.  The sharp threshold-capacity certificate [\[eq:tpc64-intro-capacity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-4bbb0b8f68d3d82a), including all endpoint extremizers.

7.  A denominator-compatible five-arrow chain, a no-double-charge exponent ledger, and the strict $1/400$ stop rule.

The incidence and minimax theorems are L0. Their use with one literal fixed-shift coefficient field is L1 and remains conditional on an arithmetic terminal-incidence estimate. No L2 cancellation, parity, prime-pair, or twin-prime conclusion is claimed.

# The literal multiscale parent and its terminal field

The multiscale construction must begin before pair projection. This section fixes the literal finite incidence, its raw parent form, and the terminal form to which every later repair will be compared. In particular, neither a rectangular completion nor the terminal-active support is used to define the parent.

## Finite arithmetic blocks and full incidences

## The activation comparison is not yet an estimate

The literal activation problem is the comparison of $\mathcal T_{X,h}$ with $\mathcal P_X$, in one declared uniform or distinguished coefficient scope. If $\mathcal P_X\equiv0$, there is no parent-visible vector and no activation constant or exponent is assigned. If $\mathcal P_X\not\equiv0$, the definitions above do not assert that every active parent row has $T_m>0$, nor that any positive ratio has a fixed-power lower bound.

Normalizing $\mathcal T_{X,h}$ by $\mathcal H_{X,h}$, or declaring $\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}$ to be the parent incidence, would delete the parent-to-terminal-support alignment loss. Such a terminal-active parent cannot be substituted for $\mathcal P_X$ in a chain whose upstream reference is the literal pre-terminal form.

# Parent-complete terminal-faithful pair lifts

A pair relation can be useful only if its rectangular lift represents the same terminal field without deleting the parent against which activation is measured. This section gives the exact existence test, the unique least relation, and the resulting parent inflation.

> 1.  *parent-complete* if $$\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}   \subseteq\operatorname{PRect}_k(I);   \label{eq:tpc64-parent-complete}$$

> 2.  *terminal-atomic faithful* if $$\operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I)   =\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}.   \label{eq:tpc64-terminal-atomic-faithful}$$

Parent completeness retains every nonzero literal pre-terminal coordinate. Terminal-atomic faithfulness demands equality before any coherent grouping; terminal-active spurious crosses are therefore forbidden even when a later grouping might cancel them.

## The exact existence and minimality theorem

###### ref-f110ffa4e68d08b6

###### ref-55df9f3e433ca1c6

###### ref-633950dff77b39a5

> **Theorem: Parent-complete faithful-lift criterion** \[thm:tpc64-parent-complete-faithful-lift\] There exists a pair relation on block $k$ which is simultaneously parent-complete and terminal-atomic faithful if and only if $$\boxed{  \operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})  =\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}.}  \label{eq:tpc64-parent-complete-lift-iff}$$ When [\[eq:tpc64-parent-complete-lift-iff\]](TPC-064.md#ref-55df9f3e433ca1c6) holds, $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$ is the unique least such relation. More precisely, $I$ is parent-complete and terminal-atomic faithful if and only if $$I_{\mathrm{pre},k}\subseteq I  \quad\text{and}\quad  \operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}      (I\setminus I_{\mathrm{pre},k})=\varnothing.  \label{eq:tpc64-all-parent-complete-faithful-relations}$$

###### ref-1df351da3e267ece

> **Proof** Every point of $\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}$ belongs to $\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}$, so its pair lies in $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$. Hence $$\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}  \subseteq  \operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k}).  \label{eq:tpc64-terminal-contained-in-parent-closure}$$ If $I$ is parent-complete, then $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}\subseteq I$ by [\[eq:tpc64-parent-complete-relation-inclusion\]](TPC-064.md#ref-f152a119aac0c631). If it is also terminal-atomic faithful, monotonicity gives $$\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}  \subseteq  \operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})  \subseteq  \operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I)  =\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+},$$ which proves necessity of [\[eq:tpc64-parent-complete-lift-iff\]](TPC-064.md#ref-55df9f3e433ca1c6). Conversely, that equality makes $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$ both parent-complete and faithful. Every parent-complete relation contains it, proving unique leastness.
>
> Assume the criterion. Adding pairs outside $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$ preserves terminal equality exactly when those pairs create no terminal-active point, which is the second condition in [\[eq:tpc64-all-parent-complete-faithful-relations\]](TPC-064.md#ref-633950dff77b39a5). The first condition is exactly parent completeness.

If [\[eq:tpc64-parent-complete-lift-iff\]](TPC-064.md#ref-55df9f3e433ca1c6) fails, every pair relation which retains all literal parent atoms creates a terminal-active spurious cross. No enlargement of the relation can remove that cross. Hence no parent-complete, terminal-atomic faithful pair-rectangular lift exists. The canonical repair used below then falls back to the literal active hypergraph. This statement excludes pair rectangles only; it makes no minimality claim among arbitrary nonlinear, quotient, or grouped representations.

## Exact parent inflation

###### ref-254ea00021b03dac

###### ref-17339db10e8bde74

###### ref-c58f2b5313a085e2

For a parent-complete relation $I$, define its populated rectangular row weight by $$\widetilde P_{k,m}(I)  :=  \sum_{\substack{u\in\operatorname{PRect}_k(I)\\m(u)=m}}  |c_u|^2  \label{eq:tpc64-rectangular-parent-weight}$$ and its added row mass by $$\Delta_{k,m}(I)  :=  \sum_{\substack{  u\in\operatorname{PRect}_k(I)        \setminus\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}\\  m(u)=m}}  |c_u|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-block-added-parent-mass}$$ Because parent completeness gives inclusion of the literal active incidence, the union is disjoint and $$\boxed{  \widetilde P_{k,m}(I)  =P_{k,m}+\Delta_{k,m}(I).}  \label{eq:tpc64-block-parent-inflation-identity}$$

###### ref-9840e5385777ca4f

###### ref-81c15989eb6cb658

Likewise define $$\widetilde T_{k,m}(I)  :=  \sum_{\substack{  u\in\operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I)\\m(u)=m}}  |t_h(u)c_u|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-rectangular-terminal-weight}$$ If $I$ is terminal-atomic faithful, then $$\boxed{\widetilde T_{k,m}(I)=T_{k,m}}  \label{eq:tpc64-block-terminal-preservation}$$ for every row. Thus a terminal-faithful rectangle can change the denominator while leaving the terminal numerator exactly unchanged.

###### ref-41f78278491bbd5b

###### ref-2e9956b7ce43b547

###### ref-ef47d3fe5cead2a2

> **Theorem: Minimal parent inflation and its zero test** \[thm:tpc64-minimal-parent-inflation\] Assume [\[eq:tpc64-parent-complete-lift-iff\]](TPC-064.md#ref-55df9f3e433ca1c6). Then, for every parent-complete terminal-atomic faithful relation $I$, $$\Delta_{k,m}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})  \le\Delta_{k,m}(I)  \qquad(m\in\mathcal M_X).  \label{eq:tpc64-rowwise-minimal-inflation}$$ The canonical pair lift has zero parent inflation on every row if and only if the literal pre-terminal active incidence is rectangular over its pair projection: $$\boxed{  \Delta_{k,m}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})=0  \text{ for every }m  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  \operatorname{PRect}_k(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})  =\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}.}  \label{eq:tpc64-zero-inflation-iff}$$ If the terminal criterion holds but [\[eq:tpc64-zero-inflation-iff\]](TPC-064.md#ref-ef47d3fe5cead2a2) fails, then some row has strictly positive added parent mass, and every added nonzero coordinate is terminal-null.

> **Proof** Every admissible $I$ contains $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$. Monotonicity of $\operatorname{PRect}_k$, followed by termwise nonnegativity in [\[eq:tpc64-block-added-parent-mass\]](TPC-064.md#ref-17339db10e8bde74), proves [\[eq:tpc64-rowwise-minimal-inflation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-2e9956b7ce43b547). The inclusion $$\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}  \subseteq\operatorname{PRect}_k(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})$$ always holds. Therefore all rowwise added sums vanish exactly when the two finite active sets are equal, proving [\[eq:tpc64-zero-inflation-iff\]](TPC-064.md#ref-ef47d3fe5cead2a2). If they are unequal, a spurious point has $c_u\ne0$ and contributes positively to its row. Terminal faithfulness forces $t_h(u)c_u=0$ at every such point.

###### ref-cfd41d9345e247b4

For $P_{k,m}>0$, define the block inflation $$\iota_{k,m}(I)  :=\frac{\widetilde P_{k,m}(I)}{P_{k,m}}  =1+\frac{\Delta_{k,m}(I)}{P_{k,m}}.  \label{eq:tpc64-block-inflation-factor}$$ If $P_{k,m}=\widetilde P_{k,m}(I)=0$, put $\iota_{k,m}(I)=1$. If $P_{k,m}=0<\widetilde P_{k,m}(I)$, put $\iota_{k,m}(I)=+\infty$. The last case cannot occur for the canonical relation $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$, but it can occur after arbitrary extra rows are inserted.

The identities [\[eq:tpc64-block-parent-inflation-identity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-c58f2b5313a085e2) and [\[eq:tpc64-block-terminal-preservation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-81c15989eb6cb658) show exactly which denominator a later activation theorem must use. They do not by themselves give a positive activation constant.

###### ref-356fd1efc4842434

> **Remark: Terminal-only least relations do not preserve the parent** \[rem:tpc64-terminal-only-relation\] The smaller relation $\operatorname{pr}_{m,r}\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}$ may represent the terminal atomic field while omitting pairs which carry nonzero pre-terminal mass. A favorable ratio obtained after that deletion is not a lower bound relative to $P_{k,m}$. Parent completeness is precisely the condition preventing this substitution.

The exact size of the resulting activation dilution, new-only row failures, and sharp finite countermodels are separate from the existence theorem. They must be evaluated from the populated repaired parent, not from the number of pair or block labels.

# Canonical hybrid repair and the activation-inflation law

The blockwise criterion permits a canonical repair without forcing a pair-only model on blocks where pair projection is unfaithful. Pair-good blocks use their unique least parent-complete rectangle; pair-bad blocks retain their literal active incidence. All block labels remain orthogonal until a separately declared physical erasure.

## Pair-good and hypergraph blocks

###### ref-fb382f26e2673bdd

###### ref-c14c3a5c17d023fc

###### ref-4cdc81693a7164f0

Define $$\begin{aligned}  K_X^{\mathrm{pair}}  &:=  \left\{k\in K_X:  \operatorname{TRect}_{h,k}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})  =\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}\right\},  \label{eq:tpc64-pair-good-blocks}\\  K_X^{\mathrm{hyp}}  &:=  K_X\setminus K_X^{\mathrm{pair}}.  \label{eq:tpc64-hypergraph-blocks}\end{aligned}$$ For each block, define the canonical repaired parent incidence $$\mathcal V_{X,k}^{\mathrm{hyb}}  :=  \begin{cases}   \operatorname{PRect}_k(I_{\mathrm{pre},k}),      &k\in K_X^{\mathrm{pair}},\\[2mm]   \mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+},      &k\in K_X^{\mathrm{hyp}}.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc64-canonical-hybrid-incidence}$$ Write $\mathcal V^{\mathrm{hyb}} :=(\mathcal V_{X,k}^{\mathrm{hyb}})_{k\in K_X}$ for this populated parent family. ## Exact hybrid identities

###### ref-196200dce5f8189a

###### ref-12b2c26a4d41bceb

###### ref-16d24bd4dc7e93bc

Put $$\Delta_m^{\mathrm{hyb}}  :=  \sum_{k\in K_X^{\mathrm{pair}}}  \Delta_{k,m}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k})  \label{eq:tpc64-hybrid-added-parent-mass}$$ and $$\widetilde P_m  :=P_m+\Delta_m^{\mathrm{hyb}}.  \label{eq:tpc64-hybrid-row-parent}$$ The hybrid parent form is $$\widetilde{\mathcal P}_X(z)  :=\|C_X^{\mathrm{hyb}}z\|_2^2  =\sum_m\widetilde P_m|z_m|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-hybrid-parent-form}$$

> **Theorem: Canonical hybrid repair** \[thm:tpc64-canonical-hybrid-repair\] For every $z\in Z_X$, $$\begin{aligned}  \widetilde{\mathcal P}_X(z)  &=  \mathcal P_X(z)  +\sum_m\Delta_m^{\mathrm{hyb}}|z_m|^2,  \label{eq:tpc64-hybrid-parent-identity}\\  \|D_{X,h}^{\mathcal V^{\mathrm{hyb}}}C_X^{\mathrm{hyb}}z\|_2^2  &=\mathcal T_{X,h}(z).  \label{eq:tpc64-hybrid-terminal-identity}\end{aligned}$$ Thus the hybrid repair is parent-complete and terminal-atomic faithful on every block. Among all repairs which use a parent-complete terminal-atomic faithful pair relation on every block in $K_X^{\mathrm{pair}}$ and retain the literal active incidence on every block in $K_X^{\mathrm{hyp}}$, it minimizes the parent weight row by row.
>

> **Proof** On a pair-good block, [\[eq:tpc64-block-parent-inflation-identity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-c58f2b5313a085e2) and [\[eq:tpc64-block-terminal-preservation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-81c15989eb6cb658) apply to $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$. On a hypergraph block both forms are the literal ones by definition. Rowwise minimality follows from [\[eq:tpc64-rowwise-minimal-inflation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-2e9956b7ce43b547) on every pair-good block. For the literal-active-hypergraph choice, every block is the original literal active coordinate set, so exact block disintegration gives [\[eq:tpc64-hybrid-hypergraph-zero-inflation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-e92a8f2b5a3989a5).

No theorem requires using a pair rectangle on a pair-good block. Within populated incidence repairs retaining every literal parent atom, the literal active hypergraph is the parent-minimal baseline. The hybrid construction records the least cost incurred when a downstream argument explicitly requires pair coordinates on the blocks where such a representation is faithful.

It does not control $\Delta_m^{\mathrm{hyb}}$, or any ratio of repaired to literal parent mass. Parent inflation of arbitrary polynomial size can already occur inside a single pair-good block.

For clarity, put $$\widetilde P_{k,m}^{\mathrm{hyb}}  :=  \begin{cases}   P_{k,m}+\Delta_{k,m}(I_{\mathrm{pre},k}),       &k\in K_X^{\mathrm{pair}},\\   P_{k,m},&k\in K_X^{\mathrm{hyp}}.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc64-hybrid-block-parent-weight}$$ If a separate estimate proves, for one common finite $I_X$, $$\widetilde P_{k,m}^{\mathrm{hyb}}  \le I_XP_{k,m}  \label{eq:tpc64-hybrid-blockwise-inflation-input}$$ on every block and active row, then summing gives $$\widetilde P_m  =\sum_k\widetilde P_{k,m}^{\mathrm{hyb}}  \le I_X\sum_kP_{k,m}  =I_XP_m.  \label{eq:tpc64-blockwise-to-global-inflation}$$ Indeed, $P_{k,m}=0$ implies $\widetilde P_{k,m}^{\mathrm{hyb}}=0$: no pair with first coordinate $m$ then occurs in $I_{\mathrm{pre},k}$, and a hypergraph block keeps the same empty active row. Its kernel and range-restricted Gram belong to the reassembly gate, not to parent inflation.

# Exact parent-inflation laws

The terminal-faithfulness test does not by itself preserve the parent denominator. A pair rectangle may add coordinates which are terminal-null but carry nonzero pre-terminal mass. This section records that mass exactly. All incidences, coefficients, and repairs are fixed before the row coefficient vector is chosen.

## Repaired parent data

Continue with the literal blocks, incidences, and row masses $$\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+},\qquad  \mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+},\qquad  P_m,\ H_m,\ T_m$$ from [2](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-064.md#src-701aa69d6162cae9). In particular, $P_m,H_m,T_m$ are not recomputed after a pair projection or terminal restriction.

###### ref-d6fa23a9944b6f12

###### ref-c31cfb29441411fc

###### ref-529da258f99b1024

Fix a populated parent enlargement $\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}$ which preserves every literal parent atom and is terminal-atomic faithful. Thus $$\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}  \subseteq\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}  \subseteq\{u\in\Omega_{X,k}:c_u\ne0\},  \qquad  \{u\in\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}:                t_h(u)c_u\ne0\}  =\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}.  \label{eq:tpc64-parent-complete-terminal-faithful}$$ The added-parent incidence $$\mathcal N_{X,k}  :=\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}       \setminus\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}  \label{eq:tpc64-new-parent-incidence}$$ therefore satisfies $$t_h(u)c_u=0  \qquad(u\in\mathcal N_{X,k}).  \label{eq:tpc64-new-parent-terminal-null}$$ For a pair lift, the first condition is parent completeness in the sense of [\[def:tpc64-parent-complete-terminal-faithful\]](TPC-064.md#ref-4cf8adb3b26730d0). For the canonical hybrid repair, take $\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+} =\mathcal V_{X,k}^{\mathrm{hyb}}$: this is the least admissible parent-complete rectangle on a pair-good block and the literal active incidence on a pair-bad block. The results below use only [\[eq:tpc64-parent-complete-terminal-faithful\]](TPC-064.md#ref-d6fa23a9944b6f12), so they also apply to any larger populated terminal-faithful repair.

For every source row $m$, define only the genuinely new quantity $$\Delta_m  :=\sum_{k\in K_X}      \sum_{\substack{u\in\mathcal N_{X,k}\\m(u)=m}}        |c_u|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-added-parent-mass}$$ The populated repaired parent mass therefore obeys $$\widetilde P_m=P_m+\Delta_m.  \label{eq:tpc64-repaired-parent-mass}$$ In the canonical hybrid specialization, $\Delta_m=\Delta_m^{\mathrm{hyb}}$, and [\[eq:tpc64-repaired-parent-mass\]](TPC-064.md#ref-bfc8fb62a364f8a3) is exactly the previously defined hybrid row parent [\[eq:tpc64-hybrid-row-parent\]](TPC-064.md#ref-12b2c26a4d41bceb); no second hybrid parent is being introduced. It changes only the parent against which terminal activation is measured.

For $z=(z_m)_{m\in\mathcal M_X}\in Z_X$, set $$\begin{aligned}  P(z)&:=\mathcal P_X(z)=\sum_mP_m|z_m|^2,  &  \Delta(z)&:=\sum_m\Delta_m|z_m|^2,  \label{eq:tpc64-parent-added-forms}\\  \widetilde P(z)&:=\sum_m\widetilde P_m|z_m|^2,  &  T(z)&:=\mathcal T_{X,h}(z)=\sum_mT_m|z_m|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-repaired-terminal-forms}\end{aligned}$$ We assume throughout that $P$ is not identically zero. Rows on which $P_m=\widetilde P_m=0$ are harmless zero rows.

## The exact inflation constant

###### ref-ed78e08e362a63bb

###### ref-8e21522fe1e73958

The row inflation ratio is an extended nonnegative number. We use the convention $$\iota_m:=  \begin{cases}   \displaystyle\frac{\widetilde P_m}{P_m}    =1+\frac{\Delta_m}{P_m},&P_m>0,\\[8pt]   1,&P_m=\widetilde P_m=0,\\   +\infty,&P_m=0<\widetilde P_m.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc64-row-inflation-convention}$$ A row in the last case is called a *new-only parent row*. Define $$\mathfrak I_X  :=\sup_{\substack{z\in Z_X\\P(z)>0}}        \frac{\widetilde P(z)}{P(z)}  \in[1,+\infty].  \label{eq:tpc64-global-inflation-constant}$$ The supremum is taken on the full row-coefficient space. A range-restricted or distinguished coefficient class has its own constant and must not be substituted for $\mathfrak I_X$.

###### ref-4f23b67a6e7188c1

###### ref-a1e39b41a07202f6

###### ref-bdc08cc7d8a016d6

###### ref-80249434a0be7231

> **Theorem: Exact parent inflation and sharp domination** \[thm:tpc64-exact-parent-inflation\] For every coefficient vector, $$\boxed{\widetilde P(z)=P(z)+\Delta(z).}  \label{eq:tpc64-exact-parent-inflation-identity}$$ Moreover, $$\boxed{\mathfrak I_X=\max_{m\in\mathcal M_X}\iota_m}  \label{eq:tpc64-inflation-row-maximum}$$ with the conventions in [\[eq:tpc64-row-inflation-convention\]](TPC-064.md#ref-ed78e08e362a63bb). Consequently, the following are equivalent:
>
> 1.  there is a finite $C$ such that $\widetilde P(z)\le C P(z)$ for every $z$;
>
> 2.  the repair has no new-only parent row;
>
> 3.  $\mathfrak I_X<\infty$.
>
> When these conditions hold, the least possible domination constant is $\mathfrak I_X$: $$\widetilde P(z)\le\mathfrak I_XP(z)  \qquad(z\in Z_X).  \label{eq:tpc64-sharp-parent-domination}$$ For the separate literal-active-hypergraph baseline, which makes no pair-rectangularization claim, $\Delta_m=0$, $\widetilde P_m=P_m$, and $\mathfrak I_X=1$ exactly, consistently with [\[eq:tpc64-hybrid-hypergraph-zero-inflation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-e92a8f2b5a3989a5).

###### ref-90ff7fde11b347f6

> **Proof** Equation [\[eq:tpc64-exact-parent-inflation-identity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-a1e39b41a07202f6) follows by summing [\[eq:tpc64-repaired-parent-mass\]](TPC-064.md#ref-bfc8fb62a364f8a3) against $|z_m|^2$. First suppose there is no new-only row. Whenever $P(z)>0$, $$\frac{\widetilde P(z)}{P(z)}  =  \sum_{\substack{m:P_m>0}}  \frac{P_m|z_m|^2}{P(z)}\,\iota_m.  \label{eq:tpc64-inflation-convex-combination}$$ This is a convex combination of the active row ratios. It is at most their maximum, and a row basis vector attaining the maximum gives equality.
>
> Now suppose $P_{m_1}=0<\widetilde P_{m_1}$. Choose a row $m_0$ with $P_{m_0}>0$ and put $$z^{(\varepsilon)}:=e_{m_1}+\varepsilon e_{m_0}.$$ Then $$P(z^{(\varepsilon)})=\varepsilon^2P_{m_0}>0,  \qquad  \widetilde P(z^{(\varepsilon)})  \ge\widetilde P_{m_1},$$ so the quotient tends to $+\infty$. This proves [\[eq:tpc64-inflation-row-maximum\]](TPC-064.md#ref-bdc08cc7d8a016d6) and all three equivalences. The hypergraph statement is the case $\widetilde{\mathcal U}_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+} =\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}$ for every block.

###### ref-bb2636a94106b506

###### ref-2894fc3ae7c81a61

> **Proposition: Sharpness of the inflation law** \[prop:tpc64-inflation-sharpness\] For every prescribed $L\in[1,\infty)$, there is a one-row, one-block terminal-faithful repair with $$P_m=1,\qquad  \Delta_m=L-1,\qquad  \widetilde P_m=L,\qquad  \mathfrak I_X=L.  \label{eq:tpc64-prescribed-inflation-model}$$ Thus neither the coefficient $1$ in [\[eq:tpc64-exact-parent-inflation-identity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-a1e39b41a07202f6) nor the constant $\mathfrak I_X$ in [\[eq:tpc64-sharp-parent-domination\]](TPC-064.md#ref-80249434a0be7231) can be improved under the stated hypotheses.

> **Proof** Take one literal parent atom of square mass $1$ and one added terminal-null parent atom of square mass $L-1$, omitting the latter when $L=1$. The repair is terminal-faithful because the added coordinate has zero terminal multiplier. All formulas are immediate. An explicit pair-rectangle realization, with arbitrarily large integral inflation, is given in [\[ex:tpc64-arbitrary-faithful-inflation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-889374fa9418c40f).

The value $\mathfrak I_X$ is a parent-denominator constant. It is not the number of dyadic labels, a terminal occupancy proportion, an erasure norm, or a cancellation exponent. In particular, an $X^{o(1)}$ label count places no bound on $\mathfrak I_X$.

# True terminal activation and its three exact factors

We now compare the shaped terminal energy with the repaired parent, not with a terminal-active parent chosen after the fact. The distinction produces three factors: literal support alignment, literal multiplier intensity, and inverse parent inflation. Only the first and third are proportions.

## Zero-safe row factors

###### ref-2fa81561c78692a9

###### ref-880c8174c38f12d7

###### ref-03a1d171d8c0955f

###### ref-728f9eb6529dce15

###### ref-453f843a95af26eb

###### ref-068273ce12983378

Continue with $P_m,H_m,T_m,\Delta_m,\widetilde P_m$ from [5](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-064.md#src-eefad688b38b02e8). The finite incidence definitions imply $$H_m=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad T_m=0,  \qquad  P_m=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad H_m=T_m=0.  \label{eq:tpc64-zero-propagation}$$ Define the support-alignment, multiplier-intensity, inverse-inflation, and repaired-activation factors by $$\begin{aligned}  \alpha_m  &:=  \begin{cases}   H_m/P_m,&P_m>0,\\   0,&P_m=0,  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc64-support-alignment-factor}\\  \vartheta_m  &:=  \begin{cases}   T_m/H_m,&H_m>0,\\   0,&H_m=0,  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc64-multiplier-intensity-factor}\\  \rho_m  &:=  \begin{cases}   P_m/\widetilde P_m,&\widetilde P_m>0,\\   1,&\widetilde P_m=0,  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc64-inverse-inflation-factor}\\  g_m  &:=  \begin{cases}   T_m/\widetilde P_m,&\widetilde P_m>0,\\   0,&\widetilde P_m=0.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:tpc64-repaired-row-activation}\end{aligned}$$ With $1/(+\infty):=0$, the row conventions give $\rho_m=\iota_m^{-1}$ on every row. Thus $0\le\alpha_m,\rho_m\le1$, but there is no upper bound $1$ on $\vartheta_m$. Indeed, when $H_m>0$, $$\vartheta_m  =  \sum_{k\in K_X}  \sum_{\substack{u\in\mathcal U_{X,h,k}^{+}\\m(u)=m}}  \frac{|c_u|^2}{H_m}\,|t_h(u)|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-intensity-weighted-average}$$ It is a weighted average of squared literal multiplier magnitudes, not a probability of terminal survival. In particular, shaped logarithmic or profile weights can make it larger than one. On a row with a zero denominator, the displayed zero values are bookkeeping conventions chosen to make the universal product identity below valid. They are not conditional probabilities and do not assign a physical multiplier intensity to an empty support.

###### ref-874caa150ca100ae

###### ref-9cad06aa45f57385

###### ref-b4888f5aac96050d

> **Theorem: Exact three-factor activation law** \[thm:tpc64-exact-three-factor-activation\] On every row for which all three raw denominators are positive, $$\boxed{  \frac{T_m}{\widetilde P_m}  =  \frac{H_m}{P_m}\,  \frac{T_m}{H_m}\,  \frac{P_m}{\widetilde P_m}.}  \label{eq:tpc64-three-raw-fractions}$$ With the zero conventions [\[eq:tpc64-support-alignment-factor\]](TPC-064.md#ref-880c8174c38f12d7)– [\[eq:tpc64-repaired-row-activation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-453f843a95af26eb), the universal statement is $$\boxed{g_m=\alpha_m\vartheta_m\rho_m}  \qquad(m\in\mathcal M_X).  \label{eq:tpc64-three-factor-activation}$$ More explicitly:
>
> 1.  if $P_m,H_m,\widetilde P_m>0$, then [\[eq:tpc64-three-raw-fractions\]](TPC-064.md#ref-9cad06aa45f57385) holds by cancellation;
>
> 2.  if $P_m>0$ but $H_m=0$, then $T_m=g_m=0$;
>
> 3.  if $P_m=0<\widetilde P_m$, then the row is new-only, $H_m=T_m=0$, and $g_m=\rho_m=0$;
>
> 4.  if $\widetilde P_m=0$, then all four row masses vanish, $g_m=0$, and the harmless convention $\rho_m=1$ agrees with the reciprocal of $\iota_m=1$; the row contributes to no quadratic form.

> **Proof** The regular identity is obtained by inserting $H_m$ and $P_m$ between the numerator and denominator. The remaining cases follow from [\[eq:tpc64-zero-propagation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-2fa81561c78692a9) and $0\le P_m\le\widetilde P_m$.

The factorization separates three logically independent tasks. Parent-to-terminal support alignment controls $\alpha_m$, the literal shaped coefficient controls $\vartheta_m$, and the chosen repair controls $\rho_m$. Completing a relation can improve neither of the first two factors, while a terminal-faithful rectangle can strictly reduce the third.

## Uniform and distinguished activation

###### ref-bd9b2e068c0f99fa

###### ref-c4acbdbd249ac816

Define the literal-hypergraph and repaired-parent uniform constants by $$\begin{aligned}  \tau_{\mathrm{hyp}}  &:=  \inf_{\substack{z\in Z_X\\P(z)>0}}        \frac{T(z)}{P(z)},  \label{eq:tpc64-hypergraph-uniform-activation}\\  \tau_{\mathrm{rep}}  &:=  \inf_{\substack{z\in Z_X\\\widetilde P(z)>0}}        \frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}.  \label{eq:tpc64-repaired-uniform-activation}\end{aligned}$$ These are full-row constants. They are not statements about one distinguished physical vector.

###### ref-a2bb8478422a9a43

###### ref-5032cc4ed4d61ba3

###### ref-d9a05d623a5445b6

> **Theorem: Sharp uniform row minima** \[thm:tpc64-uniform-activation-minima\] On the full row-coefficient space, $$\begin{aligned}  \boxed{  \tau_{\mathrm{hyp}}  =\min_{m:P_m>0}\frac{T_m}{P_m}  =\min_{m:P_m>0}\alpha_m\vartheta_m,}  \label{eq:tpc64-hypergraph-row-minimum}\\  \boxed{  \tau_{\mathrm{rep}}  =\min_{m:\widetilde P_m>0}\frac{T_m}{\widetilde P_m}  =\min_{m:\widetilde P_m>0}        \alpha_m\vartheta_m\rho_m.}  \label{eq:tpc64-repaired-row-minimum}\end{aligned}$$ In particular, one new-only parent row forces $\tau_{\mathrm{rep}}=0$, and one literal row with $P_m>0=T_m$ forces both uniform constants to vanish.

> **Proof** For $P(z)>0$, $$\frac{T(z)}{P(z)}  =  \sum_{m:P_m>0}  \frac{P_m|z_m|^2}{P(z)}\,\frac{T_m}{P_m}.$$ This is a convex combination of the active literal row ratios. A minimizing row basis vector attains the minimum. The same argument with $\widetilde P_m$ proves [\[eq:tpc64-repaired-row-minimum\]](TPC-064.md#ref-d9a05d623a5445b6). The two zero-row conclusions follow from [\[eq:tpc64-zero-propagation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-2fa81561c78692a9).

###### ref-21dd62ea6943540d

For a fixed distinguished vector $z_*$ satisfying $\widetilde P(z_*)>0$, put $$\tau_{\mathrm{rep},*}(X)  :=\frac{T(z_*)}{\widetilde P(z_*)}.  \label{eq:tpc64-distinguished-repaired-activation}$$

###### ref-f373a91dc5c845c8

###### ref-dab595ad6ac761f7

###### ref-18528a02dd5891bc

> **Theorem: Exact distinguished weighted average** \[thm:tpc64-distinguished-activation-average\] The distinguished repaired activation is exactly $$\boxed{  \tau_{\mathrm{rep},*}(X)  =  \sum_{m:\widetilde P_m>0}  \frac{\widetilde P_m|z_{*,m}|^2}       {\widetilde P(z_*)}\,  g_m.}  \label{eq:tpc64-distinguished-activation-average}$$ The displayed coefficients are nonnegative and sum to one. If also $P(z_*)>0$, then $$\tau_{\mathrm{hyp},*}(X)  :=\frac{T(z_*)}{P(z_*)}  =  \sum_{m:P_m>0}  \frac{P_m|z_{*,m}|^2}{P(z_*)}\,  \alpha_m\vartheta_m.  \label{eq:tpc64-distinguished-hypergraph-average}$$ Neither distinguished identity implies the corresponding row minimum: a distinguished vector may avoid a bad row, whereas a full-row statement must test it.

> **Proof** Insert the definitions of $T(z_*)$, $\widetilde P(z_*)$, and $g_m$. The second formula is identical with $P_m$ in place of $\widetilde P_m$.

## Sharp transfer through parent inflation

###### ref-ee4758ed683c6da9

###### ref-a78f165954fe41d0

###### ref-0d78b88710e74f8b

###### ref-89ae7e4f1fde7699

> **Theorem: Inflation-corrected activation transfer** \[thm:tpc64-inflation-corrected-activation\] Assume $\mathfrak I_X<\infty$. For every $z$ with $P(z)>0$, $$\boxed{  \frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  \ge  \frac1{\mathfrak I_X}\,  \frac{T(z)}{P(z)}.}  \label{eq:tpc64-pointwise-inflation-transfer}$$ Consequently, $$\boxed{  \tau_{\mathrm{rep}}  \ge\frac{\tau_{\mathrm{hyp}}}{\mathfrak I_X}.}  \label{eq:tpc64-uniform-inflation-transfer}$$ For every $a>0$ and $L\ge1$, a one-row terminal-faithful model attains simultaneously $$\tau_{\mathrm{hyp}}=a,\qquad  \mathfrak I_X=L,\qquad  \tau_{\mathrm{rep}}=\frac aL.  \label{eq:tpc64-inflation-transfer-sharp-model}$$ Thus the factor $\mathfrak I_X^{-1}$ is sharp.

> **Proof** By [\[thm:tpc64-exact-parent-inflation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-4f23b67a6e7188c1), $\widetilde P(z)\le\mathfrak I_XP(z)$. A finite inflation constant excludes new-only rows, so $\widetilde P(z)>0$ if and only if $P(z)>0$, up to common zero rows. Multiplying the parent domination by the nonnegative number $T(z)/(P(z)\widetilde P(z))$ proves [\[eq:tpc64-pointwise-inflation-transfer\]](TPC-064.md#ref-a78f165954fe41d0). Taking the infimum and using [\[eq:tpc64-hypergraph-uniform-activation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-bd9b2e068c0f99fa) gives [\[eq:tpc64-uniform-inflation-transfer\]](TPC-064.md#ref-0d78b88710e74f8b).
>
> For sharpness, take one literal terminal-active atom with $|c|^2=1$ and $|t_h|^2=a$, and add terminal-null parent mass $L-1$. Then $$P_m=H_m=1,\qquad T_m=a,\qquad  \widetilde P_m=L,$$ which gives [\[eq:tpc64-inflation-transfer-sharp-model\]](TPC-064.md#ref-89ae7e4f1fde7699).

If $\mathfrak I_X=+\infty$, there is no coefficient-uniform comparison from the literal parent to the repaired parent. Writing $\mathfrak I_X^{-1}=0$ makes [\[eq:tpc64-uniform-inflation-transfer\]](TPC-064.md#ref-0d78b88710e74f8b) formally true but quantitatively empty; it is not an activation theorem.

The results of this section are exact finite-dimensional identities. They do not prove that $\alpha_m$, $\vartheta_m$, $g_m$, or either activation constant has a favorable lower bound on the physical carrier. In particular, they contain no signed parity estimate and no prime-pair consequence.

# A sharp threshold-capacity certificate

The rowwise minimax is the correct answer for a homogeneous full-coefficient theorem. A different exact certificate is available for one coefficient vector, or for a declared class on which a quantitative capacity lower bound is known. This section gives that certificate with its quantifiers exposed.

## Good rows and occupied repaired-parent capacity

###### ref-2bf968ea7e5439ef

###### ref-09030d4ee1900529

###### ref-ff87ff6692c2e05b

Use the repaired row masses $\widetilde P_m$ and the unchanged terminal masses $T_m$. Delete rows with $\widetilde P_m=0$, and assume that the remaining set $$\mathcal M_X^+  :=\{m:\widetilde P_m>0\}  \label{eq:tpc64-capacity-active-rows}$$ is nonempty. Put $$\widetilde W_X:=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M_X^+}\widetilde P_m  \label{eq:tpc64-total-parent-capacity}$$ and define the exact repaired-parent row activation $$g_m:=\frac{T_m}{\widetilde P_m}\ge0  \qquad(m\in\mathcal M_X^+).  \label{eq:tpc64-row-gain}$$ There is no assertion that $g_m\le1$: the literal shaped terminal multiplier remains inside $T_m$.

###### ref-f7c920dcb5aa5a5d

###### ref-2e2c88861c7e005d

For a threshold $\eta>0$, define $$\begin{aligned}  \mathcal G_\eta  &:=\{m\in\mathcal M_X^+:g_m\ge\eta\},  \label{eq:tpc64-threshold-good-rows}\\  \delta_\eta  &:=\frac{\sum_{m\in\mathcal G_\eta}\widetilde P_m}               {\widetilde W_X}  \in[0,1].  \label{eq:tpc64-threshold-capacity-fraction}\end{aligned}$$ Thus $\delta_\eta$ is a structural fraction measured in the exact repaired-parent denominator. It is not the unweighted density of good rows and is not an actual-coefficient energy fraction.

###### ref-baf317c4477adf12

###### ref-14d7b7d33dbdf038

Let $C_X>0$ be a declared coefficient bound and let $z$ satisfy $$|z_m|\le C_X\qquad(m\in\mathcal M_X^+).  \label{eq:tpc64-coefficient-cap}$$ Whenever $\widetilde P(z)>0$, its occupied capacity is $$\kappa_X(z;C_X)  :=\frac{\widetilde P(z)}  {C_X^2\widetilde W_X}  \in(0,1].  \label{eq:tpc64-occupied-parent-capacity}$$ For a real number $x$, write $x_+:=\max\{x,0\}$.

## The exact threshold theorem

###### ref-51a926cbc0329ab7

###### ref-704545bc812c2140

###### ref-4094557a17a81d7e

###### ref-bed9c4a6712781d8

> **Theorem: Sharp threshold-capacity lower bound** \[thm:tpc64-threshold-capacity\] Fix $\eta>0$ and $0<\kappa_X\le1$. If a coefficient vector $z$ satisfies [\[eq:tpc64-coefficient-cap\]](TPC-064.md#ref-baf317c4477adf12) and $$\widetilde P(z)  \ge \kappa_X C_X^2\widetilde W_X>0,  \label{eq:tpc64-capacity-lower-hypothesis}$$ then $$\boxed{  \frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  \ge  \eta  \left(   1-\frac{1-\delta_\eta}{\kappa_X}  \right)_+.}  \label{eq:tpc64-threshold-capacity-bound}$$ More generally, if only $\delta_\eta\ge\underline\delta_\eta$ is known, then the same conclusion holds with $\underline\delta_\eta$ in place of $\delta_\eta$.
>
> Using only $\eta$, $\delta_\eta$, the coefficient cap, and the capacity lower bound, the right side of [\[eq:tpc64-threshold-capacity-bound\]](TPC-064.md#ref-4094557a17a81d7e) is optimal. In particular, it is positive exactly when $$\boxed{\kappa_X>1-\delta_\eta.}  \label{eq:tpc64-capacity-positive-threshold}$$

###### ref-b97cc449b771f156

> **Proof** Write $$E_{\rm good}(z)  :=\sum_{m\in\mathcal G_\eta}        \widetilde P_m|z_m|^2,  \qquad  E_{\rm bad}(z)  :=\widetilde P(z)-E_{\rm good}(z).  \label{eq:tpc64-good-bad-parent-energy}$$ The coefficient cap and [\[eq:tpc64-threshold-capacity-fraction\]](TPC-064.md#ref-2e2c88861c7e005d) give $$E_{\rm bad}(z)  \le C_X^2(1-\delta_\eta)\widetilde W_X.$$ Subtracting this from [\[eq:tpc64-capacity-lower-hypothesis\]](TPC-064.md#ref-704545bc812c2140), and also using $E_{\rm good}(z)\ge0$, yields $$\frac{E_{\rm good}(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  \ge  \left(   1-\frac{1-\delta_\eta}{\kappa_X}  \right)_+.$$ Since $T_m=g_m\widetilde P_m$ and $g_m\ge\eta$ on $\mathcal G_\eta$, $$T(z)  =\sum_mg_m\widetilde P_m|z_m|^2  \ge\eta E_{\rm good}(z),$$ which proves [\[eq:tpc64-threshold-capacity-bound\]](TPC-064.md#ref-4094557a17a81d7e). Monotonicity in $\delta_\eta$ proves the version with a lower bound for that fraction.
>
> For sharpness, normalize $C_X=\widetilde W_X=1$ and prescribe $\delta\in[0,1]$, $\kappa\in(0,1]$. Take $g_m=\eta$ on a good part of parent capacity $\delta$ and $g_m=0$ on its complement. If $0<\delta<1$ and $\kappa\le1-\delta$, put zero coefficient energy on the good part and constant squared coefficient $\kappa/(1-\delta)$ on the bad part. The terminal quotient is zero. If $\kappa>1-\delta$, saturate the bad part at squared coefficient one and put constant squared coefficient $(\kappa-1+\delta)/\delta$ on the good part. Then $$\widetilde P(z)=\kappa,  \qquad  \frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  =\eta\frac{\kappa-1+\delta}{\kappa}.$$ These are equality cases for the two branches. When $\delta=0$, take only bad rows; when $\delta=1$, take only good rows. Constant squared coefficient $\kappa$ gives the required occupied capacity at both endpoints. Finite weighted rows realize all of these models, so the bound cannot be improved from the stated data.

###### ref-45a0a1fe598b8d84

The theorem also has the exact actual-capacity form $$\frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  \ge  \eta  \left(   1-\frac{1-\delta_\eta}            {\kappa_X(z;C_X)}  \right)_+.  \label{eq:tpc64-actual-capacity-bound}$$ A certified lower bound for $\kappa_X(z;C_X)$ may then be substituted because the right side is increasing in the capacity variable.

## Uniform and distinguished quantifiers

###### ref-fbea14da9f3cc842

For $C_X>0$ and $0<\kappa_X\le1$, let $$\mathfrak Z_X(C_X,\kappa_X)  :=  \left\{  z:\ |z_m|\le C_X,\quad  \widetilde P(z)\ge  \kappa_XC_X^2\widetilde W_X  \right\}.  \label{eq:tpc64-capacity-restricted-class}$$ Theorem [\[thm:tpc64-threshold-capacity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-51a926cbc0329ab7) is coefficient-uniform on this declared restricted class. It is not a theorem on the entire bounded coefficient cube: that cube contains vectors with arbitrarily small occupied capacity and, whenever bad rows exist, vectors supported entirely on those rows.

###### ref-df851f4592578fb1

###### ref-48eec1f25e636ac4

> **Proposition: The full-space boundary** \[prop:tpc64-capacity-full-space-boundary\] On any homogeneous declared class $\mathfrak Z_X$ containing a nonzero multiple of each active row direction, $$\inf_{\substack{z\in\mathfrak Z_X\\\widetilde P(z)>0}}  \frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  =\min_{m\in\mathcal M_X^+}g_m.  \label{eq:tpc64-capacity-full-space-minimax}$$ Consequently a favorable value of [\[eq:tpc64-threshold-capacity-bound\]](TPC-064.md#ref-4094557a17a81d7e) for one distinguished physical vector does not imply a full-space uniform terminal lower bound.

> **Proof** Every quotient is a convex combination of the $g_m$, with weights $\widetilde P_m|z_m|^2/\widetilde P(z)$. A permitted row direction attains the minimum.

For a distinguished vector $z_*(X)$, all of $C_X$, $\kappa_X(z_*;C_X)$, and the threshold $\eta$ must concern that same vector and the same repaired parent. An averaged vector, an adaptively selected coefficient, or a capacity estimate computed with $P_m$ instead of $\widetilde P_m$ cannot be inserted into [\[eq:tpc64-threshold-capacity-bound\]](TPC-064.md#ref-4094557a17a81d7e).

## What the certificate does not supply

The theorem is an implication from $\delta_\eta$ and occupied capacity. It proves neither input favorable. In particular:

1.  a positive number of terminal-active rows does not lower-bound their $\widetilde P_m$-capacity;

2.  boundedness of the inherited coefficients does not imply $\kappa_X>1-\delta_\eta$;

3.  positivity of each $T_m$ does not give a fixed-power lower bound for $g_m$; and

4.  the raw terminal form $T$ still precedes label erasure, residual grouping, and the native physical reassembly map.

Thus the threshold theorem is a sharp L0 capacity law and, after the literal fixed-shift dictionary is verified, a conditional L1 certificate. It is not a new prime-distribution or cancellation estimate.

# Sharp countermodels and zero tests

The exact formulas above separate identities from arithmetic lower bounds. This section shows that the separation is necessary. Every example is a finite coefficient-independent incidence with raw counting-measure norms. The examples are sharp models of the stated interfaces; they are not asserted to occur inside the literal fixed-shift prime carrier.

## Faithful rectangles can have arbitrary parent inflation

###### ref-889374fa9418c40f

###### ref-0cde00291a50e479

###### ref-8500d839400a3c50

\[ex:tpc64-arbitrary-faithful-inflation\] Take one block $k$, one row $m$, one cofactor $r$, one auxiliary label $\gamma_0$, and $N\ge2$ base labels $j_0,\ldots,j_{N-1}$. Let the declared ambient incidence contain $$u_i:=(m,r,j_i,\gamma_0),\qquad 0\le i<N,$$ with $c_{u_i}=1$. Let the literal pre-terminal incidence be $$\mathcal U_{X,k}^{\mathrm{pre},+}=\{u_0\},$$ and set $$t_h(u_0)=1,\qquad t_h(u_i)=0\quad(1\le i<N).$$ The literal parent pair relation is the singleton $$I_{\mathrm{pre},k}=\{(m,r)\}.$$ Its rectangle contains all $N$ ambient points. It is parent-complete and terminal-atomic faithful because $u_0$ is the only terminal-active point. Nevertheless, $$P_m=H_m=T_m=1,\qquad  \Delta_m=N-1,\qquad  \widetilde P_m=N.  \label{eq:tpc64-arbitrary-inflation-data}$$ Consequently, $$\mathfrak I_X=N,\qquad  \tau_{\mathrm{hyp}}=1,\qquad  \tau_{\mathrm{rep}}=\frac1N.  \label{eq:tpc64-arbitrary-inflation-constants}$$ The terminal field and retained pair are unchanged; only the populated parent rectangle has grown.

Taking $N=2$ and replacing the single terminal-null unit coefficient by one of modulus $\sqrt{L-1}$ realizes every real $L\ge1$. Thus \[[prop:tpc64-inflation-sharpness](TPC-064.md#ref-bb2636a94106b506),[thm:tpc64-inflation-corrected-activation](TPC-064.md#ref-ee4758ed683c6da9)\] are sharp at every admissible value of the constants.

###### ref-5247492ff947b02d

###### ref-6518abab974bede1

> **Corollary: One block and logarithmic label count give no inflation control** \[cor:tpc64-one-block-no-inflation-control\] For every fixed $A>0$, there is a family of examples with $$|K_X|=1,\qquad  \mathfrak I_X=X^{A+o(1)}.  \label{eq:tpc64-one-block-polynomial-inflation}$$ Hence even the stronger statement $|K_X|=1$, and therefore the usual $|K_X|=O(\log X)=X^{o(1)}$, supplies no upper bound on parent inflation.

> **Proof** In [\[ex:tpc64-arbitrary-faithful-inflation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-889374fa9418c40f), take $N=\lceil X^A\rceil$. All points have the same cofactor and hence the same external cofactor-block label.

This also proves that parent inflation is not a disguised block-count loss. A polynomial loss may occur entirely inside one block.

## New-only rows and terminal-zero rows

###### ref-853412b35bc0a07d

###### ref-cab07e74afff56ba

###### ref-9174d53c4a22769c

> **Proposition: New-only row no-go** \[prop:tpc64-countermodel-new-only-row\] There are terminal-faithful populated repairs with a row $m_1$ satisfying $$P_{m_1}=H_{m_1}=T_{m_1}=0  <\widetilde P_{m_1}.  \label{eq:tpc64-countermodel-new-only-data}$$ For every such repair, $$\mathfrak I_X=+\infty,  \qquad  \tau_{\mathrm{rep}}=0.  \label{eq:tpc64-countermodel-new-only-death}$$ Thus no finite literal-to-repaired parent domination and no positive full-row repaired activation theorem can hold.

> **Proof** Take a literal terminal-active atom on a row $m_0$, with unit parent and terminal square. Add to the declared repair a different pair on a row $m_1$ whose populated rectangle contains one coordinate $v$ with $c_v=1$ and $t_h(v)=0$, and declare $v$ to be the only ambient coordinate over that added pair. The extra coordinate is terminal-null, so terminal faithfulness is preserved, but [\[eq:tpc64-countermodel-new-only-data\]](TPC-064.md#ref-cab07e74afff56ba) holds with $\widetilde P_{m_1}=1$. The row convention and \[[thm:tpc64-exact-parent-inflation](TPC-064.md#ref-4f23b67a6e7188c1),[thm:tpc64-uniform-activation-minima](TPC-064.md#ref-a2bb8478422a9a43)\] give [\[eq:tpc64-countermodel-new-only-death\]](TPC-064.md#ref-9174d53c4a22769c). Equivalently, the row direction $e_{m_1}$ has positive repaired parent and zero terminal energy.

The canonical least parent-complete relation does not insert an arbitrary extra row. The point of the proposition is that any larger populated repair, change of row coordinates, or claimed comparison must prove that new-only rows are absent; terminal faithfulness alone does not prove it.

###### ref-4eedaa34a51bd8f6

###### ref-ebdb1294697d99f8

###### ref-c395735bf3f99ed7

> **Proposition: Terminal-zero row kill** \[prop:tpc64-countermodel-terminal-zero-row\] If a required row satisfies $$P_{m_0}>0,\qquad H_{m_0}=T_{m_0}=0,  \label{eq:tpc64-countermodel-terminal-zero-data}$$ then $$\tau_{\mathrm{hyp}}=\tau_{\mathrm{rep}}=0  \label{eq:tpc64-countermodel-terminal-zero-death}$$ for every parent-complete terminal-faithful repair on a full coefficient class containing $e_{m_0}$. Enlarging the retained pair relation cannot repair the zero without changing the terminal field.

> **Proof** The literal row direction has positive parent and zero terminal energy, so $\tau_{\mathrm{hyp}}=0$. Terminal faithfulness leaves $T_{m_0}$ unchanged, while parent completeness gives $\widetilde P_{m_0}\ge P_{m_0}>0$. The same direction therefore forces $\tau_{\mathrm{rep}}=0$.

A distinguished vector can avoid either bad row by having zero coefficient there. That is a different quantifier and yields only the weighted average in [\[thm:tpc64-distinguished-activation-average\]](TPC-064.md#ref-f373a91dc5c845c8).

## The terminal-active-parent tautology

###### ref-84ac3e01af91e9db

###### ref-74875aafb1fa019d

###### ref-0ea991d0c99a313a

\[ex:tpc64-terminal-active-parent-tautology\] Fix $L\ge1$. On one row, take two literal pre-terminal atoms: $$|c_{u_0}|^2=1,\quad t_h(u_0)=1,  \qquad  |c_{u_1}|^2=L-1,\quad t_h(u_1)=0,$$ omitting $u_1$ when $L=1$. Then $$P_m=L,\qquad H_m=T_m=1,  \qquad  \frac{T_m}{P_m}=\frac1L.  \label{eq:tpc64-tautology-true-activation}$$ If one replaces the literal parent by the terminal-active incidence $\{u_0\}$, the newly defined parent mass is $H_m=1$, and the displayed ratio becomes $$\frac{T_m}{H_m}=1.  \label{eq:tpc64-tautology-false-parent-ratio}$$ The apparent improvement can be arbitrarily large, but it is obtained by deleting the literal terminal-null parent mass $L-1$. It is not a lower bound relative to the original parent.

The example is the exact reason that multiplier intensity is not itself terminal activation. In the notation of [\[thm:tpc64-exact-three-factor-activation\]](TPC-064.md#ref-874caa150ca100ae), redefining the parent as the terminal-active support silently replaces the alignment factor $\alpha_m=1/L$ by $1$.

## Multiplier intensity is not a probability

###### ref-282952f2cb24aaea

###### ref-d3ee4a174bbfb3f6

\[ex:tpc64-unbounded-multiplier-intensity\] Take one literal terminal-active atom with $|c_u|^2=1$ and $|t_h(u)|=M$, where $M>0$. Then $$P_m=H_m=1,\qquad  T_m=M^2,\qquad  \vartheta_m=M^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-unbounded-intensity-data}$$ The number $M$ is arbitrary. Hence $\vartheta_m$ is a shaped multiplier intensity and can exceed $1$ by an arbitrary factor.

Any argument which clips $T_m/H_m$ at one, interprets it as a survival probability, or removes the literal shaped weight changes the physical quadratic form.

## A largest block can be chosen only after positivity

###### ref-6e6e53a243077ec7

###### ref-864b28add434bcd8

For $z\in Z_X$, define the nonnegative shaped energy on block $k$ by $$T_k(z):=\sum_{m\in\mathcal M_X}T_{k,m}|z_m|^2.  \label{eq:tpc64-block-terminal-form}$$ Exact block disintegration gives $$T(z)=\sum_{k\in K_X}T_k(z).  \label{eq:tpc64-terminal-block-sum}$$

###### ref-255efa5b1e3b0bb4

###### ref-66a0ae2a49d35883

> **Theorem: Largest-block quantifier and sharpness** \[thm:tpc64-largest-block-after-positivity\] Assume $1\le L_X:=|K_X|<\infty$. For each fixed vector $z$, $$\boxed{  \max_{k\in K_X}T_k(z)\ge\frac{T(z)}{L_X}.}  \label{eq:tpc64-largest-block-bound}$$ The constant $L_X^{-1}$ is sharp. Consequently:
>
> 1.  if a prior theorem gives $$T(z)\ge X^{-\lambda+o(1)}P(z)$$ for one fixed distinguished $z$, and $L_X=O(\log X)$, then some block $k=k(z,X)$ satisfies the same fixed-power lower bound up to $X^{o(1)}$;
>
> 2.  if $T(z)=0$, every block energy is zero, so the largest-block step creates no activation;
>
> 3.  the maximizing block may depend on the row or coefficient vector, and [\[eq:tpc64-largest-block-bound\]](TPC-064.md#ref-66a0ae2a49d35883) alone does not select one block supporting a coefficient-uniform lower bound.

> **Proof** The maximum of $L_X$ nonnegative numbers is at least their average. Equality holds when all $T_k(z)=T(z)/L_X$, proving sharpness. The first consequence uses $L_X^{-1}=X^{-o(1)}$; the second is immediate from nonnegativity.
>
> For the last assertion, take two rows and two blocks. Put $$P_{m_1}=P_{m_2}=1,\qquad  T_{1,m_1}=1,\quad T_{2,m_2}=1,$$ with the two cross terms zero. The total terminal form equals the parent form, so its uniform activation constant is one. On block $1$, the row direction $e_{m_2}$ has zero energy; on block $2$, the direction $e_{m_1}$ has zero energy. Thus neither single block has a positive full-row activation constant.

The block theorem is therefore downstream of activation, not a source of activation. A logarithmic number of blocks costs no fixed power only after a positive total terminal estimate has been proved in the same coefficient scope.

## Claim boundary

These countermodels prove non-implications for finite parent and terminal interfaces. They show that terminal faithfulness does not control parent inflation, that label count does not control populated parent mass, that zero rows kill uniform activation, and that block selection cannot create positivity. They do not show that the literal arithmetic carrier has a bad row or large inflation. Conversely, none of the exact identities in this paper proves positive physical activation, signed Möbius cancellation, a fixed-shift parity estimate, or a prime-pair lower bound.

# Denominator compatibility and the effective activation loss

A terminal numerator becomes useful only when every paper in the chain uses the same parent denominator. This section records the exact compatibility requirements, defines the inflation and effective terminal losses, and inserts them into the inherited endpoint ledger without double charging.

## Exact constants and exponent definitions

###### ref-119d3b382e4fc5d7

###### ref-374be81c0ed68f0d

###### ref-1b56ce06234ace74

Assume first that the declared class contains at least one vector with positive literal parent. Define $$\begin{aligned}  \mathfrak I_X(\mathfrak Z_X)  &:=  \sup_{\substack{z\in\mathfrak Z_X\\\widetilde P(z)>0}}  \frac{\widetilde P(z)}{P(z)}  \in[1,+\infty],  \label{eq:tpc64-compatible-inflation-constant}\\  c_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}(X;\mathfrak Z_X)  &:=  \inf_{\substack{z\in\mathfrak Z_X\\P(z)>0}}  \frac{T(z)}{P(z)},  \label{eq:tpc64-literal-terminal-constant}\\  c_{\rm term}^{\rm eff}(X;\mathfrak Z_X)  &:=  \inf_{\substack{z\in\mathfrak Z_X\\                   \widetilde P(z)>0}}  \frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}.  \label{eq:tpc64-effective-terminal-constant}\end{aligned}$$ In [\[eq:tpc64-compatible-inflation-constant\]](TPC-064.md#ref-119d3b382e4fc5d7), the quotient is understood as $+\infty$ when $P(z)=0<\widetilde P(z)$. Thus a finite compatible inflation constant automatically excludes a new-only vector from the declared scope. For the canonical hybrid repair there is no new-only row, and the full-space value of $\mathfrak I_X$ is the maximum row inflation. For a distinguished singleton, all three quantities are ratios on that one vector and must retain the distinguished label.

###### ref-be54e24152048837

The exact comparison is $$\boxed{  c_{\rm term}^{\rm eff}(X;\mathfrak Z_X)  \ge  \frac{c_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}(X;\mathfrak Z_X)}       {\mathfrak I_X(\mathfrak Z_X)}}  \label{eq:tpc64-compatible-activation-comparison}$$ whenever the right side is defined with finite $\mathfrak I_X$. Indeed, finiteness gives $P(z)>0$ whenever $\widetilde P(z)>0$, and for every such vector $$\frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  =  \frac{T(z)}{P(z)}  \left(\frac{\widetilde P(z)}{P(z)}\right)^{-1}  \ge  \frac{c_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}(X;\mathfrak Z_X)}       {\mathfrak I_X(\mathfrak Z_X)}.$$ Taking the infimum proves [\[eq:tpc64-compatible-activation-comparison\]](TPC-064.md#ref-be54e24152048837). This is sharp already in a one-row model. A direct estimate relative to $\widetilde P$, such as [\[thm:tpc64-threshold-capacity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-51a926cbc0329ab7), can be stronger.

Define $\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}$ analogously from $c_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}$.

> **Proposition: Effective loss from the two honest routes** \[prop:tpc64-two-effective-loss-routes\] In one fixed nonempty quantifier scope. In route (ii) this scope is a normalized or capacity-restricted slice and need not be homogeneous:
>
> 1.  if $\mathfrak I_X\le X^{\lambda_{\rm infl}+o(1)}$ and $c_{\rm term}^{\rm lit} \ge X^{-\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}+o(1)}$, then $$c_{\rm term}^{\rm eff}         \ge         X^{-(\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}              +\lambda_{\rm infl})+o(1)};         \label{eq:tpc64-inflated-terminal-exponent}$$

The second is [\[thm:tpc64-threshold-capacity\]](TPC-064.md#ref-51a926cbc0329ab7). > **Theorem: Exact five-arrow compatible chain** \[thm:tpc64-five-arrow-compatible-chain\] Suppose all six forms are nonnegative quadratic forms evaluated on the same initial $z$ in the same scope. Suppose also that $$c_{\rm pre},c_{\rm term},c_{\rm post},c_{\rm reasm},c_{\rm out}\ge0$$ and that $$\begin{aligned}  \widetilde P(z)  &\ge c_{\rm pre}(X)\mathcal Q_{\rm in}(z),  \label{eq:tpc64-pre-parent-arrow}\\  T(z)  &\ge c_{\rm term}(X)\widetilde P(z),  \label{eq:tpc64-terminal-arrow}\\  \mathcal Q_A(z)  &\ge c_{\rm post}(X)T(z),  \label{eq:tpc64-post-terminal-arrow}\\  \mathcal Q_F(z)  &\ge c_{\rm reasm}(X)\mathcal Q_A(z),  \label{eq:tpc64-reassembly-arrow}\\  \mathcal Q_{\rm out}(z)  &\ge c_{\rm out}(X)\mathcal Q_F(z).  \label{eq:tpc64-downstream-arrow}\end{aligned}$$ Every output carrier and normalization in one line is required to be the literal input carrier and normalization in the next. > **Proof** Apply the five inequalities successively to the named intermediate forms generated by the same $z$. No supremum, infimum, selector, shift, or coefficient vector changes during the substitutions.

The exact TPC–47/51 dictionary may make $c_{\rm pre}=1$ on a literally identical carrier. TPC–54 selection or TPC–59 occupancy costs belong to $c_{\rm pre}$ only if they compare $\mathcal Q_{\rm in}$ with the precise repaired parent used in [\[eq:tpc64-terminal-arrow\]](TPC-064.md#ref-12333d10d89e0713). ## No-double-charge ledger and stop rule

###### ref-e44fd5218ea8cbe7

###### ref-1f116e59f20f21cb

###### ref-e577a9844593656c

Write $$\Lambda_{\rm pre}  :=\lambda_{\rm sel}+\lambda_{\rm occ}+\lambda_{\rm blk}.  \label{eq:tpc64-pre-terminal-ledger}$$ When each symbol corresponds to a genuinely distinct arrow in [\[thm:tpc64-five-arrow-compatible-chain\]](TPC-064.md#ref-7d5ee656f23db493), the inherited physical ledger is $$\boxed{  \begin{aligned}  \Lambda_{\rm tot}^{(64)}  :={}&  \lambda_{\rm sel}+\lambda_{\rm occ}+\lambda_{\rm blk}  +\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm eff}  +\lambda_{\rm sing}\\  &+\lambda_H+\lambda_{\rm aff}  +\lambda_{\rm reasm}  +\lambda_{\rm bdry}+\lambda_{\rm tail}.  \end{aligned}}  \label{eq:tpc64-complete-loss-ledger}$$ The strict endpoint test is $$\boxed{\Lambda_{\rm tot}^{(64)}<\frac1{400}.}  \label{eq:tpc64-strict-stop-rule}$$

The following no-double-charge rules are part of the formula.

1.  If $\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm eff}$ is obtained from $\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm lit}+\lambda_{\rm infl}$, only $\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm eff}$ enters [\[eq:tpc64-complete-loss-ledger\]](TPC-064.md#ref-1f116e59f20f21cb); the two diagnostic inputs are not added again.

2.  If the capacity theorem proves the terminal arrow directly relative to $\widetilde P$, then $\eta_X,\delta_{\eta_X},\kappa_X$, and $\lambda_{\rm cap}$ are ingredients of the one term $\lambda_{\rm term}^{\rm eff}$, not four further arrows.

3.  If selection, occupancy, or block losses were already used to prove the same direct ratio $T/\widetilde P$, they must be removed from $\Lambda_{\rm pre}$. Conversely, if they compare an earlier $\mathcal Q_{\rm in}$ with $\widetilde P$, they remain separate.

4.  A represented-reference exponent used only to certify $\lambda_{\rm reasm}$ is not charged again if its physical arrow is already included in $\lambda_H+\lambda_{\rm aff}$.

5.  Exact set identities, faithful hypergraph retention, literal coordinate permutations or unitary relabelings, and $O(\log X)$ diagnostic labels have zero fixed-power cost. A more general coordinate change has zero such cost only after a uniform or $X^{o(1)}$ condition-number bound is proved; otherwise its loss must enter the ledger. Erasing diagnostic labels is not a coordinate change and belongs to $\lambda_{\rm reasm}$.

If a mandatory constant in [\[thm:tpc64-five-arrow-compatible-chain\]](TPC-064.md#ref-7d5ee656f23db493) is zero infinitely often, the matching exponent route stops. If a necessary proved loss is at least $1/400$, the exponent-only endpoint route also stops, including at equality. A separate constant-sensitive endpoint argument at equality would lie outside this strict exponent ledger and would not satisfy [\[eq:tpc64-strict-stop-rule\]](TPC-064.md#ref-e577a9844593656c). A distinguished success, an averaged-shift success, or a different-denominator success does not evade either stop rule.

## What is not proved

The following statements remain absent.

1.  No favorable lower bound is proved for $\delta_{\eta_X}$ at any fixed or polynomially shrinking threshold $\eta_X$.

2.  The actual physical coefficient vector is not shown to satisfy $\kappa_X>1-\delta_{\eta_X}$, let alone with an endpoint-quality quantitative margin.

3.  No bound $$\min_{m:P_m>0}\frac{T_m}{P_m}         \ge X^{-\lambda+o(1)}$$ is proved for the full coefficient class. Finite-scale positivity of every row would still be weaker than such a bound.

4.  No subpower or endpoint-quality bound is proved for the parent inflation of a pair-populated repair. The literal-active-hypergraph baseline avoids that inflation but may not satisfy a downstream demand for pair coordinates.

5.  The labeled terminal square is not shown to survive dyadic-label erasure, residual grouping, comparison with the native affine Gram, boundary reconnection, or the ultra-tail map.

6.  None of the nonnegative endpoint losses is newly shown to fit inside the strict total budget.

The capacity theorem is therefore not evidence that $\delta_\eta$ or $\kappa_X$ is favorable. Likewise, failure of its threshold is failure of that certificate; it does not prove that the actual terminal ratio $T(z)/\widetilde P(z)$ is small.

#### Conclusion.

TPC–64 gives the exact meaning of true terminal activation relative to a parent-faithful denominator.
