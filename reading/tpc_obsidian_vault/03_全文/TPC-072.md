## Abstract

We audit the ten-paper block TPC–63 through TPC–72, using the completed results of TPC–63 through TPC–71 as input. The purpose is not to promote interface theorems into arithmetic conclusions, but to determine exactly which dependencies are closed, which proof mechanisms have failed, and which literal estimates remain necessary before a fixed-shift endpoint can be claimed.

The represented branch now has an exact chain from canonical cofactor provenance through terminal activation, activated-range Grams, actual pair-codegree geometry, and componentwise determinant mass. TPC–68 gives exact abstract certificates for the complete signed missing-native operator. TPC–69–TPC–71 then isolate one mixed scalar sector and reduce its anchored four-corner contributions through erasure-boundary words, primitive cofactor coordinates, and a divisor-lifted two-dimensional Abel certificate. In particular, the first cross-key sign becomes $\mu(a)\mu(b)\mu(u)\mu(v)$, is constant along each affine prime ray, and leaves a primitive-plane kernel. The identity $$\mathbf 1_{(u,v)=1}\mu(u)\mu(v)=\mu(uv)$$ and a diagonal divisor lift reduce regular kernels to restricted Mertens sums. Fixed-divisor prime number theory then gives cancellation when every fixed-$d$ mixed variation is $O_d(\|K\|_\infty)$. It does not give the mass-normalized variation or divisor tail required by the actual physical kernel.

The audit also closes a tempting but type-incorrect physical promotion of this certificate. The TPC–70 primitive-plane target is an anchored four-corner scalar contributing to $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$, not a two-corner matrix entry of the erasure operator $E$. Consequently, even a sharp bound for every audited primitive-plane target does not by itself imply an entrywise majorant, a row-star estimate, a Perron bound, or an operator norm estimate. The literal anchor multiplicity must be retained, and the four-corner normalization must not be applied a second time.

We prove a dependency theorem for the audited chain, reconcile its exponent notations into one no-double-charge ledger, and state exact kill criteria. Algebraic identities and isometric or subpower-conditioned relabelings have zero fixed-power cost. Every non-isometric physical comparison exposed by them must be charged exactly once in a total strictly below $1/400$. The missing-native perturbative gate $\sigma_M>\nu_M+\lambda_{\rm rep}$ and the nuisance condition $\|C_N\|=o(1)$ remain separate. As of TPC–71 there is no completed $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate satisfying the full fixed-shift quantifier. The next stage may estimate the physical primitive-plane variation and tail to control the first mixed trace, but an operator theorem additionally needs one of three new bridges: a direct two-corner representation, row-local anchored bounds dominating the relevant stars, or uniformly controlled complete decorated moments. Terminal activation and the represented lower frame must advance in parallel. No fixed-$2$, parity-breaking, prime-pair, or twin-prime conclusion is drawn.

# Introduction

A long proof program can accumulate exact lemmas while losing track of the quantifier needed at its endpoint. This danger is particularly acute in a fixed-shift parity problem. A theorem for an abstract sign matrix, a fixed row pair, an averaged shift, or a smoothed coefficient family can be correct without estimating the literal growing operator. Likewise, an exact coordinate identity can remove a modeling ambiguity without producing any arithmetic saving.

TPC–63 through TPC–71 were designed to separate these roles [`\citep{WangTPC63,WangTPC64,WangTPC65,WangTPC66,WangTPC67,WangTPC68,WangTPC69,WangTPC70,WangTPC71}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-822675dc51b7acfd); [\[WangTPC64\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-b1886457fa5c7e3a); [\[WangTPC65\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-fc6eaf1979a63b43); [\[WangTPC66\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-ad0dc0c9ae7cac49); [\[WangTPC67\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-a737718f0b4af9e0); [\[WangTPC68\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-b8c461e9c6be3261); [\[WangTPC69\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-04567db5550964e9); [\[WangTPC70\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-9d0361df6408698e); [\[WangTPC71\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-f2e0afa0bd3d4064). They form two chains. The first follows the represented packet: $$\text{provenance} \longrightarrow \text{activation} \longrightarrow \text{represented Gram} \longrightarrow \text{collision geometry} \longrightarrow \text{determinant mass}.$$ The second begins with the complete missing-native packet and then follows one mixed scalar sector: $$\begin{aligned} \text{signed native operator} &\longrightarrow \text{first mixed trace} \longrightarrow \text{primitive cofactor plane}\\ &\longrightarrow \text{two-dimensional variation}. \end{aligned}$$ The represented branch and the complete missing-native operator meet only at physical reassembly. The descendant primitive-plane chain does not itself return from a scalar trace sector to the missing-native operator. A result on one branch does not silently supply a missing hypothesis on the other.

## Why the present audit is necessary

Three developments make a route audit timely.

First, TPC–63 proves that the inherited single-band freezing certificate cannot reach the critical high face within the endpoint budget [`\citep{WangTPC63}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-822675dc51b7acfd). The failure concerns a proof mechanism, not the actual weighted mass. Second, TPC–66 gives a genuine fixed-shift arithmetic support theorem: the reduced codegree of every fixed row pair is subpower on the critical block [`\citep{WangTPC66}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-ad0dc0c9ae7cac49). Yet TPC–67 shows why pair sparsity is not a represented lower-frame theorem: long cores and phase holonomy remain [`\citep{WangTPC67}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-a737718f0b4af9e0). Third, TPC–68–TPC–71 progressively remove false sources of Möbius randomness. Same-residual signs freeze, one-key mixed traces vanish, the complete quadratic erasure response is nonnegative, and the surviving primitive sign is constant along each prime ray [`\citep{WangTPC68,WangTPC69,WangTPC70}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-b8c461e9c6be3261); [\[WangTPC69\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-04567db5550964e9); [\[WangTPC70\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-9d0361df6408698e). TPC–71 nevertheless opens a new positive door: regular primitive-plane kernels cancel by an exact divisor lift and two-dimensional Abel summation [`\citep{WangTPC71}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-f2e0afa0bd3d4064).

There is also a necessary type boundary. The primitive-plane object inherited from TPC–70 contains one erasure edge and one same-residual anchor. It is therefore a four-corner contribution to $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$, whereas an entry of $E$ is a two-corner sum. Treating the former as an entrywise majorant for the latter changes the mathematical object, loses the anchor index, and can apply the row normalization twice. TPC–71 correctly retains the anchored trace target. The present audit promotes that boundary to an explicit non-substitution law: the direct primitive-plane-to-star/Perron promotion is unavailable.

These are real advances, but of different logical types. The present paper classifies them before a local improvement is mistaken for an endpoint theorem.

## Main conclusions

The audit has seven conclusions.

1.  TPC–63–TPC–71 close a sequence of necessary $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ interfaces. No paper in the block proves a complete $\mathrm{L2}$ cancellation estimate.

2.  The represented and missing-native branches are logically independent until shorted reassembly. Determinant mass does not bound the missing-native defect, and primitive-plane cancellation does not prove a represented lower frame.

3.  Several routes are permanently closed: the inherited single-band support certificate, same-residual Möbius randomness, the one-key mixed trace, and a negative quadratic erasure correction.

4.  TPC–71 proves cancellation relative to ambient area for regular kernels. The physical denominator is actual atomic mass, so the mass-normalized mixed variation and diagonal tail are new hypotheses, not corollaries.

5.  The primitive-plane target is an anchored contribution to $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$, not an entry of $E$. A separate direct two-corner representation, row-local anchored-star bridge, or complete-moment argument is required before an operator theorem can be invoked.

6.  Algebraic identities and isometric or subpower-conditioned relabelings cost zero fixed power. Every non-isometric physical comparison must be charged once in a common ledger strictly below $1/400$.

7.  The next routes have explicit success and failure tests. One route controls the literal anchored primitive-plane kernel strongly enough to bound the complete first mixed trace. The other constructs a genuine operator-level carrier through direct entries, row-local anchored stars, or complete moments. A global-trace estimate alone is insufficient.

# Audit levels and invariant physical data

## The three levels

###### ref-8a5737d07f3c7073

> **Definition: Audit levels** \[def:audit-levels\] We use the following labels.
>
> 1.  A finite-dimensional, combinatorial, or abstract analytic theorem valid for the declared object, before specialization to the literal growing physical family.
>
> 2.  An exact identification or unconditional support statement on the literal fixed-$h_0$ carrier, retaining its coefficients, labels, support, and normalization, but not proving the required signed asymptotic saving.
>
> 3.  A uniform arithmetic estimate for the actual physical coefficients in the fixed-shift growing family, with sufficient strength and quantifiers to enter the endpoint chain.

An $\mathrm{L1}$ upper count can use number theory and still remain $\mathrm{L1}$ when it controls support geometry rather than the required signed operator. Conversely, a theorem need not solve the final problem to be $\mathrm{L2}$; a fixed-power saving on one literal endpoint arrow is already an arithmetic advance if its scope matches the physical chain.

## The six invariant checks

Every proposed promotion to $\mathrm{L2}$ is tested against:

1.  the literal physical coefficients;

2.  one prescribed fixed nonzero shift $h_0$;

3.  raw physical atomic normalization;

4.  a canonical or explicitly justified representation;

5.  the actual active support, with no coefficient-dependent deletion;

6.  the complete endpoint loss, strictly below $1/400$.

Dropping any check changes either the object, the norm, or the quantifier.

## Three kinds of progress

###### ref-ef7936934c814c32

> **Definition: Counted progress** \[def:counted-progress\] A result counts as forward proof progress in this audit if it does at least one of the following:
>
> 1.  improves a concrete exponent for literal physical coefficients;
>
> 2.  closes a necessary interface in the dependency chain; or
>
> 3.  rules out a route by an exact physical obstruction or a sharp non-implication theorem.

The definition recognizes structural progress without relabeling it as arithmetic cancellation. Numerical stability, generic phases, randomized signs, or finite fixed-dimensional experiments do not satisfy [\[def:counted-progress\]](TPC-072.md#ref-ef7936934c814c32) unless they yield an exact certificate.

## Uniform and distinguished scopes

Several papers admit two honest coefficient scopes.

-   A *uniform* theorem holds for every vector in the declared coefficient class and therefore requires rowwise or operator control.

-   A *distinguished* theorem holds for one fixed physical vector chosen independently of the later estimate.

The second cannot be entered as the first. Likewise, a fixed-pair estimate, a density-one family, or an average over shifts cannot be substituted for the complete fixed-$h_0$ growing-row statement.

###### ref-4ff21a7a35a3e277

> **Proposition: Audit monotonicity** \[prop:audit-monotonicity\] An exact $\mathrm{L0}$ identity plus an $\mathrm{L1}$ crosswalk does not imply an $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate without a quantitative arithmetic input on the crosswalked object.

> **Proof** Take any finite incidence or matrix identity in the audited chain and fill the same support with two coefficient systems having different phases. TPC–65–TPC–69 contain explicit cycle, row-capacity, star, and holonomy countermodels showing that the same structural data can yield incompatible spectral or cancellation behavior [`\citep{WangTPC65,WangTPC66,WangTPC67,WangTPC68,WangTPC69}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-fc6eaf1979a63b43); [\[WangTPC66\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-ad0dc0c9ae7cac49); [\[WangTPC67\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-a737718f0b4af9e0); [\[WangTPC68\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-b8c461e9c6be3261); [\[WangTPC69\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-04567db5550964e9). Therefore the structural identity and physical dictionary do not determine the missing quantitative estimate.

# TPC–63–TPC–64: provenance and true activation

## TPC–63: the carrier cannot be repaired by declaration

TPC–63 distinguishes a literal full incidence from its pair projection [`\citep{WangTPC63}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-822675dc51b7acfd). Three faithfulness questions are separate:

1.  preservation of the pre-terminal raw form;

2.  preservation of terminal atoms; and

3.  preservation of the grouped physical operator.

An active spurious cross can pass one test and fail another. When a terminal-faithful pair completion exists, there is a unique least relation; when it does not, the literal incidence hypergraph is the canonical faithful object.

###### ref-520dd8b4fe817a42

Let $W_H(m)$ be native-high parent mass and $W_A(m)$ the part aligned with the terminal predicate. Inside the aligned mass, let the declared retained relation supply completion ratio $c_{\rm comp}(m)$. TPC–63 proves the exact factorization $$\frac{W_{\rm exposed}(m)}{W_H(m)}  =  \frac{W_A(m)}{W_H(m)}\,c_{\rm comp}(m)  \label{eq:tpc72-exposure-factorization}$$ whenever $W_H(m)>0$. Passing to the maximal eligible relation may make $c_{\rm comp}(m)=1$, but it cannot improve the alignment factor.

The same paper proves a negative $\mathrm{L1}$ result for the inherited single-band mechanism. The balanced band certified by the old freezing inequality is eventually disjoint from the critical high face. Retuning that sufficient inequality to the critical cofactor scale already costs more than the entire $1/400$ allowance. Even after an independently justified retuning, a completely omitted dyadic block makes the inherited support-cardinality/Brun–Titchmarsh certificate yield no positive fixed-power saving.

###### ref-61d7ec00fff2d8db

> **Proposition: Scope of the TPC–63 no-go** \[prop:tpc63-no-go-scope\] The TPC–63 negative result closes the named single-band support certificate. It does not imply that the actual omitted weighted mass is large.

> **Proof** The certificate replaces literal weights by support and harmonic majorants. Its failure shows only that those upper data cannot establish the desired lower ratio. A coefficient-sensitive weighted distribution estimate or exact signed shorting uses information absent from that certificate and is not contradicted.

Orthogonally retaining all nonempty dyadic labels gives exact raw-energy coverage at $O(\log X)$ label complexity. The later erasure of these labels is a physical map, not a harmless relabeling.

## TPC–64: activation, inflation, and capacity

###### ref-aad2f0d0226aa57a

TPC–64 defines, before the free row coefficient is chosen, the parent mass $P_m$, terminal-support mass $H_m$, and shaped terminal energy $T_m$ [`\citep{WangTPC64}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-b1886457fa5c7e3a). On a row where both denominators are nonzero, $$\frac{T_m}{P_m}  =  \frac{H_m}{P_m}\frac{T_m}{H_m}.  \label{eq:tpc72-activation-factorization}$$ The two factors are terminal-support alignment and terminal multiplier intensity.

###### ref-aa7c41d6fb45068a

A parent-complete pair lift can add terminal-null coordinates. Its repaired parent is $$\widetilde P_m=P_m+\Delta_m,  \qquad  T_m\ \text{unchanged}.  \label{eq:tpc72-parent-inflation}$$ Thus a pair completion may preserve the numerator while diluting its denominator by an arbitrary polynomial factor. A newly created parent row with $P_m=0<\widetilde P_m$ makes uniform parent domination infinite. The literal-active-hypergraph baseline avoids this inflation exactly.

###### ref-dd08348edf6fe09d

For $g_m=T_m/\widetilde P_m$, let $\delta_\eta$ be the repaired-parent fraction on rows $g_m\ge\eta$, and let $\kappa_X$ measure the occupied coefficient capacity. TPC–64 proves the sharp lower certificate $$\frac{T(z)}{\widetilde P(z)}  \ge  \eta\left(1-\frac{1-\delta_\eta}{\kappa_X}\right)_+.  \label{eq:tpc72-capacity-certificate}$$ The right side is positive exactly when $\kappa_X>1-\delta_\eta$.

## The first unresolved arithmetic arrow

###### ref-504c04691056a665

###### ref-df7330195754b240

There are two honest next inputs: $$\begin{aligned}  T_m  &\ge  X^{-\lambda_{\rm row}+o(1)}\widetilde P_m  \quad\text{for every active row},  \label{eq:tpc72-uniform-activation-target}\\  \eta_X\left(  1-\frac{1-\delta_{\eta_X}}{\kappa_X(z_*)}  \right)_+  &\ge  X^{-\lambda_{\rm term,*}^{\rm eff}+o(1)}  \quad\text{for one declared }z_*.  \label{eq:tpc72-distinguished-activation-target}\end{aligned}$$ Neither is proved in TPC–63 or TPC–64. A row with $T_m=0<\widetilde P_m$ kills [\[eq:tpc72-uniform-activation-target\]](TPC-072.md#ref-504c04691056a665) on every coefficient class containing that row, but it does not kill a separately justified distinguished scope.

> **Remark: Audit classification** The incidence factorizations, minimax constants, inflation laws, and capacity extremizers are $\mathrm{L0}$. Their literal fixed-$h_0$ specialization is $\mathrm{L1}$. Establishing either [\[eq:tpc72-uniform-activation-target\]](TPC-072.md#ref-504c04691056a665) or [\[eq:tpc72-distinguished-activation-target\]](TPC-072.md#ref-df7330195754b240) at endpoint strength would be $\mathrm{L2}$.

# TPC–65–TPC–67: represented range, support, and determinants

## TPC–65: the actual activated range

###### ref-829cf26f316d9108

Let $L_X$ be the terminal map and $$Y_X=\operatorname{Ran}L_X.$$ TPC–65 places the represented comparison on $Y_X$, rather than on a free ambient atom space [`\citep{WangTPC65}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-fc6eaf1979a63b43). If $A_X:Y_X\to H_X$ is the represented map, its exact lower constant is $$\beta_X  =  \inf_{0\ne y\in Y_X}  \frac{\|A_Xy\|^2}{\|y\|^2}  =  \lambda_{\min}(K_X),  \label{eq:tpc72-represented-gram}$$ where $K_X$ is the normalized activated row Gram with literal residual-fiber entries.

Terminal-faithful canonical repair leaves downstream Grams unchanged when both descriptions factor through the same terminal map. Parent inflation is therefore charged in the parent-to-terminal arrow, not again in $\beta_X$.

###### ref-348c15e51124b889

For orthogonally labeled blocks $A_\nu$, $$\operatorname{Ker}\!\left(\bigoplus_\nu A_\nu\right)  =  \bigcap_\nu\operatorname{Ker}A_\nu.  \label{eq:tpc72-kernel-fusion}$$ Singular blocks can fuse into a positive global Gram. Coherently erasing their labels instead creates cross-block terms and requires a separate fusion-angle theorem. TPC–65 also gives a sharp counterexample to transporting physical row capacity into spectral capacity.

###### ref-064c599c625b0adf

On one common carrier, define activation, represented, and reassembly constants $\tau_X,\beta_X,\delta_X$. The exact compatible product is $$\Gamma_X\ge\tau_X\beta_X\delta_X.  \label{eq:tpc72-three-stage-product}$$ This is a compatibility law, not a proof that any physical factor is favorable.

## TPC–66: actual pair geometry

###### ref-c501acbb782fb8d2

TPC–66 opens the literal entries of $K_X$ [`\citep{WangTPC66}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-ad0dc0c9ae7cac49). A fixed physical row contributes at most one terminal atom to a fixed residual output key. After row normalization, $$(K_X)_{mm}=1  \label{eq:tpc72-unit-diagonal}$$ for every active row.

###### ref-af99aecbbb254f28

###### ref-8abb752f59461c95

For distinct literal rows $m_1,m_2$, factor exchange and simultaneous CRT give the reduced pair-codegree bound $$c^{\rm red}_{m_1,m_2}(R)  \ll_{h_0}  X^{o(1)}\left(1+\frac{J}{R}\right).  \label{eq:tpc72-codegree}$$ Consequently, $$c^{\rm red}_{m_1,m_2}(R)=X^{o(1)}  \qquad(R\asymp J)  \label{eq:tpc72-critical-codegree}$$ uniformly over fixed active row pairs.

###### ref-6edf014e47a69535

This is a genuine arithmetic support theorem at one fixed $h_0$, but its scope must be retained. It counts reduced arithmetic cells, not the raw $\gamma$-decorated intersection. A right fiber may still have polynomial degree. Private outputs, comparison matrices, weighted diagonal dominance, forest gauges, star spectra, and unique-matching minors give exact lower-frame certificates. A cycle with fixed support, degree, codegree, and entry magnitudes can nevertheless be singular or nonsingular according to its holonomy. Support sparsity alone is therefore not a lower-frame theorem.

## The physical type check

Write $$\alpha_{\iota,m}  =  \frac{\beta_{\iota,m}}{\sqrt{W_m}}$$ for the complete signed and phased coefficient of native key $\iota$ in row $m$, including atomic normalization. ## TPC–71: abstract diagonal lift and two-dimensional Abel summation

###### ref-7242911188191ae6

###### ref-bf227c592993cd25

###### ref-1f20b44a77a1f48e

###### ref-2ff5bab7cd66569a

###### ref-6bcfe94ddc9219c0

The primitive sign has the elementary but decisive identity $$\mathbf 1_{(u,v)=1}\mu(u)\mu(v)=\mu(uv).  \label{eq:tpc72-primitive-collapse}$$ For a finitely supported kernel $K$, Möbius inversion of the coprimality condition gives the exact diagonal divisor lift $$\sum_{\substack{u,v\ge1\\(u,v)=1}}  \mu(u)\mu(v)K(u,v)  =  \sum_{d\ge1}\mu(d)  \sum_{\substack{a,b\ge1\\(a,d)=(b,d)=1}}  \mu(a)\mu(b)K(da,db).  \label{eq:tpc72-diagonal-lift}$$ Define the restricted Mertens function $$M_d(x)  :=  \sum_{\substack{n\le x\\(n,d)=1}}\mu(n).  \label{eq:tpc72-restricted-Mertens}$$ Extend $K^{[d]}(a,b)=K(da,db)$ by zero outside its finite support, and put $$\mathcal V_\square(K^{[d]})  :=  \sum_{a,b\ge1}  |\Delta_1\Delta_2K^{[d]}(a,b)|.  \label{eq:tpc72-mixed-variation}$$ Two-dimensional Abel summation then gives the exact fixed-$d$ identity $$\sum_{\substack{a,b\ge1\\(a,d)=(b,d)=1}}  \mu(a)\mu(b)K(da,db)  =  \sum_{x,y\ge1}  M_d(x)M_d(y)\Delta_1\Delta_2K^{[d]}(x,y).  \label{eq:tpc72-double-Abel}$$ Zero extension places the edge and corner terms inside the mixed difference. Omitting the boundary jumps would give a false certificate.

###### ref-c9614dc89e0210dc

###### ref-46053ea936a25a49

For $$\mathfrak M_d(X)=\max_{1\le x\le X}|M_d(x)|,$$ with $\mathfrak M_d(X)=0$ when $X<1$, define the exact diagonal tail $$\mathcal T_D(K)  :=  \sum_{d>D}|\mu(d)|  \sum_{x,y\ge1}  |\mu(dx)\mu(dy)K(dx,dy)|.  \label{eq:tpc72-exact-diagonal-tail}$$ Truncation at $d\le D$ yields $$\begin{aligned}  \left|  \sum_{\substack{u\le U,\ v\le V\\(u,v)=1}}  \mu(u)\mu(v)K(u,v)  \right|  \le{}&  \sum_{d\le D}  \mathfrak M_d(U/d)\mathfrak M_d(V/d)  \mathcal V_\square(K^{[d]})  +\mathcal T_D(K).  \label{eq:tpc72-master-plane-certificate}\end{aligned}$$ This tail retains the actual support and coefficients. In particular, the unweighted primitive rectangle cancels unconditionally. This use of prime number theory is standard; see, for example, [`\citet{IwaniecKowalski2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-072.md#cite-49dfea87eaada156).

## Why the smooth theorem is not the physical theorem

The physical denominator is not the ambient area $\|K\|_\infty UV$. It is an actual anchored, trace-sector, or atomic mass, and the kernel can contain hard prime-line masks. Therefore the next physical requirements are:

1.  a mass-normalized bound for $\mathcal V_\square((\mathcal K_{\mathfrak a}^{\circ})^{[d]})$, after the literal $q$-aggregation in [\[eq:tpc72-aggregate-erasure-kernel\]](TPC-072.md#ref-13e876e3df12fe10);

2.  the exact tail $\mathcal T_D(\mathcal K_{\mathfrak a}^{\circ})$ small relative to the same physical mass;

3.  justified uniformity in the divisor cutoff and over anchors, blocks, rows, and complete keys;

4.  summation over every literal anchor and row pair to control the complete $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$, without losing an unaffordable multiplicity; and

5.  for an operator theorem, either a new direct representation of the two-corner entries [\[eq:tpc72-two-corner-entry\]](TPC-072.md#ref-4523611f3f45bfcb), row-local anchored sums that dominate the relevant stars, or a separate expansion controlling complete decorated moments.

Sign-selected hard masks show that bounded amplitude alone cannot imply these conditions.

###### ref-dbdedd69caa33c3d

> **Theorem: Primitive-frontier classification** \[thm:primitive-frontier\] TPC–70 and TPC–71 close the algebraic reduction from a cross-key rectangle to [\[eq:tpc72-master-plane-certificate\]](TPC-072.md#ref-c9614dc89e0210dc) and prove cancellation for regular kernels. They do not prove a fixed-power or mass-normalized saving for the actual physical kernel. Their physical target is the anchored mixed scalar $\mathfrak X_{\mathfrak a}$, so the reduction does not produce an erasure-entry, star, Perron, or operator certificate.

> **Proof** The exact reduction is [\[eq:tpc72-physical-plane\]](TPC-072.md#ref-53d90b3a74289f2c)–[\[eq:tpc72-master-plane-certificate\]](TPC-072.md#ref-c9614dc89e0210dc). The regular-kernel corollary normalizes by $\|K\|_\infty UV$. No audited theorem bounds the actual kernel’s mixed variation or tail relative to the physical mass, nor sums all literal anchors and rows at an endpoint-quality scale. Moreover, [\[prop:four-corners-not-entry\]](TPC-072.md#ref-5b0be2f2e326c0f9) rules out promotion of that scalar bound to an entrywise operator majorant without a new representation. The missing implications are therefore new quantitative and type-correct premises, not consequences of the regular-kernel theorem.

## Mechanisms closed by obstruction

1.  **Inherited single band.** The old freezing certificate misses the critical face, and its retuning already exceeds $1/400$.

2.  **Label-count dilution.** $O(\log X)$ labels give bookkeeping economy, not a lower bound after coherent erasure.

3.  **Capacity transport.** Row capacity does not imply spectral capacity without a reducing or basis-transport theorem.

4.  **Support-only Gram closure.** Low pair codegree does not control $\gamma$-multiplicity, energy diffuseness, or cycle holonomy.

5.  **Same-residual randomness.** Its target sign is a removable row gauge on closed walks.

6.  **One-key mixed cancellation.** $\operatorname{tr}(E_iR_i^a)=0$ identically.

7.  **Negative quadratic correction.** The full $S_{2q,2}$ is nonnegative.

8.  **Prime-ray Möbius oscillation.** The primitive sign is constant along the affine ray parameter.

9.  **Bounded-amplitude variation.** Hard sign-selected masks show that $\|K\|_\infty$ alone does not control the primitive-plane sum.

10. **Four-corner-to-entry promotion.** An anchored primitive-plane target contributes to $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$; it is not a matrix entry of $E$, and cannot be inserted directly into a pair, star, or Perron operator certificate.

# Kill criteria and their logical strength

## Object and quantifier stops

1.  **Wrong object.** Removing side, external block, opposite row, $\gamma$, physical time, or another still-orthogonal native label estimates a different operator.

2.  **Wrong support.** Replacing actual active support by a terminal image, reduced cell, or coefficient-dependent subset changes the carrier.

3.  **Wrong norm.** Fiber-normalized atoms cannot replace raw atomic normalization.

4.  **Wrong quantifier.** A fixed row pair, density-one family, averaged shift, smoothed family, or partition chosen after the coefficient vector is known does not prove the uniform fixed-$h_0$ statement.

5.  **Wrong denominator.** A lower bound relative to ambient area, a repaired parent, or a labelled norm cannot be inserted relative to physical mass without an exact comparison.

These failures invalidate the proposed proof or promotion. They are not evidence that the underlying physical quantity is large or that every route to it fails.

## Hard structural stops

1.  If a required row has $T_m=0<\widetilde P_m$, the uniform terminal activation route is dead on any class containing that row.

2.  If the represented map restricted to the declared activated domain or quotient has a kernel, its uniform lower-frame constant there is zero. A kernel outside that domain is irrelevant, and a distinguished vector remains a different question.

3.  If a full-output collision component has genuine rank deficiency, its determinant mass is zero.

4.  If the actual shorted Gram has a kernel, uniform reassembly through the declared architecture is impossible.

5.  If the complete physical loss is at least $1/400$, including equality, the present exponent-only endpoint implication stops. A different constant-sensitive theorem would be a new argument.

## Certificate-local stops

The next failures are weaker.

1.  A pair majorant with $\tau<\delta$ fails to produce a subpower pair/Perron bound by that certificate. The positive exponent $\delta-\tau$ may still be absorbed by [\[eq:tpc72-zero-cost-reassembly\]](TPC-072.md#ref-1ced12587a9b3b77), and a later star or moment route remains open.

2.  A star saving with $\sigma<\delta/2$ fails to produce a subpower direct-star bound. The exponent $\delta/2-\sigma$ may still be compatible with the mass-degree gate, and complete walks remain open.

3.  Failure at one moment length does not kill other lengths; a fixed-$q$ estimate with uncontrolled constants does not prove a growing-$q$ theorem.

4.  Matching activity at least one kills the phase-uniform dominant matching certificate, not necessarily the minor.

5.  A superpolynomially small product over many orthogonal components does not kill the represented gap. The relevant obstruction is the actual worst component.

6.  Large mixed variation kills the TPC–71 Abel certificate at the required scale, not necessarily the literal signed sum.

7.  A large diagonal tail for one cutoff $D$ kills that truncation choice; it does not rule out a different exact decomposition.

8.  If $\sigma_M\le\nu_M+\lambda_{\rm rep}$, the phase-blind mass-degree certificate does not prove a small perturbation in that scope. It does not prove that the actual missing perturbation is large.

9.  Infinite parent inflation or new-only parent rows kill the literal-to-repaired inflation transfer. A direct capacity or literal-hypergraph activation route can remain; the hard uniform stop is $T_m=0<\widetilde P_m$ on a required row.

10. Small global $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$ does not certify any uniform local star.

11. Small anchored four-corner targets do not bound the two-corner entries of $E$; using them as such is a wrong-object failure, not an insufficient exponent.

## Primitive-plane route stops

The current narrow route has five specific tests.

1.  The complete divisor and native-key census must have only controlled multiplicity. Compressing keys before the census is forbidden.

2.  The zero-extended mixed variation must include every interior, edge, and corner jump. Omitting boundary jumps invalidates the Abel identity.

3.  The exact tail $\mathcal T_D(K)$ must be small relative to actual mass. Failure of the ambient specialization $\|K\|_\infty UV/D$ kills only that ambient majorant, not the exact support tail or another decomposition.

4.  The anchor bounds must survive the complete sum in [\[eq:tpc72-correct-trace-majorant\]](TPC-072.md#ref-1996f2010460d82e). Even if they do, the output is a mixed-trace theorem, not an operator theorem. Failure after anchorwise triangle summation kills that aggregation; it does not exclude cancellation across anchors or a complete-moment route.

5.  The four-corner normalization $1/(W_mW_n)$ must be retained literally and charged once. A further division by $\sqrt{W_mW_n}$ changes the scale.

Tests P1, P2, and P5 validate the object and identity; violating them changes the theorem rather than proving a negative result. P3 and P4 are certificate-local quantitative gates.

## Status line

The ten-paper block moves the program from an ambiguous cofactor-support picture to a falsifiable primitive-plane frontier. It has closed necessary interfaces and eliminated several false sources of cancellation. As of TPC–72, however, the $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic column required by the endpoint remains empty. The next advance must control the literal physical primitive-plane variation and tail strongly enough to bound the full anchored mixed trace. A separate advance must then construct a genuine two-corner, row-local anchored-star, or complete-moment operator carrier, while terminal activation and the represented lower frame are advanced in parallel. This is progress in proof architecture, not evidence that the parity barrier has been broken.
