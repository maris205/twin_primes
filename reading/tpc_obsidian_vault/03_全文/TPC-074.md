## Abstract

The primitive cofactor plane was previously attached to a four-corner coefficient in the first mixed trace of a fixed-shift normalized missing-native Gram. That representation preserves arithmetic signs but does not control a matrix entry or operator norm. We remove this object mismatch. For two active rows $m<n$, every complete common-output key fixes one time $j$ and two squarefree targets $$mj+h_0=Q_mS_m,\qquad nj+h_0=Q_nS_n.$$ Writing $$q=(S_m,S_n),\qquad S_m=qu,\qquad S_n=qv$$ gives pairwise coprime squarefree coordinates $q,u,v$, $q\mid |h_0(n-m)|$, and an exact affine solution lattice containing every realized prime key. Without assuming row–shift coprimality its spacing is $$\operatorname{lcm}\left(\frac{qu}{(m,qu)},\frac{qv}{(n,qv)}\right);$$ the proved primitive-row property of the inherited physical carrier reduces it to $quv$. After the inherited unitary row gauge, the target sign at every key is $\mu(u)\mu(v)$.

Let $d_m=ga$, $d_n=gb$, $(a,b)=1$. We prove that the residual labels are equal if and only if $$(u,v)=(b,a).$$ Thus the same-residual part is one distinguished primitive direction for each row pair, while every other primitive direction is the erasure part. Defining a literal kernel $L_{mn,q}(u,v)$ by summing $\overline{\beta_{i,m}}\beta_{i,n}/\sqrt{W_mW_n}$ over complete keys with the indicated coordinates, and aggregating the divisor-many $q$-slices, we obtain the exact formulas $$\widetilde H_{mn}  =\sum_{(u,v)=1}\mu(u)\mu(v)L_{mn}(u,v),$$ $$\widetilde R_{mn}  =\mu(a)\mu(b)L_{mn}(b,a),\qquad  \widetilde E_{mn}  =\sum_{\substack{(u,v)=1\\(u,v)\ne(b,a)}}  \mu(u)\mu(v)L_{mn}(u,v).$$ Hermitian reversal becomes $L_{nm,q}(v,u)=\overline{L_{mn,q}(u,v)}$.

The support-faithful entry kernels are primitive-supported, so their auxiliary diagonal tails vanish and ordinary two-dimensional Abel summation gives exact location-sensitive entry majorants. Collecting these majorants in a symmetric nonnegative matrix $P$ yields $\|H\|\le\rho(P)$, together with weighted Schur and row-star certificates. These are genuine operator carriers because they retain all entries, but no arithmetic estimate showing that $\rho(P)$ is small is proved. Countermodels separate direct entries from mixed traces, fixed-pair decay from operator control, and general complete-key formulas from direct-cell compressions. No fixed-$2$, parity-breaking, or prime-pair conclusion is drawn.

# Introduction

The fixed-shift missing-native Gram has the atomic-normalized form $$G_{\rm nat}=I+H,\qquad H=R+E,$$ where $R$ contains same-residual collisions and $E$ contains unequal-residual collisions created by erasure [`\citep{WangTPC61,WangTPC68}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-074.md#cite-c0f79b0b6cb1358c); [\[WangTPC68\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-074.md#cite-b8c461e9c6be3261). The exact source-row gauge of TPC–68 turns every off-diagonal entry into a signed two-affine sum, while preserving the spectrum, entry magnitudes, row stars, and collision graph [`\citep{WangTPC68}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-074.md#cite-b8c461e9c6be3261). Consequently, a direct arithmetic representation of those gauged entries would be a genuine operator interface.

TPC–70 instead begins with the first mixed trace $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$. A same-residual key and an erasure key form a four-corner rectangle. The same-residual key freezes two signs, and the remaining erasure key is reindexed by primitive cofactor coordinates $$q=(S_m,S_n),\qquad S_m=qu,\qquad S_n=qv$$ [`\citep{WangTPC70}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-074.md#cite-9d0361df6408698e). TPC–71 gives a divisor-lifted Abel certificate for the resulting plane [`\citep{WangTPC71}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-074.md#cite-f2e0afa0bd3d4064). TPC–73 then observes that the support-faithful zero extension is already primitive-supported; its auxiliary high-diagonal tail is identically zero [`\citep{WangTPC73}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-074.md#cite-397bc50bd6422e39).

That chain is exact but remains attached to a four-corner scalar. Even a perfect estimate for $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$ does not bound $E_{mn}$, a row star, or $\|H\|$. The present paper returns to the two-corner formula of TPC–68 and applies the cofactor coordinates to the actual common-output key itself. No same-residual anchor is needed to define the coordinates. The row divisors still identify one direction: $$d_mS_m=d_nS_n  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  (u,v)=(b,a),$$ where $d_m=ga$, $d_n=gb$, and $(a,b)=1$. The point $(b,a)$ therefore is not deleted from the full plane. It is exactly $R_{mn}$; the punctured plane is exactly $E_{mn}$.

## Main results

The paper proves the following finite, literal statements.

1.  Every actual complete common-output key determines canonical squarefree, pairwise coprime coordinates $q,u,v$, with $q\mid|h_0(n-m)|$.

2.  Fixed $(q,u,v)$ keys lie in an exact affine solution lattice with a general least-common-multiple spacing; the proved primitive-row property of the inherited physical carrier simplifies it to $quv$. Their gauged Möbius sign is the frozen value $\mu(u)\mu(v)$.

3.  Same residual is equivalent to the unique primitive direction $(b,a)$.

4.  The complete gauged matrices $\widetilde H,\widetilde R,\widetilde E$ have exact entry formulas in the literal kernels $L_{mn,q}$, with normalization $1/\sqrt{W_mW_n}$.

5.  Reversing the row pair swaps $u,v$ and conjugates the kernel, so the representation is manifestly Hermitian.

6.  Primitive support removes the auxiliary diagonal tail from every entry kernel. Weighted plaquette costs give explicit entry majorants.

7.  A symmetric nonnegative matrix of these majorants controls the operator norm through its Perron radius. This is an exact carrier theorem, not a saving for that radius.

## Logical level

The coordinate and matrix identities are L1: they retain the same fixed $h_0$, raw masses, complete keys, literal coefficients, actual support, and unitary gauge as the physical Gram. The finite-matrix comparison principles are L0. Estimating the complete growing majorant matrix is the new L2 arithmetic gate. In particular, one fixed pair, one side, a direct-cell compression, or the first mixed trace cannot be substituted for that gate.

# The complete literal two-corner entry

## Frozen physical carrier

Fix a finite scale $X$, one nonzero integer shift $h_0$, one external block, one direct side, all source and opposite-row blocks, orbit classes, masks, profiles, endpoint restrictions, and the very-high cutoff before any coefficient vector is selected. The opposite direct side is included only by the orthogonal direct sum proved in TPC–68. Let $\mathcal W_M$ be the complete set of actual nonzero missing-high atoms in this exact scope, $\mathcal M_X$ the declared source-row set, and $$\mathcal I_X=\{i(w):w\in\mathcal W_M\}$$ the set of actual complete native output keys. A key $i(w)$ retains the side, fixed external block, opposite row, $\gamma$-coordinate, physical time $j(w)$, and every remaining orthogonal output label.

Every $w\in\mathcal W_M$ has a canonical source row $m(w)$, literal coefficient $\beta_w$, target, largest prime, cofactor, and residual label $$N_w=m(w)j(w)+h_0=Q_wS_w,  \qquad  s(w)=d_{m(w)}S_w.  \label{eq:key-target-residual}$$ Here $\beta_w$ is the stripped TPC–68 literal coefficient: it retains all physical shapes, masks, phases, profiles, and terminal weights, but not a second residual Möbius sign. ## Normalized missing-native Gram and row gauge

Set $$c_w=-\mu(s(w))\beta_w,\qquad  (M_X\zeta)_i  =\sum_{\substack{w\in\mathcal W_M\\i(w)=i}}  c_w\zeta_{m(w)},  \qquad  D_M=\operatorname{diag}(W_m).$$ On $S_M=\ell^2(\mathcal M_M)=\operatorname{Ran}D_M$, the atomic-normalized missing-native incidence is $$F=M_XD_M^{\dagger/2}\big|_{S_M}.$$ Its normalized missing-native Gram is $$G_{\rm nat}=F^*F=I+H,\qquad H=R+E,$$ where all three displayed matrices are Hermitian, $H,R,E$ have zero diagonal, $R$ sums common-output pairs with equal residual labels, and $E$ sums those with unequal residual labels [`\citep{WangTPC68}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-074.md#cite-b8c461e9c6be3261).

###### ref-3a959971f716951a

Define the self-adjoint unitary row gauge $$U_d=\operatorname{diag}\bigl(\mu(d_m)\bigr)_{m\in\mathcal M_M},  \qquad  \widetilde H=U_dHU_d,  \quad  \widetilde R=U_dRU_d,  \quad  \widetilde E=U_dEU_d.  \label{eq:row-gauge}$$ For $m\ne n$, put $$\mathcal I_{mn}=\{i\in\mathcal I_X:\beta_{i,m}\beta_{i,n}\ne0\}.$$ The exact TPC–68 two-affine formula is $$\boxed{  \widetilde H_{mn}  =  \frac1{\sqrt{W_mW_n}}  \sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{mn}}  \mu(N_{i,m})\mu(N_{i,n})  \overline{\beta_{i,m}}\beta_{i,n}.}  \label{eq:literal-gauged-entry}$$ Equality of complete keys fixes one common time $j(i)$. Thus [\[eq:literal-gauged-entry\]](TPC-074.md#ref-3a959971f716951a) is a literal two-corner sum, not a time-only surrogate and not the four-corner coefficient of $\operatorname{tr}(ER)$.

###### ref-8e8a8cac41924d9e

> **Remark: Sides and global row mass** \[rem:sides-global-mass\] The side is part of the complete output key, so atoms on different sides never form a Gram pair. If $W_m=W_m^{+}+W_m^{-}$, however, the normalization in [\[eq:literal-gauged-entry\]](TPC-074.md#ref-3a959971f716951a) is still the global $W_m$. The diagonal contribution of one side alone is $W_m^\pm/W_m$, not automatically $1$. Orthogonality across sides therefore does not license a separate identity-diagonal normalization on each side.

> **Theorem: Canonical primitive key coordinates** \[thm:canonical-primitive-key\] At every actual key $i\in\mathcal I_{mn}$:
>
> 1.  $q_i,u_i,v_i$ are squarefree and pairwise coprime;
>
> 2.  $q_i$ is coprime to $gab$, $u_i$ is coprime to $ga$, and $v_i$ is coprime to $gb$;
>
> 3.  $$\boxed{q_i\mid |\Delta_{mn}|;}        \label{eq:direct-q-divides-Delta}$$
>
> 4.  the two largest-prime labels satisfy $$\boxed{        n u_i Q_{i,m}-m v_iQ_{i,n}=\frac{\Delta_{mn}}{q_i};}        \label{eq:direct-prime-line}$$
>
> 5.  the residual labels are equal exactly in one primitive direction: $$\boxed{        s_{i,m}=s_{i,n}        \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad        (u_i,v_i)=(b,a).}        \label{eq:unique-residual-direction}$$

> **Proof** The residual labels are $$s_{i,m}=gaq_i u_i,\qquad  s_{i,n}=gbq_i v_i.$$ Both are squarefree by [\[eq:squarefree-key-carrier\]](TPC-074.md#ref-c465b274227f83f2). Together with $(u_i,v_i)=1$, which follows from the definition of $q_i$, this gives the stated squarefreeness and coprimality: squarefreeness of $gaq_iu_i$ makes $g,a,q_i,u_i$ pairwise coprime, and squarefreeness of $gbq_iv_i$ does the same for $g,b,q_i,v_i$.
>
> The common time in the complete key gives $$\begin{aligned}  nN_{i,m}-mN_{i,n}  &=  n(mj(i)+h_0)-m(nj(i)+h_0)\\  &=h_0(n-m)=\Delta_{mn}.\end{aligned}$$ Substitute $N_{i,m}=Q_{i,m}q_i u_i$ and $N_{i,n}=Q_{i,n}q_i v_i$. This yields $$q_i\bigl(nu_iQ_{i,m}-mv_iQ_{i,n}\bigr)=\Delta_{mn},$$ proving [\[eq:direct-q-divides-Delta\]](TPC-074.md#ref-abf59dd2ee98d5e7) and [\[eq:direct-prime-line\]](TPC-074.md#ref-9680d66b25494dd0).
>
> Finally, $$s_{i,m}=s_{i,n}  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  au_i=bv_i.$$ If $au_i=bv_i$, then $(a,b)=1$ gives $u_i=bk$, $v_i=a\ell$. The equality gives $k=\ell$, while $(u_i,v_i)=1$ forces $k=\ell=1$. The converse is immediate.

## Exact ray spacing

> **Theorem: General fixed-$(q,u,v)$ direct-entry ray** \[thm:direct-entry-ray\] Fix $m,n,h_0$, and suppose one actual key realizes coordinates $(q,u,v)$, with data $$(j_0,Q_{m,0},Q_{n,0})$$ exists, define $$\lambda_{mn}(q,u,v)  =  \operatorname{lcm}\left(        \frac{qu}{(m,qu)},        \frac{qv}{(n,qv)}       \right)  \label{eq:general-ray-spacing}$$ Then every other physical key with the same $(q,u,v)$ has a unique integer $z$ for which $$\begin{aligned}  j&=j_0+\lambda_{mn}(q,u,v)\,z,  \label{eq:direct-time-ray}\\  Q_m&=Q_{m,0}+\frac{m\lambda_{mn}(q,u,v)}{qu}\,z,  \label{eq:direct-Qm-ray}\\  Q_n&=Q_{n,0}+\frac{n\lambda_{mn}(q,u,v)}{qv}\,z  \label{eq:direct-Qn-ray}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** For two keys with the same coordinates, take differences. From $$mj+h_0=quQ_m,\qquad nj+h_0=qvQ_n$$ one obtains $$m\,\delta j=qu\,\delta Q_m,\qquad  n\,\delta j=qv\,\delta Q_n.$$ The first divisibility is equivalent to $qu/(m,qu)\mid\delta j$, and the second to $qv/(n,qv)\mid\delta j$. Their simultaneous solution lattice is therefore exactly $\delta j=\lambda_{mn}(q,u,v)z$. Substitution gives the two displayed prime increments; they are integers by the definition of $\lambda_{mn}$.

> **Remark: Containment, not prime production** The lattice theorem is one-way. It places every realized key on the displayed lattice; it does not assert that every integer $z$ produces prime largest factors or survives the largest-prime test, masks, complete output labels, physical interval, or endpoints.

###### ref-5128758381bcd72d

> **Corollary: Physical primitive-row spacing** \[cor:primitive-row-spacing\] On the inherited physical carrier $$(mn,h_0)=1,$$ one has $$(m,qu)=(n,qv)=1,\qquad  \lambda_{mn}(q,u,v)=quv.$$ Consequently, $$j=j_0+quv\,z,\qquad  Q_m=Q_{m,0}+mv\,z,\qquad  Q_n=Q_{n,0}+nu\,z.$$

> **Proof** Apply [\[eq:physical-row-primitivity\]](TPC-074.md#ref-835e7add00a77a13) to both realized rows. Since $qu\mid mj+h_0$, any common divisor of $m$ and $qu$ divides $h_0$; hence it is $1$. The argument for $n,qv$ is identical. Now $q,u,v$ are pairwise coprime, so $\operatorname{lcm}(qu,qv)=quv$. Substitute this value into \[[eq:direct-time-ray](TPC-074.md#ref-149f895bf7e4f5f7),[eq:direct-Qm-ray](TPC-074.md#ref-a59a06f188b5e076),[eq:direct-Qn-ray](TPC-074.md#ref-5bd68852f6bce00a)\].

For a complete key $i$, let $\ell(i)$ denote the tuple of all its labels other than the time coordinate. This tuple retains, in particular, the side, external block, opposite row, $\gamma$-coordinate, orbit class, and every remaining orthogonal label.

###### ref-3003829ece3611bf

> **Corollary: Ray census** \[cor:direct-ray-census\] If the allowed time interval has length $J$, then $$\#\{i\in\mathcal I_{mn}:        (q_i,u_i,v_i)=(q,u,v),\ \ell(i)=\ell_0\}  \le  1+\left\lfloor\frac{J}{\lambda_{mn}(q,u,v)}\right\rfloor$$ for each fixed label tuple $\ell_0$. The unrestricted key count requires summation over the realized $\ell_0$’s; no label multiplicity is hidden in the displayed bound, and the sum does not exploit literal phases.

> **Theorem: Primitive sign of one direct key** \[thm:direct-key-sign\] For every $i\in\mathcal I_{mn}$, $$\boxed{  \mu(N_{i,m})\mu(N_{i,n})  =  \mu(u_i)\mu(v_i).}  \label{eq:direct-primitive-sign}$$ Consequently, the Möbius sign is constant along every arithmetic ray in [\[thm:direct-entry-ray\]](TPC-074.md#ref-57aa84efe092f13d), irrespective of whether its spacing simplifies to $quv$.

> **Proof** Each largest prime is coprime to its cofactor on the squarefree target support, so $$\mu(N_{i,m})=-\mu(S_{i,m}),\qquad  \mu(N_{i,n})=-\mu(S_{i,n}).$$ The two minus signs cancel. The pairwise coprimality from [\[thm:canonical-primitive-key\]](TPC-074.md#ref-f00670405220cf48) gives $$\mu(q_i u_i)\mu(q_i v_i)  =  \mu(q_i)^2\mu(u_i)\mu(v_i)  =  \mu(u_i)\mu(v_i).$$

###### ref-40203afd73a36084

> **Corollary: Same-residual sign check** \[cor:same-residual-sign-check\] At the distinguished point $(u,v)=(b,a)$, $$\mu(u)\mu(v)=\mu(a)\mu(b)  =\mu(d_m)\mu(d_n).$$ This agrees with the same-residual sign-freezing theorem of TPC–68.

> **Proof** The first equality is symmetry. Since the squarefree factors $g,a,b$ are pairwise coprime, $$\mu(d_m)\mu(d_n)  =\mu(ga)\mu(gb)  =\mu(g)^2\mu(a)\mu(b).$$

> **Remark: What can oscillate** The theorem freezes only the target Möbius sign inside a fixed ray. The literal coefficient $\overline{\beta_{i,m}}\beta_{i,n}$ may carry complex phases and amplitude variation, and the set of surviving prime points may be highly irregular.

> **Definition: Complete-key entry kernel** \[def:complete-key-entry-kernel\] For $m\ne n$, $q,u,v\ge1$, define $$L_{mn,q}(u,v)  =  \frac1{\sqrt{W_mW_n}}  \sum_{\substack{i\in\mathcal I_{mn}\\                   (q_i,u_i,v_i)=(q,u,v)}}  \overline{\beta_{i,m}}\beta_{i,n}.  \label{eq:q-slice-entry-kernel}$$ The sum retains every complete-key label; it is zero when the indicated actual fiber is empty. > **Theorem: Exact two-corner primitive planes** \[thm:exact-two-corner-planes\] For every pair of distinct active rows $m<n$, $$\begin{aligned}  \boxed{  \widetilde H_{mn}  =  \sum_{u,v\ge1}  \mu(u)\mu(v)L_{mn}^{0}(u,v),}  \label{eq:full-direct-plane}\\  \boxed{  \widetilde R_{mn}  =  \mu(a)\mu(b)L_{mn}(b,a),}  \label{eq:residual-direct-point}\\  \boxed{  \widetilde E_{mn}  =  \sum_{u,v\ge1}  \mu(u)\mu(v)L_{mn}^{E}(u,v)  =  \sum_{\substack{(u,v)=1\\(u,v)\ne(b,a)}}  \mu(u)\mu(v)L_{mn}(u,v).}  \label{eq:erasure-direct-plane}\end{aligned}$$ The normalizing factor in every kernel is $1/\sqrt{W_mW_n}$.

> **Proposition: Atomic-mass audit** \[prop:atomic-mass-audit\] For every distinct active pair, $$\sum_{q,u,v\ge1}|L_{mn,q}(u,v)|  \le  \frac{\sum_{i\in\mathcal I_{mn}}  |\beta_{i,m}\beta_{i,n}|}{\sqrt{W_mW_n}}  \le1.  \label{eq:atomic-entry-l1-audit}$$ In particular, $$|\widetilde R_{mn}|+|\widetilde E_{mn}|\le1,  \qquad |\widetilde H_{mn}|\le1.$$ These are entry-normalization checks, not operator-norm savings.

> **Remark: One point is not one key** The value $L_{mn}(b,a)$ may aggregate many contents, times, sides, $\gamma$-coordinates, and complete keys. The theorem identifies one primitive direction, not one physical atom.

> **Remark: Two-corner versus four-corner normalization** The direct kernel uses $1/\sqrt{W_mW_n}$. The TPC–70 mixed-trace rectangle uses four coefficients and $1/(W_mW_n)$. Replacing one by the other changes both the object and its physical scale.

## Hermitian reversal

> **Theorem: Reversed-pair conjugacy** \[thm:reversed-pair-conjugacy\] For every $m\ne n$, $q,u,v\ge1$, $$\boxed{  L_{nm,q}(v,u)=\overline{L_{mn,q}(u,v)},\qquad  L_{nm}^{0}(v,u)=\overline{L_{mn}^{0}(u,v)}.}  \label{eq:kernel-Hermitian-reversal}$$ For the punctured kernels, $$L_{nm}^{E}(v,u)=\overline{L_{mn}^{E}(u,v)}.$$ Consequently, [\[eq:full-direct-plane\]](TPC-074.md#ref-4b72abd2561e3f1c)–[\[eq:erasure-direct-plane\]](TPC-074.md#ref-4f7fde3ea497c4a1) are manifestly Hermitian.

> **Proof** The complete-key set is the same: $\mathcal I_{nm}=\mathcal I_{mn}$. Reversing the rows swaps the two cofactors, hence $(q,u,v)$ becomes $(q,v,u)$. It also replaces $$\frac{\overline{\beta_{i,m}}\beta_{i,n}}{\sqrt{W_mW_n}}  \quad\text{by}\quad  \frac{\overline{\beta_{i,n}}\beta_{i,m}}{\sqrt{W_nW_m}},$$ which is its complex conjugate. Summing gives the first identity. The indexed row-divisor factors swap under reversal, so the ordered puncture $(b_{mn},a_{mn})$ becomes $(b_{nm},a_{nm})=(a_{mn},b_{mn})$. ## Direct-cell compression is optional

Under the identical-support direct-cell specialization of TPC–68, the $\gamma$-sum in [\[eq:q-slice-entry-kernel\]](TPC-074.md#ref-84bc484cc9b7dd55) may be compressed into the literal overlap $$\Gamma_{m,n}(j)  =  \sum_\gamma K_{m\gamma}K_{n\gamma}|\psi_\gamma(j)|^2.$$ Here $K_{m\gamma}\in\{0,1\}$ is the inherited static support mask and $\psi_\gamma(j)$ is the inherited opposite-row profile and output phase. Then the complete-key formula [\[eq:q-slice-entry-kernel\]](TPC-074.md#ref-84bc484cc9b7dd55), rather than a compressed $\Gamma_{m,n}$, is the theorem. No later result requires identical support.

> **Theorem: Primitive entry Abel certificate** \[thm:primitive-entry-Abel\] For every $m\ne n$, $$\begin{aligned}  \widetilde H_{mn}  &=  \sum_{x,y\ge1}  M(x)M(y)\Delta_{12}L_{mn}^{0}(x,y),  \label{eq:full-entry-Abel}\\  \widetilde E_{mn}  &=  \sum_{x,y\ge1}  M(x)M(y)\Delta_{12}L_{mn}^{E}(x,y).  \label{eq:erasure-entry-Abel}\end{aligned}$$ Consequently, $$\begin{aligned}  |\widetilde H_{mn}|  &\le \mathcal C_\square(L_{mn}^{0}),  \label{eq:full-entry-majorant}\\  |\widetilde E_{mn}|  &\le \mathcal C_\square(L_{mn}^{E}),  \label{eq:erasure-entry-majorant}\\  |\widetilde R_{mn}|  &=|L_{mn}(b_{mn},a_{mn})|.  \label{eq:residual-entry-exact-magnitude}\end{aligned}$$ Moreover, for $d\ge2$, $$(L_{mn}^{0})^{[d]}\equiv0,\qquad  (L_{mn}^{E})^{[d]}\equiv0.$$ Thus the auxiliary diagonal tails of the support-faithful entry kernels vanish for every cutoff $D\ge1$.

## Completion freedom returns at the estimate level

The physical entry samples only primitive sites. Therefore the analytic completion gauge of TPC–73 can be applied separately to every $L_{mn}^{0}$. A nonprimitive completion may reduce its $d=1$ variation, but all higher diagonal slices and the exact tail of that same completion must be restored. To obtain an operator theorem, the complete family of completion rules must be declared before the later coefficient extremizer is chosen, must respect [\[eq:kernel-Hermitian-reversal\]](TPC-074.md#ref-3b2954a5506f58d7), and must produce one controlled majorant matrix. Pairwise optimization with no uniform summability is not an L2 result.

# Countermodels and non-substitution laws

## A literal orphan erasure edge

Take the finite fixed-shift interface $$m=2,\qquad n=3,\qquad h_0=1,\qquad d_m=d_n=1,$$ with one complete common-output key at time $j=3$. Its targets are $$2j+1=7=7\cdot1,\qquad  3j+1=10=5\cdot2.$$ Thus $$q=1,\qquad (u,v)=(1,2),\qquad (b,a)=(1,1).$$ The residuals $1$ and $2$ are unequal. Choose one literal coefficient at each row, both equal to $1$; then $W_m=W_n=1$. With a finite high threshold below $5$, the largest-prime labels are literal and all target and residual Möbius values are nonzero. The exact matrices are $$R=0,\qquad  E=H=  \begin{pmatrix}  0&-1\\  -1&0  \end{pmatrix}.$$ Indeed, $$L_{mn,1}(1,2)=1,\qquad  \mu(1)\mu(2)=-1.$$

Every four-corner $ER$ anchor coefficient vanishes because $R=0$, whereas the direct erasure entry and its operator norm are nonzero. This is a literal finite-interface witness that the anchored primitive plane cannot substitute for the direct plane. It does not assert that orphan edges carry nonnegligible mass in the full asymptotic carrier.

## A literal same-residual direction

Take $$m=2,\qquad n=3,\qquad h_0=1,\qquad d_m=2,\qquad d_n=3,$$ so $g=1$, $a=2$, and $b=3$. At time $j=7$, $$2j+1=15=5\cdot3,\qquad  3j+1=22=11\cdot2.$$ Thus $$q=1,\qquad (u,v)=(3,2)=(b,a),$$ and the two residual labels agree: $$d_mS_m=2\cdot3=6=3\cdot2=d_nS_n.$$ This finite arithmetic key checks the distinguished $R$-direction. Entry into a particular asymptotic physical family still requires all of that family’s literal masks and endpoint conditions.

## The same-residual point need not cancel

Let the only nonzero direction for one row pair be $$L_{mn}^{0}=ce_{b,a}.$$ Then $$\widetilde R_{mn}=\mu(a)\mu(b)c,\qquad  \widetilde E_{mn}=0.$$ The full magnitude is $|c|$. Primitive support, affine-ray structure, and the divisor bound on $q$ do not create Möbius cancellation inside the distinguished direction. Literal phases across keys are a separate possible mechanism.

## One common key does not have unit normalized weight

Let each of two rows carry $T$ literal unit coefficients, but let only one complete output key be common to the rows. Then $$W_m=W_n=T$$ and the common key contributes $$\frac{\overline{\beta_{i,m}}\beta_{i,n}}{\sqrt{W_mW_n}}  =\frac1T,$$ not $1$. Neither the singleton property nor equality of the common key permits replacing the raw row masses by collision degrees. This also illustrates why the entry audit [\[eq:atomic-entry-l1-audit\]](TPC-074.md#ref-069d76334f74c63c) is a normalization bound rather than a uniform saving.

## Mixed-trace cancellation does not control an entry

Consider the abstract Hermitian zero-diagonal matrices $$R=  \begin{pmatrix}  0&1\\  1&0  \end{pmatrix},  \qquad  E=  \begin{pmatrix}  0&i\\  -i&0  \end{pmatrix}.$$ Then $ER\ne0$, but $$\operatorname{tr}(ER)=0,\qquad \|E\|=\|R\|=1.$$ Thus even cancellation of a nonzero first mixed carrier does not bound either factor. The direct-entry theorem is a new object interface, not a corollary of a scalar trace estimate.

## Fixed-pair decay does not control the operator

For $N\ge2$, let $A_N$ be the $N$-by-$N$ matrix with zero diagonal and every off-diagonal entry equal to $1/(N-1)$. For each fixed pair, $$(A_N)_{mn}\longrightarrow0,$$ while $$\|A_N\|=1$$ because the all-ones vector is an eigenvector. Hence fixed-pair little-oh notation for $\mathcal C_\square(L_{mn}^{0})$ is insufficient. A row, weighted Schur, or Perron estimate over the complete growing pair family is necessary.

## Pre-aggregation costs can lose literal cancellation

In the ambient finite-kernel class, two content slices $F$ and $-F$ have $$L_{mn}=F+(-F)=0,$$ although their separate weighted costs are positive. Therefore $$\mathcal C_\square\!\left(\sum_qL_{mn,q}\right)  \le  \sum_q\mathcal C_\square(L_{mn,q})$$ is valid but may be strict. This model does not assert that exact cancellation occurs in every physical family; it fixes the legal order of operations.

## A time-only compression can change the carrier

Equality of complete native outputs implies equality of time, but the converse fails. Two atoms at the same time with different side, $\gamma$, block, or remaining output labels do not form a Gram collision. Replacing $\mathcal I_{mn}$ in [\[eq:q-slice-entry-kernel\]](TPC-074.md#ref-84bc484cc9b7dd55) by a time set may therefore create spurious two-corner products. The compressed direct-cell $\Gamma_{m,n}(j)$ formula is legal only under its identical-support hypothesis.

## Failure of the majorant is not failure of cancellation

A large value of $\rho(P^H)$ proves only that the present absolute-value certificate is inconclusive. The signed matrix $H$ may still be small because of cancellations between plaquettes, row pairs, contents, sides, or literal phases discarded by $P^H$. To close the Abel–Perron route negatively, one needs an actual-carrier lower obstruction for every declared admissible completion or for the exact majorant architecture, at a nonnegligible physical mass scale.

# The new physical gate and the adaptive next door

Let $X\to\infty$ through one declared physical family. All active row sets, complete keys, kernels, and majorants now depend on $X$; the index is suppressed unless needed. Every asymptotic statement below uses the same fixed $h_0$, literal coefficient class, raw atomic normalization, and actual support.

## What the exact carrier has closed

TPC–73 left open whether a primitive plane could be attached to a two-corner matrix entry. answers this affirmatively: $$\text{every off-diagonal entry}  \quad\longleftrightarrow\quad  \text{one literal primitive plane}.$$ For each ordered row pair, the same-residual entry is its pair-dependent distinguished direction $(b_{mn},a_{mn})$, and the erasure entry is the primitive complement. Consequently, no anchor-observability hypothesis is needed to see an erasure edge, and orphan edges are included automatically.

This closes an L1 object mismatch. It does not close any of the following L2 estimates.

## Four remaining operator gates

#### Gate A: mass-normalized plaquette or completion control.

One must estimate the actual kernels $$\mathcal C_\square(L_{mn}^{0})$$ or the full divisor–variation cost of one declared nonprimitive completion. Smooth envelopes, time-only kernels, and support counts do not replace the literal complex amplitudes.

#### Gate B: complete pair aggregation.

The desired conclusion must control the complete matrix, for example $$\rho(P_X^H)\le\eta_X$$ at the declared physical scale. Fixed-pair cancellation, density-one rows, or one side do not imply this estimate. Weighted Perron vectors are allowed only when fixed before the later coefficient extremizer.

#### Gate C: the same-residual sector.

The full Gram requires $H=R+E$. The target Möbius sign of $R$ is frozen. Any saving there must come from literal phases, sparsity, geometry, or a separate deterministic mass bound. An estimate for $E$ alone is not an estimate for $H$.

#### Gate D: physical reassembly.

Even a complete operator estimate must be combined with terminal activation, a represented lower frame, missing-mass comparison, nuisance shorting, geometric endpoints, and final packet boundaries on the same carrier and coefficient scope.

## Exact stop rules

1.  If the canonical $P_X^H$ is too large by a proved lower obstruction, only the canonical zero-extension Abel–Perron certificate is closed.

2.  To close completion-based entry certificates, the obstruction must cover every admissible completion rule or the corresponding completion infimum, with Hermitian and uniform-quantifier constraints retained.

3.  If orphan edges carry nonnegligible operator mass, that closes an anchor-only observability route but not the direct-entry route.

4.  If the same-residual majorant is too large, erasure cancellation cannot repair the triangle estimate automatically; one may need a signed full-$H$ moment rather than separate $R/E$ norms.

5.  If the actual total fixed-power loss reaches $1/400$, including equality, the current endpoint implication is closed.

6.  None of these outcomes is a theorem about the special shift $h_0=2$, the sieve parity barrier, prime pairs, or twin primes.

## TPC–75 decision

The direct carrier changes the priority of the next plane paper. TPC–75 will compare two coefficient-faithful entry certificates:

1.  the canonical primitive zero extension, whose auxiliary diagonal tail is zero; and

2.  a $q$-compatible nonprimitive completion that moves common coordinate factors into existing literal content slices.

The target is a complete physical census of weighted plaquettes, large-content mass, row stars, and the resulting Perron matrix. A positive result must bound the actual growing matrix. A negative result must exhibit a nonnegligible actual-carrier obstruction, not merely a rough ambient upper bound. TPC–76 will then quantify what four-corner anchors observe and isolate the orphan complement, providing a hybrid fallback rather than replacing the direct representation.

# Claim ledger and conclusion

## Exact conclusions

The paper proves:

1.  canonical primitive coordinates for every actual complete two-corner key;

2.  the divisor condition $q\mid|h_0(n-m)|$, the exact linear Diophantine identity, and the general least-common-multiple solution lattice, with $quv$ on the inherited physical carrier;

3.  the frozen direct-entry sign $\mu(u)\mu(v)$;

4.  equivalence between equal residuals and the single direction $(u,v)=(b,a)$;

5.  exact literal formulas for every entry of $\widetilde H,\widetilde R,\widetilde E$;

6.  reversed-pair conjugacy and therefore Hermitian compatibility;

7.  tail-free Abel identities for the support-faithful entry kernels; and

8.  entrywise, Schur, row-star, weighted Collatz, and Perron operator certificates for the complete literal matrix.

## Statements not proved

The paper does not prove:

-   that a physical entry has cancellation relative to its literal mass;

-   that $\mathcal C_\square(L_{mn}^{0})$ is small uniformly in a growing row-pair family;

-   that $\rho(P_X^H)$, $\rho(P_X^E)$, or $\rho(P_X^R)$ has any desired asymptotic saving;

-   a uniformly controlled low-cost analytic completion;

-   cancellation in the same-residual sector from target Möbius signs;

-   closure of terminal activation, represented lower frame, missing-mass comparison, nuisance shorting, endpoints, or final boundary reconstruction;

-   the strict $1/400$ endpoint inequality for one complete physical chain;

-   a statement special to $h_0=2$; or

-   a parity-breaking, prime-pair, or twin-prime theorem.

## Conclusion

The primitive cofactor plane is no longer confined to a four-corner mixed trace. It represents every literal off-diagonal entry of the normalized missing-native Gram directly: $$\widetilde H_{mn}  =  \sum_{(u,v)=1}\mu(u)\mu(v)L_{mn}(u,v).$$ For each ordered row pair, the same-residual entry is its unique pair-dependent direction $(b_{mn},a_{mn})$; the erasure entry is the remaining primitive plane. This is a genuine L1 operator-carrier advance because no entry, complete key, literal coefficient, or raw normalization is discarded.

The hard question has consequently become sharper, not solved. One must control the weighted plaquette or completion cost for all actual row pairs and then prove that the resulting Perron matrix is small at the physical scale. TPC–75 will test that certificate on the literal carrier. If it fails by an actual obstruction, the fallback is a signed moment or hybrid direct/anchor architecture, not a claim that arithmetic cancellation itself has failed.
