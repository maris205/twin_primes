## Abstract

The distinguished zero determinant frequency in a fixed-shift prime–Möbius packet is controlled by a ratio, not by an amplitude estimate alone. If $a_X=(A_{C_X}(n))_n$ is the literal determinant coefficient, the required quantity is $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  =\frac{|\sum_n A_{C_X}(n)|^2}         {\sum_n|A_{C_X}(n)|^2}.$$ Earlier work isolated the threshold $\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)\le X^{1/400+o(1)}$ and an amplitude target $|\sum_n A_{C_X}(n)|\ll X^{o(1)}Q_X^2$, but did not supply the matching lower bound for the denominator.

This paper gives an exact transfer architecture for that missing denominator. A represented synthesis $\mathcal A_X$, a literal missing synthesis $\mathcal M_X$, and their reassembly $\mathcal B_X=\mathcal A_X+\mathcal M_X$ yield coefficient-specific and uniform energy survival bounds from a normalized missing-Gram Perron certificate. An explicit determinant-binning map $\mathcal J_X$, a verified restricted lower modulus $s_X$, and the dictionary defect $r_X=a_X-\mathcal J_X\mathcal B_Xz_X$ then give $$\|a_X\|_2^2\ge  \left(  s_X\sqrt{\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}}  -\frac{\|r_X\|_2}{\sqrt{D_{{\rm raw},X}}}  \right)_+^2D_{{\rm raw},X}.$$ At the high-row scales $Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)}$ and $J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)}$, an amplitude exponent $\eta_Z$ and an energy-loss exponent $\lambda_D$ give $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le X^{1/400+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}.$$ For this exponent transfer, the sharp recorded-bounds condition is $2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D$, with genuine robustness only under strict inequality. It is not claimed to be necessary for the physical packet when the recorded exponents are nonoptimal.

Two sharp countermodels show why this bridge is not formal: unsigned support and energy do not determine the zero mode, and raw atomic mass can disappear completely inside one determinant bin. Accordingly, the results are exact L0 algebra and a conditional L1 arithmetic interface. They do not prove determinant-energy survival for the physical packet, a full-block cover, a fixed-shift Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, twin primes, or a breach of the parity barrier.

# Introduction

Fix one nonzero integer shift $h_0$. The high-row branch of the fixed-shift program reaches a finite determinant coefficient $$a_X=(A_{C_X}(n))_{n\in\mathcal N_X},  \qquad C_X=\lfloor J_X\rfloor,  \label{eq:intro-coefficient}$$ formed after literal aggregation of a complete set of native prime–Möbius keys. Its zero mode and determinant energy are $$Z_X:=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}A_{C_X}(n),  \qquad  D_X:=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}|A_{C_X}(n)|^2.  \label{eq:intro-ZD}$$ The zero-frequency concentration is $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  :=  \begin{cases}  |Z_X|^2/D_X,&D_X>0,\\  0,&D_X=0.  \end{cases}  \label{eq:intro-flatness}$$ The zero convention in the second line is harmless only because then $a_X=0$ and hence $Z_X=0$. Here “zero frequency” means the normalized determinant-DFT frequency $r=0$ used in TPC-32. It is neither a generic nonzero determinant frequency nor any orbit, divisor, or transfer-operator zero mode.

## The three interfaces

There are three logically different maps: $$\text{native coefficients}  \xrightarrow{\ \mathcal A_X+\mathcal M_X\ }  \text{pre-bin atoms}  \xrightarrow{\ \mathcal J_X\ }  \text{determinant bins}  \xrightarrow{\ \bm 1^*\ }  \text{zero mode}.$$ The first arrow is a completion/reassembly problem. The second can lose energy through collisions in a determinant fiber. The third is the arithmetic signed sum. Success at one arrow does not imply success at either later arrow.

###### ref-e5357512a5d8dd30

###### ref-d2f2c39c5bcf920d

For the first arrow we prove a missing-Gram majorant theorem. If $\mathcal B_X=\mathcal A_X+\mathcal M_X$, then for every coefficient vector $z$ with $\mathcal A_Xz\ne0$, $$\left(1-\sqrt{\Xi_X(z)}\right)_+^2  \le  \frac{\|\mathcal B_Xz\|_2^2}{\|\mathcal A_Xz\|_2^2}  \le  \left(1+\sqrt{\Xi_X(z)}\right)^2,  \label{eq:intro-specific}$$ where $$\Xi_X(z)  :=  \widehat\kappa_X  \frac{\langle D_{\mathcal M,X}z,z\rangle}       {\|\mathcal A_Xz\|_2^2},  \qquad  \widehat\kappa_X=1+\rho(P_X).  \label{eq:intro-Xi}$$ Here $D_{\mathcal M,X}$ is the exact diagonal of the missing Gram and $P_X$ is an entrywise nonnegative majorant of its normalized off-diagonal part.

For the second arrow we require a literal binning map, not an analogy. If $s_X$ is a verified lower modulus of $\mathcal J_X$ on the actual reassembled subspace and $$r_X=a_X-\mathcal J_X\mathcal B_Xz_X,$$ then the lower bound displayed in the abstract follows. The residual $r_X$ records every mismatch between the completion carrier and the physical determinant coefficient.

###### ref-f3abdb470c556f50

###### ref-0d6aa8f4a1af7803

For the final arrow we prove the exact exponent arithmetic. If $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,  \qquad  D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,  \label{eq:intro-exponents}$$ then $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le  X^{1/400+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}.  \label{eq:intro-exponent-transfer}$$ Consequently these recorded bounds recover the TPC-32 threshold whenever $2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D$, and this condition is sharp for the two bounds alone. The current amplitude target has $\eta_Z=0$; within this transfer ledger it therefore permits no positive certified energy loss. Such a loss cannot be absorbed unless it is offset by a new amplitude saving.

## Level ledger

The level labels used throughout are:

-   **L0:** exact finite-dimensional algebra, including Gram majorization, reassembly inequalities, and determinant-binning identities;

-   **L1:** a verified literal interface from those identities to the actual fixed-$h_0$ coefficient, together with asymptotic bounds for every certificate statistic;

-   **L2:** a new arithmetic estimate for the actual fixed-$h_0$ packet, and then a separately verified global block cover.

This paper proves L0 theorems and states auditable L1 implications. It proves no new L2 cancellation estimate. In particular, none of the following can be inferred from the results below: a fixed-shift prime-pair asymptotic, positivity at $h_0=2$, twin primes, or a breach of the sieve parity barrier [`\citep{HeathBrown1982Parity}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-081.md#cite-bd33f7374ef1e88f).

# The literal fixed-shift packet

All spaces in the finite algebra below are complex Hilbert spaces with their standard inner products, linear in the first variable. The same statements hold over real spaces. The index $X$ is suppressed when no ambiguity is possible.

###### ref-dd38a2dfcb1c1090

> **Definition: Complete native packet** \[def:complete-packet\] A complete native packet at scale $X$ consists of:
>
> 1.  one fixed nonzero integer $h_0$, not averaged over a family;
>
> 2.  a finite native key set $\mathcal K_X$, including all row, orbit, content, divisor, orientation, source, and endpoint labels used by the construction;
>
> 3.  a coefficient space $\mathcal H_X=\mathbb C^{\mathcal K_X}$ and the literal physical coefficient $z_X\in\mathcal H_X$;
>
> 4.  a pre-bin key set $\mathcal Y_X$ and literal maps $$\mathcal A_X,\mathcal M_X:\mathcal H_X\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{\mathcal Y_X},  \qquad  \mathcal B_X:=\mathcal A_X+\mathcal M_X;$$
>
> 5.  a determinant key set $\mathcal N_X$, an exact determinant label $\nu_X:\mathcal Y_X\to\mathcal N_X$, and all literal scalar multipliers attached to the aggregation;
>
> 6.  the actual post-aggregation coefficient $a_X=(A_{C_X}(n))_{n\in\mathcal N_X}$, with exact zeros retained until the aggregation has been performed;
>
> 7.  one global raw atomic energy $D_{{\rm raw},X}>0$, computed before block deletion, renormalization, thresholding, or selection.

The words “represented” and “missing” describe provenance, not sign. Thus $\mathcal M_X$ already includes its literal signs and phases; reassembly is $\mathcal A_X+\mathcal M_X$, never an unsigned union of supports. Similarly, $D_{{\rm raw},X}$ is not recomputed on the represented support. Per-component normalizers can conceal an arbitrarily large deleted mass and are therefore inadmissible for the physical transfer.

###### ref-35163e32104b6aca

###### ref-ff109276ae6082de

###### ref-f66f9bda710f1270

> **Definition: Exact determinant binning** \[def:binning\] Let $\tau_X(y)\in\mathbb C$ be the archived multiplier on a pre-bin key $y\in\mathcal Y_X$. Define $$(\mathcal J_Xu)(n)  :=  \sum_{\substack{y\in\mathcal Y_X\\ \nu_X(y)=n}}  \tau_X(y)u(y),  \qquad n\in\mathcal N_X.  \label{eq:literal-binning}$$ The sum is taken over every native pre-bin key in the fiber. The dictionary residual is $$r_X:=a_X-\mathcal J_X\mathcal B_Xz_X.  \label{eq:dictionary-residual}$$

If the proposed carrier is literally the physical coefficient, then $r_X=0$ by exact finite arithmetic. If only a model, restriction, or surrogate carrier has been constructed, [\[eq:dictionary-residual\]](TPC-081.md#ref-f66f9bda710f1270) does not vanish automatically. The residual is the correct location for that discrepancy.

> **Remark: Actual support** The support of a synthesis map is its exact nonzero support after literal coefficient aggregation. A geometrically available edge, a thresholded numerical entry, or a union of possible carriers is not an actual edge. In particular, a spectral radius computed on an ambient support is not the Perron constant used below unless it is proved to majorize the normalized Gram on the actual active coordinates.

> **Remark: No change of target** The completion maps take values in the pre-bin space $\mathbb C^{\mathcal Y_X}$, while the arithmetic coefficient lives in $\mathbb C^{\mathcal N_X}$. An observation on another quotient, a four-corner trace, or a common-target gauge does not define $\mathcal J_X$. The determinant map and every aggregation multiplier must be constructed explicitly.

###### ref-feb73c17a6c8d4ab

For reference, write $$Z_X=\langle a_X,\bm 1\rangle,  \qquad  D_X=\|a_X\|_2^2,  \qquad  \mathfrak D_{h_0}(X)=\frac{D_X}{Q_X^3J_X^2}.  \label{eq:packet-statistics}$$ These are physical, post-aggregation statistics. None is replaced by the corresponding statistic of $\mathcal A_Xz_X$, $\mathcal B_Xz_X$, or an unsigned majorant.

# Completion-to-survival from the missing Gram

###### ref-9630ddbd51a5ea70

###### ref-e721671a85b7973d

We first prove a finite-dimensional theorem independent of arithmetic. Let $K,Y$ be finite index sets, let $\mathcal A,\mathcal M:\mathbb C^K\to\mathbb C^Y$ be linear maps, and put $\mathcal B=\mathcal A+\mathcal M$. The missing Gram is $$G_{\mathcal M}:=\mathcal M^*\mathcal M.  \label{eq:missing-Gram}$$ We use only standard Hermitian and nonnegative-matrix comparison principles [`\citep{HornJohnson2013}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-081.md#cite-25e2af110ba05633). Let $$d_j=(G_{\mathcal M})_{jj}=\|\mathcal Me_j\|^2.$$ If $d_j=0$, positivity of $G_{\mathcal M}$ gives $(G_{\mathcal M})_{ij}=0$ for every $i$, so that coordinate contributes nothing to $\mathcal M$. We therefore restrict the Gram normalization to the exact active set $$K^+:=\{j:d_j>0\}  \label{eq:active-missing-set}$$ and extend all resulting quadratic inequalities by zero on its complement.

###### ref-e0e53f338b1a72c1

###### ref-6d19dfb080f638ff

Define the full diagonal operator $$D_{\mathcal M}:=\operatorname{diag}(d_j:j\in K)  \quad\text{on }\mathbb C^K.  \label{eq:full-missing-diagonal}$$ Let $G_{\mathcal M}^+$ and $D_{\mathcal M}^+$ be the principal restrictions of $G_{\mathcal M}$ and $D_{\mathcal M}$ to $K^+$, and set $$R_{\mathcal M}:=  (D_{\mathcal M}^+)^{-1/2}  (G_{\mathcal M}^+-D_{\mathcal M}^+)  (D_{\mathcal M}^+)^{-1/2}.  \label{eq:normalized-missing-Gram}$$ Thus $R_{\mathcal M}$ acts on $\mathbb C^{K^+}$ and is Hermitian with zero diagonal, while $\langle D_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle$ below is the full zero-extended quadratic form on $\mathbb C^K$.

###### ref-14252891e177540a

###### ref-f60e71e599f44657

###### ref-a9bc9457e04a1547

> **Definition: Verified Perron majorant** \[def:Perron-majorant\] A verified Perron majorant is a real symmetric nonnegative matrix $P$ on $K^+$ such that $$|(R_{\mathcal M})_{ij}|\le P_{ij}  \quad(i,j\in K^+).  \label{eq:P-majorizes}$$ We put $$\widehat\kappa:=1+\rho(P).  \label{eq:kappa-hat}$$ If $K^+=\varnothing$, $P$ and $R_{\mathcal M}$ are the unique empty matrices and we set $\rho(P)=0$, so $\widehat\kappa=1$.

###### ref-4b757f80e923811d

###### ref-0b2fd13d2f6d9ce4

> **Lemma: Missing-energy majorization** \[lem:missing-energy\] For every $z\in\mathbb C^K$, $$\|\mathcal Mz\|^2  \le  \widehat\kappa\,  \langle D_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle.  \label{eq:missing-energy-majorization}$$

> **Proof** If $K^+=\varnothing$, then $\mathcal M=0$ and the assertion is immediate. Otherwise, after discarding the zero columns, write $z_+=z|_{K^+}$ and set $w=(D_{\mathcal M}^+)^{1/2}z_+$. Entrywise domination and Hermitian symmetry give $$|\langle R_{\mathcal M}w,w\rangle|  \le |w|^{\mathsf T}P|w|  \le \rho(P)\|w\|_2^2.$$ Hence $$\|\mathcal Mz\|^2  =\langle G_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle  =\langle G_{\mathcal M}^+z_+,z_+\rangle  =\|w\|_2^2+\langle R_{\mathcal M}w,w\rangle  \le(1+\rho(P))\|w\|_2^2,$$ and $\|w\|_2^2=\langle D_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle$, which proves [\[eq:missing-energy-majorization\]](TPC-081.md#ref-0b2fd13d2f6d9ce4).

###### ref-b5528ccb02db7fe7

###### ref-0b0373a7808f6d7b

###### ref-b2a2616f603e6407

> **Theorem: Coefficient-specific completion survival** \[thm:coefficient-specific\] For $z\in\mathbb C^K$ with $\mathcal Az\ne0$, define $$\Xi(z):=  \widehat\kappa  \frac{\langle D_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle}       {\|\mathcal Az\|^2}.  \label{eq:Xi-definition}$$ Then $$\boxed{  \left(1-\sqrt{\Xi(z)}\right)_+^2  \le  \frac{\|\mathcal Bz\|^2}{\|\mathcal Az\|^2}  \le  \left(1+\sqrt{\Xi(z)}\right)^2.}  \label{eq:coefficient-specific-survival}$$

> **Proof** By [\[lem:missing-energy\]](TPC-081.md#ref-4b757f80e923811d), $$\frac{\|\mathcal Mz\|}{\|\mathcal Az\|}  \le\sqrt{\Xi(z)}.$$ The triangle and reverse-triangle inequalities yield $$\bigl(\|\mathcal Az\|-\|\mathcal Mz\|\bigr)_+  \le\|\mathcal Az+\mathcal Mz\|  \le\|\mathcal Az\|+\|\mathcal Mz\|.$$ Divide by $\|\mathcal Az\|$ and square.

> **Remark: What the theorem does not use** does not infer the phases of the missing entries from their support. The map $\mathcal M$, and therefore its Gram, is literal. The matrix $P$ only majorizes the already constructed normalized Gram; it is not a replacement for that construction.

> **Remark: The nontrivial regime** The lower bound in [\[eq:coefficient-specific-survival\]](TPC-081.md#ref-b2a2616f603e6407) is positive only if $\Xi(z)<1$. A finite completion certificate with $\Xi(z)\ge1$ can still be exact and useful as a stop certificate, but it gives no energy survival for that coefficient.

###### ref-09418130b73b206b

> **Corollary: Uniform coefficient class** \[cor:uniform-Xi\] Let $E\subseteq\mathbb C^K$ be a verified coefficient subspace on which $$\langle D_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle  \le\chi\,\|\mathcal Az\|^2  \quad(z\in E)$$ for some $\chi\ge0$. Then $$\|\mathcal Bz\|^2  \ge  \left(1-\sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi}\right)_+^2  \|\mathcal Az\|^2  \quad(z\in E).$$

> **Proof** If $\mathcal Az=0$, the hypothesis gives $\langle D_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle=0$. By [\[lem:missing-energy\]](TPC-081.md#ref-4b757f80e923811d), $\mathcal Mz=0$, and hence $\mathcal Bz=0$, so the claim is trivial. If $\mathcal Az\ne0$, apply [\[thm:coefficient-specific\]](TPC-081.md#ref-b5528ccb02db7fe7) with $\Xi(z)\le\widehat\kappa\chi$.

# A conservative uniform reassembly certificate

The coefficient-specific ratio is the sharpest statement available from the triangle inequality. For an archive it is often convenient to compare every term with the same global raw normalization.

###### ref-30db2a31074f2e5e

###### ref-739b52d433ed288a

###### ref-300400a9edb703a7

###### ref-ba834416a9701e8a

###### ref-a0d2f54a96e8258b

> **Theorem: Raw-normalized reassembly** \[thm:raw-reassembly\] Let $D_{\rm raw}>0$, $\Gamma_{\rm pre}>0$, $0\le\eta<1$, and $\chi\ge0$. Suppose a literal coefficient $z$ satisfies $$\begin{aligned}  \|\mathcal Az\|^2  &\ge(1-\eta)\Gamma_{\rm pre}D_{\rm raw},  \label{eq:represented-lower}\\  \langle D_{\mathcal M}z,z\rangle  &\le\chi\Gamma_{\rm pre}D_{\rm raw}.  \label{eq:missing-diagonal-upper}\end{aligned}$$ Then $$\|\mathcal Bz\|^2  \ge  \delta_{\rm sharp}\Gamma_{\rm pre}D_{\rm raw},  \qquad  \delta_{\rm sharp}  :=  \left(\sqrt{1-\eta}        -\sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi}\right)_+^2.  \label{eq:sharp-reassembly}$$ In particular, $$\boxed{  \|\mathcal Bz\|^2  \ge  \delta_{\rm reasm}\Gamma_{\rm pre}D_{\rm raw},\qquad  \delta_{\rm reasm}  :=  \left[  1-2\sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi}  -\widehat\kappa\chi-\eta  \right]_+.}  \label{eq:uniform-reassembly}$$

> **Proof** and [\[eq:missing-diagonal-upper\]](TPC-081.md#ref-300400a9edb703a7) imply $$\|\mathcal Mz\|  \le  \sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi\,        \Gamma_{\rm pre}D_{\rm raw}}.$$ Together with [\[eq:represented-lower\]](TPC-081.md#ref-739b52d433ed288a), the reverse-triangle inequality gives [\[eq:sharp-reassembly\]](TPC-081.md#ref-ba834416a9701e8a).
>
> It remains to compare the displayed constants. If the bracket in [\[eq:uniform-reassembly\]](TPC-081.md#ref-a0d2f54a96e8258b) is nonpositive, the claim follows from nonnegativity. Otherwise $\sqrt{1-\eta}>\sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi}$, and expansion gives $$\begin{aligned}  \delta_{\rm sharp}  &=1-\eta  -2\sqrt{(1-\eta)\widehat\kappa\chi}  +\widehat\kappa\chi\\  &\ge  1-\eta-2\sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi}  -\widehat\kappa\chi  =\delta_{\rm reasm}.\end{aligned}$$

> **Remark: Why retain the weaker constant** The sharper constant in [\[eq:sharp-reassembly\]](TPC-081.md#ref-ba834416a9701e8a) should be used when all quantities are exact. The conservative form [\[eq:uniform-reassembly\]](TPC-081.md#ref-a0d2f54a96e8258b) separates the represented defect $\eta$, the cross-term cost $2\sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi}$, and an explicit missing self-energy reserve $\widehat\kappa\chi$. It is therefore convenient for a route ledger, but it is not advertised as sharp.

###### ref-c6422ebda0f9341d

> **Proposition: Certificate stop condition** \[prop:reassembly-stop\] The data $\rho(P)<\infty$ and $\Gamma_{\rm pre}>0$ alone do not imply $\delta_{\rm reasm}>0$. A positive conclusion requires a quantitative relation among $\widehat\kappa$, $\chi$, and $\eta$. If $$1-2\sqrt{\widehat\kappa\chi}  -\widehat\kappa\chi-\eta\le0,$$ then [\[eq:uniform-reassembly\]](TPC-081.md#ref-a0d2f54a96e8258b) supplies no positive survival estimate and this certificate branch must stop. This failure is not a proof that the actual reassembled energy vanishes.

> **Proof** The formula itself gives the second assertion. For the first, take $\mathcal M=-\mathcal A$ on the one-dimensional span of $z$. Then $\mathcal Bz=0$, although both maps have finite Gram matrices and $\Gamma_{\rm pre}$ may be positive.

The one-dimensional example also shows why a claim of “the missing part is constructible” is weaker than an energy statement. Exact constructibility can produce exact destructive interference.

# The determinant dictionary and bin-energy survival

Even a positive pre-bin energy $\|\mathcal B_Xz_X\|^2$ need not survive determinant aggregation. The correct additional statistic is a lower modulus of the literal binning map.

###### ref-51ccae00c41d2906

###### ref-9402449720f417c5

> **Definition: Verified reassembled subspace** \[def:reassembled-subspace\] Let $E_X\subseteq\mathbb C^{\mathcal Y_X}$ be a subspace constructed from literal constraints and satisfying $$\mathcal B_Xz_X\in E_X.$$ Its determinant-binning lower modulus is $$s_X=s(\mathcal J_X;E_X)  :=  \inf_{\substack{u\in E_X\\u\ne0}}  \frac{\|\mathcal J_Xu\|_2}{\|u\|_2}.  \label{eq:lower-modulus}$$ If $E_X=\{0\}$, set $s_X=1$.

The convention for the zero subspace is immaterial because then the reassembled vector is zero. For a nonzero subspace, $s_X>0$ is equivalent to injectivity of $\mathcal J_X|_{E_X}$. It is not implied by surjectivity of $\mathcal J_X$ on the ambient determinant space.

###### ref-aeaae07dc756d47e

###### ref-c835ee6876e380d4

###### ref-1886af28e193d3b5

> **Theorem: Binning–dictionary lower bound** \[thm:dictionary-lower\] Assume the packet of [\[def:complete-packet\]](TPC-081.md#ref-dd38a2dfcb1c1090), and suppose that for some $\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}>0$ and $\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\ge0$, $$\|\mathcal B_Xz_X\|_2^2  \ge  \delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}  D_{{\rm raw},X}.  \label{eq:prebin-survival}$$ Let $E_X$ be as in [\[def:reassembled-subspace\]](TPC-081.md#ref-51ccae00c41d2906). Then $$\boxed{\displaystyle  D_X\ge  \left(  s_X\sqrt{\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}}  -\frac{\|r_X\|_2}{\sqrt{D_{{\rm raw},X}}}  \right)_+^2D_{{\rm raw},X}.}  \label{eq:dictionary-lower}$$

> **Proof** By the definition of $s_X$ and [\[eq:prebin-survival\]](TPC-081.md#ref-c835ee6876e380d4), $$\|\mathcal J_X\mathcal B_Xz_X\|_2  \ge s_X\|\mathcal B_Xz_X\|_2  \ge  s_X\sqrt{\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}  D_{{\rm raw},X}}.$$ Since $a_X=\mathcal J_X\mathcal B_Xz_X+r_X$, the reverse-triangle inequality gives $$\|a_X\|_2  \ge  \left(  s_X\sqrt{\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}  D_{{\rm raw},X}}  -\|r_X\|_2  \right)_+.$$ Square and use $D_X=\|a_X\|_2^2$.

###### ref-7f793a3544305578

###### ref-1003dbe1e331ffa5

> **Corollary: Exact dictionary** \[cor:exact-dictionary\] If $r_X=0$, then $$D_X  \ge  s_X^2\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}  \Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}D_{{\rm raw},X}.  \label{eq:exact-dictionary-energy}$$

###### ref-a562a33ab528c826

###### ref-9b5cf631831a9e54

###### ref-6c82273ecd277cad

###### ref-5ba17f239b0da348

###### ref-b356f64c3ee9ebd7

###### ref-91d0ebff8ca4bfe0

###### ref-cf8805705bdfdf33

> **Definition: Polynomial-loss ledger** \[def:polynomial-ledger\] Fix nonnegative exponents $\lambda_{\rm raw},\lambda_s,\lambda_\delta,\lambda_\Gamma, \lambda_r$. Suppose the following lower bounds hold: $$\begin{aligned}  D_{{\rm raw},X}  &\ge X^{-\lambda_{\rm raw}+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,  \label{eq:raw-exponent}\\  s_X  &\ge X^{-\lambda_s+o(1)},  \label{eq:s-exponent}\\  \delta_{{\rm reasm},X}  &\ge X^{-\lambda_\delta+o(1)},  \label{eq:delta-exponent}\\  \Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}  &\ge X^{-\lambda_\Gamma+o(1)}.  \label{eq:Gamma-exponent}\end{aligned}$$ Write $$\varepsilon_{r,X}  :=\frac{\|r_X\|_2}{\sqrt{D_{{\rm raw},X}}}.  \label{eq:normalized-residual}$$ If, for some $0<\tau_X\le1$, $$\varepsilon_{r,X}  \le  (1-\tau_X)  s_X\sqrt{\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}},  \qquad  \tau_X\ge X^{-\lambda_r+o(1)},  \label{eq:residual-separation}$$ then $\lambda_r$ is called the dictionary-separation loss.

###### ref-0ae0ec6e16103826

###### ref-a4f6139fab9e91d1

> **Corollary: Explicit certified energy-loss exponent** \[cor:lambda-D\] Under [\[def:polynomial-ledger\]](TPC-081.md#ref-a562a33ab528c826), $$D_X  \ge  X^{-\lambda_D^{\rm cert}+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,  \qquad  \boxed{  \lambda_D^{\rm cert}  =  \lambda_{\rm raw}  +2\lambda_s+\lambda_\delta+\lambda_\Gamma+2\lambda_r.}  \label{eq:lambda-D-ledger}$$

> **Proof** By [\[eq:residual-separation\]](TPC-081.md#ref-cf8805705bdfdf33), the positive part in [\[eq:dictionary-lower\]](TPC-081.md#ref-1886af28e193d3b5) is at least $$\tau_Xs_X  \sqrt{\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}}.$$ Insert [\[eq:raw-exponent\]](TPC-081.md#ref-9b5cf631831a9e54)–[\[eq:Gamma-exponent\]](TPC-081.md#ref-b356f64c3ee9ebd7), square, and collect exponents.

> **Remark: A lower modulus is arithmetic data** For the sum map [\[eq:literal-binning\]](TPC-081.md#ref-ff109276ae6082de), the ambient kernel is usually large. A useful $s_X$ therefore requires a genuinely restricted subspace $E_X$, defined by identities satisfied by the actual reassembled coefficient. Choosing $E_X$ after inspecting only the target vector, or replacing it by a numerically fitted one-dimensional span, does not prove a uniform asymptotic theorem. The constraints defining $E_X$ and the lower-modulus proof must come from archived identities independently of the value of $D_X$; the proof of $s_X$ may not invoke the determinant-energy lower bound it is meant to establish.

> **Remark: Possible arithmetic hardness** Expanding $D_X=\sum_n|A_{C_X}(n)|^2$ reintroduces pairs of literal Möbius-weighted atoms constrained to have equal determinant. A lower bound at the natural scale may therefore encode a signed two-point correlation of Chowla type. The algebra above identifies that possibility; it does not remove it. Averaged results for multiplicative correlations provide important benchmarks [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015,Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-081.md#cite-b5a8969fcb0fe4a8); [\[Tao2016LogChowla\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-081.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07), but do not by themselves establish this fixed-$h_0$, determinant-fiber lower bound.

# Zero-frequency flatness and the recorded zero-slack condition

We now combine an amplitude estimate with determinant-energy survival. The calculation is elementary, but its direction is decisive.

###### ref-d4eb518da6dc6858

###### ref-1e41baa7dc155d2f

###### ref-db4244df7ebf2099

###### ref-9f1a38ba2dd80d6a

###### ref-c591d425fbebc863

> **Theorem: General exponent transfer** \[thm:general-exponent-transfer\] Fix $h_0\ne0$. Assume $$Q_X=X^{\beta+o(1)},  \qquad  J_X=X^{1-\beta+o(1)}  \label{eq:general-scales}$$ for a fixed $0<\beta<1$. Suppose that for fixed $\eta_Z,\lambda_D\ge0$, $$\begin{aligned}  |Z_X|  &\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,  \label{eq:zero-mode-exponent}\\  D_X  &\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2.  \label{eq:energy-exponent}\end{aligned}$$ Then $$\boxed{\displaystyle  \mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le  X^{3\beta-2+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}.}  \label{eq:general-flatness-transfer}$$

> **Proof** The lower bound [\[eq:energy-exponent\]](TPC-081.md#ref-9f1a38ba2dd80d6a) is positive for all sufficiently large $X$, so $D_X>0$ there. Hence $$\begin{aligned}  \mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  &=\frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}\\  &\le  X^{\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}  \frac{Q_X^4}{Q_X^3J_X^2}\\  &=X^{\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}  \frac{Q_X}{J_X^2}.\end{aligned}$$ By [\[eq:general-scales\]](TPC-081.md#ref-1e41baa7dc155d2f), $Q_X/J_X^2=X^{\beta-2(1-\beta)+o(1)} =X^{3\beta-2+o(1)}$.

###### ref-5cc5c01920746a9e

###### ref-95eb42f94d0236fb

###### ref-39df0b4adcad2568

###### ref-1200b1c6fa7dca2f

> **Corollary: The high-row zero-slack transfer** \[cor:zero-slack\] At $$\beta=\frac{267}{400},  \qquad  Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},  \qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},  \label{eq:high-row-scales}$$ the conclusion is $$\boxed{\displaystyle  \mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le  X^{1/400+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}.}  \label{eq:high-row-flatness}$$ Consequently, the two recorded exponent bounds imply the TPC-32 hypothesis $\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)\le X^{1/400+o(1)}$ whenever $$\boxed{2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D.}  \label{eq:zero-slack-condition}$$ Strict inequality gives a fixed power of robustness; equality gives no spare fixed-power margin. The condition is sharp for a deduction from the recorded bounds alone; it is not asserted to be necessary for the physical packet unless the two exponents are optimal.

> **Proof** The identity $$3\cdot\frac{267}{400}-2  =\frac{801-800}{400}  =\frac1{400}$$ and [\[thm:general-exponent-transfer\]](TPC-081.md#ref-d4eb518da6dc6858) give [\[eq:high-row-flatness\]](TPC-081.md#ref-39df0b4adcad2568). Its recorded exponent is at most $1/400+o(1)$ under [\[eq:zero-slack-condition\]](TPC-081.md#ref-1200b1c6fa7dca2f). If that condition fails, these bounds alone do not close the threshold.

###### ref-d77d2cf789fd4113

###### ref-31d4b8ce2ec2ed53

> **Corollary: The current amplitude target requires natural energy** \[cor:current-target\] The TPC-33 amplitude target $$|Z_X|\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2$$ has $\eta_Z=0$. Under that target, [\[eq:zero-slack-condition\]](TPC-081.md#ref-1200b1c6fa7dca2f) permits no positive certified energy loss. The sufficient zero-loss requirement is $$\lambda_D=0,  \qquad\text{equivalently}\qquad  D_X\ge X^{-o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2.  \label{eq:natural-energy-required}$$ Thus any positive fixed-power determinant-energy loss cannot close this transfer from the current amplitude bound unless a new zero-mode saving compensates for it.

> **Proof** For the sufficient ledger condition, both exponents are nonnegative, so $0\ge\lambda_D$ implies $\lambda_D=0$.

> **Remark: Additional losses** If a later comparison contributes an additional squared-energy loss $\lambda_{\rm aux}\ge0$, then the condition for this augmented certificate becomes $$2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D+\lambda_{\rm aux}.$$ Such a loss cannot be hidden in an $o(1)$ term when it is a fixed positive power.

> **Remark: Two distinct appearances of $1/400$** The exponent $1/400$ in [\[eq:high-row-flatness\]](TPC-081.md#ref-39df0b4adcad2568) is the geometric baseline $Q_X/J_X^2$ and is the allowed TPC-32 flatness endpoint. It is not a free account from which unrecorded losses may be spent. Separately, whenever the wider route compares accumulated fixed-power losses with an inherited $1/400$ margin, the operational rule is strict: $$\text{continue only if total loss}<\frac1{400};  \qquad  \text{stop if total loss}\ge\frac1{400}.$$ In particular equality in [\[eq:zero-slack-condition\]](TPC-081.md#ref-1200b1c6fa7dca2f) is a valid conditional implication but has zero robustness against any further fixed-power degradation.

# Sharp nonidentifiability and no-go models

The next examples show that each interface in [1](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-081.md#src-ea8d631132021089) carries information absent from the preceding one.

###### ref-1b9209a3a8194369

###### ref-27b89c12a9ad3d59

> **Proposition: Unsigned completion data do not determine the zero mode** \[prop:unsigned-no-go\] For every integer $N\ge2$, there are vectors $u,v\in\mathbb C^N$ with identical support, identical coordinate moduli, and identical energy, but $$\left|\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}u_j\right|=N,  \qquad  \sum_{j=0}^{N-1}v_j=0.  \label{eq:phase-no-go}$$ Consequently, no statistic depending only on unsigned support, coordinate masses, or total energy can determine zero-frequency flatness.

> **Proof** Take $u_j=1$ and $$v_j=\exp(2\pi i j/N).$$ Both vectors have full support, $|u_j|=|v_j|=1$, and squared norm $N$. The first sum is $N$, while the second is the sum of all $N$-th roots of unity and vanishes.

> **Remark: Real version** For even $N$, the real vectors $$u=(1,\ldots,1),  \qquad  v=(1,-1,1,-1,\ldots,1,-1)$$ already give the same conclusion. Thus the obstruction is not caused by complex phases.

###### ref-3804622d250ee8e9

> **Proposition: Raw atomic mass need not survive a determinant bin** \[prop:bin-cancellation\] There is no positive universal constant $c$ such that $$\|\mathcal Jw\|_2^2\ge c\|w\|_2^2$$ for every sum-binning map and every raw vector $w$. Indeed, two nonzero raw atoms in a single determinant bin can have raw energy $2$ and determinant energy $0$.

> **Proof** Let $\mathcal Y=\{y_1,y_2\}$, let both keys have the same determinant label $n_0$, take unit multipliers, and set $$w(y_1)=1,\qquad w(y_2)=-1.$$ Then $\|w\|_2^2=2$, while $$(\mathcal Jw)(n_0)=1-1=0.$$ Thus $w\in\operatorname{Ker}\mathcal J$, and the ambient lower modulus is zero.

###### ref-9eb15d6725621cc8

> **Corollary: Support survival is not energy survival** \[cor:support-not-energy\] The facts that every raw atom is represented, every determinant bin is nonempty, and the raw energy has its natural order do not imply a positive lower bound for $D_X$.

> **Proof** has all three properties and post-bin energy zero.

###### ref-9f9e8f485d368b2c

\[ex:same-majorant\] In one dimension take $\mathcal Az=z$. The choices $\mathcal M_+z=z$ and $\mathcal M_-z=-z$ have the same missing Gram, the same diagonal mass, and the same Perron constant $\widehat\kappa=1$. Yet $$(\mathcal A+\mathcal M_+)z=2z,  \qquad  (\mathcal A+\mathcal M_-)z=0.$$ The coefficient-specific bound correctly permits both outcomes when $\Xi(z)=1$. Therefore the Perron majorant is a bound, not a phase reconstruction theorem.

###### ref-5a0d934b9086f639

> **Proposition: A small zero mode does not force natural energy** \[prop:zero-not-energy\] For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a nonzero coefficient $a$ with $\sum_na_n=0$ and $0<\|a\|_2^2<\varepsilon$.

> **Proof** Take $a=(t,-t)$ with $0<2|t|^2<\varepsilon$.

This last example is elementary but prevents a common reversal: cancellation in $Z_X$ does not furnish a lower bound for $D_X$. The numerator and denominator in $\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)$ require separate arithmetic inputs.

> **Remark: Averaging over shifts** An averaged-$h$ lower bound for determinant energy is compatible with a zero or tiny value at one prescribed $h_0$. Hence mean-square estimates over shifts cannot be relabeled as the fixed-$h_0$ hypothesis [\[eq:energy-exponent\]](TPC-081.md#ref-9f1a38ba2dd80d6a). This is a quantifier obstruction, not a technical normalization issue.

# Conditional transfer to the matched shell and full block

We now record the strongest valid arithmetic implication. Every premise is explicit.

###### ref-689a66511907d71b

###### ref-1b3960ea219fc830

###### ref-914d8c595b099dc5

###### ref-34446b031fb8dc00

> **Theorem: Literal fixed-shift flatness transfer** \[thm:literal-flatness-transfer\] Fix $h_0\ne0$, and let $a_X$ be the actual determinant coefficient of the TPC-32 matched packet with $$C_X=\lfloor J_X\rfloor,\qquad  Q_XJ_X\asymp X,\qquad  Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)}.$$ Write $$N_{0,X}:=J_XQ_X^2\asymp XQ_X  \label{eq:matched-raw-scale}$$ for the literal raw scale of this matched shell. Suppose there are fixed exponents $\eta_Z,\lambda_D\ge0$ for which the following assumptions hold:
>
> 1.  the complete packet of [\[def:complete-packet\]](TPC-081.md#ref-dd38a2dfcb1c1090) is literal, with the same signs, units, orientation, and global raw normalization as $a_X$;
>
> 2.  the missing-Gram hypotheses of [\[thm:raw-reassembly\]](TPC-081.md#ref-30db2a31074f2e5e) give $\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}$;
>
> 3.  an exact determinant map $\mathcal J_X$, a verified subspace $E_X$, and a dictionary residual $r_X$ satisfy [\[thm:dictionary-lower\]](TPC-081.md#ref-aeaae07dc756d47e);
>
> 4.  the resulting lower bound proves $$D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2;$$
>
> 5.  independently, the actual zero mode satisfies $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2$$ with $2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D$.
>
> Then $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)\le X^{1/400+o(1)}.  \label{eq:literal-flatness-output}$$ Consequently, by the conditional zero-frequency theorem of TPC-32, every fixed $$\sigma<\frac{133}{400}  \label{eq:matched-saving}$$ is a power-saving exponent relative to its raw scale $N_{0,X}$ for the complete matched cutoff shell. Equivalently, if $\mathcal S_X$ denotes that shell, then $$|\mathcal S_X|\le X^{-\sigma+o(1)}N_{0,X}.  \label{eq:matched-shell-bound}$$

> **Proof** Assumptions (T4)–(T5) and [\[cor:zero-slack\]](TPC-081.md#ref-5cc5c01920746a9e) give [\[eq:literal-flatness-output\]](TPC-081.md#ref-e783e168b91c90f8). Assumption (T1) identifies this ratio with the distinguished coefficient used in TPC-32, rather than with a surrogate. Its conditional high-row closure theorem then gives [\[eq:matched-shell-bound\]](TPC-081.md#ref-34446b031fb8dc00) for every $\sigma$ satisfying [\[eq:matched-saving\]](TPC-081.md#ref-914d8c595b099dc5) [`\citep{WangTPC32}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-081.md#cite-49ad82103eb75eb7).

> **Remark: What completion contributes** Assumptions (T2)–(T3) can prove (T4), but they do not prove (T5). In particular, a complete carrier census, a positive reassembly constant, and an injective determinant dictionary still do not cancel the signed zero mode. The amplitude estimate remains an arithmetic premise.

The matched shell is not automatically the whole Type-II residual. The global step requires an exact block identity of the kind isolated in TPC-18 [`\citep{WangTPC18}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-081.md#cite-4abf939102bd1649).

###### ref-60976c39e842f1f7

###### ref-49f4681e6a864cb8

###### ref-c1d6724e5a6f7251

> **Theorem: Conditional full-block transfer** \[thm:full-block-transfer\] Fix $h_0\ne0$. Assume that the physical hard remainder has an exact decomposition $$\mathcal B_{h_0,\delta}(X)  =  \sum_{b\in\mathscr B_X}w_{b,X}\mathcal S_{b,X}  +R_X,  \qquad  |\mathscr B_X|=O(\log X),  \label{eq:full-block-cover}$$ where:
>
> 1.  every $\mathcal S_{b,X}$ is a literal matched packet with complete native keys and the same fixed shift $h_0$, and $N_{0,b,X}$ denotes its own literal raw scale;
>
> 2.  the hypotheses of [\[thm:literal-flatness-transfer\]](TPC-081.md#ref-689a66511907d71b) hold uniformly in $b$, with its own literal coefficient $a_{b,X}$, determinant dictionary, and certificate data for every block;
>
> 3.  $|w_{b,X}|\le X^{o(1)}$, $R_X=o(X)$, and for some fixed $0<\sigma_*<133/400$, $$\sum_{b\in\mathscr B_X}  |w_{b,X}|N_{0,b,X}X^{-\sigma_*}=o(X);  \label{eq:block-normalization-close}$$
>
> 4.  all calibration, drift, endpoint, nonprimitive, and prime-power terms omitted from [\[eq:full-block-cover\]](TPC-081.md#ref-60976c39e842f1f7) are already included in $R_X$ with their literal signs.
>
> Then $$\mathcal B_{h_0,\delta}(X)=o(X).  \label{eq:hard-remainder-small}$$

> **Proof** Choose a fixed $\sigma$ with $\sigma_*<\sigma<133/400$. Uniform application of [\[thm:literal-flatness-transfer\]](TPC-081.md#ref-689a66511907d71b) gives $$|\mathcal S_{b,X}|  \le X^{-\sigma+o(1)}N_{0,b,X}.$$ The fixed gap $\sigma-\sigma_*$ absorbs the uniform $o(1)$. Insert the resulting $|\mathcal S_{b,X}|\le X^{-\sigma_*}N_{0,b,X}$ in [\[eq:full-block-cover\]](TPC-081.md#ref-60976c39e842f1f7), sum absolutely, and use [\[eq:block-normalization-close\]](TPC-081.md#ref-49f4681e6a864cb8) and $R_X=o(X)$.

> **Remark: Two different uses of $\mathcal B$** The synthesis operator $\mathcal B_X=\mathcal A_X+\mathcal M_X$ acts on completion coefficients. The scalar $\mathcal B_{h_0,\delta}(X)$ in [\[eq:full-block-cover\]](TPC-081.md#ref-60976c39e842f1f7) is the physical hard remainder. The shared letter does not identify these differently typed objects.

> **Remark: Status of the cover** is a logical implication, not a proof of [\[eq:full-block-cover\]](TPC-081.md#ref-60976c39e842f1f7). The all-block cover was isolated earlier as an additional hypothesis. Nor does the present paper verify the uniform zero-mode or energy premises for even one growing physical block. Therefore [\[eq:hard-remainder-small\]](TPC-081.md#ref-c1d6724e5a6f7251) is not an unconditional result.

> **Remark: No prime-pair claim** For an admissible even $h_0$ with nonzero main-term integral $\mathcal I_0(W)$, in the earlier Vaughan normal form a theorem of the strength $\mathcal B_{h_0,\delta}(X)=o(X)$, together with all inherited identities, would reach a weighted fixed-shift Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic. That observation measures the strength of the missing premises. It does not prove them, and it must not be restated as a twin-prime or parity result. For the standard analytic-number-theory framework in which such reductions sit, see [`\citet{IwaniecKowalski2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-081.md#cite-49dfea87eaada156).

# Reproducible audit, claim boundary, and next gate

The transfer is useful only if every object can be checked against the literal packet. We record a minimum archive.

## Carrier and dictionary census

For each audited scale $X$, archive:

1.  the fixed value $h_0$, the exact $Q_X,J_X,C_X$, and the version of every upstream identity;

2.  the complete ordered native key sets $\mathcal K_X,\mathcal Y_X,\mathcal N_X$, including field names, integer ranges, orientations, contents, provenance labels, and cryptographic hashes;

3.  the exact sparse arrays of $\mathcal A_X,\mathcal M_X,\mathcal B_X$, before thresholding, together with dimensions, ranks, and actual nonzero supports;

4.  the literal coefficient $z_X$, the actual determinant coefficient $a_X$, and a direct exact check of $r_X=a_X-\mathcal J_X\mathcal B_Xz_X$;

5.  the determinant label $\nu_X$, every multiplier $\tau_X(y)$, every fiber cardinality, and the sparse matrix of $\mathcal J_X$;

6.  the single global $D_{{\rm raw},X}$, with its atomic measure and units; no block-renormalized replacement;

7.  $G_{\mathcal M,X}$, its exact active diagonal $D_{\mathcal M,X}$, the normalized residual $R_{\mathcal M,X}$, the majorant $P_X$, an entrywise proof of $|R_{\mathcal M,X}|\le P_X$, and a certified bound for $\rho(P_X)$;

8.  the quantities $\eta_X,\chi_X,\Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}$, $\Xi_X(z_X)$, $\delta_{{\rm sharp},X}$, and $\delta_{{\rm reasm},X}$;

9.  the defining constraints for $E_X$, an exact proof that $\mathcal B_Xz_X\in E_X$, and a certified lower bound for $s_X$;

10. the exact values $Z_X,D_X,\mathfrak D_{h_0}(X)$, and $\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)$, followed by the complete polynomial-loss ledger of [\[cor:lambda-D\]](TPC-081.md#ref-0ae0ec6e16103826).

Exact integer, rational, algebraic, or interval certificates should be used whenever feasible. Floating-point tolerances are reported separately and never used to change the exact support. A regression over finitely many $X$ may motivate an exponent, but it does not prove the asymptotic inequalities in [\[thm:literal-flatness-transfer\]](TPC-081.md#ref-689a66511907d71b).

## Six mandatory physical checks

Before any L1 claim, answer all six questions:

1.  Are the coefficients literal physical coefficients rather than majorant, trace, or averaged-family coefficients?

2.  Is the same prescribed nonzero $h_0$ retained in every map?

3.  Is the atomic normalization global and physical?

4.  Is the representation canonical, or has every multiplicity in a nonminimal representation been restored?

5.  Is the support the actual exact post-aggregation support?

6.  Does the endpoint ledger keep every fixed-power loss explicit and obey the strict $1/400$ stop rule?

Failure of any check returns the result to L0.

## Decision protocol

The next arithmetic decision is a deliberate trichotomy.

1.  Construct the literal dictionary with $r_X=0$, and prove $$s_X,\ \delta_{{\rm reasm},X},\ \Gamma_{{\rm pre},X}  \ge X^{-o(1)},  \qquad  D_{{\rm raw},X}\ge X^{-o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2.$$ Then [\[eq:natural-energy-required\]](TPC-081.md#ref-31d4b8ce2ec2ed53) follows and the remaining independent task is the zero-mode amplitude estimate.

2.  Prove an amplitude gain $\eta_Z>0$. Then a determinant-energy loss $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$ is admissible, subject to the separate strict endpoint ledger.

3.  Show that the required lower bound for $D_X$, after exact expansion, contains an unresolved fixed-shift signed correlation of the same strength as a Chowla/parity input. Then record a negative transfer theorem and stop this route rather than rename the missing arithmetic as a completion lemma.

## Conclusion

The new point is not a hidden cancellation theorem. It is an exact factorization of the remaining inference: $$\boxed{ \begin{array}{c} \text{literal completion}\\ \Downarrow\ \text{Gram survival}\\ \text{pre-bin energy}\\ \Downarrow\ \text{restricted binning + dictionary}\\ \text{determinant energy}\\ \Downarrow\ \text{independent zero-mode estimate}\\ \text{zero-frequency flatness}. \end{array}}$$ The first two downward arrows are now governed by explicit, falsifiable constants. The final arithmetic inputs remain open. That separation prevents exact finite algebra from being mislabeled as fixed-shift parity progress while leaving a precise route for a genuine future advance.
