## Abstract

TPC-73–TPC-81 close a sequence of exact primitive-support, direct-entry, convex-dual, provenance, sparse-incidence, complex-scalar forest, anchor–direct, and scalar-spectral interfaces. We audit those nine papers together with the present synthesis as a ten-paper block. The audit separates finite algebra and graph theory ($\mathrm{L0}$), literal fixed-shift transfer ($\mathrm{L1}$), and new asymptotic arithmetic input ($\mathrm{L2}$). Every unconditional theorem in the audited block lies at $\mathrm{L0}$ or $\mathrm{L1}$; none proves a new $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate.

The exact surviving architecture passes through the actual determinant coefficient $$a_X=(A_{C_X}(n))_n,\qquad  Z_X=\sum_n A_{C_X}(n),\qquad  D_X=\sum_n|A_{C_X}(n)|^2.$$ Completion geometry reaches $D_X$ only after a literal determinant-binning map, a positive lower modulus on a verified subspace, and a controlled dictionary residual. It does not control the distinguished zero mode $Z_X$. At the critical scales $$Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},$$ the hypotheses $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,\qquad  D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2$$ give the flatness bound $$\frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}  \le X^{1/400+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}.$$ Within this two-exponent transfer ledger, the sharp recorded-bounds compatibility condition is $2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D$. For the current transfer route the present zero-mode target has $\eta_Z=0$, so the sufficient ledger condition requires natural energy, $\lambda_D=0$. Support, absolute completion cost, raw energy, anchor quotient geometry, and scalar holonomy cannot supply these two arithmetic inputs without additional literal maps and estimates. We prove no-teleportation and nonidentifiability statements, give branch-specific kill rules, and reduce the next block to three honest routes: determinant-energy survival, distinguished-zero-frequency cancellation, or a negative transfer theorem. These routes concern the TPC-32 matched-shell flatness gate; the exact TPC-18 full-block cover and uniform remainder control remain an independent downstream gate. The paper proves no fixed-shift Chowla estimate, parity breach, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime conclusion.

# Introduction

The purpose of a route audit is not to make a long proof program look closer to its final conjecture than it is. It is to identify which interfaces have become exact, which quantities still require arithmetic information, and which apparent shortcuts are type errors. That distinction is especially important in a fixed-shift problem. An estimate averaged over shifts, frequencies, or carrier families may be deep, yet it does not automatically control one prescribed nonzero $h_0$. Likewise, a finite-dimensional completion theorem can be completely rigorous while remaining silent about the signed coefficient to which it is intended to apply.

TPC-73–TPC-80 develop a chain of exact algebraic tools [`\citep{WangTPC73,WangTPC74,WangTPC75,WangTPC76,WangTPC77,WangTPC78,WangTPC79,WangTPC80}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-397bc50bd6422e39); [\[WangTPC74\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-34aa1f34b4ae63af); [\[WangTPC75\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-299363b57a353dc9); [\[WangTPC76\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-5d543b3dae6e0571); [\[WangTPC77\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-0f20872e8348869c); [\[WangTPC78\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-ba82f134876d056b); [\[WangTPC79\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-1af24f01b946784c); [\[WangTPC80\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-b6acdcfa33e0c0e1). The preceding TPC-72 audit isolated the missing direct two-corner operator bridge and imposed the strict physical loss rule $\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400$ [`\citep{WangTPC72}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-d21b73d8ae01e89b). TPC-74 closes that direct entry bridge, while TPC-73–TPC-80 close or sharpen a sequence of $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ certificate subgates. Their quantitative aggregation on the small growing physical carrier remains open. TPC-81 then asks for the first time in this block how those tools could reach the determinant coefficient and the distinguished zero frequency used in the matched-shell argument [`\citep{WangTPC81,WangTPC32,WangTPC33}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-ff65f956e7cf0d6d); [\[WangTPC32\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-49ad82103eb75eb7); [\[WangTPC33\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-3dc89b1d29e24761). The answer is both useful and restrictive. Completion can contribute a lower bound for pre-bin energy, but determinant aggregation can erase that energy. Even if determinant energy survives, its unsigned size does not cancel the zero mode. The two tasks must be recorded separately.

This paper is the tenth item in the block. Its main outputs are as follows.

1.  We give formal definitions of $\mathrm{L0},\mathrm{L1},\mathrm{L2}$ and audit TPC-73–TPC-81 against them. The block contains substantial exact $\mathrm{L0}$ structure and several $\mathrm{L1}$ transfer interfaces, but no completed $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic estimate.

2.  We write the typed dependency graph. The sparse incidence graph of TPC-77/78, the common-target quotient of TPC-79, the scalar Hermitian row graph of TPC-80, and the determinant coefficient space of TPC-81 are distinct objects. Shared labels do not identify them.

3.  We isolate a no-teleportation principle: without an explicit literal synthesis or intertwining map, a theorem on one of those objects supplies no determinant-energy or zero-frequency estimate.

4.  We restate the exact anchor–direct and scalar-holonomy interfaces and record their application conditions. In particular, the orphan quotient is not the physical hybrid cost, trees supply no phase-frustration saving, and one sector gap does not give norm strictness.

5.  We crosswalk the TPC-81 reassembly and determinant-dictionary inequalities into one conditional energy-survival statement. Every factor in that statement is visible; none is silently declared to be $X^{o(1)}$.

6.  We prove the recorded-bounds zero-slack compatibility condition $$2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D.$$ Equality reaches the TPC-32 flatness threshold but leaves no fixed-power reserve. It is not permission to spend another $1/400$.

7.  We give two exact nonidentifiability examples and a three-route decision tree with hard stop rules. These permit a clean negative paper if the two remaining inputs reduce to a fixed-shift Chowla-type correlation, rather than disguising that correlation as a consequence of completion geometry.

The distinction between structural and arithmetic progress is standard in spirit. Sieve parity and multiplicative correlations are not resolved by unsigned support information [`\citep{HeathBrown1982Parity,IwaniecKowalski2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-bd33f7374ef1e88f); [\[IwaniecKowalski2004\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-49dfea87eaada156). Strong averaged correlation theorems provide essential comparison points [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015,Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-b5a8969fcb0fe4a8); [\[Tao2016LogChowla\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07), but they do not become a theorem for the literal distinguished frequency and fixed $h_0$ without a separate localization argument.

Our convention is deliberately strict. The actual coefficient is formed with all native keys, literal signs and phases, exact post-aggregation support, and one global raw normalization. An ambient support, an unsigned envelope, a fitted reduced space, or a componentwise renormalization may be a diagnostic, but it is not the physical input. This strictness turns several vague obstacles into finite checklists and makes the remaining arithmetic gate precise.

# Audit levels and the literal packet

###### ref-07af69a497c5f1df

> **Definition: The three levels** \[def:levels\] We use the following labels.
>
> 1.  An $\mathrm{L0}$ result is a finite-dimensional algebraic, convex-dual, combinatorial, graph-theoretic, or spectral theorem. It may be exact and new, but it neither depends on nor automatically applies to the growing arithmetic carrier.
>
> 2.  An *$\mathrm{L1}$-typed conditional interface* has hypotheses that preserve one fixed nonzero $h_0$, the complete physical keys, literal coefficients, actual active support, one global raw atomic normalization, and the original quantifier range, and it states how an $\mathrm{L0}$ object connects to the actual growing carrier. Its arithmetic hypotheses may remain open. A *verified physical $\mathrm{L1}$ application* additionally checks every literal packet item and every non-arithmetic interface hypothesis for the carrier under study. Neither label asserts an open asymptotic estimate.
>
> 3.  An $\mathrm{L2}$ result proves a new asymptotic estimate for that literal growing fixed-$h_0$ carrier, strong enough to reduce a positive exponent in the final physical loss ledger.

> **Remark: Interface progress versus gate closure** An $\mathrm{L1}$-typed interface may identify the right statistic without bounding it. Thus “advances the $\mathrm{L1}$ interface” and “closes the $\mathrm{L1}$ quantitative gate” are different statements. Only the latter can feed an exponent ledger, and it becomes $\mathrm{L2}$ only when the bound is a genuinely new arithmetic estimate.

###### ref-e525fdb1908a3d00

> **Definition: Literal physical packet** \[def:literal-packet\] At scale $X$, a literal physical packet contains:
>
> 1.  a fixed integer $h_0\ne0$, not averaged or selected after the estimate;
>
> 2.  the complete native key set $\mathcal K_X$, including every row, orientation, orbit, divisor, content, source, endpoint, and provenance label used by the construction;
>
> 3.  the literal coefficient $z_X\in\mathbb C^{\mathcal K_X}$, with all signs, phases, units, and multiplicities;
>
> 4.  the exact synthesis maps under discussion, with their genuine domains, targets, and active nonzero support after coefficient aggregation;
>
> 5.  the determinant labels and every multiplier used in the final binning map;
>
> 6.  the actual post-aggregation coefficient $$a_X=(A_{C_X}(n))_{n\in\mathcal N_X},$$ with $C_X=\lfloor J_X\rfloor$ in the TPC-32 matched-shell specialization, with cancellations performed only after all native contributions have been assembled;
>
> 7.  one global raw atomic energy $D_{{\rm raw},X}>0$, computed before deletion, thresholding, block selection, or local renormalization; and
>
> 8.  the original uniformity and quantifier range needed by the physical conclusion.

###### ref-19ab6f3eac00ed5c

> **Definition: Actual support** \[def:actual-support\] The actual support of a literal map or coefficient is the set of nonzero entries after exact aggregation with the physical signs and phases. It is not the union of geometrically possible edges, a thresholded numerical graph, or an ambient incidence pattern.

###### ref-06fa44b6b1abc00c

> **Proposition: Promotion test** \[prop:promotion-test\] An $\mathrm{L0}$ theorem is promoted to a verified physical $\mathrm{L1}$ application only after all items of [\[def:literal-packet\]](TPC-082.md#ref-e525fdb1908a3d00) used by the theorem have been verified. Omitting any one of fixed $h_0$, a native key, actual support, the global raw normalizer, or the required uniformity leaves the result at $\mathrm{L0}$ as a physical application, although a correctly quantified conditional statement may still be $\mathrm{L1}$-typed.

> **Proof** Each omitted datum permits two physical packets to have the same reduced model but different target coefficients or normalizations. For example, deleting a native key changes a determinant fiber, replacing actual support by ambient support changes component and spectral data, and local renormalization can hide arbitrary deleted raw mass. Therefore the reduced theorem does not identify the physical statistic. Verification of the full list supplies the needed typing; it does not by itself supply an asymptotic bound.

###### ref-ccc130521bab4794

> **Definition: Closed interface and closed arithmetic gate** \[def:closed-gates\] An interface is *closed* when the source, target, map, norm, normalization, and quantifiers are explicit and the asserted identity or inequality is proved. An arithmetic gate is closed only when its required bound is established on the literal packet with the stated asymptotic strength. A finite exact formula for an unknown quantity closes an interface but not necessarily its arithmetic gate.

This terminology is used throughout the table in [4](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-082.md#src-f54c23b2b8df95e0). It prevents an exact optimization identity from being reported as cancellation, and it prevents a numerical certificate on a truncated carrier from being reported as a uniform asymptotic theorem.

> **Theorem: Stage audit** \[thm:stage-audit\] Every unconditional theorem in TPC-73–TPC-81 is an $\mathrm{L0}$ or $\mathrm{L1}$ statement in the sense of [\[def:levels\]](TPC-082.md#ref-07af69a497c5f1df). # A no-teleportation theorem

Four spaces recur in the block:

1.  the actual nonzero provenance incidence graph $\mathcal G_X^{\rm inc}$ of TPC-77/78;

2.  the common synthesis target $K_X$ and quotient $K_X/V_{A,X}$ of TPC-79;

3.  the scalar row space on which the Hermitian pair $(H_X^{\rm hol},P_X^{\rm hol})$ of TPC-80 acts; and

4.  the determinant-bin space $\mathbb C^{\mathcal N_X}$ containing $a_X$.

They can share indices or provenance labels while carrying different operations.

###### ref-38384a5f5872b907

> **Definition: Literal bridge** \[def:literal-bridge\] A literal bridge between two audited objects is a specified linear or affine map, constructed from the complete physical packet, that:
>
> 1.  preserves all native multiplicities, signs, phases, and normalization factors;
>
> 2.  has the stated source and target, rather than an observation space with the arrows reversed;
>
> 3.  maps actual nonzero support to the actual object being estimated; and
>
> 4.  proves the norm, quadratic-form, or aggregation inequality needed for the next step uniformly in the original quantifier range.

###### ref-a59b18d4b34e2798

> **Theorem: No teleportation** \[thm:no-teleportation\] Data consisting only of $$\mathcal G_X^{\rm inc},\qquad K_X/V_{A,X},\qquad  (H_X^{\rm hol},P_X^{\rm hol}),$$ and their support, quotient-gauge, or scalar-spectral invariants do not determine a nontrivial cancellation bound for $|Z_X|$ or a positive lower bound for $D_X$. Any inference from the listed structural data to such a conclusion requires, at minimum, a literal bridge to the pre-bin coefficient and an exact determinant map $$\mathcal J_X:\mathbb C^{\mathcal Y_X}\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{\mathcal N_X}.$$ In particular, the conclusions of TPC-78, TPC-79, and TPC-80 cannot substitute for one another or for a determinant-frequency estimate.

> **Proof** The listed structural data contain no map to $\mathbb C^{\mathcal N_X}$. Keeping them fixed therefore imposes no algebraic restriction on a vector assigned to that separate space. One may assign the same structural triple to $a=(1,\ldots,1)$ or, for even dimension, to $b=(1,-1,\ldots,1,-1)$. The two determinant vectors have the same support, entrywise absolute values, and energy, while their zero modes are different. Thus the structural triple does not determine $Z_X$.
>
> Even a map into a raw atomic space does not determine $D_X$: a determinant fiber may send two atoms $(1,-1)$ to zero. Hence a positive raw norm does not give a positive post-bin norm without a lower modulus for the actual $\mathcal J_X$ on a verified range. These examples are formalized in [9](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-082.md#src-463ba93088d1a310). A literal bridge and determinant map add exactly the missing relations.

###### ref-9c6a2ceac8d80d99

> **Corollary: Three prohibited identifications** \[cor:prohibited-identifications\] Without additional proofs, none of the following is valid:
>
> 1.  treating a four-corner observation or trace as an anchor synthesis vector in the direct target;
>
> 2.  treating a block-valued carrier as a scalar Hermitian edge matrix, or replacing the actual row graph by an ambient graph; or
>
> 3.  treating raw atomic mass or pre-bin energy as determinant-bin energy.

> **Proof** Item (i) reverses or changes the target of a map, item (ii) changes the operator algebra, and item (iii) discards the kernel of $\mathcal J_X$. Each violates [\[def:literal-bridge\]](TPC-082.md#ref-38384a5f5872b907).

> **Remark: What the theorem does not say** is a typed nonidentifiability result. It does not assert that no useful bridge exists. Constructing such a bridge is precisely Route I-A of [10](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-082.md#src-0388c7dbee4d556e); the direct determinant-energy Route I-B does not require this completion bridge.

> **Proposition: Exact TPC-80 saturation audit** \[prop:tpc80-interface\] One has $$\rho(P)-\|H\|=\min\{\delta_+,\delta_-\}. \label{eq:sector-min}$$ Moreover, $$\|H\|=\rho(P)  \iff  |H|=P  \quad\text{and}\quad  H=\sigma DPD^* \label{eq:perron-saturation-audit}$$ for some $\sigma\in\{\pm1\}$ and diagonal unitary $D$. > **Proof** If $i$ and $j$ lie in different components, then $P_{ij}=0$, so $|H_{ij}|\le P_{ij}$ gives $H_{ij}=0$. The two norm identities follow from block diagonalization. Equality of the two maxima is equivalent to saturation on at least one component attaining the Perron maximum.

## The normalized missing Gram

For the actual determinant coefficient, define $$Z_X=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}A_{C_X}(n),\qquad  D_X=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}|A_{C_X}(n)|^2, \label{eq:ZD-audit}$$ and, when $D_X>0$, $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)=\frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}. \label{eq:flatness-audit}$$ If $D_X=0$, then $a_X=0$ and hence $Z_X=0$; the flatness is set to zero by convention. Both statistics in [\[eq:ZD-audit\]](TPC-082.md#ref-5304ecaf63693cd3) are post-aggregation quantities.

> **Theorem: General two-exponent transfer** \[thm:general-two-exponent\] Fix $h_0\ne0$ and $C_X=\lfloor J_X\rfloor$. Let $$Q_X=X^{\beta+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{1-\beta+o(1)}$$ for a fixed $0<\beta<1$. > **Proof** The assumed lower bound for $D_X$ is positive for all sufficiently large $X$. For those $X$, $$\frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}  \le  X^{\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}  \frac{Q_X}{J_X^2}.$$ The last ratio is $X^{\beta-2(1-\beta)+o(1)} =X^{3\beta-2+o(1)}$.

> **Theorem: Zero-slack compatibility** \[thm:zero-slack-audit\] At the critical scales $$Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)}, \label{eq:critical-scales-audit}$$ [\[thm:general-two-exponent\]](TPC-082.md#ref-2242b9537f8eeef3) gives $$\boxed{  \mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le  X^{1/400+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}.} \label{eq:critical-flatness-audit}$$ Within this two-exponent transfer ledger, the two recorded bounds imply the TPC-32 condition $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)\le X^{1/400+o(1)} \label{eq:tpc32-threshold-audit}$$ whenever $$\boxed{2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D.} \label{eq:zero-slack-audit}$$ Strict inequality supplies a fixed-power reserve. ## Two ledgers, not two copies of the margin

The $1/400$ in [\[eq:critical-flatness-audit\]](TPC-082.md#ref-4c0d51cd9cb7341c) is the geometric baseline $Q_X/J_X^2$ and the TPC-32 flatness endpoint [`\citep{WangTPC32}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-49ad82103eb75eb7). It is not a free allowance that can be spent again. Separately, the complete physical route contains activation, represented-frame, missing-mass, native-operator, nuisance-shorting, dictionary, cover, endpoint, and calibration losses. Every fixed-power loss must be derived in the same literal carrier and quantifier scope. This is the strict route rule inherited from the TPC-72 dependency audit [`\citep{WangTPC72}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-d21b73d8ae01e89b).

> **Proposition: Strict physical loss rule** \[prop:strict-loss-rule\] Let $\Lambda_{\rm phys}$ be the sum of all fixed-power exponent losses actually used in the full physical comparison, after repeated terms have been identified and open terms have been left open. # Two exact nonidentifiability counterexamples

###### ref-3b1fce4c8e7a3248

\[ex:unsigned-zero\] Let $N$ be even and define vectors in $\mathbb C^N$ by $$a_n=1,\qquad b_n=(-1)^n,\qquad 1\le n\le N.$$ They have identical support, identical entrywise absolute values, and identical energy: $$\operatorname{supp}a=\operatorname{supp}b,\qquad |a_n|=|b_n|=1,\qquad  \|a\|_2^2=\|b\|_2^2=N.$$ Nevertheless, $$\left|\sum_{n=1}^Na_n\right|=N,\qquad  \sum_{n=1}^Nb_n=0.$$

###### ref-5e19136dd78fde84

> **Proposition: First nonidentifiability consequence** \[prop:unsigned-nonidentifiability\] Support, absolute coefficient data, total energy, and any completion cost or nonnegative Perron envelope determined solely by those unsigned data do not individually or jointly determine the distinguished zero mode.

> **Proof** Every listed unsigned datum is identical for the two vectors in [\[ex:unsigned-zero\]](TPC-082.md#ref-3b1fce4c8e7a3248), while the zero modes have maximal and zero magnitude, respectively.

###### ref-166b7329240f2e2d

\[ex:bin-cancellation\] Let the raw space be $\mathbb C^2$, let the determinant space be $\mathbb C$, and let $$\mathcal J(u_1,u_2)=u_1+u_2.$$ For $$u^+=(1,1),\qquad u^-=(1,-1),$$ the raw unsigned data and raw energies agree: $$|u^+|=|u^-|=(1,1),\qquad  \|u^+\|_2^2=\|u^-\|_2^2=2.$$ After binning, $$\|\mathcal Ju^+\|_2^2=4,\qquad  \|\mathcal Ju^-\|_2^2=0.$$

###### ref-481586fb80d77b27

> **Proposition: Second nonidentifiability consequence** \[prop:raw-bin-nonidentifiability\] A positive raw atomic energy, even together with exact unsigned support and multiplicity, does not imply a positive determinant-bin energy. Such an implication requires a lower modulus for the literal binning map on a verified subspace, or an equivalent range-geometry estimate.

> **Proof** has identical positive raw energy in both cases, while the second vector lies in $\operatorname{Ker}\mathcal J$. A lower modulus as in [\[eq:lower-modulus-audit\]](TPC-082.md#ref-74bb2ed61fa96803) excludes precisely this possibility on the verified range.

> **Remark: Sharp scope of the examples** These examples do not show that the actual fixed-$h_0$ coefficient has large zero mode or vanishing determinant energy. They prove that the invariants used before TPC-81 are insufficient to decide those questions. Attaching an example to the literal physical map would upgrade it from an $\mathrm{L0}$ nonidentifiability theorem to an $\mathrm{L1}$ negative diagnostic.

# The next three routes

###### ref-27352edc00b41ea4

The audit reduces the next block to two positive inputs and one negative-transfer alternative: $$\boxed{ \begin{gathered} \text{Route I: determinant-energy survival}\\ \text{Route II: distinguished-zero-mode cancellation}\\ \text{Route III: exact nonidentifiability or no-go theorem.} \end{gathered}} \label{eq:three-routes}$$ Routes I and II can proceed in parallel, but their exponents meet only through [\[eq:zero-slack-audit\]](TPC-082.md#ref-649b047797b927bb). Route III becomes appropriate when a branch-specific obstruction is proved or both positive routes are shown to require the same unresolved arithmetic input.

## Route I: the literal dictionary and energy survival

###### ref-ef158f89ef006a0f

The target is $$D_X\ge X^{-o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2, \label{eq:routeI-natural-energy}$$ or, after an independent zero-mode gain, the more general inequality $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$. There are two nonexclusive subroutes.

#### Route I-A: completion, reassembly, and dictionary.

This certificate route has six ordered tasks.

1.  Generate a complete literal manifest at every audited scale, retaining the fixed $h_0$, all native keys, actual support, literal phases, and the global raw normalization.

2.  Construct $\mathcal J_X$ from the exact determinant label and archive every multiplier and provenance contribution in each fiber.

3.  Run the TPC-77 component census on the actual nonzero incidence graph. Use TPC-78 forest, one-defect, or motif formulas only on components whose literal graph satisfies the corresponding hypotheses.

4.  Use TPC-79 only when the anchor and direct variables are separable syntheses into the same target with the physical costs. Otherwise delete the hybrid branch and keep the direct route.

5.  Use TPC-80 only on an actual scalar row graph. Audit both sectors, and record exact switching equality rather than interpreting it as cancellation.

6.  Prove a positive pre-bin survival factor, a lower modulus $s_X$, and residual separation strong enough that [\[eq:dictionary-energy-audit\]](TPC-082.md#ref-c73023b4fd3189b5) yields the desired $\lambda_D$.

#### Route I-B: direct determinant-energy analysis.

Starting from the already defined literal coefficient $a_X=(A_{C_X}(n))_n$, expand or estimate $$D_X=\sum_n|A_{C_X}(n)|^2$$ directly. This route does not logically require a completion manifest, a pre-bin carrier, or a lower-modulus argument. It must still preserve the fixed $h_0$, the complete definition of $A_{C_X}(n)$, the actual post-aggregation support, and the global normalization. The exact expansion should separate manifestly nonnegative diagonal mass from signed equal-determinant cross-correlations. If a natural-scale lower bound is equivalent to an unresolved fixed-shift Chowla-like correlation, that equivalence is a valid Route-III negative-transfer theorem rather than a proof of energy survival.

###### ref-1a8e5a849189856e

> **Proposition: Route-I branch rules** \[prop:routeI-rules\] The following conclusions are local to their branch.
>
> 1.  Different targets or coupled variables stop the TPC-79 formula, but do not disprove direct synthesis.
>
> 2.  A nonforest component stops the forest shortcut, but leaves the general component SOCP and dual program available.
>
> 3.  A tree, or exact switching equality in a dominant scalar component, stops phase-frustration saving on that component, but does not decide amplitude completion or determinant cancellation.
>
> 4.  A dual lower bound exceeding the allowed cost disproves only the declared completion class and normalization.
>
> 5.  If the currently available Route-I-A certificate yields only $\lambda_D^{\rm cert}>0$ while $\eta_Z=0$, that certificate cannot close the present endpoint transfer. A sharper certificate or a direct Route-I-B estimate remains available. Only a proof that the literal carrier necessarily incurs positive loss is a branch obstruction.

> **Proof** Items (a)–(d) follow from the hypotheses and targets of \[[prop:tpc79-admissibility](TPC-082.md#ref-cd53cfb6f1fd14d2),[cor:tree-two-sector](TPC-082.md#ref-463778c72f864d2f),[thm:no-teleportation](TPC-082.md#ref-a59b18d4b34e2798)\]. Item (e) follows from [\[cor:natural-energy-audit\]](TPC-082.md#ref-cc22366325bb058e) together with the branch-locality principle: failure of one sufficient certificate does not prove failure of a sharper lower bound.

## Route II: the distinguished zero mode

###### ref-24009bd43f5bd657

The target is an estimate $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2, \qquad  2\eta_Z\ge\lambda_D, \label{eq:routeII-target}$$ with strict inequality preferred because it supplies reserve. A valid input must concern:

1.  the same prescribed $h_0\ne0$;

2.  the actual matched coefficient $A_{C_X}(n)$;

3.  the distinguished frequency $r=0$, not merely generic $r\ne0$;

4.  the complete physical block and the original normalization; and

5.  the original uniformity, with no unreturned averaging over shift, frequency, slope, modulus, or carrier family.

###### ref-716ea040c7421064

> **Proposition: Results that do not close Route II** \[prop:not-routeII\] None of the following, without a proved localization or transfer back to the literal packet, establishes [\[eq:routeII-target\]](TPC-082.md#ref-24009bd43f5bd657):
>
> 1.  an all-but-exceptional-frequency estimate;
>
> 2.  an average over $h$, slopes, moduli, or frozen families;
>
> 3.  squarefree-support density or an absolute-value Perron bound;
>
> 4.  cancellation on one fiber followed by absolute summation in an outer variable; or
>
> 5.  a nonzero-frequency dispersion theorem.

> **Proof** Each item changes one of (Z1)–(Z5) or discards the sign interaction defining $Z_X$. It therefore does not bound the statistic in [\[eq:routeII-target\]](TPC-082.md#ref-24009bd43f5bd657).

Expanding $D_X$ or $Z_X$ may expose a fixed-shift two-affine Möbius correlation. If the desired bound is shown to be equivalent to a currently unproved Chowla-type estimate, that estimate must be listed as a new arithmetic hypothesis. Averaged Chowla results do not automatically give the required literal form [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015,Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-b5a8969fcb0fe4a8); [\[Tao2016LogChowla\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07).

## Route III: a negative transfer theorem

If Routes I and II both reduce to the same unresolved signed correlation, continuing to rename the input creates no progress. An honest alternative is a paper of the form

> *Why Completion Geometry Does Not Control the Distinguished Frequency: Nonidentifiability and Stop Theorems for a Fixed-Shift Transfer Architecture.*

Its rigorous content can include:

1.  identical support, absolute coefficients, and energy with different zero modes;

2.  complete loss of raw energy under determinant binning;

3.  failure of anchor–direct composition without a common target;

4.  absence of scalar phase saving on trees or saturated sectors;

5.  failure of scalar Perron strictness to imply determinant flatness; and

6.  exact branch-specific dual obstructions for independent, provenance, hybrid, or holonomy models.

Such a result is an $\mathrm{L0}$ no-go theorem. It becomes an $\mathrm{L1}$ negative diagnostic only when its countermodel or dual witness is attached to the literal fixed-$h_0$ interface. It is not a proof of the general sieve parity barrier and does not disprove a twin-prime strategy outside the declared architecture.

## Operational decision tree

###### ref-ccf13b05ca3de511

$$\begin{aligned} &\text{choose a determinant-energy branch:}\\ &\quad\begin{cases} \text{I-A:} & \text{build the literal manifest and \(\mathcal J_X\), then estimate \(s_X,\delta_{\rm reasm},\Gamma_{\rm pre},r_X\);}\\ \text{I-B:} & \text{expand the literal \(D_X\) directly and isolate its signed cross-correlations.} \end{cases}\\[0.4em] &\text{a physical determinant-energy exponent \(\lambda_D\) known?}\\ &\quad\begin{cases} \text{no:} & \text{continue Route I or identify the signed correlation obstruction;}\\ \text{yes:}& \text{attack the same coefficient's \(Z_X\) in Route II.} \end{cases}\\[0.4em] &\text{compare the two exponents:}\\ &\quad\begin{cases} 2\eta_Z>\lambda_D:& \text{positive reserve; audit the full physical loss ledger;}\\ 2\eta_Z=\lambda_D:& \text{flatness threshold only; no extra loss inside this flatness transfer;}\\ 2\eta_Z<\lambda_D:& \text{stop the current TPC-32 endpoint route.} \end{cases} \end{aligned} \label{eq:operational-tree}$$

Even the first case in the last comparison reaches only the conditional matched-shell theorem. A full-block conclusion still requires the exact cover and remainder control isolated in TPC-18 [`\citep{WangTPC18}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-4abf939102bd1649).

# Unified hard stop rules

The following rules apply before any claim is promoted.

1.  If the shift is averaged, optimized, or replaced by a moving $h$, stop the fixed-$h_0$ claim.

2.  If a native key is deleted, actual support is replaced by ambient support, or the raw normalizer is recomputed locally, stop the $\mathrm{L1}$ transfer.

3.  If an observation, trace, or four-corner scalar is treated as a synthesis vector without a proved section into the same target, stop the TPC-79 combination.

4.  If a block or operator-valued carrier is treated as a scalar Hermitian graph, stop the TPC-80 application.

5.  If a numerical solver fails to improve a value but supplies no verified dual witness or exact normer, do not call the outcome an obstruction.

6.  When transferring raw or pre-bin energy, if it has not passed through the literal $\mathcal J_X$ and a verified lower modulus or residual estimate, do not call it determinant energy. This does not prohibit a direct arithmetic expansion or estimate of $D_X$.

7.  If a dispersion estimate excludes or averages the distinguished frequency, do not call it zero-frequency control.

8.  If $2\eta_Z<\lambda_D$, stop the present TPC-32 endpoint transfer.

9.  If the complete physical fixed-power *loss* is $\ge1/400$, stop; equality also stops.

10. If the TPC-18 full-block cover and its uniform remainder bounds are unverified, do not infer a fixed-shift Hardy–Littlewood, prime-pair, or twin-prime conclusion.

###### ref-1d11b3636ef8e102

> **Proposition: Scope of a stop certificate** \[prop:scope-stop\] A stop rule invalidates only the claim or branch named in that rule. It does not prove that every representation, every analytic method, or the underlying number-theoretic conjecture is impossible.

> **Proof** Each rule identifies a missing hypothesis of a specified implication. Failure of that hypothesis blocks the implication, not all possible implications with different hypotheses.

> **Remark: Equality conventions** There are two equalities with different logical roles. Equality $2\eta_Z=\lambda_D$ reaches the conditional flatness threshold but has zero fixed-power robustness. Equality $\Lambda_{\rm phys}=1/400$ fails the strict full physical loss rule and therefore stops. Neither equality may be described as spare margin.

> **Remark: Evidence archive** A future literal census should archive the native manifest, the post-aggregation nonzero graph, every connected component, the primal and dual certificate residuals, the scalarization proof where used, all determinant fibers, the lower-modulus subspace, and the global raw normalizer. Numerical evidence is useful for choosing a branch, but promotion requires exact or interval-certified bounds uniform in the stated asymptotic range.

#### Explicit exclusions.

This paper does not prove determinant-energy survival, distinguished-zero-frequency cancellation, a fixed-shift Chowla estimate, a breach of sieve parity, a fixed-shift Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, a prime-pair lower bound, or the twin-prime conjecture. It does not specialize its conclusions to $h_0=2$. The parity problem remains a genuine arithmetic boundary, not a conclusion of the present finite-dimensional analysis [`\citep{HeathBrown1982Parity,IwaniecKowalski2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-bd33f7374ef1e88f); [\[IwaniecKowalski2004\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-082.md#cite-49dfea87eaada156).

#### Operational conclusion.

Route I is worth continuing while either the literal dictionary or a direct determinant-energy analysis can preserve natural-scale energy, or while Route II supplies enough compensating zero-mode gain. Route II counts only at the actual distinguished frequency and fixed $h_0$. If both routes are shown to require the same unresolved Chowla-like correlation, Route III is a mathematically complete negative-transfer outcome. In every case, the complete fixed-power loss remains subject to the strict rule $$\Lambda_{\rm phys}<\frac1{400};  \qquad  \Lambda_{\rm phys}\ge\frac1{400}  \ \Longrightarrow\ \text{stop},$$ with equality included in the stopping case.
