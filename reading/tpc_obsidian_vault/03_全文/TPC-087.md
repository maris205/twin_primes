## Abstract

Let $$S_N=\sum_{n\le N}a_n,\qquad  L_N=\sum_{n\le N}\frac{a_n}{n},\qquad L_0=0.$$ We isolate the exact discrete Tauberian interface between logarithmic and ordinary cancellation. Summation by parts gives the identity $$\boxed{\quad  S_N=NL_N-\sum_{m<N}L_m,\qquad  \frac{S_N}{N}  =L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m.  \quad}$$ Consequently ordinary cancellation is equivalent, with identical rate, to the vanishing of the *signed backward Cesàro defect* of the unnormalized logarithmic primitive $L$. Convergence of $L_N$ is sufficient by Cesàro convergence, but boundedness is not. Indeed, for $$a_n=(-1)^k\qquad(2^k\le n<2^{k+1}),$$ one has $|a_n|=1$, $L_N=O(1)$, and even $$\frac{1}{\log\omega(x)}  \sum_{x/\omega(x)<n\le x}\frac{a_n}{n}\longrightarrow0$$ for every $1\le\omega(x)\le x$ with $\omega(x)\to\infty$, whereas $S_N/N$ has subsequential limits $1/3$ and $-1/3$. Thus normalized logarithmic cancellation alone has no structure-free implication for the ordinary mean.

We then give quantitative positive criteria. The absolute Cesàro oscillation dominates $|S_N|/N$. If $$\omega_N(L):=  \max_{\lfloor N/2\rfloor\le m\le N}|L_N-L_m| \longrightarrow0,$$ then $S_N/N\to0$; a power bound $\omega_N(L)\ll N^{-\eta}$, $0<\eta<1$, transfers with the same power. A second criterion uses the dyadic logarithmic variation $$V_k=\sum_{2^k\le n<2^{k+1}}\frac{|a_n|}{n}.$$ We prove an explicit geometrically weighted convolution bound, hence $V_k\to0$ also suffices.

Finally, we audit what these identities do and do not supply for the literal growing fixed-$h_0$ affine Möbius packet. The exact Tauberian statements and countermodel are $\mathrm{L0}$. Their faithful attachment to every physical fiber and the complete outer reassembly is an $\mathrm{L1}$ certificate. Establishing the required uniform terminal cancellation for the actual growing affine family is an unproved $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic problem. No parity breach, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime conclusion is claimed.

# Introduction

Logarithmic averaging is often substantially more accessible than ordinary averaging in multiplicative number theory. In particular, Tao proved logarithmically averaged two-point Chowla and Elliott theorems for fixed affine forms [`\citep{Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07). The literal fixed-shift route considered in TPC-32 and TPC-33, however, returns ordinary sums over a growing family of affine columns, with a prescribed nonzero determinant $h_0$, actual masks, short fibers, and a complete outer reassembly [`\citep{WangTPC32,WangTPC33}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-49ad82103eb75eb7); [\[WangTPC33\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-3dc89b1d29e24761).

TPC-85 exhibited a bounded dyadic sequence showing that no structure-free black box simply deletes the logarithmic weight [`\citep{WangTPC85}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-d8d9f98f6854badf). The purpose of the present paper is to sharpen that obstruction into an exact theorem:

> the missing datum is the signed Cesàro defect of the *unnormalized* logarithmic primitive.

This distinction matters. If $$H_N=\sum_{n\le N}\frac1n,\qquad  \lambda_N=\frac1{H_N}\sum_{n\le N}\frac{a_n}{n},$$ then $\lambda_N$ is the normalized logarithmic mean, while $$L_N=H_N\lambda_N$$ is the unnormalized logarithmic primitive. The exact discrete Abel identity is written in terms of $L_N$, not $\lambda_N$.

TPC-86 develops a different positive interface: coherent Fourier reassembly of the distinguished zero mode from a controlled low-frequency window [`\citep{WangTPC86}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-41b0792996942846). The present paper does not replace that route. It audits the separate possibility of obtaining ordinary affine cancellation from logarithmically averaged correlation information.

## Main results

###### ref-37445ff0b05b652e

For an arbitrary complex sequence $a=(a_n)$, define $$S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Na_n,\qquad  L_N=\sum_{n=1}^N\frac{a_n}{n},\qquad S_0=L_0=0.$$ Our first result is the pointwise identity $$\frac{S_N}{N}  =L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_m  =\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}(L_N-L_m). \label{eq:intro-abel}$$ Therefore $S_N/N\to0$ if and only if the signed quantity on the right tends to zero. This is not merely an implication: the two objects are equal for every $N$, so there is no hidden exponent loss.

Convergence $L_N\to\ell$ is sufficient. Boundedness $L_N=O(1)$ is sharply insufficient. The dyadic block sequence $$a_n=(-1)^{\lfloor\log_2n\rfloor}$$ has a bounded logarithmic primitive and normalized logarithmic mean $\lambda_N\to0$, yet its natural means oscillate between $\pm1/3$ along complete dyadic blocks. More strongly, every terminal multiplicative interval has uniformly bounded logarithmic sum, so every Tao-shaped normalized window with $\omega(x)\to\infty$ has cancellation.

The exact criterion also identifies useful additional hypotheses. We prove:

1.  an absolute Cesàro oscillation criterion;

2.  a terminal-half local criterion $$\max_{N/2\le m\le N}   \left|\sum_{m<n\le N}\frac{a_n}{n}\right|\to0;$$

3.  a rate-preserving version for fixed powers below one;

4.  an explicit dyadic variation convolution bound.

These are genuine Tauberian inputs: none follows from boundedness of the global logarithmic primitive alone.

## Arithmetic status

The results have three proof levels.

1.  finite discrete identities, quantitative inequalities, and structure-free countermodels;

2.  a literal transfer certificate retaining the same fixed $h_0$, growing affine coefficients, actual fibers, masks, outer reassembly, and global normalization;

3.  a new uniform Möbius-correlation estimate for that actual growing family.

This paper proves $\mathrm{L0}$ results and formulates the $\mathrm{L1}$ certificate. It proves no $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate. In particular, the dyadic sequence is not multiplicative and does not obstruct a proof that uses Möbius-specific structure.

# The exact discrete Abel interface

No boundedness, multiplicativity, or reality assumption is required in this section.

###### ref-22bea2617ae6c28c

> **Definition: Three means** \[def:three-means\] For $a_n\in\mathbb C$, put $$S_0=L_0=0,\qquad  S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Na_n,\qquad  L_N=\sum_{n=1}^N\frac{a_n}{n}.$$ Also put $$H_N=\sum_{n=1}^N\frac1n,\qquad  \lambda_N=\frac{L_N}{H_N}.$$ Thus $S_N/N$ is the ordinary mean, $L_N$ is the unnormalized logarithmic primitive, and $\lambda_N$ is the normalized logarithmic mean.

###### ref-efde1fd886a6a6d1

The increment of the logarithmic primitive is $$\Delta L_n:=L_n-L_{n-1}=\frac{a_n}{n}. \label{eq:delta-L}$$

###### ref-d83646c9065cd435

###### ref-6232c826a57d8499

###### ref-b44512c65dc70ad9

###### ref-5e5a5a775d57552a

> **Theorem: Exact discrete Abel identities** \[thm:exact-abel\] For every $N\ge1$, $$\begin{aligned}  S_N  &=\sum_{n=1}^Nn\Delta L_n  =NL_N-\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_m, \label{eq:abel-forward}\\  \frac{S_N}{N}  &=L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_m  =\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}(L_N-L_m). \label{eq:abel-defect}\end{aligned}$$ Conversely, $$L_N=\frac{S_N}{N}  +\sum_{n=1}^{N-1}\frac{S_n}{n(n+1)}. \label{eq:abel-reverse}$$

> **Proof** By [\[eq:delta-L\]](TPC-087.md#ref-efde1fd886a6a6d1), $$\sum_{n=1}^Nn\Delta L_n  =\sum_{n=1}^NnL_n-\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}(m+1)L_m  =NL_N-\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_m.$$ The left side is $S_N$, proving [\[eq:abel-forward\]](TPC-087.md#ref-6232c826a57d8499). Dividing by $N$ and rearranging gives [\[eq:abel-defect\]](TPC-087.md#ref-b44512c65dc70ad9). For the reverse identity, write $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$ and sum by parts: $$\sum_{n=1}^N\frac{S_n-S_{n-1}}n  =\frac{S_N}{N}  +\sum_{n=1}^{N-1}S_n  \left(\frac1n-\frac1{n+1}\right).$$

###### ref-1babf3325267d8ff

###### ref-203d2dd6a150d178

> **Corollary: Exact normalized formulation** \[cor:normalized-formulation\] With the notation of [\[def:three-means\]](TPC-087.md#ref-22bea2617ae6c28c), $$\frac{S_N}{N}  =H_N\lambda_N  -\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}H_m\lambda_m, \qquad H_0\lambda_0:=0. \label{eq:normalized-defect}$$

> **Proof** Substitute $L_m=H_m\lambda_m$ into [\[eq:abel-defect\]](TPC-087.md#ref-b44512c65dc70ad9).

###### ref-fb4b826dc6d04ab5

> **Remark: Normalization is not cosmetic** \[rem:normalization-not-cosmetic\] The right side of [\[eq:normalized-defect\]](TPC-087.md#ref-203d2dd6a150d178) is not $\lambda_N$ minus the Cesàro mean of $\lambda$. The harmonic factor $H_m$ remains inside both terms. Therefore $\lambda_N\to0$ alone does not imply ordinary cancellation. gives a bounded-coefficient example.

###### ref-26fa2e0c00cc659d

> **Remark: Indexing** \[rem:indexing\] The backward average in [\[eq:abel-defect\]](TPC-087.md#ref-b44512c65dc70ad9) has denominator $N$ and contains $L_0,\ldots,L_{N-1}$, but not $L_N$. Replacing it by the conventional average $N^{-1}\sum_{m=1}^NL_m$ changes the identity by $L_N/N$, which cannot be discarded without an additional growth hypothesis.

# The necessary-and-sufficient Cesàro oscillation criterion

###### ref-1d83eee02b0cd094

###### ref-ba9edf9843cb072e

###### ref-29bd063f92910556

> **Definition: Signed defect and absolute oscillation** \[def:defect-oscillation\] For a sequence $L=(L_N)_{N\ge0}$, define $$\begin{aligned}  \mathcal T_N(L)  &:=L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_m, \label{eq:signed-defect}\\  \mathcal A_N(L)  &:=\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}|L_N-L_m|. \label{eq:absolute-oscillation}\end{aligned}$$ We call $\mathcal T_N$ the signed backward Cesàro defect and $\mathcal A_N$ the absolute backward Cesàro oscillation.

###### ref-d73053517adcad1b

###### ref-04fd2c9396b4dea2

> **Theorem: Sharp discrete Tauberian criterion** \[thm:sharp-criterion\] For every complex sequence $a$, $$\boxed{\qquad  \frac{S_N}{N}=\mathcal T_N(L).  \qquad} \label{eq:sharp-criterion}$$ Consequently, $$\frac{S_N}{N}\longrightarrow0  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  \mathcal T_N(L)\longrightarrow0,$$ and any quantitative rate on one side is exactly the same rate on the other.

> **Proof** This is [\[eq:abel-defect\]](TPC-087.md#ref-b44512c65dc70ad9).

###### ref-06f61382332dfe9e

###### ref-3094827412aca611

> **Corollary: Absolute oscillation is sufficient** \[cor:absolute-sufficient\] For every $N\ge1$, $$\frac{|S_N|}{N}\le\mathcal A_N(L). \label{eq:absolute-dominates}$$ In particular, $\mathcal A_N(L)\to0$ implies $S_N/N\to0$.

> **Proof** Take absolute values in the last expression in [\[eq:abel-defect\]](TPC-087.md#ref-b44512c65dc70ad9).

###### ref-eeac891b81dcf2cb

> **Remark: Why the signed adjective is essential** \[rem:signed-essential\] The necessary-and-sufficient object is $\mathcal T_N$, not $\mathcal A_N$. Cancellation among the differences $L_N-L_m$ can make $\mathcal T_N$ small while their absolute mean is not. Thus $\mathcal A_N\to0$ is a useful stronger hypothesis, but it is not asserted to be necessary.

###### ref-a0656299e670c2d3

###### ref-d59c93ed6712ceca

###### ref-19f0c1128ac22c5a

> **Corollary: Convergent logarithmic primitive** \[cor:convergent-primitive\] If $L_N\to\ell\in\mathbb C$, then $S_N/N\to0$. More precisely, with $e_m=|L_m-\ell|$, $$\frac{|S_N|}{N}  \le e_N+\frac1N\sum_{m=1}^{N-1}e_m+\frac{|\ell|}{N}. \label{eq:convergence-rate}$$ If $$\varepsilon_M=\sup_{n\ge M}|L_n-\ell|,$$ then, for $N>M$, $$\frac{|S_N|}{N}  \le2\varepsilon_M+  \frac{|\ell|+\sum_{m=1}^{M-1}|L_m-\ell|}{N}. \label{eq:tail-convergence-rate}$$

> **Proof** Subtract $\ell$ in both terms of [\[eq:abel-defect\]](TPC-087.md#ref-b44512c65dc70ad9) and use the triangle inequality. This proves [\[eq:convergence-rate\]](TPC-087.md#ref-d59c93ed6712ceca). For [\[eq:tail-convergence-rate\]](TPC-087.md#ref-19f0c1128ac22c5a), split the backward average at $M$. The two tail contributions are bounded by $\varepsilon_M$, while the finite prefix has the displayed cost.

###### ref-ac795cb0412a19b9

> **Remark: Convergence is sufficient, not necessary** \[rem:convergence-not-necessary\] Let $a_1=0$ and $a_n=1/\log n$ for $n\ge2$. Since $a_n\to0$, Cesàro convergence gives $S_N/N\to0$, whereas $$L_N=\sum_{2\le n\le N}\frac1{n\log n}  =\log\log N+O(1)$$ diverges. Thus the exact criterion is strictly weaker than convergence of $L_N$.

> **Remark: Classical terminology** The implication in [\[cor:convergent-primitive\]](TPC-087.md#ref-a0656299e670c2d3) is the elementary discrete Kronecker lemma. We prove it here because the exact defect and its normalization, rather than the name of the lemma, are the relevant physical interface; see also [`\citet{Hardy1949}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-f4c476b1a685d0cc).

# Quantitative local and variation hypotheses

The exact criterion $\mathcal T_N(L)\to0$ can be difficult to verify directly. This section gives two stronger but local conditions with explicit costs.

## Terminal-half logarithmic cancellation

###### ref-42d3464601ff9035

###### ref-910a3d133062e4fa

> **Definition: Terminal-half modulus** \[def:terminal-modulus\] For $N\ge2$, put $$\omega_N(L):=  \max_{\lfloor N/2\rfloor\le m\le N}|L_N-L_m|  =  \max_{\lfloor N/2\rfloor\le m\le N}  \left|\sum_{m<n\le N}\frac{a_n}{n}\right|. \label{eq:terminal-modulus}$$

###### ref-40c80f4faf223236

###### ref-62ea6e92424f08e9

> **Lemma: One-step localization** \[lem:one-step-localization\] Let $M=\lfloor N/2\rfloor$. Then $$\mathcal A_N(L)\le  \omega_N(L)+\frac{M}{N}\mathcal A_M(L). \label{eq:one-step-localization}$$

> **Proof** For $m<M$, $$|L_N-L_m|  \le |L_N-L_M|+|L_M-L_m|  \le\omega_N(L)+|L_M-L_m|.$$ For $M\le m<N$, the first term alone is at most $\omega_N(L)$. Summing both ranges and dividing by $N$ gives [\[eq:one-step-localization\]](TPC-087.md#ref-62ea6e92424f08e9).

###### ref-592195b68b38d0e5

###### ref-f4805d257036eea7

###### ref-ecd3d0c0335f52e5

> **Theorem: Geometric local-to-global transfer** \[thm:local-to-global\] Let $N_0=N$ and $N_{j+1}=\lfloor N_j/2\rfloor$, stopping when $N_J=1$. Then $$\frac{|S_N|}{N}  \le\mathcal A_N(L)  \le  \sum_{j=0}^{J-1}\frac{N_j}{N}\omega_{N_j}(L)  +\frac{\mathcal A_1(L)}{N}. \label{eq:geometric-local-global}$$ In particular, $$\omega_N(L)\longrightarrow0  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad  \frac{S_N}{N}\longrightarrow0. \label{eq:slow-oscillation-sufficient}$$

> **Proof** Iterate [\[eq:one-step-localization\]](TPC-087.md#ref-62ea6e92424f08e9). The product of the successive floor ratios telescopes to $N_j/N$, giving [\[eq:geometric-local-global\]](TPC-087.md#ref-f4805d257036eea7). Also $N_j/N\le2^{-j}$.
>
> To prove the last assertion, fix $\varepsilon>0$ and choose $R$ so that $\omega_m(L)\le\varepsilon$ for $m\ge R$. The terms with $N_j\ge R$ contribute at most $2\varepsilon$. The remaining terms involve only finitely many moduli below $R$; their numerators are bounded independently of $N$, and their total coefficient is $O_R(N^{-1})$. Now let $N\to\infty$, then $\varepsilon\to0$.

###### ref-1f6df8c772194351

###### ref-d953312e6e3d78fe

> **Corollary: Fixed-power transfer** \[cor:power-transfer\] Suppose $0<\eta<1$ and $$\omega_N(L)\le C N^{-\eta}\qquad(N\ge2).$$ Then $$\frac{|S_N|}{N}  \le  \frac{C}{1-2^{-(1-\eta)}}N^{-\eta}  +O_L\left(\frac1N\right). \label{eq:power-transfer}$$ Thus the local-to-global conversion loses no fixed power below one.

> **Proof** In [\[eq:geometric-local-global\]](TPC-087.md#ref-f4805d257036eea7), use $$\frac{N_j}{N}\omega_{N_j}(L)  \le \frac{C}{N}N_j^{1-\eta}  \le C N^{-\eta}2^{-j(1-\eta)}$$ and sum the geometric series.

###### ref-97d05e424919ea80

> **Remark: The missing fixed-ratio datum** \[rem:fixed-ratio-datum\] A theorem on windows $(x/\Omega(x),x]$ with $\Omega(x)\to\infty$ does not automatically give $\omega_N(L)\to0$, which is a fixed-ratio window with ratio $2+O(N^{-1})$ after endpoint rounding, and an $o(1)$, rather than $o(\log\Omega)$, target. This is the precise local Tauberian datum exposed by [\[thm:local-to-global\]](TPC-087.md#ref-592195b68b38d0e5).

## Dyadic logarithmic variation

###### ref-46d79046ecb88c5d

###### ref-cf3a77e77e665b05

> **Definition: Dyadic variation** \[def:dyadic-variation\] For $k\ge0$, define $$V_k(L):=  \sum_{2^k\le n<2^{k+1}}|\Delta L_n|  =\sum_{2^k\le n<2^{k+1}}\frac{|a_n|}{n}. \label{eq:dyadic-variation}$$

###### ref-9c9046d011f83483

###### ref-3158e5518dd857c4

###### ref-742636a100b9ca17

> **Theorem: Variation convolution bound** \[thm:variation-convolution\] If $2^K\le N<2^{K+1}$, then $$\boxed{\quad  \frac{|S_N|}{N}  \le  V_K(L)+  \sum_{k=0}^{K-1}2^{k+1-K}V_k(L)  =  V_K(L)+2\sum_{j=1}^{K}2^{-j}V_{K-j}(L).  \quad} \label{eq:variation-convolution}$$ Consequently, $$V_k(L)\longrightarrow0  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad  \frac{S_N}{N}\longrightarrow0. \label{eq:variation-sufficient}$$

> **Proof** Use $S_N=\sum_{n\le N}n\Delta L_n$. On a completed block $2^k\le n<2^{k+1}$ with $k<K$, $$\frac{n}{N}\le2^{k+1-K}.$$ On the terminal block $n/N\le1$. Taking absolute values block by block proves [\[eq:variation-convolution\]](TPC-087.md#ref-3158e5518dd857c4).
>
> For the final assertion, the last expression in [\[eq:variation-convolution\]](TPC-087.md#ref-3158e5518dd857c4) is a geometric convolution. Split it into the finitely many recent terms, where $V_{K-j}\to0$, and the geometrically small tail. The finitely many initial values are multiplied by $O(2^{-K})$.

###### ref-fbb2c974d0459388

> **Corollary: Variation power rate** \[cor:variation-power\] If $0<\eta<1$ and $V_k(L)\le C2^{-\eta k}$, then $$\frac{|S_N|}{N}\ll_{\eta,C}N^{-\eta}.$$

> **Proof** Insert the hypothesis into [\[eq:variation-convolution\]](TPC-087.md#ref-3158e5518dd857c4) and sum $\sum_{j\ge1}2^{-j}2^{\eta j}$.

###### ref-bbbe6d45912ee8fa

> **Remark: Strength of the variation condition** \[rem:variation-strong\] The condition $V_k\to0$ is an absolute-variation hypothesis and is stronger than signed terminal cancellation. It is useful because it is representation-stable and quantitative, not because it is expected automatically for Möbius signs. The dyadic counterexample below has $V_k\asymp1$.

# A sharp bounded dyadic counterexample

###### ref-0ef511b3c8ce7f91

###### ref-8b7fb0001340117d

> **Definition: Alternating dyadic blocks** \[def:dyadic-sequence\] For $n\ge1$, define $$a_n=(-1)^k\qquad\text{when }2^k\le n<2^{k+1}. \label{eq:dyadic-sequence}$$ Thus $|a_n|=1$.

###### ref-ce994c81cf78db4e

###### ref-3db2ca274886e2df

Write $$h_k:=\sum_{n=2^k}^{2^{k+1}-1}\frac1n. \label{eq:dyadic-harmonic-mass}$$ Integral comparison gives $$\log2\le h_k<\log2+2^{-k}. \label{eq:dyadic-harmonic-estimate}$$

###### ref-60b4c2908bf17f74

###### ref-0284f0a90c569ab4

###### ref-b4b37aa594feff7e

> **Theorem: Bounded logarithmic primitive, oscillating natural mean** \[thm:dyadic-counterexample\] For the sequence [\[eq:dyadic-sequence\]](TPC-087.md#ref-8b7fb0001340117d):
>
> 1.  $L_N=O(1)$;
>
> 2.  $\lambda_N=L_N/H_N\to0$;
>
> 3.  for $N_K=2^{K+1}-1$, $$S_{N_K}=\frac{1-(-2)^{K+1}}3,  \label{eq:dyadic-natural-sum}$$ and hence $$\frac{S_{N_K}}{N_K}\longrightarrow   \begin{cases}    1/3,&K\ \text{even},\\    -1/3,&K\ \text{odd}.   \end{cases}  \label{eq:dyadic-natural-limits}$$
>
> In particular, boundedness of $L_N$, and even convergence of the normalized logarithmic mean to zero, does not imply ordinary cancellation.

> **Proof** If $2^K\le N<2^{K+1}$, then $$L_N=  \sum_{k=0}^{K-1}(-1)^kh_k  +(-1)^K\sum_{n=2^K}^{N}\frac1n.$$ Write $h_k=\log2+e_k$, where $0\le e_k<2^{-k}$. The alternating sum of the constant $\log2$ terms is bounded, the error series is absolutely summable, and the final partial block has bounded harmonic mass. This proves $L_N=O(1)$. Since $H_N\to\infty$, it also proves $\lambda_N\to0$.
>
> At the right endpoint of the $K$-th complete block, $$S_{N_K}=\sum_{k=0}^K(-1)^k2^k  =\frac{1-(-2)^{K+1}}3.$$ Dividing by $2^{K+1}-1$ gives [\[eq:dyadic-natural-limits\]](TPC-087.md#ref-b4b37aa594feff7e).

###### ref-c9cf255f813abb9b

###### ref-fb09571781dd3b8f

###### ref-fd524c7c5d6c424e

> **Theorem: Uniform Tao-shaped logarithmic windows** \[thm:uniform-log-windows\] There is an absolute constant $K_0$ such that, for every $x\ge2$ and $1\le\Omega\le x$, $$\left|  \sum_{x/\Omega<n\le x}\frac{a_n}{n}  \right|\le K_0. \label{eq:uniform-window-bound}$$ Consequently, for every function $1\le\omega(x)\le x$ with $\omega(x)\to\infty$, $$\frac1{\log\omega(x)}  \sum_{x/\omega(x)<n\le x}\frac{a_n}{n}  \longrightarrow0. \label{eq:normalized-window-cancellation}$$

> **Proof** The interval $(x/\Omega,x]$ meets at most two dyadic blocks partially. Each partial block has harmonic mass at most $\log2+1$. The remaining complete blocks form a consecutive range. By [\[eq:dyadic-harmonic-estimate\]](TPC-087.md#ref-3db2ca274886e2df), their contribution is an alternating sum of copies of $\log2$, of absolute value at most $\log2$, plus an absolutely summable error of total size at most $2$. Thus [\[eq:uniform-window-bound\]](TPC-087.md#ref-fb09571781dd3b8f) holds, for example with $K_0=8$. Divide by $\log\omega(x)\to\infty$.

###### ref-df948f5e1ec7f1b2

> **Corollary: Sharp black-box failure** \[cor:black-box-failure\] For bounded sequences in general, even normalized logarithmic cancellation on every growing terminal multiplicative window does not imply $S_N/N\to0$.

> **Proof** Combine \[[thm:dyadic-counterexample](TPC-087.md#ref-60b4c2908bf17f74),[thm:uniform-log-windows](TPC-087.md#ref-c9cf255f813abb9b)\].

###### ref-4c98694a81083535

> **Remark: Why this does not contradict the positive criteria** \[rem:counterexample-local-failure\] At the end of a dyadic block, the terminal half is essentially one constant-sign block, so $\omega_N(L)$ stays bounded below by a positive constant close to $\log2$. Also $$V_k=\sum_{2^k\le n<2^{k+1}}\frac1n=h_k\to\log2.$$ Thus the counterexample fails both additional hypotheses in [4](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-087.md#src-d4fe3a4ab393137e).

###### ref-809624727197fffe

> **Remark: Scope** \[rem:counterexample-scope\] The sequence [\[eq:dyadic-sequence\]](TPC-087.md#ref-8b7fb0001340117d) is not multiplicative. It proves a logical nonimplication for structure-free Tauberian arguments. It neither models the literal Möbius coefficient nor rules out a conversion using multiplicativity, entropy decrement, pretentious distance, or another arithmetic input.

# Quantifier audit of available arithmetic theorems

The Tauberian identity is exact only after the same ordered coefficient sequence has been fixed. A theorem that changes the forms, shift, scale, interval origin, or outer averaging measure does not automatically control its signed Cesàro defect.

## Fixed-form logarithmic correlation

###### ref-7bff19432f16d885

For fixed $a_1,a_2\in\mathbb N$ and $b_1,b_2\in\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$ with $a_1b_2-a_2b_1\ne0$, Tao proved $$\sum_{x/\omega(x)<n\le x}  \frac{\lambda(a_1n+b_1)\lambda(a_2n+b_2)}n  =o(\log\omega(x)), \label{eq:tao-logarithmic}$$ where $1\le\omega(x)\le x$ and $\omega(x)\to\infty$ [`\citep{Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07). This is a genuine two-point cancellation theorem in its stated logarithmic setting.

Equation [\[eq:tao-logarithmic\]](TPC-087.md#ref-7bff19432f16d885) does not by itself verify $\omega_N(L)\to0$. Its ratio $\omega(x)$ must grow, its conclusion is normalized by $\log\omega(x)$, and the affine coefficients are fixed. The literal packet instead contains polynomially growing slopes and intercepts, fixed-ratio terminal windows, actual masks, and a quantitative outer sum. The dyadic counterexample proves that this mismatch cannot be repaired for arbitrary bounded sequences by a Tauberian black box alone.

## Averaged shifts, almost-all scales, and short intervals

###### ref-ce58b3be79c490b3

For fixed $k$ and $1\le H=H(X)\le X$ with $H(X)\to\infty$, Matomäki, Radziwiłł, and Tao proved $$\sum_{h_1,\ldots,h_k\le H}  \left|  \sum_{n\le X}\lambda(n+h_1)\cdots\lambda(n+h_k)  \right|  =o(H^kX) \label{eq:mrt-averaged-shifts}$$ [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-b5a8969fcb0fe4a8). This is ordinary cancellation averaged over shifts; it does not select one prescribed $h_0$.

Tao and Teräväinen proved unweighted Chowla and Elliott cases at all scales outside a set of zero logarithmic density. For fixed Liouville shifts, their stated Chowla cases include odd order and order two [`\citep{TaoTeravainen2019}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-f813d8018b0720d3). A prescribed sparse sequence of physical scales can lie inside the exceptional set, so an additional scale-localization theorem is required.

Matomäki and Radziwiłł proved mean-value control for multiplicative functions on almost all short intervals, including Möbius cancellation on almost all intervals whose length tends to infinity arbitrarily slowly [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwill2016}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-a1c07472dab49106). A physical affine fiber has a prescribed origin, and some literal fibers have $O(1)$ length. Neither feature is selected by an almost-all-origin theorem. Moreover, the cited result concerns the mean of one multiplicative function; it is not itself a theorem for the two-sign affine product in [\[eq:literal-affine-sequence\]](TPC-087.md#ref-1bbb5dd2dd66efe5).

###### ref-1f7243b61d828d3e

> **Proposition: Averages do not select a literal member** \[prop:average-selection\] An estimate $$\frac1H\sum_{h\le H}|B_X(h)|=o(1)$$ does not logically imply $B_X(h_0)=o(1)$ for one prescribed $h_0$. Likewise, a theorem outside a zero-logarithmic-density set of scales does not logically imply its conclusion along a prescribed sparse scale sequence.

> **Proof** For the first statement set $B_X(h_0)=1$ and $B_X(h)=0$ otherwise. For the second, put the prescribed sparse sequence into the exceptional set. For example, $\{2^k:k\ge0\}$ has zero logarithmic density because $$\frac1{\log X}\sum_{2^k\le X}\frac1{2^k}\longrightarrow0.$$

# The literal growing fixed-$h_0$ interface

The exact Tauberian theorem is useful only after the physical coefficient has been retained. We therefore formulate the crosswalk without claiming that its arithmetic hypothesis is known.

## The affine fibers

###### ref-1bbb5dd2dd66efe5

###### ref-11cc657f10e820f2

After one physical column is opened, the literal TPC-33 affine correlation has the schematic form $$\mathfrak a_{\theta,X}(r)  =  \mu(\widetilde d_{\theta,X}+s_{\theta,X}r)  \mu(\widetilde u_{\theta,X}+\alpha_{\theta,X}r)  M_{\theta,X}(r)W_{\theta,X}(r), \label{eq:literal-affine-sequence}$$ where $M_{\theta,X}$ contains the actual coprimality, residue, and joint masks, $W_{\theta,X}$ contains the physical smooth and logarithmic weights, and $$s_{\theta,X}\widetilde u_{\theta,X}  -\alpha_{\theta,X}\widetilde d_{\theta,X}=h_0\ne0. \label{eq:fixed-determinant}$$ The parameter $\theta$ also records the opposite row, polarization, divisor layer, interval component, and native keys. The slopes and intercepts may grow with $X$.

###### ref-76210b8afb053cac

For an ordered fiber of length $N_{\theta,X}$, absorb every literal local factor into a sequence $b_{\theta,X}(n)$, $1\le n\le N_{\theta,X}$, and define $$S_{\theta,X}(N)=\sum_{n\le N}b_{\theta,X}(n),\qquad  L_{\theta,X}(N)=\sum_{n\le N}\frac{b_{\theta,X}(n)}n. \label{eq:fiber-primitives}$$ This definition does not assert that an existing logarithmic correlation theorem applies to $b_{\theta,X}$.

## An exact conditional certificate

###### ref-ca91593e5a803954

###### ref-9de3968da0191a0c

###### ref-09f315256a04d843

> **Theorem: Literal Tauberian reassembly certificate** \[thm:literal-certificate\] Suppose the complete raw hard zero mode has an exact reassembly $$Z_X=  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  c_{\theta,X}  S_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})  +E_{\mathrm{content},X}, \label{eq:literal-reassembly}$$ with all native keys and both polarizations retained. Then $$|Z_X|  \le  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  |c_{\theta,X}|N_{\theta,X}  \left|  \mathcal T_{N_{\theta,X}}(L_{\theta,X})  \right|  +|E_{\mathrm{content},X}|. \label{eq:literal-defect-bound}$$ The signed defect in [\[eq:literal-defect-bound\]](TPC-087.md#ref-09f315256a04d843) may be replaced by the absolute oscillation $\mathcal A_N$, the right side of [\[eq:geometric-local-global\]](TPC-087.md#ref-f4805d257036eea7), or the right side of [\[eq:variation-convolution\]](TPC-087.md#ref-3158e5518dd857c4).

> **Proof** By [\[thm:sharp-criterion\]](TPC-087.md#ref-d73053517adcad1b), $$S_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})  =  N_{\theta,X}  \mathcal T_{N_{\theta,X}}(L_{\theta,X}).$$ Insert this identity into [\[eq:literal-reassembly\]](TPC-087.md#ref-9de3968da0191a0c), then take one triangle inequality after the complete reassembly. The alternative bounds follow from \[[cor:absolute-sufficient](TPC-087.md#ref-06f61382332dfe9e),[thm:local-to-global](TPC-087.md#ref-592195b68b38d0e5),[thm:variation-convolution](TPC-087.md#ref-9c9046d011f83483)\].

###### ref-3ce3bd4f2c86d30d

> **Remark: What is exact and what is conditional** \[rem:exact-conditional\] The passage from [\[eq:literal-reassembly\]](TPC-087.md#ref-9de3968da0191a0c) to [\[eq:literal-defect-bound\]](TPC-087.md#ref-09f315256a04d843) is finite $\mathrm{L0}$ algebra. The claim that [\[eq:literal-reassembly\]](TPC-087.md#ref-9de3968da0191a0c) is the actual raw physical packet, with no missing mask or renormalization, is an $\mathrm{L1}$ verification. A uniform power bound for the defects of the growing Möbius family [\[eq:literal-affine-sequence\]](TPC-087.md#ref-1bbb5dd2dd66efe5) is an $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic estimate and is not proved here.

###### ref-35e02d8f7631a49b

> **Remark: Short fibers** \[rem:short-fibers\] The asymptotic condition $\omega_N(L)\to0$ says nothing about a fiber whose length stays $O(1)$. Such fibers must be returned by an exact finite estimate or controlled after a separate outer reassembly. Their mere cardinality is not a cancellation theorem.

###### ref-af1826972781f818

> **Remark: Weights and origins** \[rem:weights-origins\] The harmonic factor $1/n$ in [\[eq:fiber-primitives\]](TPC-087.md#ref-76210b8afb053cac) is tied to the chosen local ordering. Partial summation can transfer some smooth weights only when the required prefix estimates are uniform. It cannot silently change the prescribed interval origin, delete the joint mask, or freeze coefficients that grow with $X$. In particular, the $1/n$ in a fixed-form logarithmic theorem is attached to the original arithmetic variable. After translating a physical fiber with origin $r_{\theta,X}$, the inherited weight is $1/(r_{\theta,X}+n)$, not $1/n$. Replacing one by the other requires an exact change of variables or a uniform partial-summation lemma for the same prefixes; it is not a formal relabeling.

## Fixed-power and endpoint ledger

The exact identity itself spends no fixed power. A useful physical application, however, must prove a rate that survives the full outer sum. For example, if $$\omega_M(L_{\theta,X})  \ll M^{-\eta+o(1)},\qquad 0<\eta<1,$$ uniformly in every literal long fiber and every relevant prefix $2\le M\le N_{\theta,X}$, then [\[cor:power-transfer\]](TPC-087.md#ref-1f6df8c772194351) gives the same per-fiber power. Losses from the number and size of fibers, masks, short-fiber treatment, content completion, and calibrated downstream drift remain to be charged separately.

###### ref-94b4315a616dbd18

###### ref-208778be6de966fc

At the critical physical scales $$Q=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad J=X^{133/400+o(1)},$$ the recorded two-exponent transfer takes the form $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q^2,\qquad  D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q^3J^2 \label{eq:two-exponent-input}$$ and yields $$\frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}  \le X^{1/400+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}. \label{eq:two-exponent-output}$$ This is a sufficient ledger, not a claim that the displayed exponents are optimal; see TPC-81 and TPC-82 [`\citep{WangTPC81,WangTPC82}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-ff65f956e7cf0d6d); [\[WangTPC82\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-087.md#cite-cfeb22890911c08a). Retaining the baseline exponent in [\[eq:two-exponent-output\]](TPC-087.md#ref-94b4315a616dbd18) requires the determinant-flatness compatibility $$\lambda_D\le 2\eta_Z. \label{eq:determinant-flatness-ledger}$$ This is one ledger: it compares the certified lower loss for $D_X$ with the certified cancellation gain for $Z_X$.

###### ref-c7f9884ef3d6759f

###### ref-8d51953ca957d361

> **Definition: Strict physical endpoint rule** \[def:strict-endpoint\] Let $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}$ be the sum of all fixed-power losses actually incurred by localization, coefficient growth, masks, short-fiber treatment, outer reassembly, content completion, dictionary transfer, and downstream calibrated drift. A proposed route continues only if $$\boxed{\qquad \Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<\frac1{400}. \qquad} \label{eq:strict-endpoint}$$ Equality or excess is a stop condition. This is a second, independent ledger. A loss already included in $\lambda_D-2\eta_Z$ is not charged a second time; an interface or downstream loss absent from those exponents must be entered in $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}$.

###### ref-9d989b8ce0ed8928

> **Proposition: Success checklist** \[prop:success-checklist\] A logarithmic-to-ordinary advance qualifies as a literal $\mathrm{L1}$ or $\mathrm{L2}$ input only if it returns:
>
> 1.  the same fixed $h_0$ and actual growing affine forms;
>
> 2.  the ordinary physical fibers with their prescribed origins;
>
> 3.  all periodic, coprimality, residue, smooth-weight, and joint-mask factors;
>
> 4.  both polarizations and every native outer key;
>
> 5.  a separate exact or outer estimate for bounded-length fibers;
>
> 6.  a quantitative signed-defect or stronger local bound uniform over the active family and all prefixes used by the dyadic recursion;
>
> 7.  a complete fixed-power ledger satisfying [\[eq:strict-endpoint\]](TPC-087.md#ref-8d51953ca957d361).

> **Proof** Items (i)–(v) ensure that the sequence in [\[eq:fiber-primitives\]](TPC-087.md#ref-76210b8afb053cac) is the literal coefficient rather than a frozen or averaged substitute. Item (vi) supplies the hypothesis of [\[thm:literal-certificate\]](TPC-087.md#ref-ca91593e5a803954); qualitative normalized logarithmic cancellation alone is ruled out by [\[cor:black-box-failure\]](TPC-087.md#ref-df948f5e1ec7f1b2). # Claim boundary and conclusion

## What is proved

The following statements are unconditional for arbitrary complex sequences:

1.  the exact discrete Abel identity $$S_N=NL_N-\sum_{m<N}L_m;$$

2.  the necessary-and-sufficient signed Cesàro criterion $$S_N/N\to0\iff   L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m\to0;$$

3.  convergence of $L_N$ is sufficient, with the quantitative bounds [\[eq:convergence-rate\]](TPC-087.md#ref-d59c93ed6712ceca) and [\[eq:tail-convergence-rate\]](TPC-087.md#ref-19f0c1128ac22c5a);

4.  terminal-half logarithmic cancellation and vanishing dyadic logarithmic variation are sufficient, with the explicit bounds [\[eq:geometric-local-global\]](TPC-087.md#ref-f4805d257036eea7) and [\[eq:variation-convolution\]](TPC-087.md#ref-3158e5518dd857c4);

5.  a fixed-power terminal-half bound below exponent one transfers without a fixed-power loss;

6.  the bounded dyadic sequence [\[eq:dyadic-sequence\]](TPC-087.md#ref-8b7fb0001340117d) has normalized logarithmic cancellation on every growing multiplicative window but no ordinary cancellation.

These are $\mathrm{L0}$ identities, inequalities, and a structure-free $\mathrm{L0}$ countermodel.

is also an exact finite implication. Its status becomes $\mathrm{L1}$ only after [\[eq:literal-reassembly\]](TPC-087.md#ref-9de3968da0191a0c) is verified for the complete raw hard physical packet.

## What is not proved

This paper does not prove:

1.  terminal-half $o(1)$ or fixed-power cancellation for the actual growing affine Möbius family;

2.  uniformity of Tao’s fixed-form theorem in the polynomially growing physical coefficients;

3.  selection of the prescribed $h_0$ from an averaged-shift theorem;

4.  removal of the exceptional scales or interval origins in an almost-all theorem;

5.  cancellation on the $O(1)$-length physical fibers;

6.  a complete physical outer-reassembly bound;

7.  a determinant-energy lower bound for $D_X$;

8.  a fixed-$h_0$ Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic;

9.  a breach of the general sieve parity barrier;

10. a prime-pair lower bound or the twin-prime conjecture.

No conclusion is specialized from a general fixed nonzero $h_0$ to $h_0=2$.

## Conclusion

The logarithmic-to-ordinary gap is not an informal change of weight. For the same ordered coefficient sequence it is measured exactly by $$\mathcal T_N(L)=  L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m.$$ Global boundedness of $L_N$, or even normalized cancellation on every terminal window whose ratio tends to infinity, does not force this defect to vanish. The smallest exact target is therefore the signed defect itself. Terminal-half cancellation and dyadic variation are stronger, quantitative certificates that can be checked without ambiguity.

For the TPC route this produces a clean fork. A positive advance must prove one of these certificates uniformly for the literal growing fixed-$h_0$ affine fibers and survive the full outer reassembly. A negative advance may prove that a proposed logarithmic input cannot meet that certificate under its native quantifiers. Either outcome must retain the actual coefficient, global normalization, and the strict endpoint rule $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400$. The present paper identifies that gate; it does not cross the unresolved arithmetic parity boundary.
