## Abstract

TPC-83 through TPC-91 form nine consecutive audits of the fixed-shift matched-shell route; the present synthesis is the tenth paper in that block. We verify their exact dependency structure and separate what has been closed from what remains arithmetic.

On the magnitude side, TPC-83 proves the sharp equivalence between natural post-bin $\ell^1$ mass and natural determinant-energy survival, while TPC-84 shows conditionally that any determinant-energy lower bound with fixed loss $\lambda_D<1$ must be carried asymptotically by positive equal-determinant off-diagonal coherence. On the phase side, TPC-85 rules out three false normalization shortcuts. TPC-86 gives an exact coherent Fourier reconstruction and, in the zero-loss corner $\eta_Z=0$ with a second-order filter, reduces the critical zero-mode input to a low-frequency window $R=X^{133/600+\varepsilon}$ for sufficiently small fixed $0<\varepsilon<107/240$; the general window depends on the desired $\eta_Z$ and must remain below $q_X/2$. TPC-87 identifies the precise signed Cesàro defect needed to convert logarithmic cancellation to ordinary cancellation. TPC-88 factors determinant flatness as $$\mathfrak F=\mathfrak c^2S_{\mathrm{eff}}$$ and identifies the critical relative cancellation scale $\mathfrak c\le X^{o(1)}/J$ in the natural-mass regime. TPC-89 proves that a surrogate transfers this scale uniformly only at relative $\ell^2$ accuracy $O(J^{-1})$. TPC-90 completely classifies the primitive affine squarefree local support and isolates the remaining weighted Liouville sign correlation. TPC-91 models the stronger zero-residual full-block certificate as the constrained equation $Bc=w$, proves that bounded overlap does not imply it, and supplies projection and Farkas obstruction certificates. The original TPC-18 hypothesis also permits a separately controlled physical remainder, so exact coverage is a sufficient strengthening rather than the only admissible reassembly.

These are genuine $\mathrm{L0}$ closures and $\mathrm{L1}$ interfaces, but none is a new $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate for the literal growing fixed-$h_0$ coefficient. The actual post-bin mass/energy lower bound and zero-mode upper bound remain open and their recorded exponents must satisfy $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$. TPC-18 full-block reassembly (by zero residual or a proved physical remainder), primitive determinant dispersion, and the strict physical loss $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400$ remain independent. Equality $\lambda_D=2\eta_Z$ is admissible but has no determinant reserve; $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}\ge1/400$ stops the physical ledger. Losses are not counted twice.

We finish with a prioritized roadmap and explicit stop/go tests for four branches: low-frequency/log-Tauberian/surrogate zero-mode control, determinant mass and coherence, primitive affine sign arithmetic, and full-block reassembly certification. The audited outcome is forward but incomplete: no fixed-shift Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, parity breach, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime conclusion is claimed.

# Introduction

Long proof programs accumulate two different kinds of progress. Some steps establish an arithmetic estimate on the literal physical coefficient. Others remove ambiguity: they identify the correct normalization, prove an exact transfer theorem, or exhibit a counterexample to an invalid shortcut. Both are useful, but they must not be reported at the same proof level.

TPC-83 through TPC-91 were designed as a controlled exploration of the fixed-shift determinant route after the completion-to-flatness audit in TPC-82 [`\citep{WangTPC82}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-cfeb22890911c08a). They investigate three quantities and two reassembly interfaces: $$M_X=\sum_n|A_X(n)|,\qquad  D_X=\sum_n|A_X(n)|^2,\qquad  Z_X=\sum_nA_X(n),$$ together with the TPC-18 all-block reassembly and primitive determinant dispersion [`\citep{WangTPC18}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-4abf939102bd1649). Here $A_X$ is always the already aggregated literal determinant coefficient on its actual support. At the audited critical row we use $$Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad  N_{0,X}=J_XQ_X^2.$$ The padded Fourier modulus satisfies $q_X\asymp Q_X$, and $$d_q(r,0)=\min_{m\in\mathbb Z}|r+mq|$$ denotes cyclic distance. If $v\ne0$ is a coefficient on the same literal frame $\Omega$, with $S=\#\Omega$, put $$Z(v)=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}v_\omega,\qquad  D(v)=\sum_{\omega\in\Omega}|v_\omega|^2,\qquad  \rho(v)=\frac{|Z(v)|}{\sqrt{S D(v)}}.$$ Every surrogate comparison below uses this same $\Omega$; zero padding or changing the frame changes $\rho$.

The nine papers do not supply nine interchangeable estimates. Instead, they form four branches:

1.  post-bin mass, determinant energy, and equal-determinant coherence;

2.  distinguished zero-mode cancellation through Fourier, Tauberian, or surrogate interfaces;

3.  primitive affine local support versus the remaining sign correlation; and

4.  full-block reassembly of the upstream hard packet, with exact coverage as its zero-residual sufficient certificate.

These branches must merge only after their normalizations and losses have been matched literally.

## Purpose of the present audit

This paper has four tasks. First, it records the precise dependency graph for TPC-83–TPC-91 from their actual theorem statements, rather than from suggestive titles. Second, it classifies every advance as $\mathrm{L0}$, $\mathrm{L1}$, or $\mathrm{L2}$. Third, it identifies which routes have been sharpened, which shortcuts have been killed, and which arithmetic gates remain independent. Fourth, it turns those gates into a prioritized roadmap with explicit stop/go criteria.

The result is not a proof by accumulation. Exact identities do not become a growing fixed-shift correlation estimate merely because many of them are now available. Conversely, a negative transfer result can be durable progress when it prevents an invalid route from being retried.

## Headline conclusion

The nine-paper block has made the route substantially more falsifiable:

-   the correct mass variable is post-bin, not raw;

-   any determinant-energy lower bound with fixed loss below one requires equal-determinant coherence;

-   the zero mode is a coherent low-frequency or affine-sign problem, not a centering convention;

-   logarithmic-to-ordinary transfer requires a signed Tauberian defect with a rate that survives outer reassembly;

-   a surrogate must have uniform worst-case accuracy at the full $J^{-1}$ scale (or $X^{o(1)}/J_X$ in the physical convention);

-   local squarefree density does not supply sign cancellation; and

-   bounded overlap does not supply an exact full-block certificate, while a nonzero coefficient residual still needs a physical remainder theorem.

No paper in the block proves the remaining growing arithmetic bound. The honest aggregate status is therefore strong $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ progress with no new $\mathrm{L2}$ theorem.

# Audit protocol and invariant frame

## Proof levels

###### ref-91f72636a30b6781

> **Definition: Three proof levels** \[def:proof-levels\] We use the following noninterchangeable labels.
>
> $\mathrm{L0}$: finite model or abstract theorem.
>
> :   An exact algebraic, geometric, Fourier, convex, or Tauberian theorem, including a finite or structure-free countermodel.
>
> $\mathrm{L1}$: literal interface theorem.
>
> :   An exact transfer that identifies the $\mathrm{L0}$ quantity on the physical carrier while preserving every required key, mask, support, coefficient, and normalization. Its hypotheses may still include an unproved arithmetic estimate.
>
> $\mathrm{L2}$: growing arithmetic estimate.
>
> :   A new uniform bound for the actual growing fixed-$h_0$ physical coefficient, with all prefixes, exceptional sectors, polarizations, and outer reassembly returned.

> **Remark** An averaged shift, almost-all scale, fixed-form, frozen-family, support-only, or positive-square estimate is recorded at its native scope. It is not promoted to $\mathrm{L2}$ fixed-$h_0$ progress without a proved literal transfer.

## The literal invariant tuple

###### ref-e5f9ee7b894b558c

> **Definition: Literal invariant frame** \[def:literal-frame\] At scale $X$, the physical frame consists of: $$\mathfrak I_X=  (h_0,\Omega_X,\mathcal K_X,\mathcal W_X,\mathcal N_X).$$ Here $h_0\ne0$ is the prescribed shift, $\Omega_X$ is the actual active atom or determinant support, $\mathcal K_X$ is the complete native-key and provenance list, $\mathcal W_X$ contains all masks, weights, prefixes, interval origins, and polarizations, and $\mathcal N_X$ is the single global post-aggregation normalizer.

A transfer is literal only when it gives an exact map between the source and target frames. Equal numerical determinants do not justify merging distinct native atoms. Zero padding, changing a fiber mass, or replacing the post-bin mass by a raw majorant changes the relevant statistic and requires a separate theorem.

###### ref-24dfd5d087751c1f

> **Proposition: Promotion rule** \[prop:promotion-rule\] Within this audit, an $\mathrm{L0}$ or conditional $\mathrm{L1}$ statement is promoted to an $\mathrm{L2}$ statement only after a theorem verifies:
>
> 1.  the same literal frame $\mathfrak I_X$;
>
> 2.  uniformity in every growing physical coefficient and prefix;
>
> 3.  return of every exceptional, resonant, short-fiber, and outer-reassembly sector; and
>
> 4.  the exact endpoint loss in the correct ledger.

> **Proof** These items are part of the definition of the audited $\mathrm{L2}$ target. Omitting one changes either the coefficient, its quantifiers, or its normalization, so it proves a different statement.

## What counts as forward progress

An audited step is forward if it does at least one of the following:

1.  proves a new literal fixed-power or logarithmic estimate;

2.  closes a necessary exact interface in the dependency graph;

3.  reduces a required estimate to a strictly smaller literal family with controlled return; or

4.  rules out a proposed route by an exact physical counterexample or dual obstruction.

Numerical stability, a pilot census, or a phase-free upper bound may guide the next experiment, but is not by itself any of these four items.

# Exact advances and permanently removed shortcuts

## Magnitude branch: mass, energy, and coherence

Let $$S_X=\#\operatorname{supp}A_X,\qquad  L_X=\|A_X\|_\infty,\qquad  M_X=\|A_X\|_1,\qquad  D_X=\|A_X\|_2^2,$$ and $N_{0,X}=J_XQ_X^2$. TPC-83 combines the universal inequalities $$\frac{M_X^2}{S_X}\le D_X\le L_XM_X$$ with the verified literal envelopes $$S_X\ll Q_X,\qquad  L_X\ll X^{o(1)}\frac{N_{0,X}}{Q_X}.$$ It follows exactly that subpower natural mass survival is equivalent to subpower natural energy survival. This closes the choice of variable, not its lower bound [`\citep{WangTPC83}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-c5e5f0238c876731).

TPC-84 opens the post-bin coefficient into native determinant fibers. For fiber moduli $r_i$, the sharp free-phase resultant interval is $$\left[(2r_{\max}-\sum_i r_i)_+,\ \sum_i r_i\right].$$ It also proves $$D_X=E_{{\rm trip},X}+C_{{\rm eq},X},$$ where the physical triplet diagonal is at most on the loss-one scale. Thus an eventual energy lower bound with fixed loss $\lambda_D<1$ must be carried asymptotically by positive equal-determinant off-diagonal coherence. This is a necessary mechanism, not a proved lower bound [`\citep{WangTPC84}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-9acf964dd666f301).

###### ref-211d3b6e84117f65

> **Proposition: Audited magnitude output** \[prop:magnitude-output\] Under the literal support and pointwise envelopes above, natural post-bin mass survival is equivalent to natural determinant-energy survival. Moreover, if a future theorem proves $$D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2  \qquad\text{with fixed }\lambda_D<1,$$ then $$C_{{\rm eq},X}=(1-o(1))D_X>0.$$ Neither conclusion supplies the mass or energy lower bound itself.

> **Proof** The first statement is the two-sided TPC-83 exponent transfer. For the second, TPC-84 proves that the physical triplet diagonal is at most on the loss-one scale, so it is $o(D_X)$ under the displayed fixed-loss hypothesis. Insert this in $D_X=E_{{\rm trip},X}+C_{{\rm eq},X}$.

A continuation may seek dominant-native-term survival on enough actual fibers, or a positive structured same-determinant pairing with controlled remainder. These are research strategies, not an exhaustive theorem-level disjunction. Raw mass precedes fiber summation and cannot lower-bound the post-bin norm.

## Zero-mode branch: Fourier, Tauberian, and surrogate interfaces

TPC-85 proves that $$A_X(0)=0 \quad\not\Longrightarrow\quad  Z_X=\widehat A_X(0)=0.$$ Under no-wrap padding, $$A_X=A_X^\circ+\frac{Z_X}{q_X}\mathbf 1,\qquad  D_X=\|A_X^\circ\|_2^2+\frac{|Z_X|^2}{q_X},$$ and $$Z_X=-\sum_{r\ne0}\widehat A_X(r).$$ Conditional expectation preserves $Z_X$ and changes the energy normalization; content inversion returns the full shell in its $t=1$ term. These identities eliminate three normalization shortcuts but retain coherent Fourier reassembly as a valid branch [`\citep{WangTPC85}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-d8d9f98f6854badf).

TPC-86 sharpens that branch. A positive-density structural zero arc supports a fixed-order filter with bounded Wiener norm. For the literal packet, $$|Z_X|  \ll_k  \max_{0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R}  |\widehat A_X(r)|  +  X^{o(1)}N_{0,X}R^{1/2-k}.$$ Write $$R_X=X^{\varrho+o(1)}<q_X/2,\qquad  \varrho<\frac{267}{400}.$$ At the critical row, a target $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2$$ has an admissible filter tail whenever $$(k-\tfrac12)\varrho>\frac{133}{400}+\eta_Z.$$ The often quoted choice $$k=2,\qquad R_X=X^{133/600+\varepsilon}$$ is the zero-loss corner $\eta_Z=0$, with sufficiently small fixed $$0<\varepsilon<\frac{267}{400}-\frac{133}{600}  =\frac{107}{240}.$$ More generally that same window requires $\eta_Z<3\varepsilon/2$. The missing input is therefore a coherent literal low-frequency estimate at a window matched to the desired rate, not control of all $q_X$ frequencies [`\citep{WangTPC86}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-41b0792996942846).

###### ref-392725a7b99f8c7d

TPC-87 identifies the exact cost of importing logarithmic cancellation. For $$S_N=\sum_{n\le N}a_n,\qquad  L_N=\sum_{n\le N}\frac{a_n}{n},$$ one has $$\frac{S_N}{N}  =  \underbrace{L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m}_{\mathcal T_N(L)}.$$ Thus ordinary cancellation is exactly the vanishing of the signed backward Cesàro defect. Normalized logarithmic cancellation alone does not imply it. Terminal-half control or vanishing dyadic logarithmic variation can bound the defect for one sequence, but a physical application must also survive the weighted outer sum. If $$Z_X=\sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  c_{\theta,X}S_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})  +E_{\mathrm{content},X},$$ the relevant quantitative certificate is $$\sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}|c_{\theta,X}|N_{\theta,X}  \left|\mathcal T_{N_{\theta,X}}(L_{\theta,X})\right|  +|E_{\mathrm{content},X}|  \le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2. \label{eq:tauberian-aggregate-target}$$ The Abel identity itself is $\mathrm{L0}$; the growing estimate [\[eq:tauberian-aggregate-target\]](TPC-092.md#ref-392725a7b99f8c7d) is not proved [`\citep{WangTPC87}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-c10bb69652986ef9).

TPC-88 and TPC-89 turn the same target into magnitude–phase and projective geometry. For $A_X\ne0$, $$\mathfrak F_X  =  \frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}  =  \underbrace{\left(\frac{|Z_X|}{M_X}\right)^2}_{\mathfrak c_X^2}  \underbrace{\frac{M_X^2}{D_X}}_{S_{\mathrm{eff},X}}.$$ Under natural post-bin mass survival, $$S_{\mathrm{eff},X}=X^{o(1)}Q_X,$$ and at the critical endpoint $$\mathfrak F_X\le X^{1/400+o(1)}  \iff  \mathfrak c_X\le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X}  \iff  |Z_X|\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.$$ If a same-frame surrogate $b_X$ is used, its projective angle transfers with the sharp law $$|\rho(A_X)-\rho(b_X)|  \le  \frac{\|A_X-b_X\|_2}{\|b_X\|_2}.$$ Uniform transfer of the critical angle therefore requires relative accuracy $O(J_X^{-1})$ in order; $o(1)$ is insufficient [`\citep{WangTPC88,WangTPC89}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-c309474acaf11792); [\[WangTPC89\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-77cd2665bece44cd).

## Primitive affine branch

For individually primitive forms $$L_1(r)=d+sr,\qquad L_2(r)=u+ar,\qquad su-ad=h_0\ne0,$$ TPC-90 proves $$\gcd(a,s)\mid h_0,\qquad  \gcd(L_1(r),L_2(r))\mid h_0.$$ If $\nu_{p,k}$ counts residues modulo $p^k$ on which $p^k\mid L_1(r)$ or $p^k\mid L_2(r)$, then $$\nu_{p,k}  =  \mathbf 1_{p\nmid s}+\mathbf 1_{p\nmid a}  -  \mathbf 1_{p\nmid as}\mathbf 1_{p^k\mid h_0}.$$ Consequently the simultaneous squarefree local product is positive and uniformly bounded below by $$\prod_p\left(1-\frac2{p^2}\right)>0.$$ On positive intervals the exact sign split is $$\mu(L_1)\mu(L_2)  =  \mu^2(L_1)\mu^2(L_2)  \lambda(L_1)\lambda(L_2).$$ All square-power local obstructions are now explicit; the unresolved quantity is the weighted Liouville product on the actual growing physical family [`\citep{WangTPC90}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-4f3149d531dce18d).

## Full-block reassembly branch

TPC-91 places the zero-residual strengthening of the TPC-18 all-block hypothesis on the literal atom space. With exact candidate columns $B_X$, target $w_X$, and the coefficient rule $\mathscr C_X$ actually authorized upstream, this certificate is $$B_Xc_X=w_X,\qquad c_X\in\mathscr C_X.$$ For unrestricted synthesis the exact defect is $$\varepsilon_{\mathrm{cov}}  =  \|(I-B_XB_X^\dagger)w_X\|_2.$$ For nonnegative synthesis, Farkas’ alternative gives an exact dual obstruction. For a general coefficient rule, exact feasibility is the membership $w_X\in B_X\mathscr C_X$; zero distance is equivalent only when the attainable image is closed. Bounded overlap, even overlap one with every atom covered, need not give an $o(1)$-relative coefficient cover.

The original TPC-18 hypothesis is weaker: it may allow $$w_X=B_Xc_X+r_X,\qquad \mathcal T_X(r_X)=o(X)$$ in its physical scale, where $\mathcal T_X$ denotes the original literal physical reassembly functional, with the prescribed coefficients and every quantitative loss retained. Thus exact coverage is the clean zero-remainder sufficient route. Its failure does not kill TPC-18 reassembly unless the physical residual route is also ruled out [`\citep{WangTPC18,WangTPC91}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-4abf939102bd1649); [\[WangTPC91\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-8f1d4a8c9b42f76d).

# The remaining independent gates

## Current-route completion vector

The exact papers now permit the remaining hypotheses to be stated without substitution. Fix exponent parameters $\lambda_D,\eta_Z\ge0$. On the current route, write: $$\begin{aligned} \mathsf G_{\rm mass}:\quad& D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2, \\ \mathsf G_{\rm zero}:\quad& |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2, \\ \mathsf G_{\rm reasm}:\quad& w_X=B_Xc_X+r_X,\quad c_X\in\mathscr C_X^{\rm up},\quad \mathcal T_X(r_X)=o(X), \\ \mathsf G_{\rm DD}:\quad& \text{the TPC-18 primitive determinant estimate holds on every returned block}, \\ \mathsf G_{\rm phys}:\quad& \Lambda_{\rm phys}<\frac1{400}.\end{aligned}$$ Here $\mathscr C_X^{\rm up}$ is the coefficient rule actually derived upstream; in the original unit-coefficient packet it is not replaceable by an arbitrary signed solution. The reassembly gate includes coefficient amplification and the quantitative residual rate required by the physical ledger, and $\mathcal T_X$ is the original literal physical reassembly functional. The exact cover $B_Xc_X=w_X$ is the special sufficient case $r_X=0$. The determinant-dispersion gate includes every prefix and exceptional block required by TPC-18.

## Determinant and physical ledgers

The determinant compatibility [\[eq:determinant-compatibility\]](TPC-092.md#ref-2cc020d8102c64cb) compares the recorded lower bound for $D_X$ with the recorded upper bound for $Z_X$. At the critical row it is the sufficient flatness ledger derived in TPC-82 and reproduced exactly by TPC-88 [`\citep{WangTPC82,WangTPC88}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-cfeb22890911c08a); [\[WangTPC88\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-c309474acaf11792). If $\lambda_D>2\eta_Z$, those particular recorded bounds do not certify the target. This failure need not disprove a sharper bound for the actual coefficient.

###### ref-4de0d46de9f0db5e

The physical ledger records fixed-power degradation elsewhere in the literal interface and downstream comparison: $$\boxed{\Lambda_{\rm phys}<\frac1{400}.}  \label{eq:strict-physical-ledger}$$ It is strict. Equality or excess stops the route. A polynomial reassembly-amplification or physical-residual loss enters this ledger once if it is not already present in the block error. A determinant-energy loss already represented by $\lambda_D$, or a zero-mode gain represented by $\eta_Z$, is not charged again.

> **Theorem: Ledger separation** \[thm:ledger-separation\] The audited chain requires the conjunction $$\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z  \qquad\text{and}\qquad  \Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400.$$ It supplies no theorem that adds these inequalities into one budget, converts one into the other, or permits the same loss to be entered twice.

> **Proof** The first inequality compares two statistics of the same literal post-bin coefficient. The second counts losses in distinct physical maps and reassembly steps, excluding losses already represented in the first comparison. They have different definitions and inputs. TPC-87–TPC-91 explicitly preserve this separation [`\citep{WangTPC87,WangTPC88,WangTPC89,WangTPC90,WangTPC91}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-c10bb69652986ef9); [\[WangTPC88\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-c309474acaf11792); [\[WangTPC89\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-77cd2665bece44cd); [\[WangTPC90\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-4f3149d531dce18d); [\[WangTPC91\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-092.md#cite-8f1d4a8c9b42f76d).

# Explicit branch stop/go tests

A stop test kills only the stated branch or certificate unless an end-to-end impossibility theorem is proved. Numerical failure is a diagnostic, not a stop certificate.

## Branch I: zero-mode control

#### Fourier go test.

Fix the TPC-86 filter of order $k\ge2$, its literal padding, and $$R_X=X^{\varrho+o(1)}<q_X/2,\qquad  \varrho<\frac{267}{400},\qquad  (k-\tfrac12)\varrho>\frac{133}{400}+\eta_Z.$$ For the zero-loss target $\eta_Z=0$, the second-order choice $R_X=X^{133/600+\varepsilon}$ is admissible for fixed $0<\varepsilon<107/240$; for positive $\eta_Z$ the window must be enlarged according to the displayed condition while remaining below $q_X/2$. The branch advances if the multiplier-weighted low-frequency term and returned tail jointly imply $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2$$ for an $\eta_Z$ compatible with [\[eq:determinant-compatibility\]](TPC-092.md#ref-2cc020d8102c64cb). Every exceptional frequency must be charged by reconstruction-multiplier mass, not cardinality.

#### Fourier stop test.

The particular filter certificate stops if a rigorous literal lower bound on its exceptional or resonant contribution makes the target impossible, if the zero arc loses positive density without a replacement estimate, or if its unavoidable physical loss makes $\Lambda_{\rm phys}\ge1/400$. A mean-square theorem or an all-but-exceptional theorem that does not return the weighted exceptional set is merely insufficient input, not a global impossibility result.

#### Tauberian go test.

For every physical affine sequence and every prefix used by outer reassembly, prove a quantitative signed defect, terminal-half, or variation bound such that the complete returned estimate satisfies $$\sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}|c_{\theta,X}|N_{\theta,X}  \left|  L_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})  -\frac1{N_{\theta,X}}\sum_{m<N_{\theta,X}}  L_{\theta,X}(m)  \right|  +|E_{\mathrm{content},X}|  \le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2,$$ or the corresponding quantitative target for the literal block to which the certificate is attached. A per-fiber $o(1)$ statement without this outer rate does not pass. The reciprocal weight introduced on translated fibers must be transferred exactly.

#### Tauberian stop test.

A theorem controlling only normalized logarithmic means, only fixed forms, or almost all scales/origins fails this certificate until its exceptional quantifiers are removed. A proof that no available uniformity can survive the literal growing coefficients kills that transfer route, not Möbius-specific alternatives.

#### Surrogate go test.

Construct a same-frame $b_X$ and prove $$\rho(b_X)\le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X}, \qquad  \frac{\|A_X-b_X\|_2}{\|b_X\|_2}  \le\frac{X^{o(1)}}{J_X}.$$ The support map must be exact because zero padding changes $\rho$.

#### Surrogate stop test.

The chosen surrogate class is killed by a rigorous lower bound showing that every allowed $b_X$ has relative error $\gg X^c/J_X$ for some fixed $c>0$, or by an exact nonidentifiability counterexample. Merely obtaining $o(1)$ error does not pass the gate.

## Branch II: determinant mass and coherence

#### Mass go test.

Prove $$M_X\ge X^{-o(1)}N_{0,X}$$ for the literal post-bin coefficient, or prove the equivalent natural energy lower bound directly. A more general fixed-loss result is still usable, but must enter the full $(\mu,\lambda_D,\eta_Z)$ profile rather than the natural corner.

#### Census go test.

Execute the native-key TPC-84 decomposition with exact determinant keys, then prove either a dominant-term lower bound on enough fibers or a positive structured pairing whose unpaired remainder is smaller uniformly in $X$. Finite computation can certify finite identities; the growing lower bound requires a theorem.

#### Mass stop tests.

The phase-blind dominant-term strategy stops if its exact lower envelope is too small. The natural-mass branch stops if one proves on an unbounded sequence of scales the literal upper bound $$M_X\le X^{-c+o(1)}N_{0,X}$$ for some fixed $c>0$. Neither event rules out a nonnatural loss-profile route. A raw majorant or unexecuted census proves no stop.

## Branch III: primitive affine sign arithmetic

#### Go test.

Prove cancellation for $$\sum_{\substack{r\\  L_{1,\theta,X}(r)L_{2,\theta,X}(r)\ne0}}  W_{\theta,X}(r)  \mu^2(|L_{1,\theta,X}(r)|)  \mu^2(|L_{2,\theta,X}(r)|)  \lambda(|L_{1,\theta,X}(r)|)  \lambda(|L_{2,\theta,X}(r)|)$$ uniformly over the literal growing coefficients, prescribed nonzero determinant $h_0$, origins, masks, relevant prefixes, short-fiber treatment, polarizations, and outer keys. The aggregate bound must fit either the zero-mode target or the TPC-18 block target to which it is attached.

#### Weaker go tests.

A fixed-power saving on one literal residual block, a certified range of square-divisor moduli, or a smoothed/frozen one-slot theorem is publishable progress at its native level if it has an exact route back toward the physical coefficient. It is not reported as full fixed-$h_0$ cancellation.

#### Stop tests.

Further local Euler-product calculations do not address the remaining sign factor. The present implementation stops if a rigorous transfer lower bound shows that returning its excluded sectors necessarily forces $\Lambda_{\rm phys}\ge1/400$. An averaged-shift or fixed-form theorem that cannot select the prescribed growing family is an input mismatch, not proof that every affine route fails.

## Branch IV: full-block reassembly

#### Exact-cover go test.

Export the complete physical target and candidate blocks to one native row list. Solve the coefficient rule actually derived upstream; for the original unit cover the test is $$B_X\mathbf 1=w_X,$$ not merely existence of an arbitrary signed solution. Verify the coefficient-weighted block error under the original normalization.

#### Exact-cover stop test.

For the proposed block family, any verified nonzero orthogonal residual or applicable Farkas vector is a rigorous stop certificate for that zero-residual candidate family. It is not a stop for the original TPC-18 asymptotic reassembly. A coarse determinant-key solution does not pass.

#### Physical-residual go test.

If only $w_X=B_Xc_X+r_X$ is available with the upstream-prescribed coefficients, prove the required physical estimate for $\mathcal T_X(r_X)$, retain its rate in $\Lambda_{\rm phys}$, and verify the original global normalization. Small $\|r_X\|_2$ alone is not this theorem.

#### Determinant-dispersion go test.

After an admissible exact or residual reassembly, prove the TPC-18 primitive generic estimate uniformly on every returned block with the required strict exponent margin. Reassembly and dispersion are checked separately.

#### Global stop test.

Even if every local branch passes, equality or excess in $\Lambda_{\rm phys}$ stops the assembled route. Failure of $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$ stops only the certificate formed from those recorded $D_X,Z_X$ bounds unless a sharper estimate is impossible.

# Prioritized next-stage roadmap

The priorities below are ordered by how directly the existing exact reductions expose a checkable next theorem. Priorities one and two may run in parallel because zero-mode cancellation and energy survival are both required.

## Priority 1: zero-mode low-frequency, Tauberian, and surrogate branch

#### Deliverable 1A: literal low-window export.

Write every $\widehat A_X(r)$, $0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R_X$, in the native affine variables, retain the TPC-86 multiplier, and partition resonant, short-fiber, and generic sectors without deletion. The first theorem target is the complete multiplier-weighted window, not an individual generic frequency. Choose $R_X=X^{\varrho+o(1)}$ and filter order $k$ so that $R_X<q_X/2$, $\varrho<267/400$, and $(k-\tfrac12)\varrho>133/400+\eta_Z$; the $133/600+\varepsilon$ window, with fixed $0<\varepsilon<107/240$, is reserved for the second-order zero-loss corner unless $\eta_Z<3\varepsilon/2$.

#### Deliverable 1B: signed Tauberian certificate.

For any logarithmic input, compute the exact unnormalized primitive and its signed backward Cesàro defect on every physical prefix. Then prove that the coefficient-weighted fiber defects, short-fiber return, and content completion together meet [\[eq:tauberian-aggregate-target\]](TPC-092.md#ref-392725a7b99f8c7d), or the corresponding quantitative target of the attached block. A per-fiber $o(1)$ defect is not enough when the number or weights of fibers grow. If only normalized window cancellation is available, record a negative transfer result rather than treating it as ordinary cancellation.

#### Deliverable 1C: surrogate feasibility test.

Construct candidate same-frame surrogates only after an exact support map. Prove or disprove the simultaneous $$\rho(b_X),\quad  \|A_X-b_X\|_2/\|b_X\|_2  \le X^{o(1)}/J_X$$ requirements. A sharp lower bound on the best achievable error would be a publishable negative result.

## Priority 2: determinant mass and equal-determinant coherence

#### Deliverable 2A: native-key census.

Implement the TPC-84 streaming/external-sort schema with exact determinant and provenance keys. Report $M_X,D_X,E_{\rm trip}$, $C_{\rm eq}$, dominant-fiber mass, pairing-class totals, and the unpaired remainder under one normalization. This computation chooses the next theorem; it does not prove asymptotics.

#### Deliverable 2B: one growing lower bound.

Target either: $$M_X\ge X^{-o(1)}N_{0,X},$$ a positive same-scale lower bound for one literal pairing class with controlled remainder, or a rigorously quantified fixed-loss profile. If a fixed-power mass loss is proved instead, abandon the natural corner and recalculate the full TPC-88 ledger.

## Priority 3: primitive affine arithmetic

TPC-90 shows that the next useful theorem is not another local root count. Expand one literal low-frequency or determinant block into the squarefree-supported Liouville correlation and seek a bound that is uniform in the growing slopes, intercepts, origins, masks, and prefixes. The preferred progression is:

1.  one literal residual block with a fixed-power or arbitrary-log saving;

2.  a controlled family of square-divisor moduli with exact complementary return;

3.  the full multiplier-weighted low window or TPC-18 generic block; and

4.  complete outer reassembly.

At each stage, averaged shifts, frozen coefficients, and density-one ranges retain those qualifiers.

## Priority 4: full-block reassembly, exact cover first

Export the TPC-16/TPC-18 hard packet to the TPC-91 incidence format. The first calculation is exact feasibility, preferably with rational or interval-certified arithmetic. There are three publishable outcomes:

1.  an allowed exact cover with acceptable coefficient cost;

2.  a dual residual or Farkas certificate that kills the proposed zero-residual block family; or

3.  an explicit residual plus a new physical theorem controlling it.

Outcome (a) closes reassembly with zero residual. Outcome (b) does not kill the original TPC-18 residual route. Outcome (c) closes reassembly only at the rate actually proved. Primitive determinant dispersion remains a separate subsequent theorem.

## Final merge audit

No branch is declared complete in isolation. Before any endpoint claim, rerun the following audit:

1.  literal physical coefficients and one prescribed $h_0$;

2.  actual support and all native keys;

3.  one global atomic normalization;

4.  canonical, complete representation with no deleted sectors;

5.  the recorded determinant compatibility $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$; and

6.  the independent strict physical budget $\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400$.

Then verify the TPC-18 reassembly (exact cover or a controlled physical residual) and primitive determinant input separately. Any duplicated loss is removed before the comparison.

>
> 1.  a literal positive estimate, which advances the dependency graph at its proved level;
>
> 2.  an exact negative certificate, which permanently removes the specified route or family; or
>
> 3.  a conditional interface or numerical diagnostic, which is retained without promotion.
>
> None of these labels implies a prime-pair theorem unless the entire remaining conjunction is proved.

> **Proof** The positive and negative certificates are the go/stop tests in [6](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-092.md#src-92295712b54d7056); every other outcome lacks at least one promotion item in [\[prop:promotion-rule\]](TPC-092.md#ref-24dfd5d087751c1f). The final statement follows because the completion conjunction remains unproved.
