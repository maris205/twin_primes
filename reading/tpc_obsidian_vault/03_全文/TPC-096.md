## Abstract

Let $a_1,\ldots,a_N\in\mathbb C$, let $r\ge0$ be real translation, and put $$S_N=\sum_{n\le N}a_n,\qquad  L_{r,N}=\sum_{n\le N}\frac{a_n}{r+n},\qquad L_{r,0}=0.$$ The logarithmic primitive on a translated interval has an exact Tauberian inverse that differs from the unshifted one: $$\boxed{\quad  S_N=(r+N)L_{r,N}-\sum_{m<N}L_{r,m},\qquad  \frac{S_N}{N}  =  \left(1+\frac rN\right)L_{r,N}  -\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_{r,m}.  \quad}$$ Thus ordinary cancellation is equivalent to vanishing of an *anchored backward Cesàro defect*. The extra term $(r/N)L_{r,N}$ is not an error that may be dropped after translating a physical fiber.

We prove the exact terminal criterion: once the unshifted signed defect is $o(1)$, ordinary cancellation holds if and only if $(r/N)L_{r,N}=o(1)$. Thus $r=0$ needs no terminal condition; for $0<r=o(N)$ the exact scale is $L_{r,N}=o(N/r)$, with boundedness only a convenient stronger hypothesis; for $r\asymp N$ one needs $L_{r,N}=o(1)$; and for $r\gg N$ one needs $L_{r,N}=o(N/r)$. The constant sequence gives the exact obstruction: $$L_{r,N}=\sum_{n\le N}\frac1{r+n}\sim\frac Nr=o(1)  \quad(r/N\to\infty),  \qquad S_N/N=1.$$ Hence even an unnormalized $o(1)$ logarithmic statement can be useless on a short translated fiber.

For a physical packet $$Z_X=\sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  c_{\theta,X}S_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})  +E_{\mathrm{ret},X},$$ we obtain the exact signed aggregate formula $$Z_X=\sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  c_{\theta,X}N_{\theta,X}  \mathcal T_{\rho_{\theta,X},N_{\theta,X}}(L_{\theta,X})  +E_{\mathrm{ret},X}.$$ This identifies the quantitative certificate that a logarithmic theorem must return after every literal origin, prefix, outer coefficient, mask, and content term has been restored. The identities and counterexamples are $\mathrm{L0}$; attachment to a complete native packet is an $\mathrm{L1}$ certificate. No uniform estimate for the growing fixed-$h_0$ affine Möbius family is proved, so there is no new $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic saving, parity breakthrough, or prime-pair consequence.

# Introduction

Logarithmic correlation theorems naturally weight an arithmetic variable $m$ by $1/m$. A physical affine fiber, however, is often written after translation as $$m=r+n,\qquad 1\le n\le N.$$ The inherited weight is $1/(r+n)$, not $1/n$. Replacing it by $1/n$ changes both the primitive and its Tauberian inverse.

TPC-87 isolated the exact signed Cesàro defect for the unshifted primitive and warned that a translated reciprocal weight must be transferred rather than relabelled [`\citep{WangTPC87}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-c10bb69652986ef9). The present paper computes that transfer exactly. The new term is elementary, but its scale is decisive when the physical origin is comparable to or larger than the fiber length.

The distinction is also required when comparing the literal packet with logarithmically averaged Chowla and Elliott theorems. Tao’s two-point theorem has fixed affine coefficients and a logarithmic window [`\citep{Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07). Averaged-shift and almost-all-scale results have different quantifiers [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015,TaoTeravainen2019}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-b5a8969fcb0fe4a8); [\[TaoTeravainen2019\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-f813d8018b0720d3). None may be attached to a polynomially growing, prescribed fixed-$h_0$ family without checking coefficients, origins, prefixes, masks, and the outer sum.

## Main contributions

We prove:

1.  the exact anchored Abel identity and its necessary-and-sufficient signed defect;

2.  the exact necessary-and-sufficient terminal condition and its consequences in the regimes $r=o(N)$, $r\asymp N$, and $r\gg N$;

3.  exact counterexamples showing that unnormalized logarithmic smallness and per-fiber qualitative cancellation do not survive translation or growing outer mass;

4.  a prefix-uniform positive criterion for genuinely short translated fibers;

5.  the signed and absolute aggregate certificates for the complete literal affine packet; and

6.  a separate determinant and physical-loss ledger that prevents a Tauberian normalization change from being counted as arithmetic cancellation.

## What changes in the TPC route

The correct fiber-level datum is $$\mathcal T_{r,N}(L)  =  \left(1+\frac rN\right)L_N  -\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m,$$ not the unshifted defect alone. This refines the Tauberian branch of the TPC-92 roadmap [`\citep{WangTPC92}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-8444c63105c2920e). It does not estimate the datum for the actual Möbius coefficient. That remains an arithmetic problem after the literal low-frequency export of TPC-93 [`\citep{WangTPC93}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-2d5bd9665d7257b5).

# The anchored Abel identity

###### ref-599820e0760e4e4a

> **Definition: Translated logarithmic primitive** \[def:translated-primitive\] For $r\ge0$, define $$S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Na_n,\qquad  L_{r,N}=\sum_{n=1}^N\frac{a_n}{r+n},  \qquad L_{r,0}=0.$$

###### ref-bdaed6025bbe2fb2

###### ref-03ea4ff70f5cf722

###### ref-c0797467b32e94d2

> **Theorem: Exact anchored Tauberian identity** \[thm:anchored-identity\] For every complex sequence, every real $r\ge0$, and every $N\ge1$, $$\begin{aligned}  S_N  &=(r+N)L_{r,N}-\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_{r,m},  \label{eq:anchored-abel}\\  \frac{S_N}{N}  &=  \left(1+\frac rN\right)L_{r,N}  -\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_{r,m}.  \label{eq:anchored-defect-identity}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** Since $$a_n=(r+n)(L_{r,n}-L_{r,n-1}),$$ summation and a shift of index give $$\begin{aligned}  S_N  &=  \sum_{n=1}^N(r+n)L_{r,n}  -\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}(r+m+1)L_{r,m}\\  &=(r+N)L_{r,N}-\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_{r,m},\end{aligned}$$ because $L_{r,0}=0$. Division by $N$ proves the second identity.

###### ref-8e7e17214d13549c

###### ref-9db3e9faee6ebb98

> **Definition: Anchored backward defect** \[def:anchored-defect\] For a finite sequence $L=(L_m)_{0\le m\le N}$ with $L_0=0$, put $$\mathcal T_{r,N}(L)  :=  \left(1+\frac rN\right)L_N  -\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}L_m.  \label{eq:anchored-defect}$$ The usual signed backward Cesàro defect is $$\mathcal T_{0,N}(L)=L_N-\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m.$$

###### ref-3900df0a4dc1aefb

###### ref-79d46a88f62a10c5

> **Corollary: Exact decomposition of the translation cost** \[cor:translation-cost\] $$\boxed{\quad  \mathcal T_{r,N}(L)  =\mathcal T_{0,N}(L)+\frac rN L_N.  \quad} \label{eq:translation-cost}$$ Consequently $S_N/N\to0$ along any family of triples $(r,N,a)$ if and only if $\mathcal T_{r,N}(L_r)\to0$.

> **Proof** Subtract the two definitions and use [\[eq:anchored-defect-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-c0797467b32e94d2).

###### ref-5e50350ede09d493

> **Remark: The anchor is part of the main term** \[rem:anchor-main-term\] Equation [\[eq:translation-cost\]](TPC-096.md#ref-79d46a88f62a10c5) is exact. There is no reason for $(r/N)L_N$ to be small merely because $L_N=o(1)$. The relevant terminal scale is $N/r$ when $r\gg N$.

###### ref-6f440f04c1eebed0

###### ref-75f5fa4b60e6965d

> **Proposition: Absolute oscillation bound** \[prop:absolute-anchored\] Define $$\mathcal A_N(L)=\frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}|L_N-L_m|.$$ Then $$\left|\frac{S_N}{N}\right|  \le \mathcal A_N(L_r)+\frac rN|L_{r,N}|. \label{eq:absolute-anchored}$$

> **Proof** Write the unshifted defect as $$\mathcal T_{0,N}(L)=\frac1N\sum_{m<N}(L_N-L_m)$$ and apply the triangle inequality in [\[eq:translation-cost\]](TPC-096.md#ref-79d46a88f62a10c5).

# Sharp translation regimes

###### ref-b30d6ea4c1c47204

###### ref-d93b1b31b40febb3

> **Theorem: Exact terminal criterion and regime corollaries** \[thm:regime-criterion\] Consider a family indexed by $X$, with anchors $r_X$, lengths $N_X\to\infty$, primitives $L_X$, and ordinary sums $S_X$. Assume $$\mathcal T_{0,N_X}(L_X)=o(1).$$ Then $$\frac{S_X}{N_X}=o(1)  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  \frac{r_X}{N_X}L_X(N_X)=o(1). \label{eq:exact-terminal-criterion}$$ Consequently:
>
> 1.  If $r_X=0$, no terminal condition is needed.
>
> 2.  If $0<r_X=o(N_X)$, the exact terminal scale is $L_X(N_X)=o(N_X/r_X)$; the stronger bound $L_X(N_X)=O(1)$ is a convenient sufficient condition.
>
> 3.  If $r_X\asymp N_X$, then the two conditions $$\mathcal T_{0,N_X}(L_X)=o(1),\qquad L_X(N_X)=o(1)$$ imply $S_X/N_X=o(1)$.
>
> 4.  If $r_X/N_X\to\infty$, then $$\mathcal T_{0,N_X}(L_X)=o(1),\qquad   L_X(N_X)=o(N_X/r_X)$$ imply $S_X/N_X=o(1)$.
>
> The terminal condition in [\[eq:exact-terminal-criterion\]](TPC-096.md#ref-d93b1b31b40febb3) is necessary and sufficient without additional structure.

> **Proof** The identity $$\mathcal T_{r_X,N_X}(L_X)  =  \mathcal T_{0,N_X}(L_X)  +\frac{r_X}{N_X}L_X(N_X)$$ and $S_X/N_X=\mathcal T_{r_X,N_X}(L_X)$ prove [\[eq:exact-terminal-criterion\]](TPC-096.md#ref-d93b1b31b40febb3). The regime statements are immediate. The examples in \[[ex:constant-obstruction](TPC-096.md#ref-9add113e08c21512),[ex:tuned-anchor](TPC-096.md#ref-20538c3e651062d2)\] show that the anchor term cannot be removed.

###### ref-c784cc67be5fcee9

For a positive comparison scale define the translated harmonic mass $$\mathcal H_{r,N}:=\sum_{n=1}^N\frac1{r+n}. \label{eq:harmonic-mass}$$

###### ref-f4907085767c7f3f

###### ref-930b5d7c99f28e0e

> **Lemma: Short translated harmonic mass** \[lem:short-harmonic-mass\] For $r\ge N\ge1$, $$\frac{N}{r+N}\le\mathcal H_{r,N}\le\frac Nr,  \qquad  \left(1+\frac rN\right)\mathcal H_{r,N}\le2. \label{eq:short-harmonic-bounds}$$

> **Proof** Each denominator lies between $r+1$ and $r+N$. The displayed bounds follow by comparison; the last uses $N/r\le1$.

###### ref-5355e809f64e9076

###### ref-47262b81d532ee59

###### ref-a3248303a78f81c6

> **Theorem: Prefix-uniform criterion for short translated fibers** \[thm:short-prefix-uniform\] Assume $r\ge N$ and $$\max_{1\le m\le N}  \frac{|L_{r,m}|}{\mathcal H_{r,m}}  \le\varepsilon. \label{eq:relative-prefix-input}$$ Then $$\left|\frac{S_N}{N}\right|\le3\varepsilon. \label{eq:short-prefix-output}$$

> **Proof** By [\[thm:anchored-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-bdaed6025bbe2fb2), $$\begin{aligned}  \left|\frac{S_N}{N}\right|  &\le  \left(1+\frac rN\right)|L_{r,N}|  +\frac1N\sum_{m<N}|L_{r,m}|\\  &\le  2\varepsilon+  \frac{\varepsilon}{N}\sum_{m<N}\mathcal H_{r,m}.\end{aligned}$$ Since $\mathcal H_{r,m}\le m/r\le1$, the last average is at most $\varepsilon$.

###### ref-03a054a641f4c696

> **Remark: The quantifier is strong** \[rem:prefix-quantifier\] The hypothesis is uniform in every prefix and normalized by its actual translated harmonic mass. A statement only at the terminal endpoint, or one with an $o(1)$ error not measured relative to $\mathcal H_{r,m}$, does not imply [\[eq:relative-prefix-input\]](TPC-096.md#ref-47262b81d532ee59). Moreover the case $m=1$ reads $|a_1|\le\varepsilon$. For a unit-modulus arithmetic sequence this cannot hold with $\varepsilon=o(1)$. A realistic application must peel a finite initial segment, return it exactly to the outer sum, and impose the relative estimate only on the remaining prefixes. The theorem is a strong sufficient certificate, not a claim that literal Möbius fibers already satisfy it.

###### ref-3c389d26cb5fc2d9

###### ref-2ddd9c83e76920b7

> **Corollary: Cutoff-prefix certificate** \[cor:cutoff-prefix\] Assume $r\ge N$, fix $1\le M\le N$, and suppose $$\sup_{M\le m\le N}  \frac{|L_{r,m}|}{\mathcal H_{r,m}}  \le\varepsilon.$$ Then $$\left|\frac{S_N}{N}\right|  \le  3\varepsilon  +  \frac1N\sum_{m=0}^{M-1}|L_{r,m}|. \label{eq:cutoff-prefix-output}$$

> **Proof** Use [\[eq:anchored-defect-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-c0797467b32e94d2). The terminal term is at most $$\left(1+\frac rN\right)\varepsilon\mathcal H_{r,N}  \le  \varepsilon\left(1+\frac Nr\right)  \le2\varepsilon.$$ The controlled part of the backward average is at most $$\frac{\varepsilon}{N}  \sum_{m=M}^{N-1}\mathcal H_{r,m}  \le\varepsilon,$$ and the omitted prefixes give the last term in [\[eq:cutoff-prefix-output\]](TPC-096.md#ref-2ddd9c83e76920b7).

###### ref-ea6b7af7f8abc3b1

> **Proposition: Smooth terminal bound** \[prop:smooth-terminal\] Suppose, for every $0\le m\le N$, $$L_{r,m}=\ell_{r,m}+e_{r,m},$$ where $$\mathcal T_{0,N}(\ell)=0.$$ Then $$\left|\frac{S_N}{N}\right|  \le  \frac rN|\ell_{r,N}|  +\left(1+\frac rN\right)|e_{r,N}|  +\frac1N\sum_{m<N}|e_{r,m}|.$$ Thus a model primitive transfers only after its anchor term and the full prefix error have both been controlled.

> **Proof** Insert $L=\ell+e$ into [\[eq:anchored-defect-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-c0797467b32e94d2) and use the assumed cancellation of the unshifted model defect.

# Exact counterexamples and sharpness

###### ref-9add113e08c21512

\[ex:constant-obstruction\] Let $a_n=1$, and let $r_N/N\to\infty$. Then $$\frac{S_N}{N}=1, \qquad  L_{r_N,N}=\mathcal H_{r_N,N}  =\frac{N}{r_N}\{1+o(1)\}=o(1).$$ Moreover $$\mathcal T_{0,N}(L_{r_N,\cdot})=o(1), \qquad  \frac{r_N}{N}L_{r_N,N}=1+o(1).$$ Thus the entire ordinary mean is carried by the anchor term.

> **Proof** The harmonic estimate follows uniformly from $$\frac{N}{r_N+N}\le\mathcal H_{r_N,N}\le\frac{N}{r_N}.$$ The anchored identity gives the last assertion; alternatively, $L_{r_N,m}=m/r_N+O(m^2/r_N^2)$ uniformly for $m\le N$.

###### ref-20538c3e651062d2

\[ex:tuned-anchor\] Let $r\ge N$ and let $a_n=\xi$ be constant. Then $$\frac{S_N}{N}=\xi,\qquad  L_{r,N}=\xi\mathcal H_{r,N}.$$ Choosing $\xi=\delta r/N$ gives $$L_{r,N}=\delta\{1+O(N/r)\}, \qquad  S_N/N=\delta r/N.$$ Hence a terminal primitive of order $\delta$ can amplify by $r/N$, and the scale $N/r$ in [\[thm:regime-criterion\]](TPC-096.md#ref-b30d6ea4c1c47204) cannot be enlarged uniformly.

###### ref-55cda3441b6350f6

\[ex:outer-mass\] For each integer $M$, take $M$ fibers with $r_\theta=0$, $N_\theta=1$, $c_\theta=1$, and $a_{\theta,1}=1/M$. Each fiber satisfies $$|\mathcal T_{0,1}(L_\theta)|=\frac1M=o(1),$$ but $$\sum_{\theta=1}^M  c_\theta N_\theta\mathcal T_{0,1}(L_\theta)=1.$$ Thus a qualitative bound on every fiber is not an aggregate certificate when the outer coefficient mass grows.

> **Remark: Scope of the counterexamples** The examples are structure-free $\mathrm{L0}$ obstructions. They do not give lower bounds for the literal affine Möbius packet. They prove that a proposed transfer theorem using only the displayed hypotheses is false.

###### ref-6f1f176134f5d740

> **Proposition: No anchor-free black box** \[prop:no-anchor-free\] There is no function $\Phi(\varepsilon)\to0$ such that, uniformly for all $r,N$ and all $|a_n|\le1$, $$|L_{r,N}|\le\varepsilon,\qquad  |\mathcal T_{0,N}(L_r)|\le\varepsilon  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad  |S_N|/N\le\Phi(\varepsilon).$$

> **Proof** Use [\[ex:constant-obstruction\]](TPC-096.md#ref-9add113e08c21512) and choose $r_N/N\to\infty$ fast enough that both hypotheses tend to zero, while $S_N/N=1$.

# Prefix-complete aggregate reassembly

###### ref-fc311a535c026e08

Let $\Theta_X$ be a finite native-key set. For each $\theta\in\Theta_X$, let $\rho_{\theta,X}\ge0$ be the literal origin, $N_{\theta,X}\ge1$ the returned length, and $$b_{\theta,X}(n),\qquad 1\le n\le N_{\theta,X},$$ the complete local coefficient after retaining the affine Möbius factors, periodic factors, smooth weight, residue and coprimality masks, and the additive determinant phase when present. Define $$\begin{aligned}  S_{\theta,X}(N)  &:=  \sum_{n\le N}b_{\theta,X}(n),  \label{eq:fiber-sum}\\  L_{\theta,X}^{(\rho)}(N)  &:=  \sum_{n\le N}  \frac{b_{\theta,X}(n)}{\rho_{\theta,X}+n}. \label{eq:fiber-anchored-primitive}\end{aligned}$$ The symbol $\rho_{\theta,X}$ is an interval origin; it is unrelated to the Fourier frequency $r$ used in the TPC-93 low window.

> **Theorem: Exact anchored packet identity** \[thm:aggregate-identity\] Suppose the literal raw packet has the exact reassembly $$Z_X=  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  c_{\theta,X}  S_{\theta,X}(N_{\theta,X})  +E_{\mathrm{ret},X}. \label{eq:literal-reassembly}$$ Then $$\boxed{  Z_X=  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  c_{\theta,X}N_{\theta,X}  \mathcal T_{\rho_{\theta,X},N_{\theta,X}}  (L_{\theta,X}^{(\rho)})  +E_{\mathrm{ret},X}.} \label{eq:aggregate-signed}$$ In particular, $$\begin{aligned}  |Z_X|  \le{}&  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  |c_{\theta,X}|N_{\theta,X}  \left|  \mathcal T_{\rho_{\theta,X},N_{\theta,X}}  (L_{\theta,X}^{(\rho)})  \right|  \notag\\  &+|E_{\mathrm{ret},X}|. \label{eq:aggregate-absolute}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** Apply [\[thm:anchored-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-bdaed6025bbe2fb2) to every finite fiber in [\[eq:literal-reassembly\]](TPC-096.md#ref-9de3968da0191a0c). No limiting interchange is required. The inequality follows after the exact outer sum has been formed.

> **Remark: Bounded fibers** \[rem:bounded-fibers\] A fiber with $N_{\theta,X}=O(1)$ has no internal asymptotic parameter. It remains in [\[eq:aggregate-signed\]](TPC-096.md#ref-453f2e8ce4d6582c) exactly and must be controlled by its coefficient mass or by cancellation after outer reassembly. Deleting it because a long-fiber theorem does not apply changes $Z_X$.

###### ref-8ec67afdeacc977c

> **Proposition: Coefficient-mass promotion rule** \[prop:coefficient-mass\] Suppose a subset $\Theta_X^\star$ satisfies $$\left|  \mathcal T_{\rho_{\theta,X},N_{\theta,X}}  (L_{\theta,X}^{(\rho)})  \right|  \le\varepsilon_X  \quad(\theta\in\Theta_X^\star).$$ Its absolute contribution is at most $$\varepsilon_X  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X^\star}  |c_{\theta,X}|N_{\theta,X}.$$ Therefore a uniform $o(1)$ defect is useful only relative to the actual weighted outer mass.

> **Proof** This is the corresponding part of [\[eq:aggregate-absolute\]](TPC-096.md#ref-7702f3666a1eb8f8).

# Quantifier audit for logarithmic correlation theorems

The two-point logarithmically averaged Chowla theorem controls fixed affine forms on a multiplicative interval with the reciprocal weight of the original arithmetic variable [`\citep{Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07). The averaged Chowla theorem averages the shifts [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-b5a8969fcb0fe4a8). Structural results at almost all scales retain an almost-all qualifier [`\citep{TaoTeravainen2019}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-096.md#cite-f813d8018b0720d3). These are major arithmetic inputs, but their quantifiers are not interchangeable with the literal TPC packet.

###### ref-72ecb43ed84678eb

> **Proposition: Literal promotion checklist** \[prop:promotion-checklist\] A logarithmic theorem can be promoted to a certificate for [\[eq:literal-reassembly\]](TPC-096.md#ref-9de3968da0191a0c) only after verifying:
>
> 1.  the same prescribed $h_0$ and the actual slopes and intercepts, including their growth with $X$;
>
> 2.  the original arithmetic variable and therefore the true anchor $\rho_{\theta,X}$;
>
> 3.  uniformity in every prefix required by partial summation or dyadic recursion;
>
> 4.  all interval origins, masks, smooth weights, periodic factors, polarizations, and native outer keys;
>
> 5.  an exact return of short, resonant, content, and endpoint sectors; and
>
> 6.  a quantitative aggregate rate meeting [\[eq:aggregate-target\]](TPC-096.md#ref-5faf32307901f2a3), or a direct bound for the signed sum [\[eq:aggregate-signed\]](TPC-096.md#ref-453f2e8ce4d6582c).

> **Proof** Items (i)–(v) identify the sequence and frame in [\[eq:fiber-anchored-primitive\]](TPC-096.md#ref-fc311a535c026e08). Item (vi) is precisely the hypothesis needed by [\[thm:aggregate-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-ad542e4b462604c1). Omitting the anchor contradicts [\[prop:no-anchor-free\]](TPC-096.md#ref-6f1f176134f5d740); omitting outer mass contradicts [\[ex:outer-mass\]](TPC-096.md#ref-55cda3441b6350f6).

> **Remark: Fixed forms versus growing forms** Tao’s theorem is stated for fixed $a_1,a_2,b_1,b_2$ with nonzero determinant. The literal columns have slopes, intercepts, origins, and masks depending on $X$, while preserving one fixed nonzero determinant $h_0$ after the exact content reduction. No uniformity in that entire growing family is inferred here.

> **Remark: Averaged shifts do not select $h_0$** An estimate averaged over many shifts can show that most shifts are good without identifying the one prescribed physical shift. It remains at its averaged level until a literal selection theorem is proved.

> **Remark: Logarithmic normalization** A normalized bound $L_{r,N}=o(\mathcal H_{r,N})$ has different strength in different regimes. For $r\ge N$, a prefix-uniform version is enough by [\[thm:short-prefix-uniform\]](TPC-096.md#ref-5355e809f64e9076). For $r=0$, $\mathcal H_{0,N}\asymp\log N$, and qualitative $o(\log N)$ cancellation alone does not control the unshifted signed defect; this is the sharp gap of TPC-87.

###### ref-3677443dec1aa3b3

> **Proposition: Anchored branch stop test** \[prop:stop-test\] A rigorous parameter-consistent lower bound showing that every allowed logarithmic input in a specified class has $$\left|  \sum_{\theta}  c_{\theta,X}N_{\theta,X}  \mathcal T_{\rho_{\theta,X},N_{\theta,X}}(L_{\theta,X}^{(\rho)})  \right|  \ge X^{-\eta_Z+\delta+o(1)}Q_X^2$$ for some fixed $\delta>0$, together with $$|E_{\mathrm{ret},X}|  =  o\!\left(  \left|  \sum_{\theta}  c_{\theta,X}N_{\theta,X}  \mathcal T_{\rho_{\theta,X},N_{\theta,X}}  (L_{\theta,X}^{(\rho)})  \right|\right),$$ kills that transfer class at the claimed target. Equivalently, one may prove the corresponding lower bound directly for the complete $Z_X$. A lower bound merely of the same $X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2$ scale does not contradict an upper bound at that scale. Likewise, an unavoidable translation loss with $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}\ge1/400$ stops the assembled physical route. Failure of a currently available theorem to supply uniformity is not such a lower bound.

> **Proof** The fixed gap $X^\delta$, and the exclusion of cancellation by the returned remainder, force the complete coefficient above the claimed target. The physical-loss assertion is the strict endpoint rule. The last sentence distinguishes an absent input from an impossibility theorem.

## Audited conclusion

###### ref-7a1522a7a23ad73e

> **Theorem: Anchored transfer conclusion** \[thm:conclusion\] For every translated physical fiber, ordinary cancellation is equivalent to cancellation of the anchored defect $$\mathcal T_{r,N}(L)  =  \left(1+\frac rN\right)L_N  -\frac1N\sum_{m<N}L_m.$$ For a complete native packet the exact target is the signed aggregate [\[eq:aggregate-signed\]](TPC-096.md#ref-453f2e8ce4d6582c); the absolute expression [\[eq:aggregate-target\]](TPC-096.md#ref-5faf32307901f2a3) is a sufficient quantitative certificate. Neither an anchor-free defect, a terminal logarithmic bound without prefix control, nor per-fiber $o(1)$ without weighted outer mass is a valid substitute.

> **Proof** The fiber statement is [\[thm:anchored-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-bdaed6025bbe2fb2). Summing it with the literal coefficients gives [\[thm:aggregate-identity\]](TPC-096.md#ref-ad542e4b462604c1). The three forbidden substitutions are ruled out respectively by \[[prop:no-anchor-free](TPC-096.md#ref-6f1f176134f5d740),[rem:prefix-quantifier](TPC-096.md#ref-03a054a641f4c696),[ex:outer-mass](TPC-096.md#ref-55cda3441b6350f6)\].
