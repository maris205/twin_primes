## Abstract

After the conductor-one sector is returned at the endpoint scale, the unresolved TPC-94 exceptional branch has algebraic conductor equal to the strong no-wrap prime $q_X$ but insufficient archimedean variation on its affine interval. We isolate the exact finite-field geometry of this resonance.

For an odd prime $q$, put $G=\mathbb F_q^\times$ and $$E_H=\{\pm1,\ldots,\pm H\}\subset G,  \qquad 1\le H\le(q-1)/2.$$ The resonance carrier is the multiplicative incidence operator $$(K_Hb)(\omega)  =\sum_{r\in G}b(r)\mathbf 1_{E_H}(r\omega).$$ We diagonalize it exactly by multiplicative characters. Its singular values are $$\left|\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)\right|  =  \left|  (1+\overline{\chi(-1)})  \sum_{n\le H}\overline{\chi(n)}  \right|.$$ Thus every odd character is an exact null mode. The principal singular value is $2H$, and $$\sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}  |\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)|^2  =2H(q-1-2H).$$ For nonprincipal even characters the Pólya–Vinogradov inequality gives $O(\sqrt q\log q)$.

Consequently, for arbitrary weights $a,b$ on $G$, the incidence bilinear form has the exact principal/nonprincipal decomposition $$\sum_{\omega,r}a(\omega)b(r)\mathbf 1_{E_H}(r\omega)  =  \frac{2H}{q-1}\Big(\sum_\omega a(\omega)\Big)  \Big(\sum_r b(r)\Big)  +\mathcal R_H(a,b),$$ with $$|\mathcal R_H(a,b)|  \ll\sqrt q\log q\,  \|a^\circ\|_2\|b^\circ\|_2.$$ For a frequency-free literal branch $\beta=(\theta,c,\kappa)$ with resolved $z$-fiber length $1\le N_\beta\le q_X$ and nonzero phase multiplier $\omega_\beta$, resonance is exactly $$r\omega_\beta\in E_{H_{q_X}(N_\beta)},\qquad  H_q(N)=\min\{(q-1)/2,\lfloor q/N\rfloor\}.$$ Branches with $N_\beta>q_X$ have no resonant nonzero frequency. This converts the resonant-sector census into a family of multiplicative spectral diagnostics and gives an explicit sufficient native-dispersion gate. A sharp delta-mass example proves that conductor $q_X$ alone gives no saving. The paper proves exact $\mathrm{L0}$ incidence theory and an $\mathrm{L1}$ literal certificate interface, but no new signed Möbius estimate or $\mathrm{L2}$ bound for the growing fixed-shift packet.

# Introduction

TPC-93 writes the literal low-frequency reconstruction term as a coherent sum of primitive fixed-determinant affine fibers [`\citep{WangTPC93}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-099.md#cite-2d5bd9665d7257b5). TPC-94 computes the exact additive conductor: on literal support it is $1$ or the no-wrap prime $q_X$ [`\citep{WangTPC94}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-099.md#cite-61d27506f94490b2). TPC-98 returns the entire conductor-one sector at the critical amplitude scale $X^{o(1)}Q_X^2$ [`\citep{WangTPC98}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-099.md#cite-b430ed4aad3480af). The next exceptional branch therefore has conductor $q_X$.

###### ref-7b8a55f06f26e768

High conductor is not the same as visible oscillation. If a frequency-free resolved branch $\beta=(\theta,c,\kappa)$ contains $N_\beta$ points in its $z$-fiber and its reduced phase is $$e_{q_X}(-r\omega_\beta z),  \qquad \omega_\beta\in\mathbb F_{q_X}^{\times},$$ then the archimedean resonance condition is $$N_\beta  \left\|\frac{r\omega_\beta}{q_X}\right\|  \le1. \label{eq:resonance-condition}$$ Even with $q_X\nmid r\omega_\beta$, this can hold throughout a short or arithmetically aligned interval.

The product $r\omega_\beta$ suggests using the multiplicative group $\mathbb F_{q_X}^{\times}$, not the additive Fourier transform that created the low window. The resonance window is symmetric about zero, and this symmetry has an exact spectral consequence: every odd multiplicative character is annihilated. The remaining even spectrum consists of classical incomplete character sums.

## Main contributions

This paper proves:

1.  the exact multiplicative convolution model for fixed resonance width $H$;

2.  its complete character spectrum, principal mode, odd-character nullspace, and Hilbert–Schmidt identity;

3.  exact principal/nonprincipal bilinear decompositions and $L^2$ bounds;

4.  a positive literal resonance majorant with lossless frequency-free key coverage, grouped by native phase multiplier and exact width;

5.  a sufficient spectral-dispersion condition that would return that carrier at $X^{o(1)}Q_X^2$; and

6.  sharp concentration examples showing why conductor and cardinality alone cannot prove the condition.

The carrier is a positive or modulus-weighted majorant. Actual affine Möbius sums can retain $r$-dependent phases and signed weights. We therefore do not claim that character diagonalization alone evaluates the signed resonant contribution.

## Why this is progress

Before this reduction, “resonance” was only a pointwise exception. The present result identifies its precise collective geometry. A future proof can now aim at one of two concrete targets: show that the native multiplier mass has a small principal component and controlled even-character spectrum, or exploit signed arithmetic before taking the incidence majorant. A proof-carrying polynomial obstruction to the displayed termwise bounds is a stop for that sufficient implementation, not a lower bound for the signed coefficient.

# From archimedean resonance to a product window

###### ref-f4890c2b8f8f805b

Let $q$ be an odd prime and identify each nonzero residue with its unique signed representative in $(-q/2,q/2)$. For $1\le H\le(q-1)/2$, define $$E_H  =  \{x\in\mathbb F_q^\times:|\widetilde x|\le H\}  =  \{\pm1,\ldots,\pm H\}. \label{eq:EH}$$ Thus $|E_H|=2H$.

###### ref-7d6d6171fc0ddf22

For $1\le N\le q$, put $$H_q(N)  =  \min\!\left\{\frac{q-1}{2},\left\lfloor\frac qN\right\rfloor\right\}. \label{eq:HqN}$$

###### ref-a6c24a8c9409cd93

> **Lemma: Exact resonance window** \[lem:exact-resonance-window\] Let $1\le N\le q$ and $r,\omega\in\mathbb F_q^\times$. Then $$N\left\|\frac{\widetilde{r\omega}}q\right\|\le1  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  r\omega\in E_{H_q(N)}.$$

> **Proof** For $N\ge2$, the left side is equivalent to $$|\widetilde{r\omega}|\le q/N.$$ The absolute residue is a positive integer, so this is equivalent to $|\widetilde{r\omega}|\le\lfloor q/N\rfloor=H_q(N)$. For $N=1$, both sides hold for every nonzero residue because $H_q(1)=(q-1)/2$.

> **Remark: The range $N>q$** If $N>q$, no nonzero residue can be resonant because $|\widetilde{r\omega}|\ge1>q/N$. Thus [\[lem:exact-resonance-window\]](TPC-099.md#ref-a6c24a8c9409cd93) also recovers the conductor–length bound from TPC-94.

###### ref-9918726c05b02f83

###### ref-a2a1d96a94f5db95

> **Definition: Resonance incidence operator** \[def:KH\] On $G=\mathbb F_q^\times$, set $$(K_Hb)(\omega)  =  \sum_{r\in G}b(r)\mathbf 1_{E_H}(r\omega). \label{eq:KH}$$ For $a,b:G\to\mathbb C$, define $$\mathcal I_H(a,b)  =  \sum_{\omega\in G}a(\omega)(K_Hb)(\omega). \label{eq:incidence-form}$$

###### ref-381e689468ee3dc4

> **Proposition: Biregularity and total incidence** \[prop:biregular\] The bipartite graph $$r\sim\omega  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  r\omega\in E_H$$ is $2H$-regular on both copies of $G$. In particular, for every $S\subseteq G$, $$\sum_{\omega\in G}  \#\{r\in S:r\omega\in E_H\}  =2H|S|.$$

> **Proof** For fixed $r$, multiplication by $r$ is a bijection and exactly $2H$ values of $\omega$ send $r\omega$ into $E_H$. The same argument with the roles reversed proves both statements.

# Exact multiplicative character spectrum

Let $\widehat G$ be the group of multiplicative characters of $G$. For $f:G\to\mathbb C$, use $$\widehat f(\chi)  =\sum_{x\in G}f(x)\overline{\chi(x)}.$$ The inverse and Parseval formulas are $$f(x)=\frac1{q-1}\sum_{\chi\in\widehat G}  \widehat f(\chi)\chi(x),  \qquad  \sum_\chi|\widehat f(\chi)|^2  =(q-1)\sum_x|f(x)|^2.$$

###### ref-f1e59a2710df1792

> **Theorem: Complete singular spectrum** \[thm:complete-spectrum\] The singular values of $K_H$ are $$\left|\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)\right|,  \qquad \chi\in\widehat G.$$ More precisely, $$K_H\chi  =  \left(\sum_{x\in E_H}\chi(x)\right)\overline\chi.$$

> **Proof** For $\omega\in G$, substitute $x=r\omega$: $$(K_H\chi)(\omega)  =\sum_{x\in E_H}\chi(x\omega^{-1})  =\overline{\chi(\omega)}   \sum_{x\in E_H}\chi(x).$$ Characters form an orthogonal basis, and conjugation permutes that basis. The claimed singular values follow.

###### ref-17b6312516f9f489

###### ref-7a2db5f8aec685cf

> **Theorem: Parity nullspace** \[thm:parity-nullspace\] For every multiplicative character, $$\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)  =  \bigl(1+\overline{\chi(-1)}\bigr)  \sum_{n=1}^H\overline{\chi(n)}. \label{eq:parity-factor}$$ Hence every odd character, characterized by $\chi(-1)=-1$, is an exact zero singular mode of $K_H$.

> **Proof** Split $E_H$ into $1,\ldots,H$ and their negatives. The negative half contributes $\overline{\chi(-1)}\sum_{n\le H}\overline{\chi(n)}$.

> **Remark: Not the sieve parity barrier** The nullspace in [\[thm:parity-nullspace\]](TPC-099.md#ref-17b6312516f9f489) is ordinary symmetry of a finite multiplicative convolution kernel. It is not a breach of the sieve parity barrier and gives no prime-pair lower bound.

###### ref-82127261d4a2d04d

###### ref-27e9773cc9d04247

> **Proposition: Principal and Hilbert–Schmidt ledgers** \[prop:HS-ledger\] For the principal character $\chi_0$, $$\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi_0)=2H.$$ Moreover $$\sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}  |\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)|^2  =  2H(q-1-2H). \label{eq:HS-ledger}$$

> **Proof** The first identity is $|E_H|=2H$. Parseval gives $$\sum_\chi|\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)|^2  =(q-1)|E_H|=2H(q-1).$$ Subtract the principal square $4H^2$.

###### ref-f1deeacaa7e83543

> **Corollary: Classical nonprincipal bound** \[cor:PV-bound\] Uniformly for $1\le H\le(q-1)/2$, $$\max_{\substack{\chi\ne\chi_0\\\chi(-1)=1}}  |\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)|  \ll\sqrt q\log q.$$ When the indexing set is empty (in particular for $q=3$), the maximum is understood to be $0$.

> **Proof** Apply the Pólya–Vinogradov inequality to the incomplete character sum in [\[eq:parity-factor\]](TPC-099.md#ref-7a2db5f8aec685cf) [`\citep[Chapter~12]{IK2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-099.md#cite-04595d3ffdacad91).

# Exact bilinear decompositions

For $f:G\to\mathbb C$, write $$\overline f=\frac1{q-1}\sum_{x\in G}f(x),  \qquad  f^\circ=f-\overline f\,\mathbf 1_G.$$

###### ref-f2baf789baeab8e4

> **Theorem: Principal/nonprincipal identity** \[thm:principal-nonprincipal\] For arbitrary $a,b:G\to\mathbb C$, $$\begin{aligned}  \mathcal I_H(a,b)  ={}&  \frac{2H}{q-1}  \left(\sum_\omega a(\omega)\right)  \left(\sum_r b(r)\right)  +\mathcal R_H(a,b), \label{eq:principal-decomposition}\\  \mathcal R_H(a,b)  ={}&  \frac1{q-1}  \sum_{\chi\ne\chi_0}  \widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)  \widehat a(\overline\chi)  \widehat b(\overline\chi). \label{eq:nonprincipal-exact}\end{aligned}$$ Only even nonprincipal characters contribute to [\[eq:nonprincipal-exact\]](TPC-099.md#ref-8832140485f7b7cc).

> **Proof** Insert multiplicative Fourier inversion for $\mathbf 1_{E_H}(r\omega)$ into [\[eq:incidence-form\]](TPC-099.md#ref-a2a1d96a94f5db95). The principal term is the first term of [\[eq:principal-decomposition\]](TPC-099.md#ref-f2baf789baeab8e4). The parity assertion follows from [\[thm:parity-nullspace\]](TPC-099.md#ref-17b6312516f9f489).

###### ref-eb71cded3d86ea9d

> **Corollary: Spectral norm bound** \[cor:spectral-bound\] Let $$M_H=\max_{\chi\ne\chi_0}  |\widehat{\mathbf 1_{E_H}}(\chi)|.$$ Then $$|\mathcal R_H(a,b)|  \le  M_H\|a^\circ\|_2\|b^\circ\|_2  \ll  \sqrt q\log q\,  \|a^\circ\|_2\|b^\circ\|_2. \label{eq:spectral-bilinear-bound}$$

> **Proof** Use Cauchy–Schwarz in [\[eq:nonprincipal-exact\]](TPC-099.md#ref-8832140485f7b7cc), followed by Parseval and [\[cor:PV-bound\]](TPC-099.md#ref-f1deeacaa7e83543). Odd characters already vanish.

###### ref-34065f1b531887ee

> **Proposition: Exact centered operator norm** \[prop:centered-operator\] $$\left\|  K_Hb-\frac{2H}{q-1}  \left(\sum_rb(r)\right)\mathbf 1_G  \right\|_2  \le M_H\|b^\circ\|_2.$$ The best constant is exactly $M_H$.

> **Proof** On the character basis, the centered operator deletes the principal mode and has the nonprincipal singular values from [\[thm:complete-spectrum\]](TPC-099.md#ref-f1e59a2710df1792). Their maximum is $M_H$, and an extremizing character gives equality.

> **Remark: Two distinct tasks** The principal term in [\[eq:principal-decomposition\]](TPC-099.md#ref-f2baf789baeab8e4) is a density term of size $2H/(q-1)$. The remainder is a multiplicative dispersion term. A proof must control both; a small nonprincipal spectrum cannot compensate for an unaffordable principal mass.

# The literal native-multiplier interface

## The frequency-free branch key

The resolved TPC-93 key $\xi=(\theta,c,\kappa,r)$ contains the low frequency $r$. It must not be inserted into a census and then paired with every frequency a second time. We therefore use the frequency-free TPC-94 branch $$\beta=(\theta,c,\kappa).$$ Let $\mathfrak B_X^{\mathrm{nc}}$ be the finite set of soluble, nonempty literal branches $\beta$ for which all inherited masks hold, $$c\mid\Omega_\beta,\qquad q_X\nmid\Omega_\beta.$$ Throughout this section every sum over $\beta$ is restricted to $\mathfrak B_X^{\mathrm{nc}}$. Here $\theta$ retains the polarization, opposite row, $\ell,j,\sigma,v$, interval component, actual masks, projector labels, and all continuous separation data. It fixes the reflected ultra family, not one value of the opened divisor: the latter is $U_\theta(t)$ along the affine fiber.

Let $A_{\beta,X}$ be the exact TPC-94 outer coefficient and let $$\mathcal A_{\beta,X}  =  \sum_{z\in I_\beta^\ast}  |\mathcal W_{\beta,X}(z)|  \mu^2(\widetilde D_\beta(z))  \mu^2(V_\beta(z)). \label{eq:uniform-branch-mass}$$ The exact branch sum satisfies $$|S_{\beta,X}(r)|\le\mathcal A_{\beta,X}  \quad\text{for every }r,$$ so the frequency-independent proof-carrying weight is $$w_{\beta,X}  =  |A_{\beta,X}|\,\mathcal A_{\beta,X}. \label{eq:base-branch-weight}$$ This is the literal multiplier-mass return of TPC-94, not a new uniformity assumption [`\citep{WangTPC94}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-099.md#cite-61d27506f94490b2).

# Sharp obstructions and stop tests

###### ref-465500c8097ccb64

\[ex:delta-obstruction\] Take $\omega=1$, $1\le R\le H$, and let $b$ be supported on $\{1,\ldots,R\}$ with nonnegative values. Then $$r\omega=r\in E_H$$ for every active frequency, so $$(K_Hb)(1)=\|b\|_1.$$ The phase has algebraic conductor $q$ for every nonzero $r$, yet the incidence gives no saving at all.

###### ref-f44629eed952171f

> **Proposition: No conductor-only density bound** \[prop:no-conductor-only\] There is no constant $C$, independent of $q,H$, such that $$(K_Hb)(\omega)  \le C\frac{H}{q}\|b\|_1$$ for all nonnegative $b$ and all $\omega\in G$.

> **Proof** Use [\[ex:delta-obstruction\]](TPC-099.md#ref-465500c8097ccb64) with $b=\mathbf 1_{\{1\}}$. The left side is $1$, whereas $CH/q$ can tend to $0$.

###### ref-f278f84eb0f38a02

\[ex:principal-concentration\] If $a\equiv A$ with $A\ge0$, and $b\ge0$, then $a^\circ=0$ and the nonprincipal remainder vanishes exactly, but $$\mathcal I_H(a,b)  =\frac{2H}{q-1}  \|a\|_1\sum_rb(r).$$ Thus perfect multiplicative equidistribution does not remove the principal density ledger.

###### ref-441ea6a964ace709

\[ex:odd-null\] If $a=\chi$ is an odd multiplicative character and $b$ is arbitrary, then the $\chi$-component of the resonance carrier is annihilated exactly. This is the strongest possible spectral cancellation, but the literal positive envelope $a_H$ need not lie in that subspace.

## Route decision

The incidence route continues only if a proof-carrying native census or theorem supplies both [\[eq:principal-gate\]](TPC-099.md#ref-cd9d2470436f907d) and [\[eq:dispersion-gate\]](TPC-099.md#ref-32b87afc8406c602) for this termwise implementation. A proved polynomial obstruction in the principal bound calls for restoring cancellation before that split, or for a genuinely filter-sensitive positive theorem. An obstruction in the centered even-character bound calls for a second native averaging variable, the signed route, or the full-block route. These are stop tests for the displayed sufficient method, not lower bounds for the original signed coefficient. No amount of repeating “conductor $q_X$” changes them.

# Exact finite regression

The script `experiments/tpc99_certificate.py` enumerates odd primes, all admissible widths $H$, and all multiplicative characters using a primitive root. It verifies:

1.  exact resonance-window equivalence;

2.  $2H$-biregularity and total edge counts;

3.  the parity factor [\[eq:parity-factor\]](TPC-099.md#ref-7a2db5f8aec685cf) and exact annihilation of every odd character;

4.  the principal singular value and Hilbert–Schmidt identity [\[eq:HS-ledger\]](TPC-099.md#ref-27e9773cc9d04247);

5.  direct incidence sums against their multiplicative Fourier reconstruction for deterministic complex test weights; and

The spectral comparison uses high-precision floating arithmetic only to evaluate roots of unity. Graph, degree, cardinality, and resonance assertions are checked by exact integer arithmetic; character parity and complex annihilation are checked numerically in the primitive-root character enumeration. The certificate is a finite regression, not an asymptotic character-sum or Möbius theorem.

## Unconditional results

-   The resonance condition is exactly a multiplicative product window $r\omega\in E_H$.

-   The resonance incidence graph is biregular and its complete singular spectrum is given by incomplete multiplicative character sums.

-   All odd characters are exact null modes; the principal and nonprincipal Hilbert–Schmidt masses are explicit.

-   Conductor $q_X$ alone cannot yield a pointwise or aggregate density saving without multiplier dispersion.

The finite-field results are $\mathrm{L0}$. The native census and sufficient-gate crosswalk are $\mathrm{L1}$ interfaces.

-   Neither [\[eq:principal-gate\]](TPC-099.md#ref-cd9d2470436f907d) nor [\[eq:dispersion-gate\]](TPC-099.md#ref-32b87afc8406c602) is proved for the growing literal packet.

-   Nothing is specialized to $h_0=2$.

Accordingly this paper sharpens the remaining door but does not claim an $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic advance.
