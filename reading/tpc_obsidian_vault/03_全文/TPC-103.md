## Abstract

TPC-101 reduces the diagonal part of the positive multiplicative resonance carrier to two inputs: the resolved principal mass and a pointwise cap $W_X$ for the normalized opened-atom weights. TPC-102 records $W_X\le X^{o(1)}$ as the first inexpensive gate after its three-ledger audit. We close that gate in the literal TPC-33–TPC-100 frame.

An invertible opened atom is $p=(\beta,z)$, with exact weight $$w_p  =  |A_{\beta,X}|\,|\mathcal W_{\beta,X}(z)|  \mu^2(\widetilde D_\beta(z))\mu^2(V_\beta(z)).$$ We pull this coefficient back through the lossless TPC-93 source–child inverse and place every factor in exactly one of six ledgers: physical rows, the opened logarithmic leg, the matched prefix, the row-gcd projector, exact content, and bounded local/smooth data. The literal row formula gives pointwise $O(\log X)$ coefficients; the logarithmic leg is $O(\log X)$; the matched prefixes, projector coefficients, and every remaining divisor expansion are bounded by a fixed power of the maximal divisor function on $X^{O(1)}$. No second column or fiber normalization occurs. Consequently, for a fixed integer $A$, $$\boxed{\quad  W_X:=\max_{p\ {\rm active}}w_p  \ll  (\log X)^A  \left(\max_{n\le X^{A}}\tau(n)\right)^A  =X^{o(1)}.\quad}$$ The maximum is over the actual $q_X\nmid u_p$ support and retains one prescribed $h_0\ne0$, both polarizations, all source, content, projector, interval and mask labels, and the inherited physical normalization.

Combining the cap with the TPC-101 transfer shows that the width-weighted opened-atom diagonal ledger follows from the principal ledger; no separate diagonal-energy theorem is needed. This is a genuine $\mathrm{L1}$ closure of Gate W, not an $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic saving. The principal mass, cross-atom collisions, generic signed Möbius sector, full-block return, and strict physical endpoint remain open.

# The first post-audit gate

###### ref-5898a9faeb145acf

At the critical TPC scales, $$Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},\qquad q_X\asymp Q_X.$$ After the conductor-one and $q_X\mid u$ branches have been returned, TPC-102 bounds the remaining positive resonant carrier by $$\mathcal C_{\mathrm{exc},X}^{\mathrm{abs}}  \ll  X^{o(1)}Q_X^2+  B_X\left[  \mathfrak P_X+  \sqrt{W_X|\mathcal H_X|(q_X-1)\mathfrak P_X}+\mathfrak X_X  \right]. \label{eq:master}$$ Here $B_X\ll1$ is the Wiener mass of the fixed low-window filter, $\mathfrak P_X$ is the principal resonance mass, $\mathfrak X_X$ is the width-weighted cross-atom excess, and $W_X$ is the maximum opened-atom weight on the actual invertible support [`\citep{WangTPC101,WangTPC102}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-9e9161a8530e65b2); [\[WangTPC102\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-7967ea1ba73edadd).

The purpose of this paper is deliberately narrow: prove the pointwise bound $$W_X\le X^{o(1)}$$ from the literal factor list. This is not a cancellation problem. It is nevertheless not a consequence of the row $\ell^1$ bound: an abstract vector of $\ell^1$-mass $Q_X$ may place all its mass on one coordinate. The actual TPC row coefficient has a much stronger pointwise formula, and that formula must survive the entire source–child crosswalk.

# The exact opened atom

## Native record

###### ref-6235e9bc93c47256

Let $$\beta=(\theta,c,\kappa),\qquad  \theta=(L,\gamma,\ell,j,\sigma,v,\iota)$$ be a soluble, nonempty nonconstant branch in the canonical TPC-94 ledger. Put $$b_\beta=c_\beta\kappa_\beta,\qquad  g_\beta=(b_\beta,\sigma_\beta),\qquad  B_\beta=b_\beta/g_\beta.$$ The resolved progression is $$t_\beta(z)=\tau_\beta+B_\beta z,\qquad  z\in I_\beta^\ast.$$ An $r$-free opened atom is $$p=(\beta,z),\qquad  u_p=U_\theta(t_\beta(z)),\qquad  m_p=M_\theta(t_\beta(z)),$$ and it obeys the exact source identity $$m_pj_\theta+h_0=\sigma_\theta u_p. \label{eq:source}$$ We restrict to the remaining invertible support $$1\le N_\beta:=\#I_\beta^\ast\le q_X,\qquad q_X\nmid u_p.$$ The restrictions are part of the actual support, not an ambient rectangular completion.

###### ref-2dbb51518e91db01

###### ref-145ed039db7e9968

TPC-94 and TPC-100 define $$\begin{aligned}  A_{\beta,X}  &=  c_{\theta,X}\mu(\kappa_\beta)\mu(B_\beta), \label{eq:outer}\\  w_p=w_{\beta,z}  &=  |A_{\beta,X}|\,  |\mathcal W_{\beta,X}(z)|  \mu^2(\widetilde D_\beta(z))  \mu^2(V_\beta(z)). \label{eq:weight}\end{aligned}$$ On canonical support the robustness content is one. Equation [\[eq:weight\]](TPC-103.md#ref-145ed039db7e9968) is the exact positive atomization: $$w_\beta=\sum_{z\in I_\beta^\ast}w_{\beta,z}.$$ There is no division by $N_\beta$, no cell-count normalization, and no second frequency factor [`\citep{WangTPC94,WangTPC100}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-61d27506f94490b2); [\[WangTPC100\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-3a25dec8d6d49e3b).

###### ref-9b23e7dbf3af0eeb

> **Definition: Literal atom cap** \[def:cap\] Let $\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}$ be the preceding actual support after the single inherited normalization. Set $$W_X=  \begin{cases}  \max_{p\in\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}}w_p,    &\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}\ne\varnothing,\\  0,&\mathscr P_X^{\mathrm{inv}}=\varnothing.  \end{cases}$$

## Why the word “normalized” introduces no new scalar

The physical units in [\[eq:weight\]](TPC-103.md#ref-145ed039db7e9968) are exactly those of the TPC-93 low-window coefficient. TPC-93 explicitly reassembles the two polarized physical rows and states that no new fiber normalization is introduced. Thus “normalized opened atom” means an atom of the already normalized physical coefficient, not the probability weight $w_p/\sum w_p$. In particular, this paper does not insert a factor $N_\beta^{-1}$, $Q_X^{-1}$, or a columnwise renormalization.

More explicitly, this is a crosswalk to the upstream formula, not a new schematic coefficient. The factors inside $c_{\theta,X}$ and $\mathcal W_{\beta,X}(z)$ are assigned to $\mathsf R_p,\mathsf U_p,\mathsf H_p,\mathsf V_p,\mathsf S_p$; $\mu(\kappa_\beta)\mu(B_\beta)$ and the two squarefree extraction factors are assigned to $\mathsf C_p$; and the active-divisibility indicator and inherited normalization are recorded once in $\mathsf S_p$. Unit phases disappear upon taking the modulus and are not additional positive-weight factors.

###### ref-0d3674576cf7ca6a

> **Lemma: Physical row factors** \[lem:rows\] Uniformly on the actual dyadic packet, $$|\mathsf R_p|\ll_{\mathscr D}(\log X)^2.$$

> **Proof** The literal TPC-25/TPC-33 row formula is $$\gamma_\alpha^{(i)}  =  \mu(d_\alpha)(\log\ell_\alpha)  \omega_D^{(i)}(d_\alpha)  \psi_L^{(i)}(\ell_\alpha/L)\zeta_\alpha^{(i)}.$$ The final three factors are fixed and bounded on the physical packet, while $\ell_\alpha\le X^{O(1)}$. Hence each row coefficient is $O_{\mathscr D}(\log X)$ [`\citep{WangTPC25,WangTPC33}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-39e8dc14c55a2411); [\[WangTPC33\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-3dc89b1d29e24761). In fact TPC-25 records directly $\|\gamma^{(i)}\|_\infty\ll\log(2L)$. Both polarizations use the same estimate.

###### ref-3853ea4f9df49a55

> **Lemma: Divisor-type factors** \[lem:divisor\] There are fixed constants $A_0,C_0>0$, depending only on the dyadic data and the fixed decomposition, such that $$|\mathsf U_p\mathsf H_p\mathsf V_p    \mathsf C_p\mathsf S_p|  \ll  (\log X)^{A_0}  \Delta_X^{A_0},  \qquad  \Delta_X:=\max_{1\le n\le X^{C_0}}\tau(n).$$

> **Proof** All effective targets, divisors, row coordinates, and content variables are at most $X^{C_0}$ for one fixed $C_0$. The opened logarithmic coefficient is at most $O(\log X)$. TPC-98 records the uniform bounds $$\sum_{\substack{u\mid N\\T_X<u\le U_{0,X}}}  |\mu(u)|\log u=X^{o(1)}$$ and $$|\mathsf H_m(j)|+  |\mathsf A_{m,T_X}(j)|+  |\mathsf A_{m,U_{0,X}}(j)|=X^{o(1)}$$ on the literal support [`\citep{WangTPC98}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-b430ed4aad3480af). More explicitly, these are bounded by a fixed product of powers of $\log X$ and divisor functions of effective integers.
>
> For the projector, $$\lambda_{G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}(v)  =  \sum_{\substack{d\mid v\\d\le G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}}\mu(v/d),$$ so $|\lambda_{G_X^{\mathrm{row}}}(v)|\le\tau(v)$. Every Möbius factor, squarefree indicator, and coprimality mask has modulus at most one. The fixed smooth and residue factors are bounded. The absolutely convergent separation representation has bounded total projective mass, so each of its retained coefficient atoms is bounded by that mass. The number of multiplicative factor types is fixed, giving the displayed envelope.

# Closure of Gate W

###### ref-957174c48725a813

###### ref-8a4ba0c975b15fa6

> **Theorem: Normalized opened-atom cap** \[thm:cap\] For the actual TPC-100 invertible opened-atom support, with the literal TPC-33–TPC-94 factors and the single inherited physical normalization, $$\boxed{  W_X  \ll_{\mathscr D,h_0}  (\log X)^A  \left(\max_{n\le X^A}\tau(n)\right)^A  =X^{o(1)}} \label{eq:cap}$$ for one fixed $A>0$.

> **Proof** If the support is empty there is nothing to prove. Otherwise apply \[[lem:rows](TPC-103.md#ref-0d3674576cf7ca6a),[lem:divisor](TPC-103.md#ref-3853ea4f9df49a55)\] to the exact partition [\[eq:six-factor\]](TPC-103.md#ref-c462fe1aa49709e0). The classical divisor estimate, uniformly for $n\le X^A$, is $$\tau(n)  \le  \exp\!\left(O_A\!\left(\frac{\log X}{\log\log X}\right)\right)  =X^{o(1)}.$$ A fixed product of this quantity and powers of $\log X$ remains $X^{o(1)}$. No count over rows, children, contents, or branches occurs in a pointwise maximum.

> **Remark: Uniformity and the fixed shift** The exponent $o(1)$ is uniform over the fixed dyadic packet data and the one prescribed $h_0$. The result is not an average over shifts. The condition $q_X\nmid u_p$ merely selects the actual invertible support and does not enlarge the maximum.

###### ref-b2083a4cee4a8b7b

> **Corollary: The diagonal ledger is no longer independent** \[cor:diagonal\] Let $$\mathfrak P_X=  \sum_H\frac{2H}{q_X-1}M_H^{\mathrm{inv}}.$$ Then the TPC-101 principal-to-diagonal transfer becomes $$\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}  \le  X^{o(1)}  \sqrt{|\mathcal H_X|(q_X-1)\mathfrak P_X}.$$ Consequently, if $$\mathfrak P_X\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,$$ then $$\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}  \le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.$$

> **Proof** Insert [\[thm:cap\]](TPC-103.md#ref-957174c48725a813) into the exact TPC-101 transfer $$\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{\mathfrak D_H^\circ}  \le  \sqrt{W_X|\mathcal H_X|(q_X-1)\mathfrak P_X}.$$ Use $|\mathcal H_X|\le(q_X-1)/2$ and $q_X\asymp Q_X$.

> **Remark: What was and was not removed** The corollary removes the need for a separate positive opened-atom diagonal theorem. It does not prove the principal bound, and it does not touch the distinct-atom cross ledger $\mathfrak X_X$. In particular, same-branch different-$z$ atoms still collide in that cross ledger.

# Why an $\ell^1$ row bound was insufficient

###### ref-a150a844d5f0c37c

> **Proposition: Sharp abstract obstruction** \[prop:l1-obstruction\] Knowledge only of $$\sum_\alpha|\gamma_\alpha^{(i)}|\le Q_X$$ cannot imply a subpower opened-atom cap. Indeed, there are nonnegative abstract row families satisfying this inequality for which one two-row atom has weight $Q_X^2$.

> **Proof** Take one active row in each polarization and give both rows coefficient $Q_X$; set all other row coefficients to zero and all local factors to one. Each $\ell^1$ bound is saturated, while the product atom has weight $Q_X^2$.

> **Remark: The counterexample is not physical** does not contradict [\[thm:cap\]](TPC-103.md#ref-957174c48725a813): it deliberately discards the explicit TPC-33 row formula. It explains why TPC-101 and TPC-102 correctly isolated Gate W instead of inferring it from an aggregate norm.

# Finite provenance regression

The deterministic script `experiments/tpc103_atom_cap_audit.py` performs three kinds of exact checks:

2.  the source-to-child and child-to-source round trip on both polarizations for finite primitive samples; and

The committed JSON records the counts and the claim boundary. These are regression tests for finite identities; they are not numerical evidence for an asymptotic Möbius estimate. The asymptotic input in [\[thm:cap\]](TPC-103.md#ref-957174c48725a813) is only the standard divisor bound.

# Route consequence and claim boundary

## Proved here

-   The exact opened atom has a six-ledger factor audit with no duplicated normalization.

-   Every literal pointwise factor is logarithmic, bounded, or divisor-function sized on $X^{O(1)}$.

-   The actual normalized cap satisfies $W_X=X^{o(1)}$.

-   Therefore the TPC-101 width-weighted diagonal gate follows from the unresolved principal gate.

The finite factor partition is $\mathrm{L0}$; its lossless attachment to the actual growing fixed-$h_0$ carrier and the resulting cap are $\mathrm{L1}$ bookkeeping progress.

## Not proved here

-   No bound for $\mathfrak P_X$ or $\mathfrak X_X$ is proved.

-   No positive-resonance closure, generic affine Möbius cancellation, determinant lower bound, zero-mode saving, high-frequency return, or TPC-18 full-block cover is proved.

-   No fixed-power saving for the complete growing fixed-$h_0$ coefficient is obtained; hence no $\mathrm{L2}$ event occurs.

-   No Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, parity breakthrough, or twin-prime conclusion is proved or implied.

The strict physical endpoint remains $$\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<\frac1{400}.$$ The present subpower cap spends no fixed exponent in that ledger. The next gate is the resolved principal-mass disintegration; the cross-map quotient comes only after that positive principal term is understood, in accordance with TPC-MVP1 [`\citep{WangTPCMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-103.md#cite-fdc0c8b1a307bb15).
