## Abstract

The positive multiplicative-resonance route contains the unresolved principal mass $$\mathfrak P_X  =  \sum_\beta  \frac{2H_{q_X}(N_\beta)}{q_X-1}  \sum_{\substack{z\in I_\beta^\ast\\q_X\nmid u_{\beta,z}}}  w_{\beta,z},  \qquad  H_q(N)=  \min\!\left\{\frac{q-1}{2},\left\lfloor\frac qN\right\rfloor\right\}.$$ We open this quantity before grouping equal widths or multipliers and obtain an exact endpoint/long-fiber disintegration.

For $N=1,2$, one has $$\frac{2H_q(N)}{q-1}=1,\qquad  g_q(H_q(N))=0.$$ Thus every remaining invertible atom on these fibers is purely principal and enters with its full physical weight. For $3\le N\le q$, the elementary but sharp uniform comparison $$\frac1N  \le  \frac{2\lfloor q/N\rfloor}{q-1}  \le  \frac{2q}{(q-1)N}$$ holds. If $$\mathfrak E_X  =  \sum_{\substack{\beta:N_\beta\le2\\z\in I_\beta^\ast\\                   q_X\nmid u_{\beta,z}}}w_{\beta,z},  \qquad  \mathfrak L_X  =  \sum_{\substack{\beta:3\le N_\beta\le q_X}}  \frac1{N_\beta}  \sum_{\substack{z\in I_\beta^\ast\\q_X\nmid u_{\beta,z}}}  w_{\beta,z},$$ then $$\boxed{  \mathfrak E_X+\mathfrak L_X  \le\mathfrak P_X  \le  \mathfrak E_X+  \frac{2q_X}{q_X-1}\mathfrak L_X.}$$ Consequently the principal gate is equivalent, up to absolute constants and in the same physical normalization, to controlling the actual endpoint mass and inverse-length mass separately.

This is an exact $\mathrm{L1}$ reduction, not a proof of either bound. We also give sharp positive countermodels showing that the $1/N_\beta$ factor alone cannot control a polynomial number of branches, and that a long-fiber estimate cannot return the $N=1,2$ mass. No $\mathrm{L2}$ fixed-shift estimate or parity breakthrough is claimed.

# The unresolved principal term

###### ref-f64a003e5d652850

TPC-99 represents nonconstant resonance by the incidence window $$E_H=\{\pm1,\ldots,\pm H\}\subset\mathbb F_q^\times.$$ TPC-100 then opens each frequency-free branch $\beta=(\theta,c,\kappa)$ into atoms $$p=(\beta,z),\qquad  w_{\beta,z}\ge0,\qquad  w_\beta=\sum_{z\in I_\beta^\ast}w_{\beta,z},$$ without changing its resolved length $$N_\beta=\#I_\beta^\ast$$ or its resonance width $H_q(N_\beta)$ [`\citep{WangTPC99,WangTPC100}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-104.md#cite-d0d31470086cb360); [\[WangTPC100\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-104.md#cite-3a25dec8d6d49e3b). After separately returning the $q_X\mid u_{\beta,z}$ submass, the remaining principal ledger is $$\mathfrak P_X  =  \sum_{\substack{\beta\\1\le N_\beta\le q_X}}  \rho_{q_X}(N_\beta)  \sum_{\substack{z\in I_\beta^\ast\\q_X\nmid u_{\beta,z}}}  w_{\beta,z}, \qquad  \rho_q(N):=\frac{2H_q(N)}{q-1}. \label{eq:P}$$ The frequency-filter factor $B_X=\|b_X\|_1\ll1$ is not included in $\mathfrak P_X$.

TPC-102 proposes opening [\[eq:P\]](TPC-104.md#ref-f64a003e5d652850) before grouping by equal width. That order matters. Grouping first hides the fact that full-width resonance occurs at $N=1,2$ and that the remaining coefficient is exactly comparable to $1/N$ [`\citep{WangTPC101,WangTPC102}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-104.md#cite-9e9161a8530e65b2); [\[WangTPC102\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-104.md#cite-7967ea1ba73edadd).

# The exact width coefficient

Let $q$ be an odd prime and $1\le N\le q$. Recall $$H_q(N)=  \min\!\left\{\frac{q-1}{2},\left\lfloor\frac qN\right\rfloor\right\}.$$

###### ref-fa6cd8dee47b3f47

> **Lemma: The $N=1,2$ full-width endpoint lengths** \[lem:endpoints\] For $N=1,2$, $$H_q(N)=\frac{q-1}{2},  \qquad  \rho_q(N)=1.$$ For $3\le N\le q$, $$H_q(N)=\left\lfloor\frac qN\right\rfloor.$$

For $q\ge5$, these are the only full-width lengths. At the degenerate prime $q=3$, $N=3$ also has $H_3(3)=(q-1)/2$; it is nevertheless included correctly in the $N\ge3$ inverse-length ledger below.

> **Proof** For $N=1$, the minimum in $H_q(N)$ is visibly $(q-1)/2$. Since $q$ is odd, $\lfloor q/2\rfloor=(q-1)/2$, proving the same assertion for $N=2$. If $N\ge3$, then $$\left\lfloor\frac qN\right\rfloor  \le\left\lfloor\frac q3\right\rfloor  \le\frac{q-1}{2}.$$

###### ref-35263500e19cef51

###### ref-fda87b0919d5c6a9

> **Lemma: Sharp inverse-length comparison** \[lem:comparison\] For every odd prime $q$ and $3\le N\le q$, $$\frac1N  \le\rho_q(N)  \le\frac{2q}{(q-1)N}  \le\frac3N. \label{eq:comparison}$$ The first inequality also holds for $N=1,2$.

> **Proof** Write $k=\lfloor q/N\rfloor$ and $q=kN+r$ with $0\le r<N$. Then $$2Nk-(q-1)=kN-r+1\ge N-r+1>0.$$ Thus $$\rho_q(N)=\frac{2k}{q-1}\ge\frac1N.$$ The upper bound follows from $k\le q/N$. Finally $2q/(q-1)\le3$ for $q\ge3$. At $N=1,2$, use [\[lem:endpoints\]](TPC-104.md#ref-fa6cd8dee47b3f47).

> **Remark: Optimal constants** The lower constant $1$ cannot be replaced uniformly by a number larger than $1$: for $N=(q+1)/2$, $$N\rho_q(N)=\frac{q+1}{q-1}\longrightarrow1.$$ The asymptotic upper constant $2$ is also sharp, for example at $N=q$, where $N\rho_q(N)=2q/(q-1)$.

# Resolved disintegration

For one branch define its actual remaining invertible mass $$M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}  =  \sum_{\substack{z\in I_\beta^\ast\\q_X\nmid u_{\beta,z}}}  w_{\beta,z}.$$ This may be smaller than the full branch mass $w_\beta$; no replacement by $N_\beta$ or an ambient count is made.

###### ref-a0bb73b7f14155d8

###### ref-03946ba32dccb312

###### ref-090dbfb299601291

> **Definition: Endpoint and inverse-length ledgers** \[def:ledgers\] Set $$\begin{aligned}  \mathfrak E_X  &=  \sum_{\substack{\beta\\N_\beta=1,2}}  M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}, \label{eq:E}\\  \mathfrak L_X  &=  \sum_{\substack{\beta\\3\le N_\beta\le q_X}}  \frac{M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}}{N_\beta}. \label{eq:L}\end{aligned}$$ We also write $$\mathfrak E_X=\mathfrak E_{1,X}+\mathfrak E_{2,X}$$ for the exact $N=1$ and $N=2$ subledgers.

###### ref-f742cfa15496256c

###### ref-61750144ab896be5

###### ref-ebcd343074fd9076

> **Theorem: Exact endpoint/long-fiber disintegration** \[thm:disintegration\] The actual principal mass satisfies the identity $$\mathfrak P_X  =  \mathfrak E_{1,X}+\mathfrak E_{2,X}  +  \sum_{\substack{\beta\\3\le N_\beta\le q_X}}  \frac{2\lfloor q_X/N_\beta\rfloor}{q_X-1}  M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}. \label{eq:exact-disintegration}$$ Consequently, $$\boxed{  \mathfrak E_X+\mathfrak L_X  \le\mathfrak P_X  \le  \mathfrak E_X+\frac{2q_X}{q_X-1}\mathfrak L_X  \le\mathfrak E_X+3\mathfrak L_X.} \label{eq:equivalence}$$

> **Proof** Insert [\[lem:endpoints\]](TPC-104.md#ref-fa6cd8dee47b3f47) into [\[eq:P\]](TPC-104.md#ref-f64a003e5d652850) for $N_\beta=1,2$, and insert its long-fiber formula for $N_\beta\ge3$. This gives [\[eq:exact-disintegration\]](TPC-104.md#ref-61750144ab896be5). Apply [\[lem:comparison\]](TPC-104.md#ref-35263500e19cef51) term by term to the nonnegative literal weights.

###### ref-ad949ff63b7a4dcc

> **Corollary: Equivalent formulation of Gate P** \[cor:gate\] The following are equivalent: $$\begin{aligned}  \mathfrak P_X&\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,\\  \mathfrak E_X+\mathfrak L_X&\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,\\  \mathfrak E_X&\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2  \quad\text{and}\quad  \mathfrak L_X\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.\end{aligned}$$ All three statements have the same quantifier scope and physical normalization.

> **Proof** Use [\[eq:equivalence\]](TPC-104.md#ref-ebcd343074fd9076), nonnegativity, and the fact that fixed constants are absorbed in $X^{o(1)}$.

> **Remark: What “equivalent” does not mean** is an exact reduction of one sufficient positive gate. It does not identify that gate with the original signed low-window sum. A failure of $\mathfrak E_X$ or $\mathfrak L_X$ may stop the termwise positive route while signed frequency or branch cancellation remains possible.

# The endpoint mass is exactly principal

TPC-101 defines $$g_q(H)=  \sqrt{\frac{2H(q-1-2H)}{q-1}}.$$

###### ref-6720b75b09abeb31

###### ref-ea3f038dde2b8858

> **Proposition: Exact $N=1,2$ return** \[prop:endpoint-return\] For $N_\beta=1,2$, $$g_{q_X}(H_{q_X}(N_\beta))=0.$$ Moreover, for the fixed positive frequency weight $b_X$, the endpoint contribution to the multiplicative incidence carrier is exactly $$B_X\mathfrak E_X,\qquad B_X=\sum_{r\in\mathbb F_{q_X}^\times}b_X(r). \label{eq:endpoint-carrier}$$

> **Proof** By [\[lem:endpoints\]](TPC-104.md#ref-fa6cd8dee47b3f47), $H=(q_X-1)/2$, so the displayed formula for $g_q$ vanishes. At this width, $$E_H=\mathbb F_{q_X}^\times.$$ Hence the incidence indicator is one for every pair $(r,\omega)$, and its bilinear form is the product of the two total masses. The opened census mass at these two lengths is $\mathfrak E_X$.

> **Remark: No centered theorem can pay the endpoint** The vanishing of $g_q(H)$ is not a saving on $\mathfrak E_X$. It says that the incidence operator has only its principal contribution at full width. Every surviving endpoint atom therefore has to be returned through [\[eq:endpoint-carrier\]](TPC-104.md#ref-ea3f038dde2b8858), with its full physical weight.

# Consequences of the atom cap

TPC-103 proves on the same actual support that $$W_X:=\max_{\beta,z}w_{\beta,z}=X^{o(1)}.$$ This is the literal pointwise result of TPC-103 [`\citep{WangTPC103}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-104.md#cite-fbc2555fc831323c). Let $$\mathcal B_{\le2,X}^{\mathrm{inv}}  =  \{\beta:N_\beta\le2,\ M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}>0\}, \qquad  \mathcal B_{\ge3,X}^{\mathrm{inv}}  =  \{\beta:3\le N_\beta\le q_X,\  M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}>0\}.$$

###### ref-26f48c936bb474d1

###### ref-358f7b5854020363

###### ref-7a68bbb82fff0dba

> **Proposition: Exact-count sufficient conditions** \[prop:count\] One has $$\begin{aligned}  \mathfrak E_X  &\le2W_X  |\mathcal B_{\le2,X}^{\mathrm{inv}}|, \label{eq:E-count}\\  \mathfrak L_X  &\le W_X  |\mathcal B_{\ge3,X}^{\mathrm{inv}}|. \label{eq:L-count}\end{aligned}$$ Thus a subpower atom cap plus an $X^{o(1)}Q_X^2$ bound for each actual branch census is sufficient for Gate P.

> **Proof** A branch of length $N_\beta$ contains at most $N_\beta$ opened atoms. Hence $$M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}\le N_\beta W_X.$$ For $N_\beta\le2$, sum this inequality directly. For $N_\beta\ge3$, divide by $N_\beta$ before summing.

> **Remark: Why this is not an ambient-count replacement** is only a sufficient corollary after the exact weighted identities have been proved. It does not authorize replacing $M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}$ by $N_\beta$ in [\[eq:exact-disintegration\]](TPC-104.md#ref-61750144ab896be5). A future proof should use the literal weights whenever they improve the crude branch census.

# Sharp positive obstructions

###### ref-97f0b984ebcf883a

> **Proposition: Inverse length does not eliminate branch count** \[prop:branch-count\] For every $M\ge1$ and $3\le N\le q$, there is an abstract nonnegative resolved census with $M$ branches of length $N$, unit atom cap, and $$\mathfrak L_X=M,\qquad  \mathfrak P_X=M\,N\rho_q(N)\asymp M.$$ Thus the $1/N$ factor alone gives no bound independent of the number of active branches.

> **Proof** Give every branch exactly $N$ atoms of weight one. Then $M_\beta^{\mathrm{inv}}=N$, so its contribution to $\mathfrak L_X$ is one. The formula for $\mathfrak P_X$ follows from [\[eq:P\]](TPC-104.md#ref-f64a003e5d652850), and [\[lem:comparison\]](TPC-104.md#ref-35263500e19cef51) bounds $N\rho_q(N)$ between $1$ and $3$.

###### ref-f8ec9846eecb705e

> **Proposition: Long fibers cannot return the endpoints** \[prop:endpoint-obstruction\] For every $M\ge1$ there is an abstract nonnegative census with unit atom cap and $M$ one-point branches such that $$\mathfrak L_X=0,\qquad\mathfrak E_X=\mathfrak P_X=M.$$

> **Proof** Take $M$ branches with $N_\beta=1$, one atom of weight one on each, and apply [\[lem:endpoints\]](TPC-104.md#ref-fa6cd8dee47b3f47).

> **Remark: Scope of the countermodels** The two constructions are $\mathrm{L0}$ sharpness examples for the information used in a positive inequality. They do not assert that the actual TPC carrier contains such a family and are not stop certificates for the physical route. They prove that an actual mass or branch-incidence theorem is still needed.

# Route decision and claim boundary

## Established

-   The $N=1,2$ invertible mass is returned exactly as a purely principal term.

-   The long principal coefficient is uniformly and sharply comparable to $1/N_\beta$.

-   Gate P is equivalent to the pair of actual weighted bounds $\mathfrak E_X,\mathfrak L_X\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2$.

-   The TPC-103 cap reduces crude control of those ledgers to actual branch censuses, but does not prove the censuses.

These are $\mathrm{L0}$ coefficient identities and an $\mathrm{L1}$ lossless reduction on the actual fixed-$h_0$ carrier.

## Not established

-   Neither $\mathfrak E_X$ nor $\mathfrak L_X$ is bounded at the target scale for the growing physical packet.

-   No polynomial lower bound for either actual ledger is proved, so the positive route is not declared stopped.

-   No cross-map estimate, signed affine Möbius cancellation, full-block return, or strict endpoint closure is proved.

-   No $\mathrm{L2}$ fixed-power saving, parity breakthrough, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime conclusion follows.

The next exact task is to quotient identical affine maps without losing their input profile or source provenance. Only after that quotient is lossless should the genuinely distinct-map incidence be attacked, as prescribed by TPC-MVP1 [`\citep{WangTPCMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-104.md#cite-fdc0c8b1a307bb15).
