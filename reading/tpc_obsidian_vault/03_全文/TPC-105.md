## Abstract

TPC-100 linearizes every remaining invertible opened atom as $$\omega_p=A_p(m_pj_p+h_0)  \quad\text{in }\mathbb F_{q_X}^{\times},$$ but its maximally literal fine cells are singletons. Thus all distinct-atom collisions remain in the cross-cell ledger, and different cells may induce the same affine map. We construct the canonical quotient required before a genuine cross-map estimate is attempted.

Attach to an atom $p$ the map $$\phi_p(x)=s_px+t_p,\qquad  s_p=A_pm_p,\quad t_p=A_ph_0.$$ Atoms are equivalent when their pairs $(s_p,t_p)$ agree. A lossless quotient record consists of the full recoverable provenance bag together with the input profile $$\nu_\phi(j)=  \sum_{\substack{p:\phi_p=\phi\\j_p=j}}w_p.$$ The profile, not merely the total map weight, is necessary and sufficient for the positive multiplier census: $$a_H^{\mathrm{inv}}(\omega)  =  \sum_\phi\sum_j  \nu_\phi(j)\mathbf 1_{\{\phi(j)=\omega\}}.$$ We give a two-point counterexample proving that map totals alone lose the census.

Because $h_0,A_p,m_p$ are nonzero modulo the strong no-wrap prime, two maps agree exactly when $A_p=A_{p'}$ and $m_p=m_{p'}$ modulo $q_X$; no-wrap upgrades the latter to equality of the physical row integers. Within one map, output collision is exactly equality of the input coordinates. Consequently the TPC-100 centered cross ledger splits identically as $$\mathfrak X_H^\circ  =  \mathfrak K_H^\circ+\mathfrak Y_H^\circ,$$ where $\mathfrak K_H^\circ$ contains identical maps and $\mathfrak Y_H^\circ$ contains genuinely distinct maps.

If $M_{H,\phi}$ is the mass of one map class, $U_X=\max_{H,\phi}M_{H,\phi}$, and $M_H$ is the total width mass, then $$|\mathfrak K_H^\circ|\le U_XM_H,$$ and therefore $$\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)\sqrt{|\mathfrak K_H^\circ|}  \le  \sqrt{U_X|\mathcal H_X|(q_X-1)\mathfrak P_X}.$$ This is an exact $\mathrm{L1}$ reduction. It exposes a new identical-map concentration ledger $U_X$ and leaves the width-weighted distinct-map term as the honest TPC-106 geometric door. Neither ledger is bounded here at the growing physical scale; no $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic saving or parity breakthrough is claimed.

# Why a quotient is needed

###### ref-829d8ea9b38b5dc7

Fix an exact resonance width $H$. TPC-100 partitions the actual invertible opened atoms into maximally literal fine cells. Because each cell retains $m,u,\sigma$, the source identity $$mj+h_0=\sigma u$$ recovers $j$; every fine cell is therefore a singleton. The resulting multiplier census is $$a_H^{\mathrm{inv}}(\omega)  =  \sum_{\substack{p:\,\mathsf H_p=H\\q_X\nmid u_p}}  w_p\mathbf 1_{\{\omega_p=\omega\}}, \label{eq:census}$$ where $w_p\ge0$ is the exact physical atom weight [`\citep{WangTPC100}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-105.md#cite-3a25dec8d6d49e3b).

The centered energy has an atomic diagonal part and a cross part $$\|(a_H^{\mathrm{inv}})^\circ\|_2^2  =  \mathfrak D_H^\circ+\mathfrak X_H^\circ.$$ TPC-103 closes the maximum atom cap, so the diagonal part follows from the unresolved principal mass [`\citep{WangTPC101,WangTPC103}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-105.md#cite-9e9161a8530e65b2); [\[WangTPC103\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-105.md#cite-fbc2555fc831323c). The cross part remains. Before applying an incidence theorem to it, TPC-102 and TPC-MVP1 require identical affine maps to be aggregated without losing source recovery or exact weight [`\citep{WangTPC102,WangTPCMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-105.md#cite-7967ea1ba73edadd); [\[WangTPCMVP1\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-105.md#cite-fdc0c8b1a307bb15).

The subtle point is that an affine map is an operator on an input, not merely a multiplier value. Two atoms may carry the same map but occur at different $j$’s. Their total class mass does not determine where that mass is sent.

# Literal affine maps

###### ref-1a0c2a64dcd3bb10

Write $q=q_X$. For all sufficiently large $X$, the strong no-wrap choice gives $$q>|h_0|,\qquad 0<m_p<q,\qquad q\nmid u_p.$$ TPC-100 defines $$A_p=  \varepsilon_p\ell_pv_pB_p(c_pu_p)^{-1}  \in\mathbb F_q^\times$$ and proves $$\omega_p=A_p(m_pj_p+h_0). \label{eq:linearization}$$

###### ref-c68f6ccd1e5a070a

> **Definition: Literal affine-map record** \[def:map\] For an active atom $p$, define $$s_p=A_pm_p,\qquad t_p=A_ph_0,\qquad  \phi_p(x)=s_px+t_p.$$ Thus $s_p,t_p\in\mathbb F_q^\times$ and $\omega_p=\phi_p(j_p)$.

###### ref-a11054014bd3b7f8

> **Lemma: Exact identical-map classification** \[lem:classification\] For two active atoms $p,p'$, $$\phi_p=\phi_{p'}  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  A_p=A_{p'}\ \text{and}\ m_p=m_{p'}  \quad\text{in }\mathbb F_q.$$ Under the strong no-wrap bounds, equality of $m_p,m_{p'}$ in $\mathbb F_q$ is equality as physical integers.

> **Proof** Equality of the constant terms gives $$h_0(A_p-A_{p'})=0.$$ Since $q\nmid h_0$, this forces $A_p=A_{p'}$. Equality of the slopes and invertibility of $A_p$ then force $m_p=m_{p'}$. The converse is immediate. Finally both physical row integers lie strictly between $0$ and $q$ [`\citep{WangTPC98}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-105.md#cite-b430ed4aad3480af).

###### ref-366d133422591db9

> **Corollary: Collision inside one map** \[cor:same-map-collision\] If $\phi_p=\phi_{p'}$, then $$\omega_p=\omega_{p'}  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  j_p=j_{p'}\pmod q.$$ Since the actual orbit window has diameter below $q$, this is equivalent to equality of the physical integers $j_p=j_{p'}$.

> **Proof** The common slope $s_p$ is nonzero, so $\phi_p(j_p)=\phi_p(j_{p'})$ exactly when $j_p=j_{p'}$ modulo $q$. Apply no-wrap.

> **Remark: What identical maps may still hide** Equality of $(A,m,j)$ does not identify the full atom. Different content or projector children, complementary factorizations, masks, polarizations, or outer source labels may still contribute separate weights. Those records may not be discarded.

# The canonical provenance quotient

Let $\mathscr P_H$ be the finite set of active invertible atoms at width $H$. Define $$p\sim p'  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad  (s_p,t_p)=(s_{p'},t_{p'}).$$ Let $\Phi_H$ be the set of equivalence classes, identified with their common affine maps.

###### ref-c3a8c9ac0a23294b

###### ref-06bd9e7cb24103d7

###### ref-879ae4481942c063

> **Definition: Provenance bag and input profile** \[def:profile\] For $\phi\in\Phi_H$, let $$\mathscr B_\phi  =  \{(p,w_p,\mathfrak r_p):\phi_p=\phi\},$$ where $\mathfrak r_p$ is the complete TPC-100 augmented source and child record. Define $$\begin{aligned}  \nu_\phi(j)  &=  \sum_{\substack{p\in\mathscr P_H\\                   \phi_p=\phi,\ j_p=j}}w_p, \label{eq:profile}\\  M_{H,\phi}  &=  \sum_j\nu_\phi(j),\qquad  M_H=\sum_{\phi\in\Phi_H}M_{H,\phi}. \label{eq:class-mass}\end{aligned}$$ The canonical quotient record is $$\mathscr Q_H  =  \{(\phi,\nu_\phi,\mathscr B_\phi):\phi\in\Phi_H\}.$$

###### ref-d2ba6efa5c280998

###### ref-090dd2d03822c80a

> **Theorem: Lossless quotient and exact census** \[thm:quotient\] The classes $\mathscr B_\phi$ form a disjoint partition of the actual atoms, preserve every physical weight exactly once, and give $$\boxed{  a_H^{\mathrm{inv}}(\omega)  =  \sum_{\phi\in\Phi_H}\sum_{j\in\mathbb F_q}  \nu_\phi(j)\mathbf 1_{\{\phi(j)=\omega\}}.} \label{eq:quotient-census}$$ The analytic data $(\phi,\nu_\phi)$ recover the complete positive multiplier census, while the attached bags recover every original source atom and coefficient.

> **Proof** Every atom has one and only one pair $(s_p,t_p)$, so the bags form a partition. Reindex [\[eq:census\]](TPC-105.md#ref-829d8ea9b38b5dc7) first by that pair and then by $j_p$. This gives [\[eq:quotient-census\]](TPC-105.md#ref-090dd2d03822c80a) and $$\sum_{\phi,j}\nu_\phi(j)=\sum_pw_p=M_H.$$ No weight is created or deleted. Source recovery follows because the full augmented record $\mathfrak r_p$ remains in the corresponding bag.

###### ref-53e9bea318093e2c

> **Proposition: Map totals alone are not lossless** \[prop:mass-only\] The collection $\{(\phi,M_{H,\phi})\}$ does not determine $a_H^{\mathrm{inv}}$, even for one invertible affine map and one unit atom.

> **Proof** Take $q\ge5$, $h_0=1$, and $\phi(x)=x+1$. One unit atom at $j=0$ gives the census $\delta_1$, while one unit atom at $j=1$ gives $\delta_2$. Both quotient records have the same map and total mass one. Their input profiles, and hence their multiplier censuses, differ.

> **Remark: Minimal analytic payload** The proposition shows why “aggregate identical maps with exact total weight” is incomplete unless the input distribution is also retained. The profile $\nu_\phi$ is sufficient for every positive incidence calculation. The provenance bag is additionally required for literal source recovery and later signed reconstruction.

# Exact splitting of the cross ledger

###### ref-e4d40b4a1f68b18b

Since the TPC-100 fine cells are singleton, its centered cross ledger can be written directly as $$\mathfrak X_H^\circ  =  \sum_{\substack{p,p'\in\mathscr P_H\\p\ne p'}}  w_pw_{p'}  \left(  \mathbf 1_{\{\omega_p=\omega_{p'}\}}-\frac1{q-1}  \right). \label{eq:cross}$$ The sum is ordered, matching the $C\ne D$ convention in TPC-100.

###### ref-48eb7a0b914832ca

###### ref-bd6053294379b47b

###### ref-26417fcfaa1fb031

> **Definition: Identical- and distinct-map ledgers** \[def:split\] Let $$\begin{aligned}  \mathfrak K_H^\circ  &=  \sum_{\substack{p\ne p'\\\phi_p=\phi_{p'}}}  w_pw_{p'}  \left(  \mathbf 1_{\{\omega_p=\omega_{p'}\}}-\frac1{q-1}  \right), \label{eq:K}\\  \mathfrak Y_H^\circ  &=  \sum_{\substack{p,p'\\\phi_p\ne\phi_{p'}}}  w_pw_{p'}  \left(  \mathbf 1_{\{\omega_p=\omega_{p'}\}}-\frac1{q-1}  \right). \label{eq:Y}\end{aligned}$$

###### ref-2ab59a200f2f816f

###### ref-b08e3e4799765a77

###### ref-c03cded69425e3db

###### ref-478cc7a6ada9879c

> **Theorem: Canonical collision split** \[thm:split\] One has the exact identity $$\boxed{  \mathfrak X_H^\circ  =  \mathfrak K_H^\circ+\mathfrak Y_H^\circ.} \label{eq:split}$$ Moreover, put $$S_{\phi,j}  =  \sum_{\substack{p:\phi_p=\phi\\j_p=j}}w_p^2, \qquad  S_\phi=\sum_jS_{\phi,j}.$$ Then the identical-map term has the profile formula $$\boxed{  \mathfrak K_H^\circ  =  \sum_{\phi,j}  \left(\nu_\phi(j)^2-S_{\phi,j}\right)  -  \frac1{q-1}  \sum_\phi  \left(M_{H,\phi}^2-S_\phi\right).} \label{eq:K-profile}$$ The distinct-map term is $$\boxed{  \mathfrak Y_H^\circ  =  \sum_{\substack{\phi,\psi\in\Phi_H\\\phi\ne\psi}}  \sum_{j,k}  \nu_\phi(j)\nu_\psi(k)  \left(  \mathbf 1_{\{\phi(j)=\psi(k)\}}-\frac1{q-1}  \right).} \label{eq:Y-profile}$$

> **Proof** The alternatives $\phi_p=\phi_{p'}$ and $\phi_p\ne\phi_{p'}$ partition every ordered pair $p\ne p'$, giving [\[eq:split\]](TPC-105.md#ref-b08e3e4799765a77). Within one map, [\[cor:same-map-collision\]](TPC-105.md#ref-366d133422591db9) replaces output equality by $j_p=j_{p'}$. At fixed $(\phi,j)$, the mass of ordered distinct pairs is $$\nu_\phi(j)^2-S_{\phi,j}.$$ Across all inputs of the same map, the total ordered distinct-pair mass is $$M_{H,\phi}^2-S_\phi.$$ This proves [\[eq:K-profile\]](TPC-105.md#ref-c03cded69425e3db). Reindexing pairs from two different classes by their two input coordinates gives [\[eq:Y-profile\]](TPC-105.md#ref-478cc7a6ada9879c).

> **Remark: Centering may couple the two terms** Neither $\mathfrak K_H^\circ$ nor $\mathfrak Y_H^\circ$ is necessarily nonnegative. Their exact sum is the native cross excess. Bounding their absolute values separately is a sufficient route and may lose cancellation; it is not asserted to be necessary.

# The identical-map concentration ledger

###### ref-ea03bc2850f2387f

> **Definition: Maximum map-class mass** \[def:U\] Set $$U_H=\max_{\phi\in\Phi_H}M_{H,\phi}, \qquad  U_X=\max_HU_H,$$ with zero used for an empty carrier.

###### ref-a8e9eb72f1331f61

###### ref-1a8681260a9a0ad1

> **Theorem: Identical-map concentration bound** \[thm:K-bound\] For every width $H$, $$\boxed{  |\mathfrak K_H^\circ|  \le  \sum_{\phi\in\Phi_H}  \left(M_{H,\phi}^2-S_\phi\right)  \le U_HM_H.} \label{eq:K-bound}$$

> **Proof** For one ordered pair, the centered collision kernel is either $$1-\frac1{q-1}  \quad\text{or}\quad  -\frac1{q-1},$$ so its modulus is at most one. Summing the weights of all ordered distinct pairs inside identical-map classes gives the first bound. For the second, $$\sum_\phi(M_{H,\phi}^2-S_\phi)  \le\sum_\phi M_{H,\phi}^2  \le U_H\sum_\phi M_{H,\phi}  =U_HM_H.$$

Let $$\mathcal H_X=  \{H:g_{q_X}(H)>0,\ M_H>0\}, \qquad  \mathfrak P_X=\sum_H\frac{2H}{q_X-1}M_H.$$

###### ref-6a1a3f62b30ee7f2

###### ref-55cd223e51d67dd9

> **Corollary: Width-weighted transfer** \[cor:transfer\] The identical-map ledger obeys $$\boxed{  \sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)  \sqrt{|\mathfrak K_H^\circ|}  \le  \sqrt{  U_X|\mathcal H_X|(q_X-1)\mathfrak P_X}.} \label{eq:transfer}$$ Hence, if $$q_X\asymp Q_X,\qquad  U_X\le X^{o(1)},\qquad  \mathfrak P_X\le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2,$$ then the left side of [\[eq:transfer\]](TPC-105.md#ref-55cd223e51d67dd9) is $X^{o(1)}Q_X^2$.

> **Proof** By [\[thm:K-bound\]](TPC-105.md#ref-a8e9eb72f1331f61), $$\sum_Hg_q(H)\sqrt{|\mathfrak K_H^\circ|}  \le  \sqrt{U_X}\sum_{H\in\mathcal H_X}  g_q(H)\sqrt{M_H}.$$ Cauchy–Schwarz and $g_q(H)^2\le2H$ give $$\le  \sqrt{  U_X|\mathcal H_X|  \sum_H2HM_H}  =  \sqrt{  U_X|\mathcal H_X|(q-1)\mathfrak P_X}.$$ Use $|\mathcal H_X|\le(q-1)/2$.

###### ref-3f9541d033616b13

###### ref-00f94b1c101e9982

> **Corollary: Exact sufficient distinct-map reduction** \[cor:distinct\] Suppose the hypotheses in [\[cor:transfer\]](TPC-105.md#ref-6a1a3f62b30ee7f2) hold and $$\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)  \sqrt{|\mathfrak Y_H^\circ|}  \le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2. \label{eq:distinct-target}$$ Then the complete width-weighted cross gate satisfies $$\sum_Hg_{q_X}(H)  \sqrt{|\mathfrak X_H^\circ|}  \le X^{o(1)}Q_X^2.$$

> **Proof** Use $\mathfrak X_H^\circ=\mathfrak K_H^\circ+ \mathfrak Y_H^\circ$ and $$\sqrt{|x+y|}\le\sqrt{|x|}+\sqrt{|y|}.$$ Then apply [\[cor:transfer\]](TPC-105.md#ref-6a1a3f62b30ee7f2) and [\[eq:distinct-target\]](TPC-105.md#ref-00f94b1c101e9982).

# Two exact nonimplications

###### ref-a9ec3977c912a913

> **Proposition: Atom cap does not imply map-class cap** \[prop:atom-vs-map\] For every $R\ge1$, there is an abstract positive opened census with $$W_X=1,\qquad U_X=R.$$ It can be supported on one invertible affine map.

> **Proof** Take $R$ distinct provenance records, each of weight one, and attach all of them to the same map $\phi(x)=x+1$. Their map-class mass is $R$, while every atom has weight one.

###### ref-7e76155446ce7403

> **Proposition: A quotient does not create dispersion** \[prop:no-dispersion\] Canonical aggregation preserves the census and its centered energy exactly. In particular, no bound for $\mathfrak Y_H^\circ$ or $U_X$ follows from the quotient identity alone.

> **Proof** Equation [\[eq:quotient-census\]](TPC-105.md#ref-090dd2d03822c80a) is an equality with the original census. Therefore its $\ell^2$ norm and centered energy are unchanged. shows that one quotient class may have arbitrary positive mass in the abstract information model.

> **Remark: Physical status** The countermodels prove nonimplications between abstract ledgers. They do not establish polynomial concentration in the actual TPC coefficient and hence do not stop the physical positive route. A future theorem may use the exact classification in [\[lem:classification\]](TPC-105.md#ref-a11054014bd3b7f8) to bound actual class multiplicity or weight.

# Route consequence and claim boundary

## Established

-   Identical affine maps are classified exactly by the pair $(A_p,m_p)$, with physical integer recovery for $m_p$.

-   The canonical quotient retains the input profile needed for the positive census and the full bag needed for source recovery.

-   The TPC-100 cross ledger splits exactly into identical- and distinct-map terms.

-   The identical-map term is controlled by the new class-mass ledger $U_X$, and [\[cor:distinct\]](TPC-105.md#ref-3f9541d033616b13) gives a precise sufficient door for TPC-106.

These are $\mathrm{L0}$ algebraic identities and an $\mathrm{L1}$ provenance-preserving reduction on the actual fixed-$h_0$ carrier.

## Not established

-   No bound $U_X\le X^{o(1)}$ is proved for the actual carrier.

-   The principal bounds isolated in TPC-104 remain unproved [`\citep{WangTPC104}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-105.md#cite-b5b9dc7cf6113ec6).

-   The distinct-map target [\[eq:distinct-target\]](TPC-105.md#ref-00f94b1c101e9982) is not proved.

-   No signed affine Möbius cancellation, full physical reassembly, strict $1/400$ endpoint closure, or $\mathrm{L2}$ fixed-power saving is obtained.

-   No parity breakthrough, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime conclusion is proved or implied.

The honest next step is width-weighted incidence on the genuine distinct-map quotient, with $U_X$ carried as a separate concentration gate. If either has a literal polynomial obstruction, the response is to retain the signed filter before majorization, not to claim that the original signed coefficient is large.
