## Abstract

The positive TPC resonance route takes absolute values before the low-window reconstruction filter. This is safe, but TPC-106 shows that distinct affine maps can remain perfectly aligned. We return to the exact TPC-93 expansion and move the filter ahead of majorization.

For the completely opened, frequency-free literal atoms $p$, let $a_p$ retain the source coefficient, both Möbius signs, the extracted $\mu(B)$, all masks and weights, and the one physical normalization. Let $\psi_p(r)$ be the exact unit phase, $\omega_p$ the native multiplier, and $H_p$ the inherited resonance width. With the signed reconstruction multiplier $\mathfrak m_X(r)$, the resonant contribution is exactly $$\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}  =  \sum_r\mathfrak m_X(r)  \sum_p a_p\psi_p(r)  \mathbf 1_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p).$$ Finite reordering gives the filter-first identity $$\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}  =  \sum_pa_p\mathcal K_p,\qquad  \mathcal K_p  =  \sum_r\mathfrak m_X(r)\psi_p(r)  \mathbf 1_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p).$$ Consequently the filtered-atom majorant $\sum_p|a_p||\mathcal K_p|$ is always no larger than the pre-filter positive carrier.

We also identify the canonical unfiltered signed frequency energy $$\mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}  =  \sum_r\left|  \sum_pa_p\psi_p(r)\mathbf 1_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p)  \right|^2$$ and prove its exact positive-semidefinite $TT^*$ Gram formula. Thus $$|\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}|  \le\|\mathfrak m_X\|_2  \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}^{1/2}.$$ Two sharp examples delimit the result. Signed atoms can cancel completely while the positive carrier is nonzero; conversely, a phase gauge can saturate the positive carrier. Hence filtering before majorization preserves a real cancellation channel but does not manufacture cancellation. The reconstruction and inequalities are $\mathrm{L0}$, and their imported literal dictionary is $\mathrm{L1}$. The growing fixed-$h_0$ bound for $\mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}$ is a new $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic gate and is not proved.

# Why the order of operations matters

TPC-93 exports the complete low window into fixed-determinant affine fibers while retaining the two Möbius signs, content extraction, both polarizations, exact phases, and provenance [`\citep{WangTPC93}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-107.md#cite-2d5bd9665d7257b5). TPC-99–TPC-102 then construct a positive resonance carrier by taking absolute values and derive exact finite-group bounds for it [`\citep{WangTPC99,WangTPC102}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-107.md#cite-d0d31470086cb360); [\[WangTPC102\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-107.md#cite-7967ea1ba73edadd). This route is sufficient, not equivalent to the signed low window.

TPC-105 removes identical-map duplication, while TPC-106 proves that pairwise distinct maps can still align perfectly [`\citep{WangTPC105,WangTPC106}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-107.md#cite-2fd91988ccb7887e); [\[WangTPC106\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-107.md#cite-4b3822ed37938691). The prescribed fallback in TPC-MVP1 is therefore $$\text{signed filter}  \longrightarrow  \text{arithmetic estimate}  \longrightarrow  \text{majorization},$$ with no early deletion of phases or Möbius signs [`\citep{WangTPCMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-107.md#cite-fdc0c8b1a307bb15). The purpose of this paper is to make that fallback exact.

# The frequency-free literal atom dictionary

Let $q=q_X$ and $G=\mathbb F_q^\times$. Extend every frequency weight by zero outside the actual low window. Let $\mathcal P_X$ be the finite set of fully opened, frequency-free atoms in the unresolved $q_X$-invertible nonconstant carrier with inherited branch length $1\le N_p\le q_X$. Branches with $N_p>q_X$ have no resonant nonzero frequency and remain in the separate generic return. The conductor-one and $q_X\mid u$ pieces may be absent only because their disjoint exact returns were already recorded in TPC-98 and TPC-100 [`\citep{WangTPC102}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-107.md#cite-7967ea1ba73edadd). An atom $p\in\mathcal P_X$ retains:

-   the source row, polarization, opposite row, interval component, content $c$, projector $\kappa$, extracted factor $B$, and resolved point $z$;

-   the two reduced affine values $$D_p=d_p+s_pz,\qquad V_p=u_p+a_p'z,  \qquad s_pu_p-a_p'd_p=h_0\ne0;$$

-   the factors $\mu(\kappa)\mu(B)\mu(D_p)\mu(V_p)$, $\mathbf 1_{(B,V_p)=1}$, every inherited mask, and the exact physical weight;

-   the native multiplier $\omega_p\in G$, inherited width $H_p$, and every $r$-dependent unit phase.

To avoid a collision of notation, write $\alpha_p\in\mathbb C$ for the complete $r$-independent signed coefficient of the atom, including one copy of the global normalization. Write $\psi_p:G\to\mathbb C$ for its exact phase, with $|\psi_p(r)|\in\{0,1\}$; a zero records an inactive source condition. In the TPC-93 notation, the additive part of this phase contains $$e_{cq_X}(-r\varepsilon_\theta\Omega_pz),$$ and the remaining $r$-dependent source phase is retained rather than set to one.

Equivalently, the allowed atom set and factor split may be recorded as $$\mathcal P_X=  \left\{(\theta,c,\kappa,z):  \begin{array}{l}  z\in I_{\theta,c,\kappa}^{\ast},\  1\le N_{\theta,c,\kappa}\le q_X,\  q_X\nmid u_p,\\  \omega_p\in G,\ \text{and every inherited literal mask is active}  \end{array}\right\},$$ and $$\begin{aligned}  \alpha_p  &=  \mathcal N_Xc_{\theta,X}  \mu(\kappa_p)\mu(B_p)\mu(D_p)\mu(V_p)  \mathbf 1_{(B_p,V_p)=1}\mathcal W_p^{(0)},\\  \psi_p(r)  &=  \zeta_{p,r}  e_{c_pq_X}(-r\varepsilon_\theta\Omega_pz)  \mathcal W_p^{(r)}.\end{aligned}$$ Here $\mathcal N_X$ is the single inherited global normalization, $\mathcal W_p^{(0)}$ is the product of all remaining $r$-independent literal weights and masks, and $\mathcal W_p^{(r)}$ is the product of the remaining $r$-dependent unit phases or zero source masks. This is a partition of the TPC-93 master coefficient, not a new renormalization.

Let $$\rho_p(r)  =  \mathbf 1_{E_{H_p}}(r\omega_p), \qquad  \mathfrak m_X(r)=\mathfrak m_{K,X}(r)  \mathbf 1_{\{0<d_{q_X}(r,0)\le R_X\}}.$$ The filter is signed and complex; the previously used positive weight is $|\mathfrak m_X(r)|$.

> **Theorem: Exact signed resonant reconstruction** \[thm:literal-reconstruction\] Under the TPC-93 source-to-child inverse and exact-content crosswalk, the complete signed resonant contribution is $$\boxed{  \mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}  =  \sum_{r\in G}\mathfrak m_X(r)  \sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}  \alpha_p\psi_p(r)\rho_p(r).} \label{eq:signed-frequency-form}$$ Every literal opened atom occurs once. > **Proof** Start with the finite TPC-93 sector master formula, split the complete outer coefficient into the signed reconstruction multiplier and the remaining source phase, and open the resolved fiber point $z$. Reindexing finite sums gives [\[eq:signed-frequency-form\]](TPC-107.md#ref-3b026ebaf7e61037). The exact content extraction is the step that retains determinant $h_0$ and the factor $\mu(B)\mathbf 1_{(B,V_p)=1}$.

> **Theorem: Filter-before-majorant identity and domination** \[thm:filter-first\] One has $$\begin{aligned}  \mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}  &=  \sum_{p\in\mathcal P_X}\alpha_p\mathcal K_p, \label{eq:filter-first-identity}\\  |\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}|  &\le  \underbrace{\sum_p|\alpha_p||\mathcal K_p|}  _{\mathcal C_{\mathrm{filt},X}}  \le  \underbrace{\sum_p|\alpha_p|  \sum_r|\mathfrak m_X(r)|\rho_p(r)}  _{\mathcal C_{\mathrm{preabs},X}}. \label{eq:majorant-chain}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** Interchange the two finite sums in [\[eq:signed-frequency-form\]](TPC-107.md#ref-3b026ebaf7e61037). The first inequality is the triangle inequality after filtering. For the second, apply the triangle inequality inside [\[eq:atom-kernel\]](TPC-107.md#ref-f130dc269d4933c2) and use $|\psi_p(r)|\le1$.

> **Remark: Relation to the positive census** After the literal fiber mass is opened into the atoms $p$, the last term of [\[eq:majorant-chain\]](TPC-107.md#ref-89874018c01b25f3) is the corresponding pre-filter positive carrier. If one first replaces the signed fiber by a uniform absolute mass, it may be still larger.

###### ref-05fcd7688fb0aa1d

> **Proposition: The separation can be complete** \[prop:complete-separation\] There exist two signed atoms with identical $(\psi,\rho)$ and coefficients $\alpha_1=1$, $\alpha_2=-1$ for which $$\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res}}=0,\qquad  \mathcal C_{\mathrm{filt}}=2|\mathcal K_1|, \qquad  \mathcal C_{\mathrm{preabs}}  =2\sum_r|\mathfrak m(r)|\rho_1(r).$$ If $\mathcal K_1\ne0$, both positive quantities are nonzero.

> **Proof** The two atom contributions cancel at every frequency before the filter is applied.

###### ref-69c1f2abf63f867f

> **Proposition: No automatic phase saving** \[prop:gauge-saturation\] Given any nonzero filter $\mathfrak m$ and nonempty resonance support $\rho$, there is a unit phase $\psi$ on that support such that $$\left|  \sum_r\mathfrak m(r)\psi(r)\rho(r)  \right|  =  \sum_r|\mathfrak m(r)|\rho(r).$$

> **Proof** On points where $\mathfrak m(r)\ne0$, choose $\psi(r)=\overline{\mathfrak m(r)}/|\mathfrak m(r)|$; choose any unit value elsewhere.

The last proposition is a coefficient-blind sharpness statement. The literal phases are not freely chosen, but no theorem may infer a saving merely from their unit modulus.

> **Theorem: Exact signed Gram identity** \[thm:signed-gram\] Let $$\Gamma_{p,p'}  =  \sum_{r\in G}  \psi_p(r)\overline{\psi_{p'}(r)}  \rho_p(r)\rho_{p'}(r). \label{eq:gram-kernel}$$ Then $\Gamma$ is positive semidefinite and $$\boxed{  \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}  =  \sum_{p,p'}  \alpha_p\overline{\alpha_{p'}}  \Gamma_{p,p'}.} \label{eq:signed-ttstar}$$ Moreover, $$|\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}|  \le  \|\mathfrak m_X\|_2  \mathcal E_{\mathrm{sig},X}^{1/2}. \label{eq:energy-bound}$$

> **Proof** Expand [\[eq:signed-energy\]](TPC-107.md#ref-ea6f46d1f785ccf7) using [\[eq:signed-profile\]](TPC-107.md#ref-0cc7accc8bf6474f) to obtain [\[eq:signed-ttstar\]](TPC-107.md#ref-913c81f5aa2ceee8). The matrix is the Gram matrix of the vectors $r\mapsto\psi_p(r)\rho_p(r)$. Finally, $\mathcal S_{\mathrm{res},X}=\langle\overline{\mathfrak m_X}, F_X\rangle$ up to the chosen inner-product convention, so Cauchy–Schwarz gives [\[eq:energy-bound\]](TPC-107.md#ref-224282f10801a3b8).

# What information survives

The signed profile in [\[eq:signed-profile\]](TPC-107.md#ref-0cc7accc8bf6474f) preserves simultaneously:

1.  the signed reconstruction multiplier;

2.  the $r$-dependent source and determinant phases;

3.  both Möbius signs and the extracted $\mu(B)$;

4.  the coprimality mask $\mathbf 1_{(B,V)=1}$;

5.  the actual moving affine row and fixed determinant $h_0$;

6.  both polarizations and all outer keys; and

7.  the one physical post-aggregation normalization.

Replacing any item by an independent model variable changes the Gram matrix. Conversely, identical signed atoms may be combined before absolute values provided their complete provenance remains recoverable.

# Claim boundary and route verdict

## Proved

The exact literal signed resonant sum can be filtered before majorization. The resulting atomwise majorant never exceeds the corresponding pre-filter positive carrier. The canonical unfiltered signed frequency energy has the positive-semidefinite $TT^*$ identity [\[eq:signed-ttstar\]](TPC-107.md#ref-913c81f5aa2ceee8), and [\[eq:signed-gate\]](TPC-107.md#ref-960fb98c11c03358) is a sufficient replacement gate. Sharp finite examples show both complete improvement and complete saturation. These are $\mathrm{L0}$ statements; the imported source-to-atom crosswalk is $\mathrm{L1}$.

## Not proved

This paper does not prove:

-   [\[eq:signed-gate\]](TPC-107.md#ref-960fb98c11c03358) for the growing physical coefficient;

-   a Möbius or Liouville cancellation theorem on the actual fixed-$h_0$ carrier;

-   that the literal phases realize either sharp finite example;

-   closure of the generic affine sector, high-frequency return, full-block reassembly, or the strict physical endpoint; or

-   a parity breakthrough, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem.

Nothing is specialized to $h_0=2$.

## Verdict

TPC-107 opens the signed fallback corridor exactly, but it does not walk through the parity-facing door. Its gain is architectural: cancellation discarded by the positive route is now represented by one explicit Gram energy. TPC-108 must determine what arithmetic input could control the analogous literal generic-affine Möbius sums without disguising an unproved Chowla-type estimate.
