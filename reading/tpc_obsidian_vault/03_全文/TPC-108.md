## Abstract

We audit the first parity-facing arithmetic gate in TPC-MVP1. After the exact TPC-93 content extraction, a literal generic block has two primitive affine forms $$D(z)=d+sz,\qquad V(z)=u+az,\qquad su-ad=h_0\ne0,$$ the extracted sign and coprimality mask, the actual smooth and periodic weights, and an additive phase $\mathrm e(-\alpha z)$ satisfying $N\|\alpha\|>1$. We write this block without suppressing any coefficient and prove the exact $TT^*$ identity $$\left|\sum_{z\in I}A(z)\mathrm e(-\alpha z)\right|^2  =  \sum_{|h|<N}\mathrm e(-\alpha h)  \sum_{\substack{z,z+h\in I}}  A(z+h)\overline{A(z)}.$$ The off-diagonal kernel contains four literal Möbius values. Both shifted affine pairs still have determinant $h_0$, but no local-support theorem makes this four-sign correlation small.

For a $q$-invertible phase slope we also prove the complete frequency identity $$\sum_{r\in\mathbb F_q}  \left|\sum_{z\in I}A(z)e_q(-r\omega z)\right|^2  =  q\sum_{u\in\mathbb F_q}  \left|\sum_{\substack{z\in I\\z\equiv u\;(\mathrm{mod}\ q)}}  A(z)\right|^2.$$ This is a rigorous average-frequency ceiling, with an explicit aliasing term, but it does not select the literal low-frequency filter or control coherent outer reassembly.

Two obstructions explain why the gate remains open. Genericity alone yields only a constant geometric-series gain and is saturated by bounded phase-matched coefficients. More seriously, a broad prefix-uniform power-saving theorem for all generic twists of $\lambda(n)\lambda(n+h_0)$ would, by Abel summation, imply a power-saving ordinary two-point Chowla estimate. Existing logarithmically averaged or shift-averaged results do not have the literal growing-family quantifiers.

We therefore formulate a restricted hypothesis only for the actual TPC block family and give an exact outer-loss ledger at the numerical exponent $1/200$; the physical $TT^*$ crosswalk remains a separate requirement. The identities, frequency theorem, and obstruction are $\mathrm{L0}$; the literal dictionary is $\mathrm{L1}$. No new $\mathrm{L2}$ fixed-$h_0$ Möbius estimate is proved, and H3 remains an explicit hypothesis.

# Exact $TT^*$ and the four-sign kernel

###### ref-51cc6834c0db2df7

For $h\in\mathbb Z$, define $$C_{\xi,X}(h)  =  \sum_{\substack{z\in I_\xi\\z+h\in I_\xi}}  A_{\xi,X}(z+h)\overline{A_{\xi,X}(z)}. \label{eq:shift-correlation}$$

###### ref-89cd7c10df0d20a7

###### ref-a2eb77c337f23d78

###### ref-839bbd9a1659ce16

> **Theorem: Literal one-block $TT^*$** \[thm:block-ttstar\] For every resolved block, $$\boxed{  |S_{\xi,X}|^2  =  \sum_{|h|<N_\xi}  \mathrm e(-\alpha_\xi h)C_{\xi,X}(h).} \label{eq:block-ttstar}$$ The diagonal is $$C_{\xi,X}(0)=\sum_{z\in I_\xi}|A_{\xi,X}(z)|^2.$$ For $h\ne0$, the arithmetic factor in $C_{\xi,X}(h)$ is $$\begin{aligned}  &\mu(D_\xi(z+h))\mu(V_\xi(z+h)) \mu(D_\xi(z))\mu(V_\xi(z)), \label{eq:four-mobius}\end{aligned}$$ times the two exact coprimality masks, the shifted periodic/local factor $$\chi_\theta(\tau_\xi+B_\xi(z+h))  \overline{\chi_\theta(\tau_\xi+B_\xi z)},$$ and the corresponding shifted physical weights.

> **Proof** Expand $|S_{\xi,X}|^2$, set $h=z-z'$, and group terms with the same difference.

###### ref-e9a8c73a8f5750f6

> **Proposition: Fixed determinant survives the shift** \[prop:shifted-determinant\] For every integer $h$, the shifted pair $$D_{\xi,h}(z)=D_\xi(z+h),\qquad  V_{\xi,h}(z)=V_\xi(z+h)$$ has the same determinant $h_0$: $$s_\xi(u_\xi+a_\xi h)  -a_\xi(d_\xi+s_\xi h)=h_0.$$

> **Proof** The two $a_\xi s_\xi h$ terms cancel.

> **Remark: Why $TT^*$ is not yet a saving** TPC-90 classifies the squarefree local support and writes the remaining signs as a Liouville parity correlation [`\citep{WangTPC90}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-108.md#cite-4f3149d531dce18d). In [\[eq:four-mobius\]](TPC-108.md#ref-839bbd9a1659ce16), $TT^*$ raises the correlation order from two signs to four. A useful $TT^*$ argument must gain from frequency or outer averaging; the formal identity alone does not bound the off-diagonal kernel.

# A complete-frequency theorem and the aliasing ledger

On the nonconstant $q_X$-invertible branch, literal provenance gives $c\mid\Omega_\xi$ and $$\omega_\xi  =  \varepsilon_\theta\frac{\Omega_\xi}{c}  \pmod{q_X}  \in\mathbb F_{q_X}^\times.$$ Thus the phase is $e_{q_X}(-r\omega_\xi z)$.

###### ref-3660b625fc350faa

###### ref-ee74becefd85d5e3

###### ref-774d8cbcdd59d7b5

> **Theorem: Exact full-frequency alias identity** \[thm:full-frequency\] Let $q$ be prime, $\omega\in\mathbb F_q^\times$, $I\subset\mathbb Z$ finite, and $A:I\to\mathbb C$. Put $$S(r)=\sum_{z\in I}A(z)e_q(-r\omega z).$$ Then $$\boxed{  \sum_{r\in\mathbb F_q}|S(r)|^2  =  q\sum_{u\in\mathbb F_q}  \left|  \sum_{\substack{z\in I\\z\equiv u\;(\mathrm{mod}\ q)}}  A(z)  \right|^2.} \label{eq:alias-parseval}$$ If the residues of $I$ modulo $q$ are distinct, in particular if $I$ is an interval of length at most $q$, then $$\sum_{r\in\mathbb F_q}|S(r)|^2  =  q\sum_{z\in I}|A(z)|^2. \label{eq:no-wrap-parseval}$$

> **Proof** Expanding the left side and using additive orthogonality gives $$\sum_{r\in\mathbb F_q}e_q(-r\omega(z-z'))  =  q\mathbf 1_{\{z\equiv z'\pmod q\}},$$ because $\omega\ne0$. Group the remaining pairs by their residue class.

###### ref-357c928a35eb1e81

###### ref-d907a9ace1b370be

> **Corollary: Filtered one-block ceiling** \[cor:filtered-ceiling\] For any $\mathcal R\subseteq\mathbb F_q$ and filter $m$ supported on $\mathcal R$, $$\left|\sum_{r\in\mathcal R}m(r)S(r)\right|  \le  \|m\|_2\sqrt q  \left(  \sum_{u\in\mathbb F_q}  \left|\sum_{\substack{z\in I\\z\equiv u\pmod q}}A(z)\right|^2  \right)^{1/2}. \label{eq:filtered-ceiling}$$

> **Proof** Apply Cauchy–Schwarz on $\mathcal R$, enlarge to all frequencies, and use [\[thm:full-frequency\]](TPC-108.md#ref-3660b625fc350faa).

> **Remark: Average frequency is not the literal gate** is unconditional, but it has a $\sqrt q$ cost and must still be summed with the physical outer coefficients. It does not prove [\[eq:h3-target\]](TPC-108.md#ref-d5663d5783a01821); nor may the complete-frequency average be reported as control of one prescribed low-window combination.

# Two sharp barriers

## Genericity alone gives no power

###### ref-690961eec2bd3dd7

> **Proposition: Geometric sum and coefficient-blind saturation** \[prop:generic-saturation\] For an interval $I$ of length $N$, $$\left|\sum_{z\in I}\mathrm e(-\alpha z)\right|  \le  \min\left\{N,\frac1{2\|\alpha\|}\right\}.$$ Hence $N\|\alpha\|>1$ supplies at most a constant-factor conclusion near the generic boundary. Moreover, among coefficients satisfying $|A(z)|=1$, the choice $A(z)=\mathrm e(\alpha z)$ gives $$\left|\sum_{z\in I}A(z)\mathrm e(-\alpha z)\right|=N.$$

> **Proof** The first statement is the standard finite geometric-series bound. The second is immediate term by term.

The saturation coefficient is not claimed to be the literal Möbius coefficient. It proves that generic phase, bounded weights, and interval length alone cannot imply a power saving.

## A broad generic theorem reaches ordinary Chowla

###### ref-d255183e09f3e907

###### ref-73ea3ba0152e4186

> **Lemma: Twisted-prefix removal** \[lem:twist-removal\] Let $|b_n|\le1$, $0<\delta<1$, and $\alpha_N=N^{-1+\delta}$. If $$\max_{1\le T\le N}  \left|\sum_{n\le T}b_n\mathrm e(\alpha_Nn)\right|  \le C N^{1-\eta},$$ then $$\left|\sum_{n\le N}b_n\right|  \le C'(1+N^\delta)N^{1-\eta}  \ll N^{1-\eta+\delta}. \label{eq:abel-transfer}$$

> **Proof** Let $B(T)=\sum_{n\le T}b_n\mathrm e(\alpha_Nn)$. Partial summation applied to $b_n=B(n)-B(n-1)$ after removal of the twist gives $$\left|\sum_{n\le N}b_n\right|  \le  \max_{T\le N}|B(T)|  \left(1+N|1-\mathrm e(-\alpha_N)|\right).$$ Use $|1-\mathrm e(-t)|\le2\pi\|t\|$.

###### ref-9f486e3d8d541c3e

> **Corollary: Broad-family Chowla barrier** \[cor:chowla-barrier\] Let $\lambda$ denote the Liouville function. Fix $h_0>0$. Suppose a theorem gave, for some $\eta>0$, the preceding prefix bound with $$b_n=\lambda(n)\lambda(n+h_0)$$ for every sufficiently large $N$ and every generic phase $\alpha_N=N^{-1+\delta}$, where $0<\delta<\eta$. Then $$\sum_{n\le N}\lambda(n)\lambda(n+h_0)  \ll N^{1-(\eta-\delta)},$$ a power-saving ordinary two-point Chowla estimate.

> **Proof** Since $N\|\alpha_N\|=N^\delta>1$, these twists are generic. Apply [\[lem:twist-removal\]](TPC-108.md#ref-d255183e09f3e907).

> **Remark: Restricted does not mean equivalent** The literal H3 family contains only the phases, forms, masks, and weights generated by the TPC dictionary. It is not asserted to contain every pair $(n,n+h_0)$ and every $\alpha_N=N^{-1+\delta}$. Therefore [\[cor:chowla-barrier\]](TPC-108.md#ref-9f486e3d8d541c3e) does not prove H3 equivalent to Chowla. It explains why a convenient uniform theorem over arbitrary affine forms and generic phases would be unexpectedly deep.

# Why existing averaged theorems do not fill the gap

The logarithmically averaged two-point Chowla theorem controls fixed affine forms with reciprocal weight [`\citep{Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-108.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07). Shift-averaged results average the prescribed displacement [`\citep{MatomakiRadziwillTao2015}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-108.md#cite-b5a8969fcb0fe4a8). The literal TPC blocks instead require:

1.  growing slopes and intercepts but one prescribed nonzero determinant $h_0$;

2.  ordinary smooth physical weights and actual interval origins;

3.  uniform control of every prefix needed by partial summation;

4.  exact content and coprimality masks;

5.  the specified low-frequency multiplier and both polarizations; and

6.  coherent outer reassembly at a quantitative power rate.

TPC-96 proves that these quantifiers cannot be supplied by an anchor-free Tauberian conversion [`\citep{WangTPC96}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-108.md#cite-b1a041931cf4c7cf). This comparison is a quantifier audit, not a criticism of the averaged theorems.

# The restricted hypothesis and an exact loss ledger

###### ref-f3d5f1cdcfe2b113

For a block, put $$V_{\xi,X}=\sum_{z\in I_\xi}|A_{\xi,X}(z)|, \qquad  \Delta_{\xi,X}  =  \begin{cases}  |S_{\xi,X}|/V_{\xi,X},&V_{\xi,X}>0,\\  0,&V_{\xi,X}=0.  \end{cases}$$ Define the actual outer absolute mass $$\mathcal M_{\mathrm{Gen},X}  =  \sum_{\xi\in\Xi_{\mathrm{Gen}}}  |\mathfrak c_{\xi,X}|V_{\xi,X}. \label{eq:outer-mass}$$

###### ref-a87e34e3b100109c

###### ref-b08f90156c3fa86e

###### ref-072f825cb9c02b2f

###### ref-9def80fe49760c1d

> **Theorem: Exact termwise exponent ledger** \[thm:outer-ledger\] If, for fixed exponents $\eta,\ell_{\mathrm{out}}\ge0$, $$\begin{aligned}  \sup_{\xi\in\Xi_{\mathrm{Gen}}}\Delta_{\xi,X}  &\le X^{-\eta+o(1)}, \label{eq:block-saving}\\  \mathcal M_{\mathrm{Gen},X}  &\le X^{\ell_{\mathrm{out}}+o(1)}XQ_X, \label{eq:outer-loss}\end{aligned}$$ then $$|\operatorname{Low}_{\mathrm{Gen},X}|  \le XQ_X\,X^{-(\eta-\ell_{\mathrm{out}})+o(1)}. \label{eq:net-saving}$$ Thus this termwise route reaches the numerical exponent $1/200$ for the displayed linear generic window if $\eta-\ell_{\mathrm{out}}\ge1/200$. Identifying that bound with the normalized quadratic object in [\[eq:h3-target\]](TPC-108.md#ref-d5663d5783a01821) still requires the exact physical $TT^*$ crosswalk and prefactor.

> **Proof** Apply the triangle inequality to [\[eq:generic-low-window\]](TPC-108.md#ref-b6004ffa2e5589d8), insert $|S_{\xi,X}|=\Delta_{\xi,X}V_{\xi,X}$, and use [\[eq:block-saving\]](TPC-108.md#ref-b08f90156c3fa86e)–[\[eq:outer-loss\]](TPC-108.md#ref-072f825cb9c02b2f).

> **Remark: Sufficient, deliberately not necessary** discards cancellation between blocks. A coherent $TT^*$ theorem may be strictly stronger. Conversely, the scale $XQ_X$ in [\[eq:outer-loss\]](TPC-108.md#ref-072f825cb9c02b2f) is not asserted for the physical coefficient; it is a separately auditable normalization condition.

###### ref-17f4c40e9edf8195

\[hyp:restricted-gate\] For every actual generic packet $b$, the complete normalized signed $TT^*$ object built from [\[eq:literal-coefficient\]](TPC-108.md#ref-f47e7e2d31768aa8)–[\[eq:generic-low-window\]](TPC-108.md#ref-b6004ffa2e5589d8), including all prefixes, satisfies $$\mathcal G_{b,\mathrm{gen}}^{\mathrm{signed}}  \ll XQ_b\,X^{-1/200+o(1)}.$$ The exact physical $TT^*$ prefactor is recorded separately and converts this quadratic saving to the packet target without an unrecorded loss.

# Claim boundary and next gate

## Proved

The literal generic block has an exact $TT^*$ correlation kernel with four Möbius factors, and both shifted pairs preserve determinant $h_0$. Complete-frequency averaging has the exact alias formula [\[eq:alias-parseval\]](TPC-108.md#ref-ee74becefd85d5e3). Genericity alone cannot give a power saving, and a broad prefix-uniform generic-twist theorem would imply a power-saving ordinary Chowla estimate. The termwise outer-loss law [\[eq:net-saving\]](TPC-108.md#ref-9def80fe49760c1d) is exact. These results are $\mathrm{L0}$; their attachment to the imported TPC-93 coefficient is $\mathrm{L1}$.

## Not proved

This paper does not prove:

-   [\[hyp:restricted-gate\]](TPC-108.md#ref-17f4c40e9edf8195) or [\[eq:h3-target\]](TPC-108.md#ref-d5663d5783a01821);

-   a growing fixed-$h_0$ Möbius or Liouville cancellation estimate;

-   the physical outer-mass bound [\[eq:outer-loss\]](TPC-108.md#ref-072f825cb9c02b2f);

-   conversion of a complete-frequency mean into the literal low-window combination;

-   the separate $1/400$ packet normalization, full-block return, determinant/zero-mode compatibility, or endpoint budget; or

-   a parity breakthrough, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem.

Nothing is specialized to $h_0=2$.

## Verdict

TPC-108 advances the route by replacing the phrase “generic affine dispersion” with one exact arithmetic object and two precise obstructions. It does not open H3. The next rational move is TPC-109’s coherent prime-square block test: exploit special structure of one actual residual block, or prove a coefficient-blind saturation theorem. Such a theorem stops only the subroute based on support, moduli, occupancy, and diagonal energy; it does not rule out gains from literal arithmetic phase relations or a proved dominant-prime statistic.
