## Abstract

In the literal fixed-shift residual carrier, the terminal-prime coordinate is injective before it is forgotten, while the physical output coherently adds all terminal primes in one residual fiber. We isolate one actual fiber $y=(\gamma,s,j)$, write its inherited amplitudes as $b_{y,p}$, and study the prime square $$\mathcal G_y=\left|\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_y}b_{y,p}\right|^2$$ against the resolved diagonal $\mathcal D_y=\sum_p|b_{y,p}|^2$. The exact Gram operator is the rank-one all-ones matrix. For fixed moduli $a_{y,p}=|b_{y,p}|$, the complete free-phase range is $$\left[(2a_{y,\max}-R_y)_+^2,\ R_y^2\right],  \qquad R_y=\sum_pa_{y,p}.$$ Consequently the usual Bessel bound $\mathcal G_y\le|\mathcal P_y|\mathcal D_y$ is sharp, and no coefficient-blind improvement follows from terminal-prime injectivity, support size, occupancy, or the diagonal energy. Equal moduli already admit both perfect saturation and maximal alignment on the same resolved support.

We give an exact coefficient-specific route certificate in terms of the physical alignment ratio, and a dominant-atom lower certificate. Applied to the literal TPC residual map, these are $\mathrm{L1}$ identities and stop/go tests. They do not estimate the actual growing coefficients and therefore provide no $\mathrm{L2}$ fixed-shift arithmetic saving, parity breakthrough, prime-pair asymptotic, or twin-prime conclusion.

# Question and claim boundary

TPC-MVP1 lists a coherent prime-square test as an alternative attack on the generic signed gate [`\citep{WangMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-109.md#cite-934c301de52ef9a3). The phrase “prime square” here means the squared norm of a coherent sum over terminal primes, not a restriction to integers $p^2$.

The literal residual map of TPC-55 first keeps the terminal-prime label and then forgets it [`\citep{WangTPC55}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-109.md#cite-3087863e74e38aea). At one prescribed $h_0\ne0$, its physical output fiber has key $$y=(\gamma,s,j),\qquad  \mathcal P_y=\{p:\ (\gamma,s,j,p)\text{ is active}\}.$$ The terminal-prime coordinate is injective before the forgetful map. Thus every $p\in\mathcal P_y$ has one inherited complex amplitude $b_{y,p}$, but all of them are coherently added at the output. The static-mask and resolved-energy results upstream do not estimate this final coherent sum [`\citep{WangTPC50,WangTPC55}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-109.md#cite-0d8078e2652e9912); [\[WangTPC55\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-109.md#cite-3087863e74e38aea).

This paper answers the coefficient-blind part of the question completely. It does not claim a bound for the literal amplitudes as $X\to\infty$.

# The literal block and its exact Gram operator

> **Definition: Resolved and coherent energies** For a nonempty finite fiber $\mathcal P_y$, put $$k_y=|\mathcal P_y|,\qquad  \mathcal D_y=\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_y}|b_{y,p}|^2,\qquad  \mathcal G_y=\left|\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_y}b_{y,p}\right|^2.$$ Also write $$a_{y,p}=|b_{y,p}|,\qquad  R_y=\sum_pa_{y,p},\qquad  a_{y,\max}=\max_pa_{y,p},$$ and $$d_y=(2a_{y,\max}-R_y)_+.$$ The zero fiber is treated separately, with all quantities equal to zero.

###### ref-40cd61833db72aad

> **Proposition: Exact coherent-prime Gram identity** \[prop:gram\] Order $\mathcal P_y=\{p_1,\dots,p_{k_y}\}$, put $b_y=(b_{y,p_i})_i$, and let $\mathbf J_{k_y}=\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^*$. Then $$\boxed{\mathcal G_y=b_y^*\mathbf J_{k_y}b_y.}$$ The spectrum of $\mathbf J_{k_y}$ is $\{k_y,0,\dots,0\}$. Hence $$0\le\mathcal G_y\le k_y\mathcal D_y,$$ and the upper constant $k_y$ is optimal.

> **Proof** The quadratic form expands to $$b_y^*\mathbf 1\mathbf 1^*b_y  =|\mathbf 1^*b_y|^2  =\left|\sum_pb_{y,p}\right|^2.$$ The spectral statement is immediate. Cauchy–Schwarz gives the upper bound, and equality holds when all entries are equal.

> **Remark: Where the obstruction sits** Prime-resolved injectivity controls the map before $\mathbf J_k$ is applied. It does not diagonalize $\mathbf J_k$. Distinct terminal primes are precisely the off-diagonal modes inside this rank-one quadratic form.

# Sharp phase saturation

###### ref-4987a1e6c6550fcc

> **Theorem: Complete free-phase interval** \[thm:interval\] Fix a fiber and its moduli $(a_p)_{p\in\mathcal P}$. Allow the phases of $b_p=a_pe^{i\theta_p}$ to vary independently. Then $$\boxed{  \left\{\left|\sum_pb_p\right|^2:         \theta_p\in\mathbb R\right\}  =  [d^2,R^2],}  \qquad  d=(2a_{\max}-R)_+.$$ Every point of the interval is attained.

> **Proof** The possible lengths of a polygonal sum of segments of prescribed lengths $a_p$ form the interval $[d,R]$. The lower endpoint is the reverse triangle inequality, attained by placing a longest segment opposite all others; when the longest segment does not dominate, the segments close to a polygon and the lower endpoint is zero. Continuous rotation of one segment, together with induction on the number of segments, fills the whole interval. Squaring gives the result.

###### ref-acdb1a8725de0ac0

> **Corollary: Sharp saturation dichotomy** \[cor:dichotomy\] The moduli alone force a positive coherent square if and only if $$a_{\max}>\frac{R}{2}.$$ In that case every phase assignment satisfies $$\mathcal G\ge(2a_{\max}-R)^2.$$ If $a_{\max}\le R/2$, the strongest universal modulus-only lower bound is zero.

###### ref-8e28c5aff0e00a55

> **Corollary: Same-support nonidentifiability** \[cor:roots\] For every $k\ge2$, there are two coefficient vectors on the same $k$-prime support with the same unit moduli and the same diagonal energy $k$, but with coherent squares $0$ and $k^2$, respectively.

> **Proof** Use the $k$-th roots of unity in the first vector and $k$ copies of $1$ in the second.

> **Remark: Physical versus comparison phases** The lower bound $d_y^2$ applies to the literal fiber because it uses its actual moduli. Attainability in [\[thm:interval\]](TPC-109.md#ref-4987a1e6c6550fcc) belongs to the larger free-phase comparison class. It does not say that the arithmetic coefficient realizes a root-of-unity phase pattern.

# Coefficient-specific certificates

The finite geometry identifies exactly which additional statistic a coherent-prime argument must control.

> **Definition: Alignment and cancellation ratios** For $\mathcal D_y>0$, set $$\alpha_y=\frac{\mathcal G_y}{k_y\mathcal D_y}\in[0,1],  \qquad  \beta_y=\frac{\mathcal G_y}{R_y^2}\in[0,1].$$ For a finite family $\mathcal B$ of disjoint fibers, define $$\mathcal D_{\mathcal B}=\sum_{y\in\mathcal B}\mathcal D_y,\qquad  \mathcal G_{\mathcal B}=\sum_{y\in\mathcal B}\mathcal G_y,\qquad  K_{\mathcal B}=\max_{y\in\mathcal B}k_y.$$

###### ref-fafa2d9744021416

> **Proposition: Exact block go certificate** \[prop:go\] For every nonzero block, $$\frac{\mathcal G_{\mathcal B}}{K_{\mathcal B}\mathcal D_{\mathcal B}}  =  \sum_{y\in\mathcal B}  \frac{k_y\mathcal D_y}{K_{\mathcal B}\mathcal D_{\mathcal B}}\,\alpha_y.$$ In particular, a coefficient-specific improvement $$\mathcal G_{\mathcal B}\le\varepsilon_X K_{\mathcal B}\mathcal D_{\mathcal B}$$ is equivalent to the displayed weighted alignment average being at most $\varepsilon_X$.

> **Proof** Substitute $\mathcal G_y=k_y\mathcal D_y\alpha_y$ and sum.

###### ref-e8415028a9b1c5c8

> **Proposition: Dominant-prime lower certificate** \[prop:dominance\] Let $\mathcal B_\epsilon$ be the fibers satisfying $$a_{y,\max}\ge\left(\frac12+\epsilon\right)R_y.$$ Then $$\boxed{  \mathcal G_{\mathcal B}  \ge4\epsilon^2  \sum_{y\in\mathcal B_\epsilon}R_y^2.}$$ This bound is phase-independent and sharp at the single-fiber level.

> **Proof** On such a fiber, $d_y=2a_{y,\max}-R_y\ge2\epsilon R_y$. Apply [\[thm:interval\]](TPC-109.md#ref-4987a1e6c6550fcc) and sum.

> **Remark: Upper saving and lower survival are different tasks** A dispersion route may seek small $\alpha_y$, while an energy-survival route may seek a positive dominant share. The same unsigned occupancy statement cannot supply both. The two directions must be labeled separately.

# Application to the TPC-109 queue item

On the literal TPC-55 carrier, the amplitudes $b_{y,p}$ include the source coefficient, masks, terminal-prime weight, fixed $h_0$, and all retained profile factors. Therefore \[[prop:gram](TPC-109.md#ref-40cd61833db72aad),[thm:interval](TPC-109.md#ref-4987a1e6c6550fcc),[prop:go](TPC-109.md#ref-fafa2d9744021416),[prop:dominance](TPC-109.md#ref-e8415028a9b1c5c8)\] apply without changing the physical output map. This is the positive content of the test.

The test also gives a rigorous stop certificate:

###### ref-4b6ccbb4d620ecd4

> **Theorem: TPC-109 route decision** \[thm:decision\] Terminal-prime injectivity, the exact active prime support, subpower fiber multiplicity, and the resolved diagonal energy do not imply any coefficient-blind improvement over $$\mathcal G_{\mathcal B}\le K_{\mathcal B}\mathcal D_{\mathcal B}.$$ Nor do they imply a positive lower comparison $\mathcal G_{\mathcal B}\gg\mathcal D_{\mathcal B}$. Any improvement in either direction must use one of:
>
> 1.  the literal phase relation among the actual $b_{y,p}$;
>
> 2.  a proved dominant-prime share;
>
> 3.  an arithmetic average that is subsequently localized back to the same prescribed $h_0$; or
>
> 4.  cancellation across a larger physical reassembly that retains all provenance.

> **Proof** The upper bound and its sharpness are [\[prop:gram\]](TPC-109.md#ref-40cd61833db72aad). The lower obstruction is [\[cor:roots\]](TPC-109.md#ref-8e28c5aff0e00a55). These examples retain support, moduli, diagonal energy, and multiplicity. Hence none of those unsigned invariants can decide the coherent square.

The coherent-prime corridor therefore remains open only as a *coefficient-specific* route. TPC-110 should not recycle the resolved diagonal as if it were the post-bin determinant energy. It must analyze the exact binning kernel and the angle of the literal coefficient to that kernel.

#### Explicit exclusions.

Nothing in this paper proves the generic affine Möbius hypothesis of TPC-MVP1, the determinant-energy bound, the distinguished zero-mode bound, the compatibility $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$, the independent physical endpoint $\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400$, a Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, or the twin-prime conjecture. No statement is specialized to $h_0=2$.
