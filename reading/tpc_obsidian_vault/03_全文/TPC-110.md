## Abstract

Let $\mathcal T_X$ be the canonical parent archive of the literal fixed-$h_0$ matched coefficient, let $\nu:\mathcal T_X\to\mathcal N_X$ be its determinant map, and write $$A_X(n)=\sum_{\nu(t)=n}u_t,\qquad  D_X=\sum_n|A_X(n)|^2.$$ We analyze this *post-bin* energy on its actual finite support, without replacing the coefficient by an ambient or random model. The normalized determinant-binning operator is a coisometry. Its adjoint projection is exact averaging on every determinant fiber, and its kernel is the direct sum of the zero-sum subspaces of those fibers. If $\Pi_X$ denotes this projection and $m_n$ the actual fiber multiplicity, then $$D_X=\sum_nm_n\|(\Pi_Xu)_{F_n}\|_2^2.$$ Thus determinant-energy survival is exactly a weighted angle problem between the literal coefficient and the binning kernel.

This gives a sharp structural dichotomy. If all determinant fibers are singletons, then $D_X$ equals the pre-bin diagonal energy, whose imported upper envelope is one full power below the desired natural scale. If collisions occur, the support map has a nontrivial kernel and support or occupancy alone supplies no lower angle. Therefore any lower bound with loss $\lambda_D<1$ must use positive equal-determinant off-diagonal coherence of the actual coefficient. We give exact necessary and sufficient certificate statistics, but prove no growing arithmetic lower bound. The result is an $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ obstruction and interface theorem, not an $\mathrm{L2}$ parity advance.

# Literal packet and the question

Fix one prescribed $h_0\in\mathbb Z\setminus\{0\}$. In the canonical parent archive of TPC-95, a native key $t\in\mathcal T_X$ carries its literal coefficient $u_t$ and determinant $\nu(t)\ne0$ [`\citep{WangTPC95}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-110.md#cite-4fd2f609889e1b73). Computational children have already been inverse-aggregated, so duplicates are not counted as new physical keys. Put $$F_n=\{t\in\mathcal T_X:\nu(t)=n\},\qquad  m_n=|F_n|,\qquad  A_X(n)=\sum_{t\in F_n}u_t.$$ Only nonempty fibers occur in $\mathcal N_X=\nu(\mathcal T_X)$.

The two energies are $$E_X=\sum_{t\in\mathcal T_X}|u_t|^2,\qquad  D_X=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}|A_X(n)|^2.$$ The imported scale ledger is $$Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)}$$ and $$E_X\ll X^{-1+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2.$$ The open natural-scale target has the form $$D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2$$ with, at minimum, a fixed $\lambda_D<1$ [`\citep{WangTPC81,WangTPC82,WangTPC102}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-110.md#cite-ff65f956e7cf0d6d); [\[WangTPC82\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-110.md#cite-cfeb22890911c08a); [\[WangTPC102\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-110.md#cite-7967ea1ba73edadd).

# Exact SVD of determinant binning

Let $H_X=\mathbb C^{\mathcal T_X}$, $K_X=\mathbb C^{\mathcal N_X}$, both with counting measure. Define the raw and normalized binning maps $$(B_Xv)_n=\sum_{t\in F_n}v_t,\qquad  (U_Xv)_n=\frac1{\sqrt{m_n}}\sum_{t\in F_n}v_t.$$

###### ref-4c1a991b265ce971

> **Theorem: Actual-support binning SVD** \[thm:svd\] The normalized map satisfies $$U_XU_X^*=I_{K_X}.$$ Consequently $\Pi_X=U_X^*U_X$ is the orthogonal projection onto the subspace of vectors constant on each determinant fiber, and $$(\Pi_Xv)_t=\frac1{m_n}\sum_{s\in F_n}v_s  \quad(t\in F_n).$$ Moreover, $$\operatorname{Ker}B_X=\operatorname{Ker}U_X  =\bigoplus_{n\in\mathcal N_X}  \left\{v\in\mathbb C^{F_n}:\sum_{t\in F_n}v_t=0\right\},$$ so $$\dim\operatorname{Ker}B_X=|\mathcal T_X|-|\mathcal N_X|.$$

> **Proof** For $w\in K_X$, the adjoint has $$(U_X^*w)_t=\frac{w_n}{\sqrt{m_n}}  \quad(t\in F_n).$$ Summing this over $F_n$ and dividing by $\sqrt{m_n}$ returns $w_n$, proving $U_XU_X^*=I$. The projection formula follows. The kernel statements are the defining zero-sum conditions, one independent equation per nonempty fiber.

###### ref-af8c87d4c40c7b06

> **Corollary: Exact post-bin energy identity** \[cor:energy\] For the literal coefficient $u=(u_t)$, $$\boxed{  D_X  =\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}  m_n\|(\Pi_Xu)|_{F_n}\|_2^2.}$$ If the actual carrier is empty, then $D_X=0$ and the following extrema are omitted. Otherwise, writing $$m_{\min}=\min_nm_n,\qquad  m_{\max}=\max_nm_n,$$ one has $$m_{\min}\|\Pi_Xu\|_2^2  \le D_X\le  m_{\max}\|\Pi_Xu\|_2^2.$$

> **Proof** On $F_n$, the projected vector has constant value $A_X(n)/m_n$, hence its squared norm is $|A_X(n)|^2/m_n$. Multiply by $m_n$ and sum.

# The exact kernel-angle certificate

> **Definition: Actual alignment statistics** For $u\ne0$, define $$\alpha_X=\frac{\|\Pi_Xu\|_2^2}{\|u\|_2^2}\in[0,1].$$ If $\Pi_Xu\ne0$, define its effective multiplicity $$\overline m_X  =\frac{D_X}{\|\Pi_Xu\|_2^2}.$$ If $\Pi_Xu=0$, set $\overline m_X=0$.

###### ref-d26ebeb530d52594

> **Proposition: Necessary and sufficient energy statistic** \[prop:criterion\] For every nonzero literal coefficient, $$\boxed{D_X=E_X\alpha_X\overline m_X,}$$ where either $\alpha_X=\overline m_X=0$, or $$m_{\min}\le\overline m_X\le m_{\max}.$$ Therefore a proposed lower bound for $D_X$ is equivalent to the same lower bound for the product $E_X\alpha_X\overline m_X$.

> **Proof** The identity is the definition of $\overline m_X$, with $\|\Pi_Xu\|_2^2=E_X\alpha_X$. The range follows from [\[cor:energy\]](TPC-110.md#ref-af8c87d4c40c7b06).

> **Remark: Not a circular proof device** is a diagnostic factorization. It is useful only if $E_X$, $\alpha_X$, and $\overline m_X$ are estimated by independent literal arguments. Renaming $D_X/E_X$ as an angle is not an arithmetic lower bound.

###### ref-0aafdc01dba17408

> **Corollary: Forced amplification below loss one** \[cor:amplification\] Suppose the imported upper envelope for $E_X$ holds and, as an additional future theorem, $$D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2 \quad\text{for fixed }\lambda_D<1.$$ Then $$\boxed{  \alpha_X\overline m_X  =\frac{D_X}{E_X}  \ge X^{1-\lambda_D-o(1)}.}$$

> **Proof** Divide the assumed lower bound by the imported upper bound for $E_X$.

# Sharp structural obstruction

###### ref-bbffc7964bfad968

> **Theorem: Singleton-or-kernel dichotomy** \[thm:dichotomy\] Exactly one of the following holds.
>
> 1.  Every actual determinant fiber is a singleton. Then $$D_X=E_X\ll  X^{-1+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,$$ so the diagonal route alone cannot prove a lower bound with fixed $\lambda_D<1$.
>
> 2.  At least one actual determinant fiber has multiplicity at least two. Then $B_X$ has a nontrivial zero-sum kernel on the actual carrier, and the support partition alone gives no positive lower bound for $\alpha_X$.

> **Proof** In the singleton case $B_X$ is a coordinate relabeling, so $D_X=E_X$, and the imported diagonal envelope applies. In the collision case, choose two coordinates in one fiber with values $1,-1$ and all other coordinates zero. This nonzero vector lies in $\operatorname{Ker}B_X$, so its alignment is zero. Hence no positive lower angle follows from the partition alone.

###### ref-627b8c506061258c

> **Proposition: Full-support sharpness** \[prop:full-support\] Assume at least one collided fiber. Among coefficient vectors nonzero on every carrier coordinate, the ratio $$\frac{D(v)}{\|v\|_2^2}$$ can approach zero whenever every fiber is collided; if singleton fibers also occur, the contribution of those fibers can be made arbitrarily small relative to a zero-sum collided component. Thus merely declaring every carrier key active does not create a uniform lower angle.

> **Proof** On each collided fiber use nonzero roots of unity, whose sum is zero. If singleton fibers occur, assign them a common amplitude $\varepsilon$ and let $\varepsilon\to0$, while retaining a fixed zero-sum component on one collided fiber.

# Off-diagonal coherence is unavoidable

Define the ordered equal-determinant coherence $$C_{\mathrm{eq},X}  =\sum_n\sum_{\substack{t,s\in F_n\\t\ne s}}  u_t\overline{u_s}.$$ Then the exact identity is $$D_X=E_X+C_{\mathrm{eq},X}.$$

###### ref-08d7987acaf34842

> **Theorem: Necessary coherent main term** \[thm:coherence\] Under the hypothetical lower bound in [\[cor:amplification\]](TPC-110.md#ref-0aafdc01dba17408), with fixed $\lambda_D<1$, $$C_{\mathrm{eq},X}=(1-o(1))D_X.$$ In particular, any successful below-loss-one energy theorem must produce asymptotically positive equal-determinant off-diagonal coherence on the actual coefficient.

> **Proof** The imported diagonal envelope and the hypothetical lower bound give $$\frac{E_X}{D_X}\ll X^{\lambda_D-1+o(1)}=o(1).$$ Use $C_{\mathrm{eq},X}=D_X-E_X$.

TPC-95 proves that complete shared-target collisions are already lower order under any such future energy theorem [`\citep{WangTPC95}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-110.md#cite-4fd2f609889e1b73). Therefore a successful coherent main term would have to lie in the remaining determinant-preserving structure, not in duplicated native targets.

# Audited conclusion

| Statement                             | Status                                                                                  |
|:--------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Actual-support binning SVD and kernel | Exact finite theorem ($\mathrm{L0}$); literal identification is $\mathrm{L1}$.          |
| Natural-scale lower bound for $D_X$   | Not proved.                                                                             |
| Support-only determinant-energy route | Stopped by the singleton-or-kernel dichotomy.                                           |
| Required new input                    | Arithmetic control of the literal kernel angle or positive equal-determinant coherence. |
| Distinguished zero mode               | Not estimated here; TPC-111 treats it independently.                                    |
| Parity or prime-pair consequence      | None; no $\mathrm{L2}$ claim.                                                           |

The paper establishes the strongest support-only statement available: the complete SVD, the exact diagnostic product, and a sharp obstruction. It does not show that the actual coefficient is close to the binning kernel, nor that it stays away from it.

#### Independent ledgers.

Even a future determinant exponent $\lambda_D$ must be paired with an independently proved zero-mode exponent $\eta_Z$ satisfying $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$. That compatibility is separate from the physical synthesis condition $\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400$. No loss in this paper is credited to both ledgers.

#### Explicit exclusions.

No result is specialized to $h_0=2$. This paper proves no Möbius or Liouville cancellation, no determinant-energy lower bound at a growing scale, no zero-mode estimate, no breach of the sieve parity barrier, no Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, and no twin-prime theorem.
