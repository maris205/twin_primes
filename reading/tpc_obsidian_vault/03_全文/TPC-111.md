## Abstract

For the literal post-bin determinant coefficient $A_X(n)$, the distinguished frequency is $$Z_X=\sum_nA_X(n).$$ We first prove that $Z_X$ is exactly invariant under every provenance-preserving partition and coarsening of the native atoms: determinant binning can change energy but cannot create zero-mode cancellation. We then insert the literal fixed-$h_0$ affine Möbius split. On each ordered physical fiber the remaining sign is $$\sigma(r)=\mu^2(L_1(r))\mu^2(L_2(r))             \lambda(L_1(r))\lambda(L_2(r)).$$ If $S(k)=\sum_{i\le k}\sigma(r_i)$, discrete summation by parts gives the sharp dual identity $$\sup_{\|W\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}\le1}  \left|\sum_i\sigma(r_i)W(r_i)\right|  =\max_k|S(k)|.$$ Thus the exact structure-free norm controlling the weighted zero mode is a signed-prefix discrepancy, not determinant energy, support density, or nonzero-frequency dispersion.

For the actual outer synthesis we obtain a fully explicit sufficient criterion involving its literal coefficients, fiber variations, signed-prefix discrepancies, and content remainder. The criterion is fiberwise sharp for uniform control over the declared weight class; after summing outer keys it is sufficient and need not be necessary. No power saving is proved for the growing physical signs. This is an $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ reduction and obstruction theorem. It does not establish the exponent $\eta_Z>0$, a parity breakthrough, or a prime-pair conclusion.

# The distinguished frequency

Fix a prescribed $h_0\ne0$. Let $\mathcal T_X$ be the canonical native archive and $\nu:\mathcal T_X\to\mathcal N_X$ the determinant map. For literal coefficients $u_t$, define $$A_X(n)=\sum_{\nu(t)=n}u_t,\qquad  Z_X=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}A_X(n),\qquad  D_X=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N_X}|A_X(n)|^2.$$ This is the normalized determinant-DFT frequency $r=0$ of TPC-32, not an orbit or transfer-operator zero mode [`\citep{WangTPC32,WangTPC81}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-111.md#cite-49ad82103eb75eb7); [\[WangTPC81\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-111.md#cite-ff65f956e7cf0d6d).

###### ref-6ee2b61fce40a4c1

> **Theorem: Exact coarsening invariance** \[thm:invariance\] Let $\pi:\mathcal T_X\to Y$ be any finite partition map, and form $$C_\pi(y)=\sum_{\pi(t)=y}u_t.$$ Then $$\boxed{\sum_{y\in Y}C_\pi(y)=\sum_{t\in\mathcal T_X}u_t=Z_X.}$$ In particular, determinant binning, content binning, or any further exact coarsening preserves the distinguished zero mode.

> **Proof** The fibers of $\pi$ form a disjoint partition of $\mathcal T_X$, so the finite sums may be interchanged.

> **Remark: Energy behaves differently** The identity in [\[thm:invariance\]](TPC-111.md#ref-6ee2b61fce40a4c1) has no analogue for $\sum_y|C_\pi(y)|^2$: coarsening creates cross terms and can either raise or lower energy. Hence an energy census and a zero-mode estimate are logically independent.

# Literal affine sign decomposition

###### ref-2dbb51518e91db01

TPC-90 resolves a primitive physical branch into affine forms $$L_{1,\theta,X}(r)=d_{\theta,X}+s_{\theta,X}r,\qquad  L_{2,\theta,X}(r)=u_{\theta,X}+a_{\theta,X}r$$ with the same prescribed determinant $$s_{\theta,X}u_{\theta,X}  -a_{\theta,X}d_{\theta,X}=h_0.$$ Assume, on the declared block, that content, masks, both polarizations, and all native outer keys have been retained and that the literal outer reassembly has been verified. Its zero-mode interface then has the form $$Z_X  =  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  c_{\theta,X}  \sum_{r\in\mathcal I_{\theta,X}}  W_{\theta,X}(r)\sigma_{\theta,X}(r)  +E_{\mathrm{content},X}, \label{eq:outer}$$ where $$\sigma_{\theta,X}(r)  =  \mu^2(L_1(r))\mu^2(L_2(r))  \lambda(L_1(r))\lambda(L_2(r))  \in\{-1,0,1\}.$$ This is the exact split $\mu=\mu^2\lambda$ [`\citep{WangTPC90}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-111.md#cite-4f3149d531dce18d). Local squarefree density determines where $\sigma$ vanishes, but not the signs on its active support.

# Sharp signed-prefix duality

We now prove the energy-free analytic statement used by the literal zero-mode route.

> **Definition: Terminal variation norm** For an ordered finite list $w=(w_1,\dots,w_N)$, define $$\|w\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}  =  |w_N|+\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}|w_k-w_{k+1}|.$$

###### ref-2acf94bc2b313113

> **Theorem: Exact Abel duality** \[thm:duality\] Let $\sigma_1,\dots,\sigma_N\in\mathbb C$, and put $$S_k=\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_i.$$ Then every $w\in\mathbb C^N$ satisfies the exact identity $$\sum_{i=1}^N\sigma_iw_i  =  S_Nw_N+  \sum_{k=1}^{N-1}S_k(w_k-w_{k+1}),$$ and $$\boxed{  \sup_{\|w\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}\le1}  \left|\sum_{i=1}^N\sigma_iw_i\right|  =  \max_{1\le k\le N}|S_k|.}$$

> **Proof** Put $$d_k=w_k-w_{k+1}\quad(k<N),\qquad d_N=w_N.$$ Then $w_i=\sum_{k=i}^Nd_k$, so $$\sum_i\sigma_iw_i=\sum_kS_kd_k.$$ Also $\|w\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}=\sum_k|d_k|$. The upper bound is $\ell^1$-$\ell^\infty$ duality. Equality is obtained by placing all $d$-mass at an index where $|S_k|$ is maximal, with the conjugate phase of $S_k$.

###### ref-b2553a4f89b453fa

> **Corollary: Literal fiber bound** \[cor:fiber\] Order one actual interval $\mathcal I_{\theta,X}=\{r_1<\cdots<r_N\}$, and define $$\Delta_{\theta,X}  =  \max_{1\le k\le N}  \left|\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_{\theta,X}(r_i)\right|, \qquad  V_{\theta,X}  =  \|(W_{\theta,X}(r_i))_{i=1}^N\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}.$$ Then $$\left|  \sum_{r\in\mathcal I_{\theta,X}}  W_{\theta,X}(r)\sigma_{\theta,X}(r)  \right|  \le  \Delta_{\theta,X}V_{\theta,X},$$ with optimal constant for the declared variation norm.

> **Remark: The ordering is part of the statement** The order is the literal translated integer order on the physical fiber. Reordering signs to improve their prefixes changes the coefficient and is not permitted.

# A complete energy-independent go criterion

###### ref-f0c7cef77e70736c

> **Theorem: Outer zero-mode certificate** \[thm:outer\] Assume the exact literal reassembly [\[eq:outer\]](TPC-111.md#ref-2dbb51518e91db01). Then $$\boxed{  |Z_X|  \le  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}  |c_{\theta,X}|  \Delta_{\theta,X}V_{\theta,X}  +|E_{\mathrm{content},X}|.}$$ Consequently, the desired estimate $$|Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2$$ follows if the right-hand side has that size.

> **Proof** Apply [\[cor:fiber\]](TPC-111.md#ref-b2553a4f89b453fa) to every term of [\[eq:outer\]](TPC-111.md#ref-2dbb51518e91db01), then use the triangle inequality.

> **Remark: Sufficient, not necessary for one physical weight** The criterion allows no cancellation between different outer keys, so it may be stronger than the scalar target. For uniform estimates over all weights with prescribed variation, however, [\[thm:duality\]](TPC-111.md#ref-2acf94bc2b313113) shows that the prefix discrepancy is the exact operator norm. A future proof may instead exploit literal outer cancellation, but it must retain the same coefficients and provenance.

###### ref-a955fa959c436fac

> **Proposition: Unsigned data cannot pass the criterion** \[prop:unsigned\] If the Liouville product in $\sigma_{\theta,X}$ is replaced by the constant sign $1$, then $$\Delta_{\theta,X}  =  \#\{r_i:\mu^2(L_1(r_i))\mu^2(L_2(r_i))=1\}.$$ Thus squarefree-support density, by itself, gives the full active mass rather than cancellation.

> **Proof** Every nonzero summand is $+1$, so the prefix sums are nonnegative and maximal at the terminal prefix.

# Why determinant energy does not estimate the zero mode

For a vector $A=(A(n))_{n\in\mathcal N}$, Cauchy–Schwarz gives only $$|Z(A)|^2\le|\mathcal N|\,D(A).$$ It is sharp for a constant vector. On the same support and with the same moduli, roots of unity can have zero sum. This finite nonidentifiability was isolated in TPC-82 [`\citep{WangTPC82}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-111.md#cite-cfeb22890911c08a). TPC-97 further shows that constant-preserving projection preserves $Z$ while reducing energy, so it cannot manufacture a better numerator [`\citep{WangTPC97}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-111.md#cite-2cbb989294fda1ea).

###### ref-7a6abca588604019

> **Theorem: Independent-gate verdict** \[thm:verdict\] None of the following data implies a positive exponent $\eta_Z$ for the actual growing coefficient:
>
> 1.  determinant support and multiplicities;
>
> 2.  the value or a lower bound of $D_X$;
>
> 3.  unsigned squarefree density;
>
> 4.  cancellation at every nonzero determinant-DFT frequency; or
>
> 5.  exact provenance-preserving coarsening.
>
> A proof of $\eta_Z>0$ must estimate the literal signed prefixes, exploit a comparably strong outer cancellation, or introduce another arithmetic theorem that controls the same scalar $Z_X$.

> **Proof** Items (i)–(ii) are defeated by aligned and root-of-unity vectors with the same support and moduli. Item (iii) is [\[prop:unsigned\]](TPC-111.md#ref-a955fa959c436fac). A constant signal has no nonzero Fourier frequencies but has maximal zero mode, proving (iv). Item (v) is [\[thm:invariance\]](TPC-111.md#ref-6ee2b61fce40a4c1).

# Audited conclusion and next gate

| Statement                                | Status                                                                                        |
|:-----------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Coarsening invariance and Abel duality   | Exact finite theorems ($\mathrm{L0}$).                                                        |
| Literal affine-sign crosswalk            | Exact provided the TPC-90 outer reassembly is verified on the declared block ($\mathrm{L1}$). |
| Bound for the actual $\Delta_{\theta,X}$ | Not proved; parity-facing arithmetic input.                                                   |
| Positive exponent $\eta_Z$               | Not established.                                                                              |
| Determinant energy or physical endpoint  | Independent and not charged here.                                                             |

TPC-111 therefore produces a sharp *criterion*, not the desired cancellation estimate. TPC-112 may combine a future $\eta_Z$ with a future determinant exponent $\lambda_D$, but it must record the current values as “unproved” and keep the physical endpoint ledger separate.

#### Explicit exclusions.

No theorem here controls a growing two-form Liouville or Möbius correlation at fixed $h_0$. Nothing is specialized to $h_0=2$. The paper proves no $\mathrm{L2}$ saving, parity breach, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem.
