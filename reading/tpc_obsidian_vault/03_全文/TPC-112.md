## Abstract

For the same literal fixed-$h_0$ post-bin coefficient, suppose one has separately proved $$D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,\qquad  |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2.$$ At the high-$\beta$ scales $$Q_X=X^{267/400+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{133/400+o(1)},$$ we recover the exact flatness ledger $$\frac{|Z_X|^2}{D_X}  \le  X^{1/400-(2\eta_Z-\lambda_D)+o(1)}.$$ Hence the recorded pair reaches the TPC-32 threshold exactly in the closed exponent cone $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$. Equality is compatible but has zero determinant reserve.

We prove that this cone is logically independent of the strict physical synthesis condition $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400$. A direct-product countermodel shows that no determinant reserve can, without a new intertwining theorem, be spent as physical condition-number slack. Equality in the physical ledger is therefore a stop even though equality in the determinant cone is allowed. Applying the audit to TPC-109–111, we find no certified exponent pair: the preceding papers prove exact saturation, kernel, and signed-prefix criteria, but neither a growing lower bound for $D_X$ nor a growing upper bound for $Z_X$. The compatibility route remains open rather than passed or globally disproved.

**Keywords:** determinant energy; distinguished zero mode; exponent cone; reserve accounting; fixed shift; endpoint audit.

# Typed inputs

Fix a prescribed $h_0\ne0$. The determinant statistics are $$Z_X=\sum_nA_X(n),\qquad  D_X=\sum_n|A_X(n)|^2.$$ Both must be formed after literal aggregation on the same actual support and under one normalization [`\citep{WangTPC81,WangTPC82}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-ff65f956e7cf0d6d); [\[WangTPC82\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-cfeb22890911c08a). Define $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)=  \begin{cases}  |Z_X|^2/D_X,&D_X>0,\\  0,&D_X=0.  \end{cases}$$ The zero branch is consistent because $D_X=0$ forces $A_X=0$ and hence $Z_X=0$.

> **Definition: Typed exponent certificate** A pair $(\lambda_D,\eta_Z)\in[0,\infty)^2$ is *certified* only if the two displayed estimates $$D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2,\qquad  |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2$$ are both proved for the same literal growing coefficient, the same fixed $h_0$, and the same quantifier range.

An upper bound for pre-bin energy cannot fill the first slot. A finite saturation theorem or a diagnostic ratio cannot fill either slot. An averaged-shift zero-mode theorem cannot fill the second slot without a proved return to the prescribed $h_0$.

# The exact exponent cone

###### ref-f31bce1265a2c20d

> **Theorem: General flatness transfer** \[thm:general\] Let $$Q_X=X^{\beta+o(1)},\qquad  J_X=X^{1-\beta+o(1)}$$ with $0<\beta<1$. Every certified pair satisfies $$\boxed{  \mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le  X^{3\beta-2+\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}.}$$

> **Proof** Divide the square of the zero-mode upper bound by the energy lower bound: $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le  X^{\lambda_D-2\eta_Z+o(1)}  \frac{Q_X}{J_X^2}.$$ The last ratio is $X^{3\beta-2+o(1)}$.

###### ref-757e5889e549a614

> **Corollary: Critical exponent cone** \[cor:cone\] At $$\beta=\frac{267}{400},\qquad  1-\beta=\frac{133}{400},$$ put $$\rho_{\mathrm{det}}=2\eta_Z-\lambda_D.$$ Then $$\boxed{  \mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)  \le X^{1/400-\rho_{\mathrm{det}}+o(1)}.}$$ The two recorded estimates imply the TPC-32 threshold [`\citep{WangTPC32}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-49ad82103eb75eb7) $$\mathfrak F_{h_0}(X)\le X^{1/400+o(1)}$$ exactly when $$\boxed{\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z.}$$

> **Proof** The scale identity is $$3\frac{267}{400}-2=\frac1{400}.$$ Now apply [\[thm:general\]](TPC-112.md#ref-f31bce1265a2c20d).

> **Remark: Sharp scope** The word “exactly” concerns logical deduction from the two recorded bounds. If $\lambda_D>2\eta_Z$, those bounds do not certify the threshold; the actual coefficient might satisfy sharper estimates.

# Boundary and reserve classifications

> **Definition: Determinant reserve** The number $$\rho_{\mathrm{det}}=2\eta_Z-\lambda_D$$ is the reserve of the two-exponent transfer, provided the pair is certified.

###### ref-184fb0dca1cfcf41

> **Proposition: Three determinant statuses** \[prop:statuses\] For a certified pair:
>
> 1.  if $\rho_{\mathrm{det}}>0$, the flatness estimate has a fixed power reserve;
>
> 2.  if $\rho_{\mathrm{det}}=0$, it reaches the threshold with no reserve;
>
> 3.  if $\rho_{\mathrm{det}}<0$, the recorded pair fails this certificate, but does not prove the target false.

> **Proof** Read the exponent in [\[cor:cone\]](TPC-112.md#ref-757e5889e549a614).

If only $\eta_Z=0$ is available, compatibility forces $\lambda_D=0$. Thus a positive determinant-energy loss requires a compensating positive zero-mode gain in this ledger.

# Independence from the physical endpoint

The complete route also has a synthesis operator whose separately derived fixed-power cost is denoted by $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}$. The inherited rule is $$\boxed{\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<\frac1{400}.}$$ Equality is a stop [`\citep{WangTPC82,WangTPC92,WangMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-cfeb22890911c08a); [\[WangTPC92\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-8444c63105c2920e); [\[WangMVP1\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-934c301de52ef9a3).

###### ref-21b8c2e377089de9

> **Theorem: No exchange rate between ledgers** \[thm:independence\] Without an additional literal intertwining theorem, neither $\rho_{\mathrm{det}}$ nor the pair $(\lambda_D,\eta_Z)$ imposes any bound on $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}$. Conversely, the value of $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}$ imposes no determinant-energy or zero-mode estimate. Therefore the audited route requires the conjunction $$\boxed{  \lambda_D\le2\eta_Z  \quad\text{and}\quad  \Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<\frac1{400},}$$ not a merged inequality such as $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400+\rho_{\mathrm{det}}$.

> **Proof** Take any finite coefficient vector realizing prescribed nonzero statistics $D$ and $Z$. On an independent two-dimensional synthesis space, let the physical comparison operator be $\operatorname{diag}(1,X^L)$, with arbitrary $L\ge0$. Changing $L$ changes its condition-number exponent but leaves $D,Z$, and their reserve unchanged. Conversely, fix the synthesis map to be an isometry and vary the coefficient vector between a constant vector and a zero-sum vector. This changes the determinant flatness without changing the physical operator norm.
>
> These direct-product models do not describe the arithmetic coefficient. They prove the logical point: a relationship between the ledgers requires a new map-specific theorem and cannot be created by bookkeeping.

> **Corollary: Different equality rules** The boundary $$\lambda_D=2\eta_Z$$ passes the determinant compatibility test with zero reserve, while $$\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}=\frac1{400}$$ fails the strict physical test. The first equality may not be cited as slack for the second.

# No-double-charging protocol

> **Definition: Typed loss record** A fixed-power record consists of a source identifier, an exponent, and exactly one role: $$\texttt{energy},\quad  \texttt{zero},\quad  \texttt{physical},\quad  \texttt{soft remainder}.$$ If one analytic estimate genuinely enters two inequalities, the proof must display the joint inequality. Merely copying its exponent into two rows is duplicate charging.

###### ref-dd728e119aec6408

> **Proposition: Valid ledger composition** \[prop:ledger\] A proof-carrying compatibility certificate must:
>
> 1.  cite distinct proved bounds for $D_X$ and $Z_X$, or one explicit joint theorem;
>
> 2.  compute $\rho_{\mathrm{det}}$ once;
>
> 3.  list every physical map cost once under $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}$;
>
> 4.  reject any repeated source identifier unless an exact factorization shows two disjoint uses; and
>
> 5.  leave open exponents open rather than assigning them zero.

> **Proof** Items (i)–(iii) reproduce the inequalities used in \[[cor:cone](TPC-112.md#ref-757e5889e549a614),[thm:independence](TPC-112.md#ref-21b8c2e377089de9)\]. Repeating a multiplicative factor does not create a new estimate, proving (iv). Assigning zero to an open term asserts a subpower theorem that has not been proved, proving (v).

# Audit of TPC-109–111

TPC-109 proves a sharp phase-saturation theorem for the coherent terminal-prime square. It supplies neither a growing energy lower bound nor a zero-mode upper bound [`\citep{WangTPC109}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-44c57899bbdf255a).

TPC-110 proves the exact determinant-binning SVD and factors $$D_X=E_X\alpha_X\overline m_X.$$ It shows that support alone cannot lower-bound the kernel angle, but does not estimate that angle for the growing literal coefficient [`\citep{WangTPC110}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-8b17291da7179c73).

TPC-111 proves exact coarsening invariance and sharp signed-prefix duality. Its outer criterion would imply a zero-mode exponent if the literal signed discrepancies were bounded, but no such growing arithmetic bound is proved [`\citep{WangTPC111}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-112.md#cite-942c4e427ec261c3).

###### ref-7a6abca588604019

> **Theorem: Current compatibility verdict** \[thm:verdict\] The established results through TPC-111 certify no pair $(\lambda_D,\eta_Z)$ in the exponent cone. Therefore the determinant–zero route is currently $$\boxed{\text{open inputs; compatibility not yet testable}.}$$ It is neither a passed gate nor a global impossibility theorem.

> **Proof** TPC-110 supplies no lower bound of the required form for $D_X$, and TPC-111 supplies no upper bound of the required form for $Z_X$. By the typed-certificate definition, there is no certified pair to insert into [\[cor:cone\]](TPC-112.md#ref-757e5889e549a614).

# Stop/go table and claim boundary

| Gate              | Go certificate                                  | Stop or reroute certificate                                 |
|:------------------|:------------------------------------------------|:------------------------------------------------------------|
| Energy            | Literal lower bound with a recorded $\lambda_D$ | Actual upper obstruction excluding the desired loss profile |
| Zero mode         | Literal upper bound with a recorded $\eta_Z$    | Unavoidable signed-prefix or outer residual above target    |
| Compatibility     | $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$                           | Recorded pair has $\lambda_D>2\eta_Z$; seek sharper input   |
| Physical endpoint | $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}<1/400$                 | $\Lambda_{\mathrm{phys}}\ge1/400$, including equality       |

#### What is proved.

The exponent formula, compatibility cone, reserve classification, ledger independence, no-double-charging protocol, and current “inputs open” verdict are rigorous $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ audit results.

#### What is not proved.

Neither input exponent is established for the actual growing coefficient. No physical synthesis bound is established. No $\mathrm{L2}$ fixed-$h_0$ saving, parity breach, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem is claimed. Nothing is specialized to $h_0=2$.
