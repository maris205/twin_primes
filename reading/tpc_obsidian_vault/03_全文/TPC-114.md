## Abstract

The current TPC physical route contains two distinct identifications. Source records first collide at the literal residual label $$q(u)=(\gamma_u,j_u,s_u),\qquad s_u=d(m_u)r_u,$$ and residual labels may then collide after passage to a native frequency label $\pi(q(u))$. Treating these operations as one informal “grouping” hides both their kernels and their losses.

We separate them as a weighted residual coarea map $$\mathcal R_a e_u=a_u e_{q(u)}$$ and an unweighted native forgetting map $\mathcal Pe_y=e_{\pi(y)}$. Three exact results follow. First, $dr=d'r'$ has the complete coprime parametrization $$d=ga,\quad d'=gb,\quad r=bt,\quad r'=at,  \qquad (a,b)=1.$$ Second, $$\mathcal R_a\mathcal R_a^*=\operatorname{diag}(W_y),  \qquad  W_y=\sum_{q(u)=y}|a_u|^2,$$ so every nonzero singular value of $\mathcal R_a$ is exactly $\sqrt{W_y}$. Third, the residual and native Grams are $$G_{\rm res}=\mathcal R_a^*\mathcal R_a,\qquad  G_{\rm nat}=\mathcal R_a^*\mathcal P^*\mathcal P\mathcal R_a,$$ and their difference is exactly the exchange between unequal residuals sharing one native label. For any upstream synthesis $\mathcal S$, the complete native observation satisfies $$(\mathcal P\mathcal R_a\mathcal S)^*(\mathcal P\mathcal R_a\mathcal S)  =\mathcal S^*G_{\rm nat}\mathcal S.$$

The upper $\ell^2$-energy factor is the largest native multiplicity, and the corresponding $\ell^2$-amplitude norm is its square root. Neither is automatically the H9 max-packet-to-scalar cost. When a native fiber contains at least two residual labels, no positive lower transfer exists on the full space without excluding the cancellation kernel: two residuals may add or cancel exactly after native forgetting. Thus this paper supplies an exact $\mathrm{L0}$ algebra and an $\mathrm{L1}$ provenance dictionary, but no growing multiplicity bound, fixed-$h_0$ $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate, parity breakthrough, or prime-pair theorem.

# Why two groupings are necessary

TPC-46 proves that Bohr–Fourier orthogonality retains a literal residual label and that positivity-only localization can spend the full alias mass [`\citep{WangTPC46}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-114.md#cite-2f37865d719a8eb5). TPC-58 introduces a native affine alias Gram, while TPC-61 identifies the remaining cofactor/parity kernel [`\citep{WangTPC58,WangTPC61}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-114.md#cite-578b76b7099ebcf1); [\[WangTPC61\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-114.md#cite-c0f79b0b6cb1358c). TPC-113 gives the correct quotient condition number for any declared finite physical synthesis [`\citep{WangTPC113}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-114.md#cite-0988df134318b83f). The missing dictionary is the exact map between the source, residual, and native levels.

Let $U$ be a finite set of source records. A record carries $$u=(m_u,r_u,\gamma_u,j_u,\xi_u),\qquad  a_u\in\mathbb C,\qquad d_u=d(m_u)\in\mathbb N.$$ The auxiliary label $\xi_u$ records any remaining provenance but does not enter the residual key $$q(u)=(\gamma_u,j_u,d_ur_u)\in Y.$$ Let $\pi:Y\to I$ be the exact native label map supplied by the application. We do not assume it is injective.

# The divisor-collision normal form

###### ref-4987c85d9716cc80

> **Lemma: Complete product-collision parametrization** \[lem:collision\] For positive integers $d,d',r,r'$, the equality $dr=d'r'$ holds if and only if there exist $g,a,b,t\in\mathbb N$ such that $$d=ga,\qquad d'=gb,\qquad (a,b)=1,\qquad  r=bt,\qquad r'=at.$$ The parameters are unique with $g=(d,d')$.

> **Proof** Write $d=ga$, $d'=gb$, where $g=(d,d')$ and $(a,b)=1$. Then $dr=d'r'$ is $ar=br'$. Coprimality gives $b\mid r$ and $a\mid r'$, hence $r=bt$ and $r'=at$ for the same $t$. The converse is immediate, and the gcd choice proves uniqueness.

> **Remark: What the lemma does not count** identifies every arithmetic collision, but it does not bound how many admissible quadruples occur in the actual active support. Such a bound must retain the masks on $m,r,\gamma$ and $j$. Replacing them by a rectangular ambient box would change the physical fiber.

# Weighted residual coarea

Let $\{e_u:u\in U\}$ and $\{e_y:y\in Y\}$ be orthonormal bases. Define $$\mathcal R_a:\mathbb C^U\to\mathbb C^Y,\qquad  \mathcal R_a e_u=a_u e_{q(u)}.$$ Zero-weight records are allowed. Put $$W_y=\sum_{\substack{u\in U\\q(u)=y}}|a_u|^2.$$

###### ref-5f967ca33cefef7f

> **Theorem: Exact coarea singular values** \[thm:coarea\] The weighted grouping map satisfies $$\boxed{\quad  \mathcal R_a\mathcal R_a^*=\operatorname{diag}_{y\in Y}(W_y).  \quad}$$ Consequently its nonzero singular values, with multiplicity, are $\{\sqrt{W_y}:W_y>0\}$, and $$\|\mathcal R_a\|^2=\max_yW_y,\qquad  \|\mathcal R_a^\dagger\|^2=\frac1{\min_{W_y>0}W_y},$$ $$\kappa_{\rm grp}(\mathcal R_a)^2  =\frac{\max_yW_y}{\min_{W_y>0}W_y}.$$

> **Proof** For $y,y'\in Y$, $$\langle e_y,\mathcal R_a\mathcal R_a^*e_{y'}\rangle  =\sum_{u\in U}|a_u|^2    \mathbf 1_{\{q(u)=y\}}\mathbf 1_{\{q(u)=y'\}}.$$ The expression vanishes for $y\ne y'$ and equals $W_y$ on the diagonal. The remaining assertions follow from the singular-value decomposition and the quotient convention of TPC-113.

> **Remark: Weighted, not cardinality-only** If $|a_u|$ varies, the fiber cardinality is not the coarea weight. The exact quantity is $W_y$. A divisor-bound estimate for the number of records is therefore insufficient unless the physical weights are controlled on the same support.

# The native Gram and alias exchange

Define the native forgetting map $$\mathcal P:\mathbb C^Y\to\mathbb C^I,\qquad \mathcal Pe_y=e_{\pi(y)}.$$ For $i\in I$, write $$k_i=\#\{y\in Y:\pi(y)=i\}.$$

###### ref-6230ede14b4be1c6

> **Proposition: Native multiplicity spectrum** \[prop:native\] One has $$\mathcal P\mathcal P^*=\operatorname{diag}_{i\in I}(k_i),  \qquad  \|\mathcal P\|^2=\max_i k_i.$$ The nonzero singular values are $\sqrt{k_i}$ for $k_i>0$.

> **Proof** The rows of $\mathcal P$ have disjoint supports, and row $i$ has exactly $k_i$ unit entries. The result is [\[thm:coarea\]](TPC-114.md#ref-5f967ca33cefef7f) with all weights equal to one and grouping map $\pi$.

The two source-side Grams are $$G_{\rm res}=\mathcal R_a^*\mathcal R_a,\qquad  G_{\rm nat}=\mathcal R_a^*\mathcal P^*\mathcal P\mathcal R_a.$$

###### ref-6b74fd5dfd700757

> **Theorem: Exact Gram dictionary** \[thm:dictionary\] For all $u,v\in U$, $$\begin{aligned}  (G_{\rm res})_{uv}  &=\overline{a_u}a_v\,\mathbf 1_{\{q(u)=q(v)\}},\\  (G_{\rm nat})_{uv}  &=\overline{a_u}a_v\,    \mathbf 1_{\{\pi(q(u))=\pi(q(v))\}},\end{aligned}$$ and hence $$\boxed{\quad  (G_{\rm nat}-G_{\rm res})_{uv}  =\overline{a_u}a_v\,   \mathbf 1_{\{q(u)\ne q(v),\ \pi(q(u))=\pi(q(v))\}}.  \quad}$$ Thus the difference contains exactly the exchange between distinct residual fibers that alias to the same native label.

> **Proof** The first formula is the matrix coefficient of $\mathcal R_a^*\mathcal R_a$. Since $(\mathcal P^*\mathcal P)_{yy'}=\mathbf 1_{\{\pi(y)=\pi(y')\}}$, insertion between $\mathcal R_a^*$ and $\mathcal R_a$ gives the second. Subtraction leaves precisely equal-native, unequal-residual pairs.

> **Remark: The exchange is not a positive correction** Although both $G_{\rm nat}$ and $G_{\rm res}$ are positive semidefinite, their difference need not be. The exchange terms can add or cancel depending on the coefficient phases, so they may not be entered as an automatic energy gain.

###### ref-9fc463e6e09cfca2

> **Corollary: Exact provenance intertwining** \[cor:intertwining\] Let $\mathcal S:\mathbb C^\Theta\to\mathbb C^U$ be any upstream literal synthesis and set $T=\mathcal P\mathcal R_a\mathcal S$. Then $$\boxed{\qquad T^*T=\mathcal S^*G_{\rm nat}\mathcal S.\qquad}$$ In particular, every native quadratic estimate on the inherited coefficient is a statement about this compressed physical Gram, not about $G_{\rm res}$ alone.

> **Proof** Expand $T^*T$ and use the definition of $G_{\rm nat}$.

# Sharp lower-transfer obstruction

###### ref-6f5e16fcde013232

> **Proposition: Addition and cancellation in one native fiber** \[prop:cancellation\] Suppose $y_1\ne y_2$ and $\pi(y_1)=\pi(y_2)$. On their span, $$\|e_{y_1}+e_{y_2}\|^2=2,\qquad  \|\mathcal P(e_{y_1}+e_{y_2})\|^2=4,$$ whereas $$\|e_{y_1}-e_{y_2}\|^2=2,\qquad  \mathcal P(e_{y_1}-e_{y_2})=0.$$ Therefore the upper energy factor $k_{\max}$ is sharp, while no positive lower comparison $\|\mathcal Pz\|^2\ge c\|z\|^2$ can hold on the full residual space.

> **Proof** Both residual basis vectors map to the same native basis vector. The displayed identities follow by addition and subtraction.

> **Remark: Collision is necessary for this obstruction** If every native fiber is a singleton, then $\mathcal P$ is an isometry onto its range and the cancellation example is unavailable. An actual stop certificate must also show that the distinguished coefficient, or the relevant range of $\mathcal R_a\mathcal S$, meets the collision kernel.

> **Remark: Why separate losses must not be multiplied blindly** computes the complete Gram before any condition-number estimate. Bounding $\mathcal S$, $\mathcal R_a$, and $\mathcal P$ separately can be convenient, but multiplying the three worst-case costs may charge directions that never coexist. The native physical condition number should be measured from $\mathcal S^*G_{\rm nat}\mathcal S$ whenever that matrix is available.

# Finite exact certificate

The script `experiments/tpc114_grouping_certificate.py` uses rational arithmetic. It enumerates product collisions, constructs $\mathcal R_a,\mathcal P,G_{\rm res},G_{\rm nat}$, and checks:

1.  the coprime normal form in [\[lem:collision\]](TPC-114.md#ref-4987c85d9716cc80);

2.  $\mathcal R_a\mathcal R_a^*=\operatorname{diag}(W_y)$;

3.  $\mathcal P\mathcal P^*=\operatorname{diag}(k_i)$;

4.  both entrywise Gram formulas and the exchange identity; and

5.  the sharp addition/cancellation pair.

The JSON certificate is finite exact algebra. It is not evidence that actual TPC fibers have bounded multiplicity.

#### Established.

give a complete finite-dimensional dictionary. The matrix identities and sharp obstruction are $\mathrm{L0}$. Declaring $q(u)=(\gamma,j,d(m)r)$ and retaining all literal weights is the $\mathrm{L1}$ arithmetic interface anticipated by TPC-MVP1 [`\citep{WangMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-114.md#cite-934c301de52ef9a3).

#### Not established.

We prove no asymptotic bound for $W_y$, $k_i$, or the positive spectrum of the actual growing native Gram. We do not identify a protected complement to the cancellation kernel, recover one prescribed $h_0$, or prove a fixed-power arithmetic saving. There is no automatic conversion from the $\ell^2$ norm $\sqrt{k_{\max}}$ to the H9 max-packet-to-scalar norm; that requires the actual operator chain. There is no $\mathrm{L2}$ advance, parity breakthrough, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem. Nothing is specialized to $h_0=2$.
