## Abstract

TPC-111 showed that the distinguished determinant zero mode survives every provenance-preserving coarsening and proved the sharp signed-prefix/BV duality. We turn that finite criterion into a complete exponent ledger. For a growing family, a literal prefix saving $X^{-\delta}$, an outer BV-envelope cost $X^{\ell_Z}$, and a content-remainder exponent $\eta_{\rm cont}$ imply $$\eta_Z^{\rm cert}  =\min\{\delta-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}.$$ Here $\delta\ge\ell_Z$ is required; otherwise this certificate provides no nonnegative zero-mode exponent for the TPC-112 cone. Combined with the TPC-112 determinant compatibility cone, sufficient thresholds for this Abel/triangle certificate are $$\delta\ge\ell_Z+\frac{\lambda_D}{2},  \qquad  \eta_{\rm cont}\ge\frac{\lambda_D}{2}.$$ We also give the minimum rule for a subpower family of differently scaled fiber classes. This is an exact transfer statement, not a proof of any growing input. The finite duality is imported and restated self-contained; the typed fixed-$h_0$ ledger is $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$. No $\mathrm{L2}$ cancellation theorem is claimed.

# The literal ordered zero-mode pairing

Fix the prescribed nonzero shift $h_0$. At the verified outer-reassembly interface of TPC-111—that is, conditional on its content, masks, polarizations and native keys being retained and the literal outer reassembly being verified—the literal zero mode is indexed by outer affine keys $\theta\in\Theta_X$, with canonically ordered intervals $$\mathcal I_{\theta,X}  =\{r_{\theta,1}<\cdots<r_{\theta,m_\theta}\},$$ and writes $$Z_X=  \sum_{\theta\in\Theta_X}c_{\theta,X}  \sum_{i=1}^{m_\theta}  W_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i})  \sigma_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i})  +E_{\rm content,X}^{(111)}$$ [`\citep{WangTPC111}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-122.md#cite-942c4e427ec261c3). Choose a predeclared retained set $\mathcal F_X\subseteq\Theta_X$. For $f=\theta\in\mathcal F_X$, set $$t_{f,i}=r_{\theta,i},\qquad  \sigma_{f,i}=\sigma_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i}),\qquad  w_{f,i}=c_{\theta,X}W_{\theta,X}(r_{\theta,i}).$$ Define $E_{\rm cont,X}$ to be the original content term plus the exact contributions of every outer key in $\Theta_X\setminus\mathcal F_X$. Then the complete literal zero mode is $$Z_X=\sum_{f\in\mathcal F_X}Z_f+E_{\rm cont,X},\qquad  Z_f=\sum_{i=1}^{m_f}\sigma_{f,i}w_{f,i}.$$ Thus $\sigma_{f,i}$ retains the literal arithmetic sign and affine data, while $w_{f,i}$ retains the outer coefficient and reconstruction multiplier. The retained/content partition and this factor allocation are fixed before applying the estimate. Moving a rough arithmetic factor between the two changes the gate and must be recorded.

Define signed prefixes $$S_{f,k}=\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_{f,i},\qquad  \Delta_f=\max_{1\le k\le m_f}|S_{f,k}|,$$ and the endpoint-anchored variation norm $$\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}  =  |w_{f,m_f}|  +\sum_{k=1}^{m_f-1}|w_{f,k}-w_{f,k+1}|.$$

# Imported Abel identity and sharp duality

The next theorem is the finite duality already proved in TPC-111. We include its short proof to fix the exact norm used by the exponent ledger; it is not presented as a second new duality theorem.

###### ref-d88b28a49801eda5

> **Lemma: Finite Abel identity** \[lem:abel\] For every complex pair of sequences $(\sigma_i)_{i=1}^m$ and $(w_i)_{i=1}^m$, $$\boxed{  \sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_iw_i  =  S_mw_m+\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}S_k(w_k-w_{k+1}),} \qquad  S_k=\sum_{i=1}^k\sigma_i.$$

> **Proof** Use $\sigma_i=S_i-S_{i-1}$, with $S_0=0$, and telescope.

###### ref-2acf94bc2b313113

> **Theorem: Sharp signed-prefix/BV duality** \[thm:duality\] For a fixed signed sequence $\sigma=(\sigma_i)_{i=1}^m$, $$\boxed{  \sup_{\|w\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}\le1}  \left|\sum_{i=1}^m\sigma_iw_i\right|  =\max_{1\le k\le m}|S_k|.}$$

> **Proof** and the triangle inequality give the upper bound. Choose $k_*$ maximizing $|S_k|$. If $S_{k_*}\ne0$, set $$\omega=\frac{\overline{S_{k_*}}}{|S_{k_*}|},  \qquad  w_i=\begin{cases}  \omega,&i\le k_*,\\  0,&i>k_*.  \end{cases}$$ If $k_*<m$, the norm has one jump of size one and zero endpoint; if $k_*=m$, it has endpoint one and no jump. Thus $\|w\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}=1$, while the pairing equals $\omega S_{k_*}=|S_{k_*}|$. The zero sequence is immediate.

> **Remark: Fiberwise sharpness** The theorem is sharp for each fixed fiber. After summing fibers, the triangle inequality $$|Z_X|\le\sum_f\Delta_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}$$ is a sufficient outer certificate, not a necessary one: additional inter-fiber cancellation may occur.

# The growing exponent transfer

Let $A_f\ge0$ be a literal comparison scale for the signed coefficient on fiber $f$. The future prefix theorem and the outer reassembly theorem must be stated separately.

> **Definition: Typed prefix and envelope inputs** \[def:inputs\] For exponents $\delta,\ell_Z\ge0$, define: $$\begin{aligned} \text{\rm(Prefix)}\qquad &\Delta_f\le  X^{-\delta+o(1)}A_f  \quad\text{uniformly on the retained actual outer fibers}; \label{eq:prefix-input}\\ \text{\rm(Envelope)}\qquad &\sum_{f\in\mathcal F_X}  A_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}  \le X^{\ell_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2. \label{eq:envelope-input}\end{aligned}$$ In addition, a complete certificate must record $$|E_{\rm cont,X}|  \le X^{-\eta_{\rm cont}+o(1)}Q_X^2 \label{eq:content-input}$$ for some $\eta_{\rm cont}\ge0$. All three statements use the same factor allocation and quantifier range.

###### ref-00e06779acd0c21d

> **Theorem: Prefix-to-zero-mode transfer** \[thm:transfer\] If [\[eq:prefix-input\]](TPC-122.md#ref-bc41bcbef6792cd4)–[\[eq:content-input\]](TPC-122.md#ref-33f88d61abc22fd3) hold and $\delta\ge\ell_Z$, then $$\boxed{  |Z_X|  \le  X^{-\eta_Z^{\rm cert}+o(1)}Q_X^2,}$$ where the admissible zero-mode exponent is $$\boxed{  \eta_Z^{\rm cert}  =\min\{\delta-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}.}$$

> **Proof** Apply [\[thm:duality\]](TPC-122.md#ref-2acf94bc2b313113) fiberwise and sum: $$|Z_X|  \le\sum_f\Delta_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}  \le X^{-\delta+o(1)}  \sum_fA_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}.$$ Now use the envelope input and add the content remainder. The slower of the two powers controls the sum.

> **Remark: A negative displayed exponent** If $\delta<\ell_Z$, the prefix branch still gives $X^{-(\delta-\ell_Z)+o(1)}Q_X^2$, but it does not certify a nonnegative saving exponent. One must not enter a negative $\eta_Z^{\rm cert}$ into the TPC-112 cone.

# Compatibility with determinant energy

TPC-112 requires $$D_X\ge X^{-\lambda_D+o(1)}Q_X^3J_X^2, \qquad  |Z_X|\le X^{-\eta_Z+o(1)}Q_X^2, \qquad  \lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$$ on the same literal post-bin coefficient [`\citep{WangTPC112}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-122.md#cite-151d0a1039e9f894).

###### ref-56f3c5ca8c28cae6

> **Corollary: Exact threshold for this Abel/triangle certificate** \[cor:threshold\] Under the typed inputs of [\[def:inputs\]](TPC-122.md#ref-49e7daef61a3ffe9), a separately certified determinant exponent $\lambda_D$ is compatible whenever both $$\boxed{  \delta\ge\ell_Z+\frac{\lambda_D}{2},  \qquad  \eta_{\rm cont}\ge\frac{\lambda_D}{2}.}$$ The reserve in the determinant-zero cone is $$\rho_{\rm det}  =  2\min\{\delta-\ell_Z,\eta_{\rm cont}\}-\lambda_D.$$

> **Proof** Insert the admissible exponent $\eta_Z^{\rm cert}$ from [\[thm:transfer\]](TPC-122.md#ref-00e06779acd0c21d) into $\lambda_D\le2\eta_Z$.

Combining with the phase certificate of TPC-121, where one admissible recorded exponent is $$\lambda_D^{\rm cert} =(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+,$$ gives the explicit joint target $$\boxed{\begin{aligned}  \delta&\ge  \ell_Z+  \frac{(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+}{2},\\  \eta_{\rm cont}&\ge  \frac{(\lambda_E+2\lambda_\phi-\gamma_R)_+}{2}.  \end{aligned}}$$ This equation is bookkeeping, not a joint arithmetic theorem [`\citep{WangTPC121}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-122.md#cite-d3e88dbca3ebc492).

###### ref-affd5f239a1d18c4

> **Corollary: Uniform subpower-class ledger** \[cor:classes\] Let $\{\mathcal F_{X,j}:j\in\mathcal J_X\}$ be a predeclared partition of the retained actual fibers. Suppose there are a common constant $C$, a common sequence $\varepsilon_X\to0$, and nonnegative numbers $\delta_{X,j},\ell_{X,j}$ such that $$|\mathcal J_X|\le X^{\varepsilon_X},$$ $$\Delta_f\le  C X^{-\delta_{X,j}+\varepsilon_X}A_f  \quad(f\in\mathcal F_{X,j}),  \qquad  \sum_{f\in\mathcal F_{X,j}}  A_f\|w_f\|_{\mathrm{BV}_*}  \le C X^{\ell_{X,j}+\varepsilon_X}Q_X^2$$ for every $j\in\mathcal J_X$. Assume also the content estimate $$|E_{\rm cont,X}|  \le C X^{-\eta_{\rm cont}+\varepsilon_X}Q_X^2.$$ For any fixed $\eta_{\rm cls}\ge0$ satisfying $$\eta_{\rm cls}  \le  \liminf_{X\to\infty}  \inf_{j\in\mathcal J_X}(\delta_{X,j}-\ell_{X,j}),$$ where the infimum of the empty set is $+\infty$ (in that case the retained sum is zero), one admissible recorded exponent is $$\boxed{  \eta_Z^{\rm cert}  =\min\{\eta_{\rm cont},\eta_{\rm cls}\}.}$$

> **Proof** On class $j$, [\[thm:duality\]](TPC-122.md#ref-2acf94bc2b313113) and the two uniform bounds give $$\sum_{f\in\mathcal F_{X,j}}  |Z_f|  \le  C^2X^{-(\delta_{X,j}-\ell_{X,j})+2\varepsilon_X}Q_X^2.$$ The liminf condition makes $\delta_{X,j}-\ell_{X,j}\ge\eta_{\rm cls}-o(1)$ uniformly in $j$. Summing at most $X^{\varepsilon_X}$ classes and adding the content remainder proves the claim.

> **Remark: Typed losses and no double charging** The quantity $\delta-\ell_Z$ and the content exponent $\eta_{\rm cont}$ have zero-mode/soft-remainder roles; $\lambda_D$ has the determinant-energy role; and $\Lambda_{\rm phys}$ has the physical-reassembly role. A source identifier may not be spent in two roles unless a theorem supplies the corresponding joint inequality and records the dependency replacement. In particular, determinant reserve is not physical endpoint slack.

# Why all prefixes are necessary

###### ref-a152e3dfb726b453

> **Proposition: The final sum is insufficient** \[prop:final\] For every $N\ge1$ there is a real sequence of length $2N$ with $$S_{2N}=0,\qquad \max_{k\le2N}|S_k|=N.$$ Hence cancellation of the complete unweighted fiber sum does not control the zero-mode pairing against varying multipliers.

> **Proof** Take $N$ entries equal to $1$, followed by $N$ entries equal to $-1$.

> **Proposition: Ordering is part of the certificate** The multiset of signed atoms determines the final sum but not the maximum prefix. Therefore a prefix theorem must use the canonical literal order, or prove a permutation-uniform estimate strong enough to include it. Choosing a favorable order after seeing the signs is not a provenance-preserving argument.

> **Proof** For $N$ signs $+1$ and $N$ signs $-1$, grouping equal signs gives maximum prefix $N$, whereas alternating signs gives maximum prefix $1$.

The signed sequence imported from TPC-107 and TPC-108 contains actual Möbius/Liouville-type factors [`\citep{WangTPC107,WangTPC108}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-122.md#cite-7c9e22ccbb160724); [\[WangTPC108\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-122.md#cite-493205aca788aded). A bound for independent random signs, an averaged shift, or a rearranged sequence does not prove [\[eq:prefix-input\]](TPC-122.md#ref-bc41bcbef6792cd4).

# Audit certificate and next decision

The deterministic script `experiments/tpc122_prefix_transfer_audit.py` checks the complex Abel identity, the BV bound, the sharp step extremizer, the large-prefix/final-zero counterexample, the TPC-111 factor-allocation crosswalk, the content-aware compatibility/reserve ledger, and the uniform subpower-class minimum rule.

| Statement                                                                                                                    | Level and status                            |
|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------|
| Abel identity and sharp BV duality                                                                                           | Exact finite theorem ($\mathrm{L0}$).       |
| Crosswalk $f=\theta$, $t_{f,i}=r_{\theta,i}$ to the TPC-111 ordered affine outer fibers, with the declared factor allocation | Fixed-$h_0$ interface ($\mathrm{L1}$).      |
| Growing prefix input [\[eq:prefix-input\]](TPC-122.md#ref-bc41bcbef6792cd4)                                                  | Not proved; parity-facing arithmetic input. |
| Outer BV envelope [\[eq:envelope-input\]](TPC-122.md#ref-a20445b3144b4f06)                                                   | Not proved on the complete actual family.   |
| Content remainder [\[eq:content-input\]](TPC-122.md#ref-33f88d61abc22fd3)                                                    | Not proved at the required exponent.        |
| Positive zero-mode exponent or fixed-$h_0$ saving                                                                            | Not proved; no $\mathrm{L2}$ advance.       |

TPC-122 reduces the zero-mode side to three typed estimates and computes the exact saving delivered by this certificate. It does not establish any of them. A next paper should inspect the literal ordering and factor allocation on one actual fiber class; if the multiplier has polynomial BV cost, the signed prefix saving must exceed that cost before the determinant exponent is even considered.

#### Explicit exclusions.

Nothing here proves H3, the actual prefix estimate, a Möbius or Liouville cancellation theorem, a parity breach, a prime-pair lower bound, a Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, or the twin-prime conjecture. The strict physical endpoint $\Lambda_{\rm phys}<1/400$ remains independent.
