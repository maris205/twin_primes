## Abstract

A fixed-shift hard packet cannot be recovered from a frozen or averaged family merely by retaining the same notation $h_0$. We give two exact and disjoint invocation routes. For a shift-tagged archive with stages $T_j$, prescribed-shift selectors $P_j$, and declared sliced stages $T_j^{(0)}$, define $$D_j=P_jT_j-T_j^{(0)}P_{j-1}.$$ The full slicing defect is $$P_sT_s\cdots T_1-T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_1^{(0)}P_0  =  \sum_{j=1}^s  T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_{j+1}^{(0)}  D_jT_{j-1}\cdots T_1.$$ Thus stagewise commutation is a proof-carrying sufficient condition for exact direct slicing, while a nonzero composite defect on the literal packet stops that declared route. If only averaged observations are available, the sharp squared evaluation cost is $$K_X(h_0)=b(h_0)^*(A^*\Omega A)^\dagger b(h_0),$$ finite exactly when $b(h_0)\in\operatorname{ran}(A^*\Omega A)$. An averaged amplitude saving $\sigma$ and $K_X(h_0)\le X^{2\lambda_{\rm loc}+o(1)}$ yield at most the fixed-shift saving $\sigma-\lambda_{\rm loc}$. Direct slicing and de-aliasing are not additive evidence for the same operation. The identities are $\mathrm{L0}$, and their attachment to the TPC-123/124 archive is conditional $\mathrm{L1}$. The actual growing shift archive and profile space are absent, so the interface remains $\textsc{not-testable}$. No $\mathrm{L2}$ arithmetic estimate or prime-pair theorem is proved.

# Two routes that must not be conflated

The original hard packet is defined at one prescribed nonzero shift $h_0$. TPC-119 therefore requires later operations to retain that shift, while TPC-115 shows that evaluation at one shift can have a nontrivial or infinite cost after averaging [`\citep{WangTPC115,WangTPC119}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-5133744dd356e573); [\[WangTPC119\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-6287e7598dfbed80). TPC-123 and TPC-124 provide the appropriate archive and physical-reassembly schemas [`\citep{WangTPC123,WangTPC124}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-6ff895d094f015b3); [\[WangTPC124\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-ecc9b818c6056a02). They still leave two logically different possibilities:

1.  *direct slicing*: every stage preserves shift labels and the $h_0$ subspace is selected exactly;

2.  *de-aliasing*: only averaged observations are bounded, and fixed evaluation is reconstructed from the actual profile space.

If the first route holds, there is no localization inversion. If only the second route is available, its Christoffel cost must be charged. Neither route proves an arithmetic bound.

> **Definition: Claim levels** Finite operator and pseudoinverse identities are $\mathrm{L0}$. Their verified attachment to the literal fixed-$h_0$ packet is $\mathrm{L1}$. A growing coefficient-specific fixed-shift power saving is $\mathrm{L2}$.

# Shift selectors and local defects

For $0\le j\le s$, let $E_j$ be the full shift-tagged record space at archive stage $j$, and let $E_j^{(0)}$ be the declared prescribed-$h_0$ record space. Let $$P_j:E_j\to E_j^{(0)}$$ be coordinate selection and $$T_j:E_{j-1}\to E_j,\qquad  T_j^{(0)}:E_{j-1}^{(0)}\to E_j^{(0)}$$ be the full and declared sliced stages.

###### ref-a2e203cfae840481

> **Definition: Stage shift defect** The $j$-th intertwining defect is $$D_j=P_jT_j-T_j^{(0)}P_{j-1}.  \label{eq:Dj}$$

The equality $D_j=0$ says more than equality of two scalar outputs: it says that selection can be moved through the stage for every input column. A path implementation may check this entrywise by verifying that every nonzero edge keeps its shift tag.

# The exact composite-defect telescope

###### ref-4d177eb6b92c3cef

###### ref-81e5a18c74c7081b

> **Theorem: Prescribed-shift intertwining telescope** \[thm:telescope\] For arbitrary compatible stage maps, $$\boxed{  P_sT_s\cdots T_1  -  T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_1^{(0)}P_0  =  \sum_{j=1}^s  T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_{j+1}^{(0)}  D_j  T_{j-1}\cdots T_1.} \label{eq:telescope}$$ Empty products are identities.

> **Proof** Insert the intermediate composites $$T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_{j+1}^{(0)}  P_jT_j\cdots T_1$$ for $j=s,s-1,\ldots,0$. The difference between consecutive terms is exactly $$T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_{j+1}^{(0)}  (P_jT_j-T_j^{(0)}P_{j-1})  T_{j-1}\cdots T_1.$$ Summing telescopes to [\[eq:telescope\]](TPC-125.md#ref-81e5a18c74c7081b).

###### ref-66b38f173273ddba

> **Corollary: Stagewise sufficient certificate** \[cor:stagewise\] If $D_j=0$ for all $j$, then $$P_sT_s\cdots T_1  =  T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_1^{(0)}P_0.$$ The direct $h_0$ slice then intertwines coefficientwise with the full archive.

###### ref-cbc2478b89e12e5d

> **Corollary: Actual-packet stop witness** \[cor:packet\] Let $$\Delta_X=  P_sT_s\cdots T_1  -  T_s^{(0)}\cdots T_1^{(0)}P_0.$$ If $\Delta_Xc_X\ne0$, the declared sliced chain does not reproduce the direct fixed-$h_0$ output on the literal packet $c_X$.

> **Remark: Operator and one-vector quantifiers** $\Delta_Xc_X=0$ verifies one literal vector. The stronger archive identity $\Delta_X=0$ verifies every native atom and is the appropriate coefficientwise H8 interface. Conversely, nonzero local $D_j$ may cancel in the sum [\[eq:telescope\]](TPC-125.md#ref-81e5a18c74c7081b); it stops a claimed stagewise intertwining proof but does not alone prove $\Delta_Xc_X\ne0$.

# A minimal cross-shift leakage example

###### ref-9597234581874faa

> **Proposition: Frozen notation does not preserve a shift** \[prop:leak\] There is a two-shift stage whose selected output contains a coefficient from the other shift, even though the declared fixed-shift submatrix is unchanged.

> **Proof** Let $$P=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad  T=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad  T^{(0)}=(1).$$ Then $$PT-T^{(0)}P=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\end{pmatrix}\ne0.$$ The second source coordinate leaks into the selected output. Renaming that output “$h_0$” does not remove the defect.

# Sharp de-aliasing on an actual profile space

Suppose direct slicing is unavailable and an estimate controls only observations of a finite-dimensional profile $f\in V_X$. Let $$A:V_X\to Y_X,\qquad \Omega=\Omega^*>0$$ be the declared observation and weight, and put $$G_{\rm prof}=A^*\Omega A.$$ Let the fixed-shift functional be $$\operatorname{ev}_{h_0}(f)=\langle b_0,f\rangle.$$

###### ref-c8c828c8ed109dbb

> **Theorem: Sharp finite fixed-shift evaluation** \[thm:localization\] There is a finite $K$ such that $$|\langle b_0,f\rangle|^2  \le K\langle Af,\Omega Af\rangle  \qquad(f\in V_X)$$ if and only if $$b_0\in\operatorname{ran}(G_{\rm prof}).$$ When finite, the sharp constant is $$\boxed{  K_X(h_0)=b_0^*G_{\rm prof}^\dagger b_0.}$$

> **Proof** Diagonalize the positive semidefinite matrix $G_{\rm prof}$. If $b_0\notin\operatorname{ran}(G_{\rm prof})$, its projection onto $\operatorname{ker}(G_{\rm prof})=\operatorname{ker}(A)$ is nonzero. A corresponding kernel vector has zero observation and nonzero target evaluation, so no finite inequality exists.
>
> If $b_0\in\operatorname{ran}(G_{\rm prof})$, write $$\langle b_0,f\rangle  =  \langle G_{\rm prof}^{\dagger/2}b_0,  G_{\rm prof}^{1/2}f\rangle.$$ Cauchy–Schwarz gives the asserted constant. Equality is attained along the nonzero vector $f=G_{\rm prof}^\dagger b_0$ when $b_0\ne0$. When $b_0=0$, the sharp constant is $K=0$ and the target functional vanishes identically, so no nonzero extremizer is needed. Thus the constant is sharp [`\citep{BenIsraelGreville,WangTPC115}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-9cca6c50d69939f8); [\[WangTPC115\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-5133744dd356e573).

###### ref-770adc36459d76d1

> **Corollary: Exact exponent transfer** \[cor:exponent\] Assume the actual averaged profile satisfies $$\|Af_X\|_\Omega  \le X^{1-\sigma+o(1)}$$ and $$K_X(h_0)\le X^{2\lambda_{\rm loc}+o(1)}.$$ Then $$|f_X(h_0)|  \le  X^{1-(\sigma-\lambda_{\rm loc})+o(1)}.$$ This certificate gives a positive fixed-shift power saving only when $\sigma>\lambda_{\rm loc}$.

> **Proof** Take square roots in [\[thm:localization\]](TPC-125.md#ref-c8c828c8ed109dbb) and insert the two assumptions.

> **Remark: Squared versus amplitude cost** $K_X$ is a squared evaluation cost. The amplitude ledger charges $\sqrt{K_X}$, hence $\lambda_{\rm loc}$, not $2\lambda_{\rm loc}$. The actual endpoint registry must record this convention exactly.

# Direct slicing is not averaged recovery

###### ref-fd51c8925c7d8335

> **Proposition: An average can erase fixed evaluation** \[prop:average\] Two profiles can have the same average and different values at $h_0$.

> **Proof** On two shifts, let $A(x_1,x_2)=(x_1+x_2)/2$ and evaluate the first coordinate. The profiles $(1,-1)$ and $(0,0)$ have the same average but different first coordinates. Equivalently, $(1,-1)\in\operatorname{ker}(A)$ is a witness that the evaluation vector is not in $\operatorname{ran}(A^*A)$.

Thus a proof of [\[cor:stagewise\]](TPC-125.md#ref-66b38f173273ddba) uses no Christoffel inversion, whereas a proof based only on $A f_X$ must invoke [\[thm:localization\]](TPC-125.md#ref-c8c828c8ed109dbb). Charging both as independent losses for the same map double-counts; using the direct identity to erase a localization loss when only averaged evidence is available is equally invalid.

# Relation to the determinant and zero-mode interfaces

TPC-121 and TPC-122 deliberately retain literal fixed-$h_0$ coefficients [`\citep{WangTPC121,WangTPC122}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-d3e88dbca3ebc492); [\[WangTPC122\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-af7e5d5ca960c85e). Their conditional outputs are, schematically, $$\lambda_D^{\rm cert}  \quad\text{and}\quad  \eta_Z^{\rm cert}.$$ This input type does not prove that a preceding frozen family was de-aliased correctly. To attach either formula after the physical archive, the following must be supplied:

1.  the shift-tagged TPC-123 path archive;

2.  the literal TPC-124 grouping and block reassembly;

3.  either the direct operator identity $\Delta_X=0$, or the actual profile space and its finite $K_X(h_0)$;

4.  one inherited normalization and no reuse of determinant reserve as localization or physical endpoint slack.

Even after these items, the growing inputs in TPC-121 and TPC-122 remain unproved. In particular, this paper cannot fill H3, H5, H7 or H8 by itself.

The current TPC-123/124 outputs are exact schemas and finite regression models, not the complete growing shift-tagged archive. The actual profile space required by TPC-115 is likewise absent. Therefore $$\boxed{  \operatorname{FixedShift}_{125}  =  \textsc{not-testable}\ \text{from current artifacts}.}$$ This does not show that localization is impossible or that the direct slice must fail.

#### Explicit exclusions.

This paper proves no low-dimensional model for the actual shift profile, no growing Christoffel bound, no signed affine Möbius estimate of TPC-108, no determinant or zero-mode exponent, no physical endpoint below $1/400$, no parity breakthrough, no Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, and no prime-pair or twin-prime theorem [`\citep{WangTPC108,WangTPC120}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-493205aca788aded); [\[WangTPC120\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-125.md#cite-dfdd5b7639f97227).
