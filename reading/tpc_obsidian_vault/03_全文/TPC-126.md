## Abstract

TPC-108 isolated the generic-affine arithmetic object, while TPC-123–125 specify the archive, physical reassembly, and prescribed-shift interfaces needed to call it without replacing the literal coefficient. We give a self-contained invocation theorem for one complete fixed-$h_0$ packet. For every archived block $$T_b=\sum_{z\in I_b}A_b(z)\mathrm e(-\alpha_bz),  \qquad s_bu_b-a_bd_b=h_0,$$ we prove the exact lag identity $$|T_b|^2  =\sum_r\mathrm e(-\alpha_br)    \sum_{\substack{z,z+r\in I_b}}    A_b(z+r)\overline{A_b(z)}$$ and the exact canonical-order Abel identity. For the complete block family $S_X=\sum_b\gamma_bT_b$, the normalized literal ratios obey $$\frac{|S_X|}{\sum_b|\gamma_b|M_b}  \le  \left(\sum_bq_b\frac{|T_b|^2}{M_b^2}\right)^{1/2},  \quad  q_b=\frac{|\gamma_b|M_b}{\sum_c|\gamma_c|M_c}.$$ This identifies a precise growing theorem that would close the H3 subgate after its separately audited outer loss. The present paper proves the finite identities and their typed invocation only. It does not prove the required uniform bound, a fixed-shift $\mathrm{L2}$ saving, a parity breakthrough, or a twin-prime result.

# Why another block statement is needed

The hard packet of TPC-15 is an exact scalar remainder whose $o(X)$ estimate is equivalent, within that decomposition, to the corresponding weighted fixed-shift prime-pair asymptotic [`\citep{WangTPC15}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-b0805195de3a7444). TPC-93 exports its low window into affine coordinates while preserving content, projector, polarization, and the extracted Möbius factors [`\citep{WangTPC93}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-2d5bd9665d7257b5). TPC-107 insists that the signed filter be applied before positive majorization [`\citep{WangTPC107}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-7c9e22ccbb160724); TPC-108 then writes the exact $TT^*$ kernel and explains why an unrestricted replacement would reach a Chowla-strength problem [`\citep{WangTPC108,Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-493205aca788aded); [\[Tao2016LogChowla\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07).

Those identities are not callable merely because a sequence with the same support has been constructed. TPC-119 found that the audited snapshot lacked a complete native reconnection archive [`\citep{WangTPC119}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-6287e7598dfbed80). TPC-123–125 therefore separate three interfaces: path conservation, leaf-to-physical cover, and direct prescribed-$h_0$ intertwining [`\citep{WangTPC123,WangTPC124,WangTPC125}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-6ff895d094f015b3); [\[WangTPC124\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-ecc9b818c6056a02); [\[WangTPC125\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-126.md#cite-52f31cd720b99fdf). This paper starts *after* those interfaces. Its purpose is to state exactly what the extracted arithmetic block is, what finite equalities may be checked, and what genuinely growing assertion is still missing.

#### Claim boundary.

An exact finite equality is $\mathrm{L0}$. Its verified attachment to the literal fixed-$h_0$ packet is $\mathrm{L1}$. Only a uniform coefficient-specific estimate on the complete growing family can be $\mathrm{L2}$. A finite computation, a frozen form, an averaged shift, or a surrogate sign model does not change this typing.

# The typed literal block

Fix one prescribed nonzero integer $h_0$. For each scale $X$, let $\mathcal B_X$ be the *complete* set of long generic blocks selected by the verified archive and physical-cover maps. A block record is $$b=(I_b,d_b,s_b,u_b,a_b,\alpha_b,\gamma_b,\mathfrak m_b,     \mathfrak p_b,\mathfrak n_b),$$ where:

###### ref-93ee6874eae87211

1.  $I_b\subset\mathbb Z$ is a finite ordered interval;

2.  $D_b(z)=d_b+s_bz$, $V_b(z)=u_b+a_bz$, and $$s_bu_b-a_bd_b=h_0;     \label{eq:determinant}$$

3.  $\alpha_b\in\mathbb R/\mathbb Z$ is the literal frequency, and $\gamma_b\in\mathbb C$ is the literal outer multiplier;

4.  $\mathfrak m_b,\mathfrak p_b,\mathfrak n_b$ contain, respectively, all masks, the path/provenance key, and the unique inherited physical normalization.

###### ref-f47e7e2d31768aa8

The coefficient is not abbreviated to its two visible Möbius factors. We write $$A_b(z)  =  \mu(D_b(z))\mu(V_b(z))\,  \omega_b(z)\,  \mathbf 1_{\mathcal M_b}(z),  \label{eq:literal-coefficient}$$ where $\omega_b$ is the full complex reconstruction weight and $\mathbf 1_{\mathcal M_b}$ is the product of the actual content, coprimality, prefix, interval, projector, and polarization masks. In particular, the extracted factor $\mu(B)\mathbf 1_{(B,V_b(z))=1}$ from TPC-93 is part of [\[eq:literal-coefficient\]](TPC-126.md#ref-f47e7e2d31768aa8); it may not be deleted.

###### ref-d68a81f474016fa4

> **Definition: Archive extraction** Let $c_X$ be the native coefficient vector, $M_X$ the path-expanded archive matrix of TPC-123, and $E_b$ the row selector and exact reconstruction map for block $b$. The block is *literally extracted* if $$(A_b(z))_{z\in I_b}=E_bM_Xc_X  \label{eq:archive-extraction}$$ with equality of values and of the metadata listed above.

> **Remark** Equation [\[eq:archive-extraction\]](TPC-126.md#ref-d68a81f474016fa4) is stronger than agreement of one scalar block sum. Null pairs can preserve a scalar sum while changing the lag kernel, a prefix, or a determinant fiber. It is also stronger than equality after absolute values, which erases the signed channel isolated in TPC-107.

Define $$\mathrm e(t)=e^{2\pi it},\qquad  T_b=\sum_{z\in I_b}A_b(z)\mathrm e(-\alpha_bz),\qquad  M_b=\sum_{z\in I_b}|A_b(z)|.$$ Zero-mass blocks are retained in the archive but omitted from ratios.

# Exact $TT^*$ and prefix identities

###### ref-0488200455b244d0

###### ref-4c27c78e19fc4ae2

> **Theorem: Literal lag expansion** \[thm:ttstar\] For every finite literal block, $$|T_b|^2  =  \sum_{r\in I_b-I_b}\mathrm e(-\alpha_br)C_b(r),  \qquad  C_b(r)=  \sum_{\substack{z\in I_b\\z+r\in I_b}}  A_b(z+r)\overline{A_b(z)}.  \label{eq:ttstar}$$ Moreover $C_b(-r)=\overline{C_b(r)}$, so the right side is real and nonnegative even though its individual lag terms need not be.

> **Proof** Expanding the square gives $$|T_b|^2  =  \sum_{x,z\in I_b}  A_b(x)\overline{A_b(z)}  \mathrm e(-\alpha_b(x-z)).$$ Set $r=x-z$, hence $x=z+r$, and collect all admissible pairs. The symmetry follows by conjugating and replacing $r$ by $-r$. Nonnegativity follows from the left side, not from termwise positivity.

> **Remark: The four-Möbius interior** Substituting [\[eq:literal-coefficient\]](TPC-126.md#ref-f47e7e2d31768aa8) into $C_b(r)$ exposes $$\mu(D_b(z+r))\mu(V_b(z+r))  \mu(D_b(z))\mu(V_b(z))$$ times the literal masks and weights. Both shifted affine pairs still have determinant $h_0$. Replacing this expression by independent random signs, or dropping the mask intersection at $z,z+r$, proves a statement about another kernel.

The ordered form supplies a second exact diagnostic. Write $I_b=\{z_1<\cdots<z_m\}$, and suppose one declared factorization is $$A_b(z_i)\mathrm e(-\alpha_bz_i)=\sigma_iw_i.$$ The choice of $\sigma_i$ and $w_i$, and the order $z_i$, are part of the archive. Put $P_k=\sum_{i\le k}\sigma_i$.

###### ref-eb836a8249dcc9f5

###### ref-5d910aa869d955dc

> **Theorem: Canonical-order Abel crosswalk** \[thm:abel\] For every such factorization, $$T_b=P_mw_m+\sum_{k=1}^{m-1}P_k(w_k-w_{k+1}).  \label{eq:abel}$$ Consequently $$|T_b|  \le  \max_{1\le k\le m}|P_k|  \left(  |w_m|+\sum_{k<m}|w_k-w_{k+1}|  \right).$$

> **Proof** Since $\sigma_i=P_i-P_{i-1}$, with $P_0=0$, $$\sum_{i=1}^m(P_i-P_{i-1})w_i  =P_mw_m+\sum_{i=1}^{m-1}P_i(w_i-w_{i+1}).$$ The inequality is the triangle inequality.

> **Remark** Theorems [\[thm:ttstar\]](TPC-126.md#ref-0488200455b244d0) and [\[thm:abel\]](TPC-126.md#ref-eb836a8249dcc9f5) are two exact views of the same literal block, not two interchangeable hypotheses. $TT^*$ asks for a weighted aggregate of four-point lags. Abel asks for every signed prefix under one canonical order. A bound in one language must be transferred with all literal weights before it can be used in the other.

# Complete-family normalization

###### ref-46ffea73f3410127

The physical generic contribution has the exact form $$S_X=\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_X}\gamma_bT_b.  \label{eq:family}$$ Set $$\mathcal A_X=\sum_b|\gamma_b|M_b,\qquad  q_b=\frac{|\gamma_b|M_b}{\mathcal A_X}$$ when $\mathcal A_X>0$. Then $(q_b)$ is a probability vector.

###### ref-24a44a6eb9d86b9e

###### ref-40ffe3c332e58fa0

> **Theorem: Literal family invocation** \[thm:family\] For the complete archived block family, $$\frac{|S_X|}{\mathcal A_X}  \le  \sum_{b:M_b>0}q_b\frac{|T_b|}{M_b}  \le  \left(  \sum_{b:M_b>0}  q_b\frac{|T_b|^2}{M_b^2}  \right)^{1/2}.  \label{eq:family-bound}$$ Both inequalities have sharp finite examples.

> **Proof** The first inequality follows from $$|S_X|\le\sum_b|\gamma_b||T_b|  =\mathcal A_X\sum_bq_b|T_b|/M_b.$$ The second is Cauchy–Schwarz in the probability measure $q_b$. Equality in both occurs when all nonzero $\gamma_bT_b$ have a common phase and all ratios $|T_b|/M_b$ are equal.

###### ref-bb63a152c7b8ac39

###### ref-4f26a3a48d56bdf1

###### ref-882ab1d7d284e34e

> **Corollary: Typed termwise sufficient condition** \[cor:h3\] Assume, on the complete actual growing family and in one physical normalization, that $$\sum_{b:M_b>0}  q_b\frac{|T_b|^2}{M_b^2}  \le X^{-2\eta+o(1)}.  \label{eq:growing-input}$$ If the separately proved outer reconstruction and norm-conversion cost is $X^{\lambda_{\rm out}+o(1)}$, then the net relative saving delivered by [\[eq:family-bound\]](TPC-126.md#ref-40ffe3c332e58fa0) is $$X^{-(\eta-\lambda_{\rm out})+o(1)}.$$ In the deliberately termwise TPC-108 ledger, this certificate reaches the stronger displayed *linear generic-window* exponent when $$\eta-\lambda_{\rm out}\ge\frac1{200}.  \label{eq:h3-threshold}$$ This is a conservative sufficient condition for that linear window, not the exact quadratic-to-amplitude threshold of MVP1. Calling the MVP1 packet saving $1/400$ still requires the separately verified physical $TT^*$ crosswalk and prefactor.

> **Proof** Take the square root in [\[eq:growing-input\]](TPC-126.md#ref-4f26a3a48d56bdf1), apply [\[eq:family-bound\]](TPC-126.md#ref-40ffe3c332e58fa0), and multiply by the separately certified outer cost. The last condition is the stronger termwise linear-window threshold used in TPC-108; it is not derived from a finite block and is not claimed necessary for every coherent H3 proof.

#### No hidden averaging.

The quantifier in [\[eq:growing-input\]](TPC-126.md#ref-4f26a3a48d56bdf1) is over the actual $X$-indexed family. It cannot be replaced by any of the following:

1.  a fixed finite list of blocks;

2.  an average over shifts when $h_0$ is prescribed;

3.  an average over unrelated phases or moduli;

4.  a density-one subfamily without charging its complement;

5.  a family selected after inspecting favorable phase cancellation.

The archive rule, the block family, and any good/bad selection must be predeclared.

# What finite audits can and cannot decide

###### ref-dbc140799d599e49

> **Proposition: Finite saturation** \[prop:saturation\] For every finite support $I$, every nonnegative magnitudes $(m_z)_{z\in I}$, and every phase $\alpha$, there are coefficients with $|A(z)|=m_z$ such that $$\left|\sum_{z\in I}A(z)\mathrm e(-\alpha z)\right|  =\sum_{z\in I}m_z.$$ There are also coefficients with the same magnitudes whose sum is zero whenever the polygon inequality $\max m_z\le\frac12\sum m_z$ holds.

> **Proof** For saturation take $A(z)=m_z\mathrm e(\alpha z+\theta)$. For cancellation, the polygon inequality is the classical necessary and sufficient condition that vectors of lengths $m_z$ close to a polygon; rotate those vectors back by $\mathrm e(\alpha z)$.

Thus support, occupancy, and magnitudes alone do not determine the signed ratio. Conversely, one unusually small finite ratio does not prove [\[eq:growing-input\]](TPC-126.md#ref-4f26a3a48d56bdf1). A finite experiment is valuable for checking the literal extraction and choosing a candidate analytic lemma, but not for certifying an asymptotic exponent.

> **Definition: Four verdicts for this interface** The block audit uses:
>
> $\textsc{go-interface}$
>
> :   The complete finite archive-to-block extraction, metadata, normalization, $TT^*$, and prefix identities pass. This is at most $\mathrm{L1}$.
>
> $\textsc{go-h3}$
>
> :   In addition, a theorem proves [\[eq:growing-input\]](TPC-126.md#ref-4f26a3a48d56bdf1) on the complete actual growing family and the separate cost satisfies [\[eq:h3-threshold\]](TPC-126.md#ref-882ab1d7d284e34e). Only this can close the declared H3 subgate.
>
> $\textsc{stop-declared-route}$
>
> :   An exact growing counterfamily inside the quantifiers of a declared candidate lemma violates it. This stops that lemma, not all signed routes.
>
> $\textsc{not-testable}$
>
> :   The archive extraction, complete block census, metadata, physical normalization, or growing theorem is absent.

# Executable certificate and status

The standard-library script `experiments/tpc126_literal_packet_audit.py` checks on exact integer metadata and ordinary double-precision finite data:

1.  the determinant identity and the presence of a declared sample metadata subset, not completeness of the literal archive metadata;

2.  the complex lag identity and its Hermitian symmetry;

3.  the canonical Abel identity and BV bound;

4.  both family inequalities and a saturation example;

5.  the logical separation between $\textsc{go-interface}$ and $\textsc{go-h3}$.

It writes a deterministic JSON certificate. It does not sample primes, estimate a growing exponent, or promote a finite regression to arithmetic evidence.

| Statement                                                                      | Level and present status                                                      |
|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

#### Conclusion.

The literal arithmetic target is now an executable object with an exact normalization and a sharp family-level invocation rule. This is forward movement at the interface: a future theorem has one explicit left-hand side, one complete set of quantifiers, and one loss threshold. The hard step remains the growing signed estimate itself. Nothing here proves Möbius cancellation for the actual family, breaches the parity barrier, gives a prime-pair lower bound, establishes a Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, or proves the twin-prime conjecture.
