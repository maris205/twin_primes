## Abstract

TPC-108 gives the exact full-range $TT^*$ identity for one literal generic affine Möbius block, but its off-diagonal contains four Möbius values. We turn that observation into a quantitative, truncated, all-prefix certificate. For a block $$S=\sum_{z=1}^N A_z\mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha z}, \qquad  C(h)=\sum_zA_{z+h}\overline{A_z},$$ sliding-window Cauchy–Schwarz gives, for $1\le H\le N$, $$|S|^2\le  \frac{N+H-1}{H}  \sum_{|h|<H}  \left(1-\frac{|h|}{H}\right)  \mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha h}C(h).$$ The Fejér sum is real and nonnegative. Writing $V=\sum|A_z|$, $E=\sum|A_z|^2$, and $\mathfrak p=V^2/(NE)$, we obtain $$\frac{|S|^2}{V^2}  \le  2\left\{  \frac1{H\mathfrak p}  +  \frac{N}{HV^2}(\mathcal O_H)_+  \right\},$$ where $\mathcal O_H$ is the signed Fejér off-diagonal. In the TPC-127 shift-two frame its arithmetic factor is exactly $$\lambda(n+qh-2)\lambda(n+qh)  \lambda(n-2)\lambda(n),$$ with every shifted squarefree, coprimality, periodic, physical, and prefix factor retained. This yields a precise sufficient four-sign gate and an exponent ledger. It also proves a sharp diagonal stop: sparse support with $H\mathfrak p=O(1)$ can saturate the original sum even when every short off-diagonal vanishes. The finite inequality is $\mathrm{L0}$, its complete literal crosswalk is $\mathrm{L1}$, and the required growing four-sign estimate remains unproved $\mathrm{L2}$ input.

# The finite sliding-window inequality

###### ref-2b8d52cdb0bd3c18

###### ref-65d243375a9259a4

Let $A_1,\ldots,A_N\in\mathbb C$, extend $A_z=0$ outside $[1,N]$, and define $$\begin{aligned}  S(\alpha)&=\sum_{z=1}^NA_z\mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha z}, \label{eq:S}\\  C(h)&=\sum_{z\in\mathbb Z}A_{z+h}\overline{A_z}. \label{eq:C}\end{aligned}$$ Thus $C(-h)=\overline{C(h)}$.

###### ref-96375694567b2bd9

###### ref-72ddc792796c14bb

###### ref-1fd85788915695ab

###### ref-4ba5af412d3a33ff

> **Theorem: Sliding-window Fejér $TT^*$** \[thm:fejer\] For every integer $H\ge1$, $$\boxed{  |S(\alpha)|^2  \le  \frac{N+H-1}{H}\,  \mathcal K_H(\alpha),} \label{eq:fejer-bound}$$ where $$\mathcal K_H(\alpha)  =  \sum_{|h|<H}  \left(1-\frac{|h|}{H}\right)  \mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha h}C(h)  \ge0. \label{eq:fejer-energy}$$ The identity behind the inequality is $$H\mathcal K_H(\alpha)  =  \sum_{t=2-H}^{N}  \left|  \sum_{j=0}^{H-1}  A_{t+j}\mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha(t+j)}  \right|^2. \label{eq:window-identity}$$

> **Proof** Put $b_z=A_z\mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha z}$, extended by zero, and $$B_t=\sum_{j=0}^{H-1}b_{t+j}.$$ Every $b_z$ occurs in exactly $H$ of the $N+H-1$ windows, so $$\sum_{t=2-H}^{N}B_t=HS(\alpha).$$ Cauchy–Schwarz gives $$H^2|S(\alpha)|^2  \le(N+H-1)\sum_t|B_t|^2.$$ Expanding the last square and grouping by $h=j-k$ yields $$\sum_t|B_t|^2  =  \sum_{|h|<H}(H-|h|)  \mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha h}C(h),$$ which is [\[eq:window-identity\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4ba5af412d3a33ff) and proves the theorem.

> **Remark: Relation to TPC-108** Taking all differences gives the exact full $TT^*$ identity of TPC-108 [`\citep{WangTPC108}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-130.md#cite-493205aca788aded). instead inserts a predeclared short shift scale $H$, positive Fejér weights, and the precise Cauchy cost needed for a sufficient estimate. The finite Fejér/van der Corput device is classical [`\citep[Chapter~8]{IwaniecKowalski2004}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-130.md#cite-49dfea87eaada156); the new content here is its literal fixed-two four-sign crosswalk and audited all-prefix stop ledger.

# Diagonal participation and the signed off-diagonal

###### ref-4322b9fc46bdf3c1

###### ref-0c57a3ff10a6b0ce

Put $$V=\sum_{z=1}^N|A_z|, \qquad  E=\sum_{z=1}^N|A_z|^2. \label{eq:VE}$$ If $E>0$, define the participation ratio $$\mathfrak p=\frac{V^2}{NE}\in(0,1]. \label{eq:participation}$$ The upper bound is Cauchy–Schwarz. If $V=0$, then every $A_z=0$, so $E=S=0$; such a prefix is discharged trivially and omitted from normalized maxima. On every remaining prefix $V>0$, hence $E>0$ and [\[eq:participation\]](TPC-130.md#ref-0c57a3ff10a6b0ce) is defined.

###### ref-4dadb619ffe90d90

###### ref-168a1769d15205a7

Write $$\mathcal K_H(\alpha)=E+\mathcal O_H(\alpha), \label{eq:offdiag-split}$$ where $$\mathcal O_H(\alpha)  =  2\operatorname{Re}  \sum_{h=1}^{H-1}  \left(1-\frac hH\right)  \mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha h}C(h). \label{eq:OH}$$

###### ref-1bd05cdbef8ba2d8

###### ref-6f511e7154b09b70

> **Corollary: Normalized Fejér certificate** \[cor:normalized\] If $1\le H\le N$ and $V>0$, then $$\boxed{  \frac{|S(\alpha)|^2}{V^2}  \le  2\left\{  \frac1{H\mathfrak p}  +  \frac{N}{HV^2}  \bigl(\mathcal O_H(\alpha)\bigr)_+  \right\}.} \label{eq:normalized}$$

> **Proof** Since $N+H-1\le2N$, [\[thm:fejer\]](TPC-130.md#ref-96375694567b2bd9) gives $$\frac{|S|^2}{V^2}  \le\frac{2N}{HV^2}(E+\mathcal O_H).$$ Use $NE/V^2=1/\mathfrak p$ and replace the off-diagonal by its positive part.

###### ref-938426370085d261

###### ref-27fc080f023c054e

###### ref-4f7ef7e16aec89f2

> **Definition: Typed four-sign certificate** \[def:typed\] For exponents $\eta_{\rm diag},\eta_4\ge0$, a block-prefix and a shift scale $H\le N$ pass the Fejér certificate if $$\begin{aligned}  \frac1{H\mathfrak p}  &\le X^{-2\eta_{\rm diag}+o(1)}, \label{eq:diag-input}\\  \frac{N}{HV^2}  \bigl(\mathcal O_H(\alpha)\bigr)_+  &\le X^{-2\eta_4+o(1)}. \label{eq:four-input}\end{aligned}$$ The same $o(1)$ must be uniform over the predeclared actual block-prefix family.

###### ref-00e06779acd0c21d

###### ref-b08f90156c3fa86e

> **Theorem: Exact exponent transfer** \[thm:transfer\] If [\[eq:diag-input\]](TPC-130.md#ref-27fc080f023c054e)–[\[eq:four-input\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4f7ef7e16aec89f2) hold uniformly, then $$|S(\alpha)|  \le  V X^{-\eta_{\rm block}+o(1)}, \qquad  \eta_{\rm block}  =  \min\{\eta_{\rm diag},\eta_4\}. \label{eq:block-saving}$$

> **Proof** Insert the two inputs in [\[eq:normalized\]](TPC-130.md#ref-6f511e7154b09b70); fixed constants are absorbed by the uniform $X^{o(1)}$, and take square roots.

# The complete fixed-two arithmetic crosswalk

###### ref-508688a7c84125aa

###### ref-10a4a58b476b4f01

We now identify $\mathcal O_H$ without suppressing its coefficient. For one actual TPC-108 block, TPC-127 gives $$n(z)=n_0+qz,\qquad q=as,$$ and $$A_z=  \lambda(q)\epsilon_z  \lambda(n(z)-2)\lambda(n(z))\Psi_z. \label{eq:actual-A}$$ Here $$\begin{aligned}  \epsilon_z  &=  \mu^2(D(z))\mu^2(V(z)),\notag\\  \Psi_z  &=  \mathbf 1_{(B,V(z))=1}  \chi(\tau+Bz)W_X(\tau+Bz). \label{eq:epsilon-Psi}\end{aligned}$$ Both factors are literal; in particular, the physical weight has not been replaced by $1$ [`\citep{WangTPC127}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-130.md#cite-82838990f4215d0c).

###### ref-7c43326b16a25406

###### ref-4492e8536b1737b5

> **Proposition: Actual four-sign kernel** \[prop:four-sign\] For $h\ne0$, $$\begin{aligned}  C(h)  =\sum_{\substack{z\in I\\z+h\in I}}  &\epsilon_{z+h}\epsilon_z  \lambda(n(z)+qh-2)\lambda(n(z)+qh) \notag\\  &\times  \lambda(n(z)-2)\lambda(n(z))  \Psi_{z+h}\overline{\Psi_z}. \label{eq:four-sign-kernel}\end{aligned}$$ Consequently [\[eq:four-input\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4f7ef7e16aec89f2) is a signed Fejér average of the complete actual four-sign kernel, including the additive factor $\mathrm e^{-2\pi i\alpha h}$.

> **Proof** Insert [\[eq:actual-A\]](TPC-130.md#ref-508688a7c84125aa) in $C(h)=\sum A_{z+h}\overline{A_z}$. Since $\lambda(q)^2=1$ and $n(z+h)=n(z)+qh$, the displayed formula follows.

> **Remark: TPC-128 components** One may first use the finite CRT expansion of TPC-128 and reparametrize a retained component. The same theorem then holds with the enlarged progression step $ask^2\ell^2$, while its tail and census remain separate inputs [`\citep{WangTPC128}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-130.md#cite-4e0fc7932d9675d8). The complete formula [\[eq:four-sign-kernel\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4492e8536b1737b5) does not require that optional truncation.

# All prefixes and complete-family return

H3 requires the declared prefix family, not just one terminal interval. For every actual block $\xi$ and required prefix $T$, form $$A_{\xi,T},\quad N_{\xi,T},\quad V_{\xi,T},\quad  E_{\xi,T},\quad\mathfrak p_{\xi,T},\quad  C_{\xi,T}(h)$$ by restricting the literal coefficient before applying [\[thm:fejer\]](TPC-130.md#ref-96375694567b2bd9). Choose $H_{\xi,T}$ by a rule fixed before estimating the correlations. Prefixes with $V_{\xi,T}=0$ are zero and pass trivially; the normalized certificate is imposed only on the remaining prefixes.

> **Corollary: Termwise H3 ledger** \[cor:h3-ledger\] Suppose every retained actual block-prefix passes [\[def:typed\]](TPC-130.md#ref-938426370085d261), and suppose the verified complete-family outer mass has cost $X^{\ell_{\rm out}+o(1)}$ in the normalization of TPC-108/TPC-126. Then the termwise return exponent is $$\eta_{\rm net}  =  \min\{\eta_{\rm diag},\eta_4\}-\ell_{\rm out}. \label{eq:net}$$ At the local amplitude level, the square-root counterpart of a quadratic $X^{-1/200+o(1)}$ target is $$\min\{\eta_{\rm diag},\eta_4\}  -\ell_{\rm out}\ge\frac1{400}. \label{eq:h3-threshold}$$ The legacy $1/200$ termwise linear-window screen is a deliberately stronger sufficient condition. It is not the exact quadratic-to-amplitude conversion and is not necessary for the local Fejér certificate.

does not identify the local mass $V$ with the final physical quadratic normalization, nor does it supply the prefactor and synthesis crosswalk needed at the strict endpoint. Those remain separate certificates. # Sharp structural stops

###### ref-a8e4e40bfb34666e

> **Proposition: Short-off-diagonal vanishing can still saturate** \[prop:sparse\] Fix $H\ge2$. Let $A_z=1$ on $m$ positions separated by at least $H$, and $A_z=0$ elsewhere in an interval of length $N\asymp mH$. At $\alpha=0$, $$C(h)=0\quad(0<|h|<H),\qquad  |S|=V=m,$$ while $$H\mathfrak p  =H\frac{m^2}{Nm}\asymp1.$$ Thus no saving follows from vanishing short off-diagonals when the diagonal feasibility condition fails.

> **Proof** The support separation proves the correlation statement. The remaining identities follow from $V=E=m$ and $N\asymp mH$.

###### ref-ed91b5682414fb46

> **Proposition: Generic phase alone is insufficient** \[prop:phase-match\] For any $\alpha$, the bounded coefficient $A_z=\mathrm e^{2\pi i\alpha z}$ satisfies $$S(\alpha)=N.$$ Hence neither $N\|\alpha\|>1$ nor the Fejér identity gives cancellation without information about the literal coefficient.

> **Proof** Every summand in [\[eq:S\]](TPC-130.md#ref-2b8d52cdb0bd3c18) equals $1$.

#### DIAGONAL STOP.

If no predeclared $H\le N$ makes $H\mathfrak p$ grow at the required power on every retained prefix, this certificate cannot give that saving. Increasing $H$ beyond the prefix length is not permitted.

#### CIRCULARITY STOP.

The four-sign input must be proved directly from [\[eq:four-sign-kernel\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4492e8536b1737b5). Deducing it from the desired bound for $S$ and feeding it back into [\[thm:transfer\]](TPC-130.md#ref-00e06779acd0c21d) is circular. For a visibly local reduction, one should predeclare $H\le N^{1-\varepsilon}$ (or another audited sublength range); choosing $H$ only after observing a favorable global sum is not allowed.

#### ABSOLUTE-VALUE STOP.

The minimal gate is the signed positive part in [\[eq:four-input\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4f7ef7e16aec89f2). Replacing it by $\sum_{h<H}|C(h)|$ is a valid but stronger sufficient condition. Failure of that stronger condition does not close the signed Fejér route.

#### ARITHMETIC STOP.

No theorem in this paper estimates the growing four-Liouville expression [\[eq:four-sign-kernel\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4492e8536b1737b5) with its actual progression, masks, phase, physical weights, origins, and all-prefix uniformity. That is the remaining $\mathrm{L2}$ gate.

# Verdict

|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:------------------------------------------------------------|
| Sliding-window identity and Fejér inequality                                   | Exact finite theorem ($\mathrm{L0}$).                       |

| Four-sign formula for the complete fixed-$2$ literal block                     | Proven crosswalk ($\mathrm{L1}$).                           |
| Uniform diagonal participation on the full actual family                       | Must be audited; not asserted globally.                     |
| Signed four-sign estimate [\[eq:four-input\]](TPC-130.md#ref-4f7ef7e16aec89f2) | Not proved; new arithmetic input ($\mathrm{L2}$ if proved). |
| H3 threshold, physical $1/400$, or twin-prime theorem                          | Not proved.                                                 |

TPC-130 advances H3 by replacing “control the $TT^*$ off-diagonal” with one normalized, all-prefix, non-circular four-sign inequality and a separate diagonal feasibility test. The road remains open only if both tests pass on the actual family. This is a rigorous reduction, not a parity breakthrough, prime-pair lower bound, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, or proof of the twin-prime conjecture.
