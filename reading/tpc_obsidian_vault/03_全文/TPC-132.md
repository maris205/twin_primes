## Abstract

TPC-MVP2 separated exact interfaces from open arithmetic inputs but returned a broad <span class="smallcaps">not-testable</span> verdict. TPC-121–131 refine the determinant, zero-mode, archive, fixed-shift, arithmetic-reduction, and end-to-end synthesis branches. We give a stricter MVP3 decision calculus with seven ordered, exhaustive route outcomes after manifest validation: $\textsc{go}$, $\textsc{architecture-infeasible}$, $\textsc{reroute}$, $\textsc{stop-route}$, $\textsc{not-testable}$, $\textsc{arithmetic-frontier}$, and $\textsc{open}$. Contradictory route, scope, or stop records are rejected before verdict selection. The new arithmetic-frontier label is awarded only after every structural, provenance, fixed-shift, and endpoint interface is proved and every unresolved node is an explicitly typed status-$\textsc{open}$, positive-L2 growing coefficient-specific arithmetic target. Reductions, finite regressions, and conditional interfaces cannot trigger it. The audited snapshot has no complete actual-carrier native archive, no complete theorem-backed endpoint registry, and no proved positive L2 estimate. Its first machine-readable missing record is H1’s native path archive, so the verdict remains $\textsc{not-testable}$. This is not $\textsc{go}$, not the arithmetic frontier, and not an impossibility theorem for the architecture or for twin primes. We finish with certificate-specific branches for repair, arithmetic attack, rerouting, and publication of route obstructions.

# From MVP2 to MVP3

TPC-MVP1 resolved a conditional fixed-shift proof program into gates H1–H9 [`\citep{WangMVP1}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-934c301de52ef9a3). TPC-MVP2 gave each gate a typed status and returned <span class="smallcaps">not-testable</span> for the TPC-103–119 snapshot [`\citep{WangTPC120}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-dfdd5b7639f97227). The next papers sharpen two kinds of input.

First, TPC-121 and TPC-122 replace opaque determinant and zero-mode symbols by explicit conditional exponent formulas [`\citep{WangTPC121,WangTPC122}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-d3e88dbca3ebc492); [\[WangTPC122\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-af7e5d5ca960c85e). Second, TPC-123–130 specify a native path archive, physical reassembly, prescribed-shift intertwining, a literal affine block, and an exact route from determinant two to masked shift-two Liouville correlations and a Fejér four-Liouville target [`\citep{WangTPC123,WangTPC124,WangTPC125, WangTPC126,WangTPC127,WangTPC128,WangTPC129,WangTPC130}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-6ff895d094f015b3); [\[WangTPC124\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-ecc9b818c6056a02); [\[WangTPC125\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-52f31cd720b99fdf); [\[WangTPC126\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-5f10c2df1a061157); [\[WangTPC127\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-82838990f4215d0c); [\[WangTPC128\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-4e0fc7932d9675d8); [\[WangTPC129\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-fc3203528821dfb5); [\[WangTPC130\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-a49691a1c51bd81c). TPC-131 then states the general endpoint formula $$|B_{h_0,\delta}(X)|  \le o(X)+  X^{1-\sigma_{\rm raw}+\Lambda_{\rm phys}+o(1)}$$ under a proof-carrying acyclic certificate [`\citep{WangTPC131}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-132.md#cite-332ee14c8ba7a4fc).

These advances make the missing inputs more precise. They do not prove those inputs. MVP3 therefore needs a state between a generic open program and a genuinely isolated arithmetic frontier, while preserving route-specific stop certificates.

# Node status and evidence level are different

###### ref-28214ac17e4f62b6

> **Definition: Node status** \[def:node\] For one fully typed node:
>
> -   $\textnormal{\textsc{proved}}$: a theorem proves the declared statement;
>
> -   $\textnormal{\textsc{conditional}}$: an exact implication is proved but a displayed premise remains unproved;
>
> -   $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$: the literal object and test are available but the target theorem is absent;
>
> -   $\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}}$: an archive, type, exponent, or compatibility record needed to execute the test is absent; and
>
> -   $\textnormal{\textsc{refuted}}$: a theorem-backed witness contradicts that particular declared statement.

###### ref-27725412019fa88a

> **Definition: Evidence level and direction** \[def:level\] An $\mathrm{L0}$ record is an abstract finite identity or model obstruction. An $\mathrm{L1}$ record is an exact identity or executable crosswalk on a literal physical carrier. A conditional $\mathrm{L1}$ record remains $\mathrm{L1}$. A positive $\mathrm{L2}$ record is a proved, uniform, growing, coefficient-specific power estimate at the prescribed $h_0$. A negative $\mathrm{L2}$ record is an actual growing counterfamily or lower obstruction in the same quantifier scope.

Finite computation over many values of $X$, a frozen-family estimate, a density-one result, an averaged-shift theorem, and a reduction to an unproved bilinear or four-sign form do not meet the positive $\mathrm{L2}$ definition.

###### ref-fea402fc89fdb3c4

> **Proposition: No promotion by naming** \[prop:nopromotion\] A node labeled “arithmetic,” “spectral,” “Liouville,” “bilinear,” or “four-sign” is not thereby $\mathrm{L2}$. In particular the exact pullback and decompositions of TPC-127–130 remain $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ until their growing actual-family estimates are proved.

> **Proof** The evidence level is determined by quantifiers, carrier, shift, normalization, and the proved inequality, not by the formula’s vocabulary. Exact transformations preserve a target but do not supply its missing bound.

# Seven ordered exhaustive route outcomes

###### ref-073f503b89f3e461

> **Definition: Verdict certificates** \[def:verdicts\] For a selected literal route and a declared route universe:
>
> 1.  $\textnormal{\textsc{go}}$: every required node is $\textnormal{\textsc{proved}}$ in one compatible scope and $\sigma_{\rm raw}-\Lambda_{\rm phys}>0$.
>
> 2.  $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$: a theorem-backed cover exhausts the declared route universe and every covered route has an unavoidable stop certificate.
>
> 3.  $\textnormal{\textsc{reroute}}$: the selected route is stopped and a typed alternative has been selected with a fresh registry.
>
> 4.  $\textnormal{\textsc{stop-route}}$: the selected route has a complete literal stop certificate, but no typed alternative has yet been selected.
>
> 5.  $\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}}$: a required artifact or type is absent.
>
> 6.  $\textnormal{\textsc{arithmetic-frontier}}$: every structural, provenance, fixed-shift, and endpoint node is $\textnormal{\textsc{proved}}$, strict endpoint slack is certified, and every unresolved node has status $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$ and is an explicitly typed positive-$\mathrm{L2}$ target. A $\textnormal{\textsc{conditional}}$, $\textnormal{\textsc{refuted}}$, $\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}}$, or scope-mismatched residual node blocks this outcome.
>
> 7.  $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$: every remaining case.

###### ref-59f3c60df6cf3546

> **Theorem: Verdict precedence** \[thm:precedence\] Given a snapshot manifest passing the consistency checks of [\[def:snapshot\]](TPC-132.md#ref-81a5631cc79ea1d6), apply [\[def:verdicts\]](TPC-132.md#ref-073f503b89f3e461) in the displayed order. The first applicable test returns exactly one of the seven outcomes. An inconsistent manifest returns no route verdict and must first be repaired.

> **Proof** Manifest validation first removes malformed node tables, scopes, route selections, stop covers, and reroute records. On a valid manifest, the displayed list is an ordered classifier: $\textnormal{\textsc{go}}$ is tested first, the two global and selected-route stop branches are tested next, and the first absent artifact then gives $\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}}$. With no earlier outcome, $\textnormal{\textsc{arithmetic-frontier}}$ requires strict structural closure and a residual set consisting only of $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$, positive-$\mathrm{L2}$ targets; the final catch-all is $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$. Hence some test always applies and precedence selects a unique first applicable outcome. This does not assert that the unprioritized raw predicates are intrinsically pairwise disjoint.

> **Remark: Conditional synthesis is metadata** The statement “conditional synthesis available” records that a valid implication has been proved. It is not a route verdict and is not a weakened form of $\textnormal{\textsc{go}}$.

> **Remark: Architecture-infeasible is scoped** The route universe must itself have a proof-carrying cover. Stopping all currently fashionable examples is insufficient. Even a valid $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$ verdict excludes only the declared architecture; it does not contradict the twin-prime conjecture.

# Frozen snapshots and controlled changes

A route verdict is meaningful only relative to a frozen object. The word “current” must therefore be backed by data rather than by the latest paper number.

###### ref-81a5631cc79ea1d6

> **Definition: Snapshot manifest** \[def:snapshot\] An MVP3 snapshot manifest contains:
>
> 1.  the audit date and repository commit or content hashes;
>
> 2.  the declared route universe and the selected route;
>
> 3.  every node statement, scope, carrier hash, and evidence source;
>
> 4.  the proof-DAG edges and one topological order;
>
> 5.  the physical occurrence registry and every allowed joint-token replacement; and
>
> 6.  the endpoint threshold, parameter cells, and exact certificate status.
>
> Changing any field creates a new snapshot rather than silently updating the old verdict.

Before verdict selection, the validator requires exact agreement between the node table, the DAG node set, and the supplied topological order; checks acyclicity and parent-before-child order; validates every status, evidence tag, and scope; and requires the selected route to belong to the declared universe. Each stop record must carry a source, carrier, and scope. An $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$ claim must cover the route universe exactly. A reroute alternative must be distinct, declared, and not already stopped, while the selected route must be stopped. An endpoint certificate must name its source and route cell. Contradictions are rejected rather than mapped silently to a verdict.

The dated v2 manifest used here records content hashes for TPC-120 and TPC-123–132, with a joint hash for TPC-121–122; the route universe $$\begin{split} \mathcal R=\{& \texttt{signed\_native},\ \texttt{positive\_resonance},\\ &\texttt{coherent\_prime\_square}\}; \end{split}$$ the selected route $\texttt{signed\_native}$; the explicit H1–H9 DAG and its topological order; an $\texttt{INCOMPLETE}$ occurrence registry; and an endpoint record with both $\texttt{proved=false}$ and $\texttt{strict\_slack=false}$. A source change changes its content hash and therefore creates a new snapshot.

###### ref-16af9e87156f9af8

> **Proposition: What is and is not monotone** \[prop:monotone\] Fix the node statements, route universe, carrier, and physical event registry.
>
> 1.  Replacing a missing artifact by a valid typed artifact can move the first-missing pointer downstream, but does not by itself prove the target at that node.
>
> 2.  Adding a redundant proof to an already complete $\textnormal{\textsc{go}}$ certificate preserves $\textnormal{\textsc{go}}$.
>
> 3.  A route-specific stop certificate remains valid when unrelated alternatives are added.
>
> 4.  An $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$ certificate need not survive enlargement of the declared route universe.
>
> 5.  Revealing the value of a formerly unknown nonnegative endpoint token may lead either to a strict pass or to a stop; evidence completion is not numerical improvement.

> **Proof** For (a), the topological scan proceeds to the next absent record, but the new artifact may have status $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$ or $\textnormal{\textsc{conditional}}$. Item (b) follows because neither a premise nor the strict inequality changes. A witness for one fixed route does not depend on the existence of a different route, proving (c). By contrast, $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$ has a universal quantifier over the declared route universe; adding an uncovered route invalidates that quantifier and proves (d). Finally, an unknown token is not zero. Its theorem-backed value can lie on either side of the strict threshold, which proves (e).

> **Remark: The first missing item is not the hardest theorem** The topological first-missing report identifies the earliest point at which the present certificate cannot be executed. It does not claim that this item is analytically deepest. H3 may remain the parity-facing gate even while H1 is the first absent artifact.

> **Remark: Rerouting is a new certificate** Changing from an averaged frame to direct native-$h_0$ dispersion, or from a positive majorant to a signed four-sign route, changes maps, scopes, and event occurrences. The old endpoint optimizer is not a warm-start proof: the alternative requires a new manifest and a new canonical registry.

> **Definition: First-missing report** \[def:first\] The machine manifest stores an explicit gate-certificate DAG and a candidate topological order. It first rejects cycles, missing or extra node IDs, and any order that fails exact node coverage or parent-before-child precedence. It then scans the validated order. # MVP3 snapshot verdict

###### ref-4b6ccbb4d620ecd4

> **Theorem: MVP3 decision** \[thm:decision\] For the declared TPC-MVP1 architecture and the TPC-121–131 certificate bundle audited on 27 July 2026, $$\boxed{\operatorname{Verdict}_{121:131}=\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}}.}$$ The snapshot is neither $\textnormal{\textsc{go}}$, nor $\textnormal{\textsc{arithmetic-frontier}}$, nor $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$.

> **Proof** H1’s complete actual-carrier archive is absent, so [\[thm:precedence\]](TPC-132.md#ref-59f3c60df6cf3546) reaches $\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}}$ before either $\textnormal{\textsc{arithmetic-frontier}}$ or $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$. The $\textnormal{\textsc{go}}$ premise fails additionally at the open H2–H4 arithmetic and tail targets and the not-testable H5–H9 records. The arithmetic-frontier premise fails because several structural nodes are not proved and no strict actual endpoint is certified. Finally, the stop records cover only $\texttt{positive\_resonance}$ and $\texttt{coherent\_prime\_square}$; the selected $\texttt{signed\_native}$ route has no stop certificate. Hence no exact cover of the declared route universe exists and $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$ is unavailable.

> **Corollary: No positive L2 progress claim** The TPC-121–131 bundle may sharpen arithmetic targets and exact crosswalks, but it contains no theorem that can be entered as a positive $\mathrm{L2}$ gate in [\[tab:gates\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/TPC-132.md#src-1a858e1fbff76ed7).

> **Proof** Each row of the paper audit either proves a finite identity, conditional implication, reduction, or quantifier obstruction, or states a growing estimate as the missing item. None proves the required uniform coefficient-specific power estimate.

# Machine regression and claim boundary

The standard-library script `experiments/tpc132_mvp3_route_audit.py` encodes the gate records and verifies:

1.  reachability and precedence of all seven ordered outcomes after manifest validation;

2.  rejection of arithmetic-frontier labels with a conditional or refuted residual node;

3.  refusal to return $\textnormal{\textsc{architecture-infeasible}}$ without a complete route-universe certificate and rejection of contradictory manifests;

4.  presence of the content-hash manifest, explicit DAG, and valid topological order;

5.  absence of a proved positive-$\mathrm{L2}$ record; and

6.  the DAG-checked machine-readable first-missing report for H1.

The script is a decision-logic regression. It does not inspect primes or establish any growing analytic estimate.

#### Established.

The verdict precedence, certificate requirements, first-missing format, and snapshot classification are $\mathrm{L0}/\mathrm{L1}$ audit results. They prevent circular implication, false route closure, and false promotion to the arithmetic frontier.

#### Not established.

No complete actual archive, H1–H9 conjunction, positive fixed-shift $\mathrm{L2}$ theorem, strict physical endpoint, hard-packet $o(X)$ estimate, Hardy–Littlewood asymptotic, prime-pair lower bound, or twin-prime theorem is proved.

# Conclusion

MVP3 turns “we are near the arithmetic core” into a checkable statement rather than a rhetorical one. The current program has a sharper map and several exact transformations, but its first missing object is still structural. The correct verdict is therefore $\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}}$, not $\textnormal{\textsc{arithmetic-frontier}}$. If the archive, reassembly, fixed-shift, and endpoint certificates are eventually closed, the same decision calculus will promote the route to the arithmetic frontier only when every remaining unresolved node is $\textnormal{\textsc{open}}$, positive $\mathrm{L2}$, and scope-compatible, without calling a conditional interface or reduction a theorem. If a literal stop appears first, the calculus records the stopped route and the permitted alternative without implying anything negative about twin primes.
