## Abstract

Let $$D(z)=d+sz,\qquad V(z)=u+az,\qquad su-ad=2$$ be fixed primitive positive affine forms, and let $\rho$ be a fixed bounded periodic sequence. We prove the unconditional fixed-data theorem $$\sum_{x/\omega(x)<z\le x}  \frac{\mu(D(z))\mu(V(z))\rho(z)}{z}  =o(\log\omega(x))$$ for every $1\le\omega(x)\le x$ with $\omega(x)\to\infty$. The analytic input is Tao’s proved logarithmically averaged two-point theorem for fixed affine forms. To close the two squarefree masks without changing its quantifiers, we use the Boolean prime-square cutoff $$\mathsf S_P(m)=\prod_{p\le P}(1-\mathbf 1_{p^2\mid m}).$$ For fixed $P$ it has a finite signed CRT expansion, whereas its full-squarefree error has logarithmic relative mass $O(P^{-1})+o(1)$. The order of limits is essential: $P$ is fixed before $x\to\infty$, and only then tends to infinity. The result is a genuine fixed-shift arithmetic shadow, but it is logarithmically weighted and frozen. It proves no uniformity in growing coefficients, physical weights or phases, no ordinary all-prefix power saving, and no positive $\mathrm{L2}$ input.

# Fixed determinant-two data

###### ref-01bd98f14129d0ba

Fix integers $a,s\ge1$ and $d,u\in\mathbb Z$ satisfying $$(d,s)=(u,a)=(a,s)=1,\qquad su-ad=2. \label{eq:primitive}$$ After a fixed translation of $z$, if necessary, assume $$D(z)=d+sz>0,\qquad V(z)=u+az>0$$ throughout the asymptotic range. The determinant condition makes the coefficient pairs $(s,d)$ and $(a,u)$ nonparallel. TPC-127 identifies these forms with the literal fixed-two Liouville carrier, while TPC-128 opens their squarefree indicators by a magnitude cutoff [`\citep{WangTPC127,WangTPC128}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-137.md#cite-82838990f4215d0c); [\[WangTPC128\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-137.md#cite-4e0fc7932d9675d8).

Let $\rho:\mathbb Z\to\mathbb C$ be fixed, periodic of period $M$, and satisfy $|\rho|\le1$. The words “fixed” and “periodic” are theorem quantifiers: neither $M$ nor the values of $\rho$ may grow with $x$ in the theorem below.

# A Boolean prime-square cutoff

###### ref-0da89e3216ddad8f

Let $$P^\#=\prod_{p\le P}p$$ and define $$\mathsf S_P(m)  =  \prod_{p\le P}(1-\mathbf 1_{p^2\mid m}). \label{eq:SP-product}$$

###### ref-13b48f0d78d3e383

###### ref-8d9990a88f24f961

###### ref-25f8bec546d0e71d

> **Lemma: Exact finite expansion** \[lem:expansion\] For every positive integer $m$, $$\mathsf S_P(m)  =  \sum_{k\mid P^\#}\mu(k)\mathbf 1_{k^2\mid m}  \in\{0,1\}. \label{eq:SP-expansion}$$ Consequently $$\begin{aligned}  \mathsf S_P(D(z))\mathsf S_P(V(z))  =  \sum_{k,\ell\mid P^\#}  \mu(k)\mu(\ell)  \mathbf 1_{k^2\mid D(z)}  \mathbf 1_{\ell^2\mid V(z)}. \label{eq:two-expansion}\end{aligned}$$

> **Proof** Expand the finite Euler product in [\[eq:SP-product\]](TPC-137.md#ref-0da89e3216ddad8f). Its value is one exactly when no prime $p\le P$ has $p^2\mid m$, and is zero otherwise. Multiplying the two one-variable expansions gives [\[eq:two-expansion\]](TPC-137.md#ref-25f8bec546d0e71d).

For a compatible pair $(k,\ell)$, the two square divisibilities select one residue modulo $\operatorname{lcm}(k,\ell)^2$. In the determinant-two primitive setting, compatibility in fact forces the conditions recorded in TPC-128, including $(k,\ell)=1$, but the following upper bound does not require equality of moduli.

###### ref-4aad49894f8afeda

###### ref-b319c385d4270712

> **Proposition: Correct combined modulus** \[prop:modulus\] After also resolving the period $M$, every nonempty component of [\[eq:two-expansion\]](TPC-137.md#ref-25f8bec546d0e71d) is a union of residue classes modulo $$L_{M,k,\ell}  =  \operatorname{lcm}(M,k^2,\ell^2),$$ and $$L_{M,k,\ell}  \le  M\operatorname{lcm}(k,\ell)^2  \le M(P^\#)^2. \label{eq:modulus}$$ There are at most $4^{\pi(P)}$ ordered component pairs.

> **Proof** Intersect the three periodic conditions. Since $\operatorname{lcm}(k^2,\ell^2)=\operatorname{lcm}(k,\ell)^2$, the first inequality follows from $\operatorname{lcm}(M,L)\le ML$. Both $k$ and $\ell$ divide $P^\#$, proving the second inequality. There are $2^{\pi(P)}$ divisors of $P^\#$, independently in each coordinate.

> **Remark: Why no fourth power appears** The two divisors $k,\ell$ are drawn from the same prime set. The universal bound is $M(P^\#)^2$, not $M(P^\#)^4$. Writing a product $k^2\ell^2$ is harmless only after compatibility has forced $(k,\ell)=1$; even then $k\ell\mid P^\#$.

###### ref-a025b2bd4efcc4d6

###### ref-9f0433bf0e9fe927

> **Lemma: Residue and content preserve nonparallelity** \[lem:reduced-determinant\] Let $L\ge1$ and $0\le r<L$. On the progression $z=r+Lt$, put $$c_D=(sL,d+sr),\qquad c_V=(aL,u+ar).$$ After extracting these positive contents, the two primitive forms $$\widetilde D(t)=\frac{sL}{c_D}t+\frac{d+sr}{c_D},  \qquad  \widetilde V(t)=\frac{aL}{c_V}t+\frac{u+ar}{c_V}$$ have coefficient determinant $$\det(\widetilde D,\widetilde V)  =  \frac{L(su-ad)}{c_Dc_V}  =  \frac{2L}{c_Dc_V}\ne0. \label{eq:reduced-determinant}$$

> **Proof** The two displayed quotients are primitive by the definitions of $c_D,c_V$. Before content extraction their determinant is $$(sL)(u+ar)-(aL)(d+sr)=L(su-ad)=2L.$$ Dividing the two coefficient pairs by their contents proves [\[eq:reduced-determinant\]](TPC-137.md#ref-9f0433bf0e9fe927). In particular $c_Dc_V\mid2L$, and no CRT residue can make the reduced forms parallel.

# A tail suited to a two-limit theorem

###### ref-190c5690297e9554

###### ref-1e1a06c910c83fe2

> **Lemma: One-form large-prime-square tail** \[lem:tail\] Let $L(z)=b+cz$ be fixed, primitive and positive on an integer interval $I$ of length $N$, and suppose $L(z)\le Y$. Then $$\sum_{z\in I}  |\mu^2(L(z))-\mathsf S_P(L(z))|  \le  \sum_{\substack{p>P\\p\le\sqrt Y}}  \left(\frac{N}{p^2}+1\right)  \ll_L \frac NP+\sqrt Y. \label{eq:tail}$$

> **Proof** If $\mu^2(m)\ne\mathsf S_P(m)$, then $p^2\mid m$ for some prime $p>P$. For $p\nmid c$, the condition $p^2\mid L(z)$ selects one residue modulo $p^2$; for $p\mid c$, primitivity makes it insoluble. Summing $N/p^2+1$ and using $\sum_{n>P}n^{-2}\ll P^{-1}$ proves the claim.

###### ref-8acdab81621b14b3

Unlike the magnitude-truncated divisor sum, $\mathsf S_P$ is Boolean. Therefore $$\begin{aligned}  &\left|  \mu^2(D)\mu^2(V)  -\mathsf S_P(D)\mathsf S_P(V)  \right| \notag\\  &\qquad\le  |\mu^2(D)-\mathsf S_P(D)|  +|\mu^2(V)-\mathsf S_P(V)|. \label{eq:product-tail}\end{aligned}$$ No divisor-function maximum is introduced.

###### ref-c278d5da851a39a7

###### ref-2415738f387eedde

> **Proposition: Terminal logarithmic tail** \[prop:log-tail\] For the fixed forms above, $$\sum_{x/\omega<z\le x}  \frac{  |\mu^2(D(z))\mu^2(V(z))  -\mathsf S_P(D(z))\mathsf S_P(V(z))|}  {z}  \ll_{D,V}  \frac{\log\omega}{P}+1 \label{eq:log-tail}$$ uniformly for $1<\omega\le x$.

> **Proof** Partition the terminal interval into dyadic pieces $(T,2T]$, with at most one truncated end piece. On one such piece, \[[lem:tail](TPC-137.md#ref-190c5690297e9554),[eq:product-tail](TPC-137.md#ref-8acdab81621b14b3)\] and $z^{-1}\le T^{-1}$ give $$\sum_{T<z\le2T}\frac{\text{tail}(z)}z  \ll_{D,V}P^{-1}+T^{-1/2}.$$ There are $O(1+\log\omega)$ pieces, while their $T^{-1/2}$ terms form a geometric sum when ordered from the smallest scale. The finitely many pieces below a fixed positivity threshold are absorbed in the constant.

# Full-Möbius logarithmic closure

###### ref-abcb55c935ae21d5

We use the following source theorem only with its native fixed-data quantifiers. For fixed positive affine forms $$L_i(z)=a_i z+b_i,\qquad  a_1b_2-a_2b_1\ne0,$$ Tao proved $$\sum_{x/\omega(x)<z\le x}  \frac{\lambda(L_1(z))\lambda(L_2(z))}{z}  =  o(\log\omega(x)) \label{eq:tao}$$ whenever $1\le\omega(x)\le x$ and $\omega(x)\to\infty$ [`\citep{Tao2016LogChowla}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-137.md#cite-a15c9a9edf426c07).

###### ref-50231042444a4803

###### ref-b550f02a87ea80a1

> **Theorem: Fixed determinant-two full-squarefree closure** \[thm:main\] Under [\[eq:primitive\]](TPC-137.md#ref-01bd98f14129d0ba), for every fixed bounded periodic $\rho$ and every admissible $\omega$, $$\boxed{  \sum_{x/\omega(x)<z\le x}  \frac{\mu(D(z))\mu(V(z))\rho(z)}{z}  =  o(\log\omega(x)).} \label{eq:main}$$

> **Proof** Fix $P$. Insert [\[eq:two-expansion\]](TPC-137.md#ref-25f8bec546d0e71d) and split $\rho$ into its finitely many residue classes. On each nonempty combined residue write $z=r+Lt$. The two forms become fixed affine forms in $t$. Extract their fixed positive contents. gives their exact reduced determinant $2L/(c_Dc_V)$, so Tao’s nonparallelity hypothesis survives both the residue split and content extraction. Complete multiplicativity extracts fixed Liouville signs.
>
> Discard the finitely many terms with $t\le0$. The transformed terminal interval is $$\frac{x/\omega-r}{L}<t\le\frac{x-r}{L}.$$ If its lower endpoint is at least one, take the ratio of the two endpoints; otherwise start at $t=1$. In either case this gives an admissible transformed terminal ratio $\omega'(x)\to\infty$ with $$\log\omega'(x)=\log\omega(x)+O_{r,L}(1).$$ Equation [\[eq:tao\]](TPC-137.md#ref-abcb55c935ae21d5), together with $$\frac1{r+Lt}=\frac1L\frac1t+O_{r,L}(t^{-2}),$$ then gives $o(\log\omega)$ for every component. The number of components is fixed because $P,M,D,V$ are fixed.
>
> Since $\mu=\mu^2\lambda$, the difference between the full Möbius correlation and this truncated correlation is bounded by [\[prop:log-tail\]](TPC-137.md#ref-c278d5da851a39a7). After division by $\log\omega$, $$\limsup_{x\to\infty}  \frac{|\text{full sum}|}{\log\omega(x)}  \ll_{D,V}\frac1P.$$ Now let $P\to\infty$.

> **Remark: Order of limits** The theorem does not choose a prescribed $P=P(x)$. For each accuracy request, one first fixes a sufficiently large $P$, and then takes $x$ sufficiently large depending on all fixed data. This is exactly why [\[thm:main\]](TPC-137.md#ref-50231042444a4803) is not a growing-family theorem.

# Claim boundary and next gate

| Statement                                                                     | Level and status                                        |
|:------------------------------------------------------------------------------|:--------------------------------------------------------|
| Prime-square identity and component bound                                     | Exact finite theorem ($\mathrm{L0}$).                   |
| Large-prime-square and logarithmic tails                                      | Unconditional elementary theorem ($\mathrm{L0}$).       |
| Full $\mu(D)\mu(V)$, fixed periodic logarithmic cancellation                  | Unconditional frozen arithmetic shadow ($\mathrm{L1}$). |
| Prescribed growing $P$, affine height, period, or CRT modulus                 | Not proved.                                             |
| Physical weight, arbitrary additive phase, ordinary all-prefix power estimate | Not proved; actual $\mathrm{L2}$ gate.                  |
| H3, $1/400$, or a prime-pair theorem                                          | Not proved.                                             |

TPC-137 closes a precise gap left by the finite-mask statement of TPC-129 [`\citep{WangTPC129}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-137.md#cite-fc3203528821dfb5): the full squarefree masks can be returned for fixed data without claiming a quantitative cutoff rate. The next question is whether stronger quantitative results known for the consecutive shift-one carrier touch the active odd shift-two carrier. They do not do so by a parity-preserving relabeling.
