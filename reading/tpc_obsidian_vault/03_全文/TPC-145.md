## Abstract

The fixed shift stored on a cut record and the fixed-shift selector after physical grouping are different mathematical objects. We formalize their relation through a commuting square. For an exact grouping matrix $G$ with source and target shift selectors, $$P_{\rm phys}G=GP_{\rm src}$$ if and only if every aggregate cross-selector block vanishes. This operator identity is still not a pathwise provenance certificate: opposite cross-shift occurrence edges can cancel in the same matrix entry. On the row-separated occurrence lift, commutation is equivalent to every nonzero occurrence edge preserving membership in the prescribed-$h_0$ slice. We prove this exact criterion and a sharp cancellation counterexample. Applying it to the current archive gives one positive structural result, $P_{h_0}^{\rm cut}=I$, already supported by cut metadata intertwining. The physical grouping $G$, later stage matrices and downstream selector are absent, so their totality and commuting square remain <span class="smallcaps">not-testable</span>. Treating the cut selector as a downstream selector, or aggregate cancellation as shift provenance, stops only those declared implementations. No physical cover or positive arithmetic saving is obtained.

# Cut selection versus downstream selection

###### ref-999dbbc5cac423dc

Fix a prescribed nonzero shift $h_0$. TPC-136 proves that every nonzero edge through the complete cut archive retains the native shift tag [`\citep{WangTPC136}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-145.md#cite-e306939829878a03). If $E_{\rm cut}$ is its row space, define the cut coordinate selector by $$P_{h_0}^{\rm cut}e_c  =  \begin{cases}  e_c,&h(c)=h_0,\\  0,&h(c)\ne h_0.  \end{cases}$$ Every row in the fixed-$h_0$ archive has the first case, whence $$\boxed{P_{h_0}^{\rm cut}=I_{E_{\rm cut}}.}  \label{eq:cutidentity}$$ This is a literal $\mathrm{L1}$ fact, not a localization estimate.

TPC-125’s downstream selector is defined on later shift-tagged record spaces and must intertwine every later stage [`\citep{WangTPC125}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-145.md#cite-52f31cd720b99fdf). Those stages can split, aggregate or leak between shift labels. Equation [\[eq:cutidentity\]](TPC-145.md#ref-999dbbc5cac423dc) says nothing about an unarchived stage.

> **Definition: Two selector namespaces** We write $P_{h_0}^{\rm cut}$ only for the proved cut selector and $P_{h_0}^{\rm down}$ only for a selector on a completed downstream archive. Equality of their names or numerical $h_0$-values is not an intertwining theorem.

# Physical occurrence grouping

Let $\mathcal O$ be a fully typed source occurrence set and $\mathcal P$ a physical occurrence set. An exact physical grouping matrix $$G:\mathbb C^{\mathcal O}\longrightarrow\mathbb C^{\mathcal P}$$ must store, on every nonzero edge:

1.  source occurrence, physical occurrence and physical group IDs;

2.  exact reconstruction multiplier;

3.  physical and computational multiplicities and any inverse-aggregation relation;

4.  masks, cover class and reconnection destination;

5.  source and target shift tags; and

6.  the one inherited physical normalization.

The physical group key is not determined by a determinant label or by equality of two numerical coefficients. TPC-124 shows why a physical aggregate can hide a native residual, while TPC-117 reports that the actual growing physical map is unavailable [`\citep{WangTPC117,WangTPC124}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-145.md#cite-41bb314f911953d0); [\[WangTPC124\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-145.md#cite-ecc9b818c6056a02).

Let $P_{\rm src}$ and $P_{\rm phys}$ be the coordinate projections onto records tagged by $h_0$ in the source and target, respectively.

###### ref-4399c54cc5113dd5

###### ref-637cc68cfacfa90c

> **Theorem: Aggregate block criterion** \[thm:aggregate\] The operator square $$P_{\rm phys}G=GP_{\rm src}  \label{eq:square}$$ commutes if and only if $$G_{p,o}=0$$ whenever exactly one of $h(p),h(o)$ equals $h_0$.

> **Proof** On a matrix entry $(p,o)$, the left side of [\[eq:square\]](TPC-145.md#ref-637cc68cfacfa90c) is $$\mathbf1_{h(p)=h_0}G_{p,o},$$ while the right side is $$G_{p,o}\mathbf1_{h(o)=h_0}.$$ They agree on same-selector blocks. On a cross-selector block they agree precisely when the entry vanishes.

# Why aggregate commutation is not lineage

A grouping matrix records the sum of all transformation paths between one source and one target. Let $$\widehat{\mathcal E}  =  \{e:o(e)\to p(e)\}$$ be the row-separated set of individual occurrence edges, with exact nonzero multipliers $g_e$. The corresponding path lift $\widehat G$ has one row for each $e$, so two edges never cancel inside one row.

> **Definition: Occurrence-level shift preservation** The grouping is pathwise shift preserving at $h_0$ when every edge with $g_e\ne0$ satisfies $$\mathbf1_{h(o(e))=h_0}  =  \mathbf1_{h(p(e))=h_0}.$$

###### ref-61a25fed4c1bf01a

> **Theorem: Row-separated commuting-square criterion** \[thm:pathwise\] For the row-separated occurrence lift, selector commutation is equivalent to occurrence-level shift preservation.

> **Proof** The row associated with $e$ contains one source entry $g_e$. The corresponding selector defect is $$\left(  \mathbf1_{h(p(e))=h_0}  -  \mathbf1_{h(o(e))=h_0}  \right)g_e.$$ Because rows are separate, a defect in one occurrence cannot cancel a defect in another. The full defect vanishes exactly when every displayed scalar vanishes.

###### ref-5c9f90f38a7243e8

> **Proposition: Sharp hidden-leakage example** \[prop:hidden\] Aggregate commutation does not imply the pathwise condition.

> **Proof** Take a source $o$ tagged $h_0$ and a target $p$ tagged $h_1\ne h_0$. Add two occurrence edges $o\to p$, with multipliers $1$ and $-1$. Their aggregate matrix entry is zero, so the aggregate square commutes. Each row-separated edge crosses the selector boundary, so both fail the pathwise condition.

This is the grouping analogue of the distinction between a scalar zero defect and the row-separated matrix defect in TPC-123 and TPC-136 [`\citep{WangTPC123,WangTPC136}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-145.md#cite-6ff895d094f015b3); [\[WangTPC136\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-145.md#cite-e306939829878a03). A route that claims literal shift provenance must use the pathwise statement.

# The current field boundary

The TPC-143 occurrence obligations require a one-to-many row-separated lift from every nonsoft cut path [`\citep{WangTPC143}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-145.md#cite-2dda9635c69dc81e). The required domain is $$\mathcal C_X^{\mathsf{ETO}}\sqcup  \mathcal C_X^{\mathsf{FUM}}.$$ No finite sample census can replace that typed domain.

For $G$, the current records lack physical occurrence IDs, group keys, reconstruction multipliers, multiplicity rules, cover classes and reconnection destinations. For $P_{h_0}^{\rm down}$, they lack every later source/target shift pair and stage matrix. Consequently neither matrix in [\[eq:square\]](TPC-145.md#ref-637cc68cfacfa90c) can be formed.

The two stopped implementations are therefore precisely: $$\begin{split} &\text{cut metadata as a downstream selector},\\ &\text{aggregate cancellation as shift provenance}. \end{split}$$ Both are scoped $\textnormal{\textsc{stop-declared-route}}$ outcomes. They do not stop a future occurrence-augmented map.

# Claim boundary

> **Theorem: Audited physical-shift conclusion** The cut-stage identity [\[eq:cutidentity\]](TPC-145.md#ref-999dbbc5cac423dc) is proved, but the current source artifacts do not define a literal physical grouping or downstream fixed-shift map. Thus the selected augmented route remains open at the occurrence-lift node and is not architecture-infeasible.

This paper proves no physical cover, quotient observability bound, frontier cancellation, localization cost, positive fixed-shift $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate, endpoint below $1/400$, parity advance, prime-pair lower bound or twin-prime theorem. Determinant reserve is not charged as a physical grouping gain. The only positive application to the current archive is the exact and deliberately stage-limited identity $P_{h_0}^{\rm cut}=I$.
