## Abstract

TPC-170 proves a fixed-$X$-power, all-prefix packet theorem for Lebesgue-almost every fixed phase on a prescribed determinant-two schedule. TPC-180 shows that the frozen source corpus does not yet contain the value-bearing production phase registry required to name the physical atom. We isolate the remaining selector question even conditionally on a future positive registry.

For the schedule-dependent bad sets $E_n$, Borel–Cantelli proves $\operatorname{meas}(\limsup E_n)=0$. It does not prove that a prespecified singleton $\alpha_\star$ lies outside that limsup: the null set may be exactly $\{\alpha_\star\}$. We formulate the minimal source-backed bridge contract, whose load-bearing theorem is the schedule-specific assertion $\alpha_\star\notin\limsup E_n$. No artifact currently supplies this assertion. Therefore $\mathsf{H2.metric\_fixed\_atom\_crosswalk}$ remains $\mathsf{NOT\_TESTABLE}$, and only the uncontrolled atomic promotion method receives a scoped stop. The pointwise bad-endpoint and direct additive-twist routes remain parent-ready and open. This is a rigorous $\mathrm{L1}$ quantifier obstruction, not a program-positive $\mathrm{L2}$ estimate.

###### ref-9cd081c6a33bef7a

At scale $n$, let $\mathcal P_n$ be the packet list prescribed before its exceptional phases are read. For $p\in\mathcal P_n$, write $$G_{n,p}(\alpha)  =  \frac{q_{n,p}}{T_{n,p}}  \max_{k\le L_{n,p}}|S_{n,p,k}(\alpha)|. \tag{1}\label{eq:G}$$ TPC-169 proves $$\int_{\mathbb T}G_{n,p}(\alpha)^2\,d\alpha\le V_{n,p}, \tag{2}\label{eq:energy}$$ where $$V_{n,p}  =  D_{n,p}^2\|\rho_{n,p}\|_\infty^2  \left(\frac{q_{n,p}}{T_{n,p}}        +\frac{q_{n,p}^2}{T_{n,p}^2}\right) \tag{3}\label{eq:V}$$ [`\citep{WangTPC169}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-181.md#cite-23a5e7f1b8899554). For thresholds $\lambda_n>0$, define $$E_n  =  \left\{\alpha\in\mathbb T:  \max_{p\in\mathcal P_n}G_{n,p}(\alpha)>\lambda_n  \right\}. \tag{4}\label{eq:En}$$ TPC-170 proves that $$\sum_n\lambda_n^{-2}  \sum_{p\in\mathcal P_n}V_{n,p}<\infty \tag{5}\label{eq:summability}$$ implies $$\operatorname{meas}\!\left(\limsup_{n\to\infty}E_n\right)=0. \tag{6}\label{eq:limsup}$$ Equivalently, for Lebesgue-almost every fixed $\alpha$, every declared packet and every prefix is eventually at most $\lambda_n$ [`\citep{WangTPC170}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-181.md#cite-dcda1b3bac6854e7).

###### ref-e0f42fdd6eacd3f6

###### ref-c73328ccd85c31e3

Under the dyadic, polylogarithmic packet envelope, one may choose $$\lambda_n=X_n^{-\delta},\qquad \delta<\frac14. \tag{7}\label{eq:power}$$ The exact imported signature is $$\begin{array}{c|c} \text{axis}&\text{proved value}\\ \hline \text{carrier}&\mathsf{EXPLICIT\_PACKET\_CORRIDOR}\\ \text{phase}&\mathsf{LEBESGUE\_AE\_FIXED\_PHASE}\\ \text{endpoint}&\mathsf{ALL\_PREFIX\_THETA\_SHELL}\\ \text{scale}&\mathsf{EVENTUALLY\_PRESCRIBED\_SCHEDULE}\\ \text{decay}&\mathsf{FIXED\_X\_POWER\_PHASE\_METRIC}\\ \text{support}&\mathsf{ACTUAL\_CORE}. \end{array} \tag{8}\label{eq:metric-signature}$$ The word “fixed” in the phase row means that the same $\alpha$ is followed across $n$. It does not change the almost-everywhere quantifier to evaluation at a named atom.

# The two independent missing inputs

###### ref-0715544b5b16c367

###### ref-c4b5225bdf166688

Even if a later source supplies $\alpha_\star$, one more theorem is needed: $$\boxed{\alpha_\star\notin\limsup_{n\to\infty}E_n} \tag{10}\label{eq:avoid}$$ for the exact production schedule and bad sets [\[eq:En\]](TPC-181.md#ref-a38f67f2f0ff0d76). The registry is a data input; [\[eq:avoid\]](TPC-181.md#ref-c4b5225bdf166688) is an arithmetic or selector theorem. Their roles and evidence cannot be merged.

###### ref-7c3dc46af70b34eb

> **Definition: Legal metric-to-fixed-atom bridge** \[def:bridge\] A legal bridge contains:
>
> 1.  the exact source-located named atom and phase value;
>
> 2.  a hash and source locator for the exact production packet schedule;
>
> 3.  the schedule’s precise sets $E_n$;
>
> 4.  a theorem proving [\[eq:avoid\]](TPC-181.md#ref-c4b5225bdf166688);
>
> 5.  the same fixed $\alpha_\star$ at every scale; and
>
> 6.  coverage of every production packet to which the conclusion is applied.

A source hash certifies only identity. It does not prove item (iv). Nor do a phase-$L^2$ estimate, density-one control on a separated registry, a phase selected after the bad set, or phases $\alpha_n$ that drift with $n$ [`\citep{WangTPC167,WangTPC168,WangTPC171,WangTPC172}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-181.md#cite-598162e13aeccd50); [\[WangTPC168\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-181.md#cite-8068f3317002ce2e); [\[WangTPC171\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-181.md#cite-335bbef0ad6756f5); [\[WangTPC172\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-181.md#cite-b31419d19c36dd89).

# The singleton obstruction

###### ref-b516ce95bd9615f5

> **Proposition: Full measure does not select a singleton** \[prop:singleton\] Let $\alpha_\star\in\mathbb T$ be prescribed. From $\operatorname{meas}(G)=1$ alone one cannot infer $\alpha_\star\in G$.

> **Proof** Take $$N=\{\alpha_\star\},\qquad G=\mathbb T\setminus N.$$ Then $\operatorname{meas}(N)=0$ and $\operatorname{meas}(G)=1$, but $\alpha_\star\notin G$.

###### ref-e508a127a6e7ff38

> **Corollary: Borel–Cantelli selector nonimplication** \[cor:BC\] Equation [\[eq:limsup\]](TPC-181.md#ref-9cd081c6a33bef7a), without an independent theorem about $\alpha_\star$, does not imply [\[eq:avoid\]](TPC-181.md#ref-c4b5225bdf166688).

> **Proof** Apply [\[prop:singleton\]](TPC-181.md#ref-b516ce95bd9615f5) with $$N=\limsup E_n,\qquad G=\mathbb T\setminus\limsup E_n.$$ The metric theorem controls the measure of $N$, not membership of a prespecified singleton.

TPC-170 also realizes the logical sharpness with constant coefficients at phase zero. That example is not asserted to be the literal Möbius packet. The general obstruction in [\[prop:singleton\]](TPC-181.md#ref-b516ce95bd9615f5) is a quantifier nonimplication, not a coefficient lower bound.

###### ref-3182e76f54e0cd41

###### ref-c2df40f3e770c183

###### ref-f15cc2168b701919

> **Theorem: Exact scoped stop** \[thm:stop\] The implication $$\mathsf{LEBESGUE\_AE\_FIXED\_PHASE} \Longrightarrow \mathsf{NAMED\_FIXED\_ATOM} \tag{11}\label{eq:bad-implication}$$ is invalid without a bridge satisfying [\[def:bridge\]](TPC-181.md#ref-7c3dc46af70b34eb). Therefore the arithmetic method $$\mathsf{phase\_metric\_uncontrolled\_atomic} \tag{12}\label{eq:method}$$ has status $\textnormal{\textsc{stop-scoped}}$ in scope $\mathsf{UNCONTROLLED\_ATOMIC\_PROMOTION\_ONLY}$.

> **Proof** disproves the inference at the only step where the almost-everywhere theorem is promoted to the named atom. Adding a source-backed proof of [\[eq:avoid\]](TPC-181.md#ref-c4b5225bdf166688) changes the premise and lies outside the uncontrolled method. A direct pointwise theorem also changes the phase quantifier rather than using [\[eq:bad-implication\]](TPC-181.md#ref-c2df40f3e770c183). Hence the stop has exactly the stated scope.

# Selector verdict and sufficient bridge hypotheses

###### ref-c6ba6d30ce600a13

The present state fails both inputs to [\[def:bridge\]](TPC-181.md#ref-7c3dc46af70b34eb): the value-bearing registry in [\[eq:registry\]](TPC-181.md#ref-0715544b5b16c367) is absent, and no schedule-specific avoidance theorem [\[eq:avoid\]](TPC-181.md#ref-c4b5225bdf166688) is source-locked. Thus $$\boxed{ \begin{gathered} \mathsf{H2.metric\_fixed\_atom\_crosswalk}=\textnormal{\textsc{not-testable}},\\ \mathsf{SelectorConstructed}=\mathsf{false},\\ \mathsf{FixedAtomDecayObtained}=\mathsf{false}. \end{gathered}} \tag{13}\label{eq:verdict}$$

Three forms of genuinely new input could change a future snapshot:

1.  a theorem proving [\[eq:avoid\]](TPC-181.md#ref-c4b5225bdf166688) for the exact named phase and exact schedule;

2.  a source-compatible point-evaluation or continuity estimate strong enough to dominate $G_{n,p}(\alpha_\star)$ by controlled values with a summable error; or

3.  an independent pointwise fixed-phase theorem, which exits the metric bridge and enters a pointwise route.

Merely declaring the named phase “generic” is not one of these hypotheses unless a theorem with the exact schedule-dependent quantifiers is supplied.

# Conclusion

The metric route ends at a precise theorem interface. TPC-170 gives full-measure eventual control on its exact prescribed schedule; TPC-181 proves that this measure statement cannot select a prespecified singleton. Since TPC-180 also lacks the named value and locator, the source-backed selector is not testable. The failure is method-scoped, so the program returns to the two pointwise O161 targets without relabeling them as stopped and without claiming any new $\mathrm{L2}$ progress.
