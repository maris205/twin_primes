# Three noninterchangeable formula types

The repository also contains $$F_N^{\rm blk}=\frac{q}{N}\sum_{N<t(z)\leq2N}  c_z \mathrm{e}(-\alpha z) \quad\hbox{and}\quad  F_T^{\rm cum}=\frac{q}{T}\sum_{0<t(z)\leq T}  c_z \mathrm{e}(-\alpha z).$$ These are respectively a terminal block and a cumulative prefix. Neither is definitionally equal to [\[eq:physical-prefix\]](TPC-194.md#ref-796a84a73ecbf9b3). In particular, we do not set $N=T$, replace $q_X$ by $a_\xi s_\xi$, identify an archive key with a production schedule, or absorb the physical decorations into $c_z$.
