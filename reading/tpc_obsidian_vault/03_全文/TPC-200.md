The Fejér/four-sign reduction itself is imported from TPC-130 [`\citep{WangTPC130}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-200.md#cite-a49691a1c51bd81c). Then $$\begin{array}{ll}  L_1=d+sz,&L_2=u+az,\\  L_3=d+s(z+h),&L_4=u+a(z+h). \end{array}$$ For $L_i=b_i+m_i z$, use $\det(L_i,L_j)=m_i b_j-m_j b_i$.

At that point $a=s=1$, $u-d=2$, and $L_2=L_3$.

> **Proof** Expansion gives the six entries. All except $qh-2$ are visibly nonzero. The equation $qh=2$ with $q$ positive odd and $h$ positive integral forces $q=1,h=2$. Then $as=1$ forces $a=s=1$, and $su-ad=u-d=2$, proving coincidence.
