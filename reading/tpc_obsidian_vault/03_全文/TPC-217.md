## Abstract

TPC-216 proved a complete-period direct-sum envelope for the literal V46 reciprocal emitter but left the physical interval open. This paper attaches that result to the interval $I_x=(x/2,x]\cap\mathbb Z$. The common-source kernel is first regrouped exactly by reduced rational frequencies: divisor rows with denominator $d$ contribute to the same frequency $a/h$ through the single coefficient $C_h=\sum_{h\mid d}\mu(d)\log(d)/d$. Distinct reduced fractions with denominators at most $U$ have circular spacing at least $U^{-2}$, so the additive large sieve gives a finite-window factor $N+U^2$, where $N=|I_x|$. TPC-215 and TPC-216 then yield $$N^{-1}\sum_{n\in I_x}|\mathcal K(n)|^2  \ll_{\psi} x^{11/32}(\log x)^5.$$ The $U^2$ term is lower order since $U^2/x=x^{-67/200}$, giving an unnormalized exponent $43/32+o(1)$. A finite aligned-shell fixture has exact one-point coherent-to-diagonal ratio $2$, so the attachment cannot be replaced by free finite-window orthogonality. This is a structural result only: prime- shell reassembly, arithmetic $L^2$, the strict $1/400$ budget, and the twin-prime endpoint remain open.

# Introduction

The transition-native twin-prime route has reached a useful intermediate object. Its physical reciprocal emitter is coupled across a squarefree divisor band, so a divisor-by-divisor orthogonal decomposition is not literal. TPC-213 constructed the common physical source and its cross-divisor Gram [`\cite{WangV46,WangTPC214}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-217.md#cite-7bb4294ad89b6eed); [\[WangTPC214\]](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-217.md#cite-9f30a2da6509a67e). TPC-214 then showed that on a complete period the correct coordinates are reduced rational frequencies. If $h$ is the reduced denominator, all rows with $h\mid d$ share one emitter row $B_h$ and their coefficients add to $$C_h=\sum_{\substack{d\in\mathcal D_x\\h\mid d}} c_d,  \qquad c_d=\frac{\mu(d)\log d}{d}.$$

TPC-215 bounded the resulting cluster coefficient energy by an $O((\log x)^2)$ multiple of the divisor direct-sum energy, and TPC-216 bounded that direct-sum energy by $$L^{-1}\mathcal E_{\mathrm{dir}}\ll_{\psi}x^{11/32}(\log x)^3  \cite{WangTPC215,WangTPC216}.$$ Those results still used a complete common period. The next question is whether the same object can be evaluated on the actual interval $I_x$ without silently restoring orthogonality.

The answer is a clean structural attachment. Reduced fractions with denominator at most $U$ form a $U^{-2}$-separated set modulo one. The additive large sieve therefore controls the physical Gram by $(N+U^2)$ times the reduced-frequency coefficient energy. Since $U^2=o(x)$, the power exponent is unchanged. The result does not exploit signs of $\mu$, cancellation among primes, or the four-packet polarization. It establishes the finite-window interface and leaves the arithmetic bridge visible.

Our contributions are:

1.  an exact finite-window regrouping of the literal common-source kernel;

2.  a source-locked large-sieve attachment with normalized exponent $11/32$ and logarithmic cost $(\log x)^5$;

3.  an independent finite certificate and a one-point aligned-shell adversary showing why free finite-window orthogonality is invalid.

# Exact reduced-frequency representation

###### ref-06e9c68283b6949a

> **Lemma: divisor dilation** \[lem:dilation\] If $h\mid d$, $d=kh$, and $(a,h)=1$, then $$B_d(ka)=B_h(a).$$

> **Proof** The congruence in [\[eq:emitter\]](TPC-217.md#ref-2d7c2f1ad5ea8e95) becomes $$m\overline q\equiv ka\pmod{kh}.$$ It forces $m=kn$ because $q$ is a unit modulo $kh$. The cutoff becomes $|n|\le\lfloor hq/H\rfloor$, and the profile argument satisfies $Hm/(dq)=Hn/(hq)$. The summands are therefore identical.

###### ref-644e1a8ee223957f

###### ref-2a6b1a0d68216d04

###### ref-0ee9ca53d4798f10

> **Theorem: exact reduced-frequency regrouping** \[thm:regroup\] Define the common-source kernel $$\mathcal K(n)=\sum_{d\in\mathcal D_x}c_d\sum_{r\bmod d}B_d(r)\mathrm e(nr/d).  \label{eq:kernel}$$ Then, for every integer $n$, $$\mathcal K(n)=  \sum_{h\le U}\sum_{\substack{a\bmod h\\(a,h)=1}}  C_hB_h(a)\mathrm e(na/h).  \label{eq:regroup}$$

> **Proof** For a residue $r\bmod d$, let $g=(r,d)$ and put $h=d/g$, $a=r/g$. Then $r/d=a/h$ in lowest terms. If $r=0$, use $h=1$ and $a=0$. For nonzero $r$, the reduced numerator is coprime to $h$. The map from residues $r\bmod d$ to their reduced fractions partitions every row. Lemma [\[lem:dilation\]](TPC-217.md#ref-06e9c68283b6949a) identifies the row value in each divisor $d$ with $B_h(a)$, so the coefficients of the same reduced frequency add to $C_h$. The zero row is absent because $\lfloor q/H\rfloor=0$ for $q<H$.

# Finite-window large-sieve theorem

###### ref-9f929f8162d017f0

We use the standard additive large-sieve inequality in the following form [`\cite{MontgomeryVaughan1973}`](../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E5%8F%82%E8%80%83%E6%96%87%E7%8C%AE/TPC-217.md#cite-01c0e84a68b92cef). If $\alpha_j$ are separated modulo one by $\delta>0$, then for every consecutive interval $I$ of $N$ integers, $$\sum_{n\in I}\left|\sum_j z_j\mathrm e(n\alpha_j)\right|^2  \le (N-1+\delta^{-1})\sum_j|z_j|^2.  \label{eq:large-sieve}$$ The inequality follows from the dual Hilbert-inequality proof of the additive large sieve; no arithmetic property of the coefficients is required here.

###### ref-7d987b974c0758bd

> **Lemma: Farey spacing** \[lem:spacing\] Distinct reduced fractions $a/h$ and $a'/h'$ with $h,h'\le U$ satisfy $$\|\!\|\!\|(a/h,a'/h')\ge U^{-2},$$ where the distance is taken modulo one.

> **Proof** The difference, or its complement modulo one, is a nonzero rational with denominator dividing $hh'$. Its numerator is a nonzero integer, so its absolute value is at least $(hh')^{-1}\ge U^{-2}$.

###### ref-306824973dec2056

###### ref-a4b06937e1df64b7

###### ref-266ca40d57a54711

> **Theorem: finite-window attachment** \[thm:finite\] Let $I_x=(x/2,x]\cap\mathbb Z$ and $N=|I_x|$. Then $$\sum_{n\in I_x}|\mathcal K(n)|^2  \ll_{\psi}(N+U^2)x^{11/32}(\log x)^5.  \label{eq:finite-bound}$$ In particular, $$\frac1N\sum_{n\in I_x}|\mathcal K(n)|^2  \ll_{\psi}x^{11/32}(\log x)^5,  \label{eq:normalized-bound}$$ and the unnormalized exponent is $43/32+o(1)$.

###### ref-46ada71f8fbfebe6

> **Proof** Insert the exact representation [\[eq:regroup\]](TPC-217.md#ref-0ee9ca53d4798f10) into [\[eq:large-sieve\]](TPC-217.md#ref-9f929f8162d017f0). Lemma [\[lem:spacing\]](TPC-217.md#ref-7d987b974c0758bd) permits $\delta=U^{-2}$, giving $$\begin{aligned}  \sum_{n\in I_x}|\mathcal K(n)|^2  &\le (N+U^2)  \sum_h\sum_{(a,h)=1}|C_hB_h(a)|^2\\  &\le (N+U^2)A_x\frac{\mathcal E_{\mathrm{dir}}}{L}.  \label{eq:chain}\end{aligned}$$ Use $A_x=O((\log x)^2)$ from [\[eq:tpc215\]](TPC-217.md#ref-744bcb968e7c09cc) and [\[eq:tpc216\]](TPC-217.md#ref-af9266a24ca298d8) to obtain [\[eq:finite-bound\]](TPC-217.md#ref-a4b06937e1df64b7). Finally, $U^2/x=x^{-67/200}$ and $N\asymp x$, so division by $N$ gives [\[eq:normalized-bound\]](TPC-217.md#ref-266ca40d57a54711). The unnormalized power is $1+11/32=43/32$.

> **Remark: scope** The proof controls the finite-window off-frequency Gram by spacing. It does not use cancellation in $\mu(d)$, cancellation among primes, or the signed four-packet reassembly. The result is consequently structural and does not pay the arithmetic Gate-B ledger.

# Finite certificates and the crowding obstruction

The reproducible finite fixture uses $Q=\{11,13,17\}$, $H=40$, the full squarefree family in $(2,35]$, and $\psi(t)=(1+t^2)^{-2}$. It contains 14 active divisors and 16 reduced denominators. The direct divisor expansion and the reduced-frequency expansion agree pointwise to floating-point rounding in three translated windows of lengths 1, 17, and 60. The statement is scoped: it does not contradict the long-interval large sieve and is not an asymptotic lower bound for the V46 prime shell.

# Route evaluation and open gates

TPC-217 advances the route from a complete-period envelope to a physical-window attachment. The state is $$\begin{aligned}  &\texttt{TPC217\_STRUCTURAL\_THRESHOLD\_A = PASS},\\  &\texttt{TPC217\_FINITE\_WINDOW\_ATTACHMENT    = PROVED\_X\_11\_OVER\_32\_LOG\_FIVE\_NORMALIZED},\\  &\texttt{TPC217\_ARITHMETIC\_ADVANCE = NO},\\  &\texttt{TPC217\_PRIME\_SHELL\_REASSEMBLY = OPEN},\\  &\texttt{TPC217\_FULL\_GATE\_B = OPEN}.\end{aligned}$$ The next theorem must preserve this finite-window control while restoring the literal prime-only shell, four-packet signs, and the remaining Euler and zero/nonunit interfaces. # Conclusion

The common-source V46 kernel admits an exact reduced-frequency representation on every integer, not only on a complete period. Farey spacing and the additive large sieve then attach the TPC-216 direct-energy envelope to the physical interval with normalized exponent $11/32$ and five logarithmic factors. The $U^2$ spacing term is lower order at the frozen source scales. The one-point aligned-shell fixture shows why this attachment must use a length-dependent large-sieve argument rather than free orthogonality. The remaining work is arithmetic: reassemble the prime shell and four-packet signed scalar without losing the literal Gate-B interface.
