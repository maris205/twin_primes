## Source qualifications and counterexamples

TPC305’s [alignment proof](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-8d13008cc4c5a701) says that flipping a parent flips u and the aligned neighboring label, leaving aligned products invariant. For `u!=0` the alignment sign flips, and the claimed full-vector gauge relation follows. At `u=0`, the prescribed tie sign stays +1 after the flip. Consider an overlap of two coordinates, one exclusive coordinate on each side, and native labels `a_L=(1,1;1)`, `a_R=(1,-1;1)`, where the semicolon separates each exclusive part. Initially the transported vectors are `t_L=(1,-1;1)`, `t_R=(1,1;1)`. After negating a\_L alone, they are `t_L=(1,-1;-1)`, `t_R=(-1,-1;1)`. Neither new transported vector is a global sign multiple of its old version. The overlap mismatch count remains one and the maximum overlap inner product remains zero; those weaker statements survive. This is a missing tie qualification in the general proposition, not a claim to have found a failing published physical cell. The original proposition and deterministic protocol remain preserved.

TPC306’s [definition of q](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-987076f3d5d1f463) and its scaling proposition need the extra condition `m!=0`. Positive cells do not supply it: `(B_LL,B_LR,B_RL,B_RR)=(1,2,2,1)` gives `d_L=log 2`, `d_R=-log 2`, `m=0`, and `i=log 2`. The exact contrast identity and strict interaction dominance hold, but q is undefined as a real quotient. This example does not invalidate the separately reported finite q values; their denominators were not replayed here.

TPC307’s [proof-package sign argument](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-c1c53057f5389eb5) correctly pairs optimal coefficients under negation but does not justify an arbitrary minimizer selection having the same holdout loss. For the general PSD assumptions written there, take `M=diag(1,0)`, overlap row `V_O=(1,0)`, holdout row `V_E=(0,1)`, target `b=1`, and tolerance zero. Every `(1,t)` minimizes the cost. Selecting `(1,1)` with holdout target `h=1` gives loss zero. For `b=-1,h=-1`, selecting `(-1,1)` is also optimal but has holdout loss four; selecting the paired `(-1,-1)` instead gives zero. Thus the general holdout assertion needs paired selection, unique predictions, or additional source/response compatibility. A positive-definite metric and a nonempty feasible convex set are one sufficient uniqueness route. This toy example addresses the abstract PSD statement, not the rank of the published profile systems or their solver.

TPC309’s [prefix prose](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-6c9763c818df1c1b) compares a “least-squares residual” to `tau^2*||t||^2`; this must mean the squared residual. The inspected [call site](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-9d5e1904c8b40851) uses the parent [prefix routine](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-3f27683f4b90817f), which computes `sqrt(residual_squared/target_norm_squared)` and compares it to tau plus a declared numerical threshold tolerance. Its [least-squares routine](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-49efcb4ada4f2166) returns the squared residual. These are consistent after that interpretation; the finite tolerance is not exact boundary certification.

The [proof-package phrase](../../99_%E7%BB%B4%E6%8A%A4/%E6%BA%90%E5%AE%9A%E4%BD%8D/DOC-ee02e4a2adf0d5d2.md#src-d2b99eef7a4d95e6) “up to the row rank” must not be treated as an identity between prefix length and rank.
