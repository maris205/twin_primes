# Claim firewall

-   Exact full `C_4` Fourier spectrum of the four Hilbert energies.
-   Exact annihilation of a genuinely common additive scalar in every nontrivial phase character, including `F_1`.
-   Exact sharp disk of possible `F_1` values at fixed `F_0=S`.
-   Exact defect identity and boundary equality characterization.
-   Source-typed zero direct transfer from TPC-241 to literal V59 `F_1`.

## Forbidden promotions

-   Do not identify the standalone TPC-241 common-profile kernel with `T beta`, `T w`, or a V59 marginal.
-   Do not call the TPC-241 floor a common additive scalar in all four physical energies without an equality proving that statement.
-   Do not infer that the physical top-prime mode vanishes, is annihilated, or cancels.
-   Do not claim a signed `C_h` theorem, arithmetic `L2`, fixed-atom credit, strict `1/400`, full Gate B, or a twin-prime result.
-   Do not present the finite Gaussian-rational fixtures or bounded census as asymptotic or arithmetic evidence.
