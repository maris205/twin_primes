# Route evaluation

## Strongest positive result

The complete four-mode spectrum, the sharp fixed-total-energy disk for the selected cross coefficient, and the exact imbalance/Gram defect decomposition are proved in the literal V59 phase convention.

## Strongest obstruction

Even exact knowledge of the trivial-character energy `F_0=S` permits every `F_1` in the disk `|F_1|<=S/2`. It supplies no positive lower bound, phase, sign, or nonvanishing statement for the selected mode. A lower bound on a different unsigned object supplies still less.

## First blocker

There is no source-backed phase-by-phase identification of the TPC-241 top-prime common-profile contribution inside the four literal V59 packet energies. Therefore no actual `k=1` top-prime coefficient can yet be computed.

## Narrowest next action

Express the literal top-prime contribution separately for each packet `beta+i^j w` before taking an absolute square, prove the attachment to the V59 remainder, and then compute its actual `k=1` Fourier coefficient. Until that step, survival, attenuation, cancellation, and vanishing all remain open.
