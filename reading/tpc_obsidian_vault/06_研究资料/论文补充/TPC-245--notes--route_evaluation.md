# TPC-245 route evaluation

## Strongest positive result

The local covariance feasible set is classified exactly: a closed disk in transverse dimension at least two, a circle or singleton in dimension one, and a singleton or empty set in dimension zero. Exact cancellation margins and a sharp phase cone follow.

## Strongest obstruction

One transverse direction cannot realize disk interior. More importantly, the committed physical chain supplies neither a literal V59 two-lane attachment nor a source-native one-dimensional block direction. TPC-219’s similarly named decomposition is a different constant-`q` subspace object.

## Open theorem

Construct the literal V59 coefficient lanes in one common primitive synthesis space, define a source-native block projection, and prove payable longitudinal moments and transverse energies.
