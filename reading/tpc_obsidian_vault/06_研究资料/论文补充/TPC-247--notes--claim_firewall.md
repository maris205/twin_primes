# Claim firewall

The exact covariance is a physical-index identity only. It is forbidden to promote it to:

-   a primitive-rational coefficient attachment;
-   a factorization `A_x=T^*T` or any PSD statement;
-   TPC-243 near-isometry or a paid bilinear error;
-   TPC-244 common-multiplier phase blindness;
-   TPC-246 Cartesian product realizability;
-   arithmetic cancellation, `L2`, fixed-atom credit, strict `1/400`, full Gate B, or a twin-prime result.

The exact maximum is `PROVED_STRUCTURAL_L1_LITERAL_V59_SOURCE_OPERATOR_ATTACHMENT_WITH_NORM_OBSTRUCTION`.
