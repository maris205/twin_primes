# Claim Firewall

Maximum supported claim:

```text
PROVED_STRUCTURAL_L1_COHERENCE_CONTROLLED_GRAM_QUADRATIC_SHARPNESS
```

Mandatory boundary:

```text
TPC250_ACTUAL_V59_COHERENCE_ASYMPTOTIC=OPEN
TPC250_ARITHMETIC_ADVANCE=NO
TPC250_FIXED_ATOM_CREDIT=0
TPC250_L2=NONE
TPC250_FULL_GATE_B=OPEN
TPC250_FULL_GATE_B_STRICT_1_OVER_400=UNPAID_GLOBAL
TPC250_TWIN_PRIME_RESULT=NONE
```

Allowed claims are finite Hilbert-space Gram expansion, coherence envelopes, `D>0` normalization, noncancellation certification, transfer to the exact TPC-249 radii, and universal sharpness of the displayed coefficients and nonnegative floor. Forbidden claims include actual V59 coherence or Gram asymptotics, arithmetic saving, Route A progress, fixed-atom credit, Gate-B closure, or any twin-prime conclusion.
