The phrase “full-output norm floor” refers to `||P_Z A_x beta||` or `||P_T A_x beta||`, both finite projections. It must not be read as a bound on `||A_x beta||` from above. The transverse lower floor is compatible with arbitrarily large additional orthogonal energy.

The asymptotic is complex coefficient-wise. Only norms are taken after the coefficient asymptotics; no scalar reality or principal-argument claim is introduced.
