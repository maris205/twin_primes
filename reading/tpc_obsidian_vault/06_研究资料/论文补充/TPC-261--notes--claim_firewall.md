# TPC-261 claim firewall

```text
PROVED = exact baseline-to-target identity; finite-lane budget theorem;
         strict 1/400 threshold; log/power separation; scaled structural witness
CONDITIONAL_THEOREM = the finite-lane conclusion under its displayed lane bounds
NUMERICALLY_CERTIFIED = exact rational fixture, stress grid, and mutation audit
REFUTED_SCOPED = automatic promotion from current packet marginals/Haar/null data
OPEN = literal common-clock mode-zero or signed cross-Gram estimate
NOT_CLAIMED = growing prime-shell counterexample; arithmetic L2; fixed atom;
              full Gate B; strict global endpoint credit; twin-prime theorem
```

The phrase “global fixed-power credit = NONE” means that no such credit has been proved by the current interface. It is not an impossibility theorem for the literal prime shell. The scaled witness is a finite synthetic family and must not be relabelled as prime data.
