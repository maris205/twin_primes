# TPC-283 proof package

## Exact theorem

In a real or complex Hilbert space, the Riesz representative of the evaluation functional at a nonzero `S` is `S`. The orthogonal decomposition `w = (C/Y)S + w_perp` has `w_perp` in `H_S`. Thus the nearest zero-attached source is `w_perp`, and the distance and uniqueness formulas in the derivation package follow. No compactness or finite-dimensional assumption is needed.

## Finite transfer

The parent certificate supplies outward intervals for `C^2/(WY)`. The checks verify positive lower endpoints, upper endpoints below `9/100` on every row, and upper endpoints below `1/100` on six rows. The parent hash is part of the certificate envelope. Since the adversary is allowed to move in the full projected Hilbert space, the transfer is explicitly marked `INFORMATION_MODEL_ONLY`.
