# TPC-285 claim firewall

The centered factorization and deleted-diagonal full-rank conclusion are exact linear algebra. The kernel-Schur rank result is a finite exact modular certificate on 20 registered rows. Neither statement bounds an operator norm or a signed source output.

Full rank is an obstruction to a low-rank shortcut, not an obstruction to all cancellation. Cross-prime cancellation, singular-value decay, source orthogonality, and arithmetic `L2` remain open possibilities.
