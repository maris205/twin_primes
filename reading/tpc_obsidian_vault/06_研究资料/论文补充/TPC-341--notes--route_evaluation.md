# TPC-341 route evaluation

The available local evidence is the exact finite projection identity, a parent-locked fresh-window replay, an independent reverse-shell checker, leave-one-control-out stress, PDF audit, and a fail-closed Bridge-B wrapper.

The Session-named `propose.md`, `skills/route-a-evaluator.md`, and `skills/route-b-evaluator.md` files are absent:

```text
Route-A = NOT_EVALUATED_OFFICIAL
Route-B = LOCAL_FAIL_CLOSED_FALLBACK
ARITHMETIC_ADVANCE = NO
FIXED_POWER_CREDIT = 0
FULL_GATE_B = OPEN
```

The finite positive signal is that an aggregate nuisance projection removes most twin-mean energy. The stronger negative result is that this removal does not transfer to any of the 27 held-out controls under the declared guard.
