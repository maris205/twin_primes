# TPC-347 claim firewall

-   The Fourier multiplier belongs to the explicitly unmasked convolution `K_e`; it is not silently identified with the physical masked matrix.
-   The finite defect ratios are numerical observations on the declared origins/counts and do not imply a growing lower bound.
-   The Young envelope is a sign-free baseline and supplies no cancellation or negative power of the main variable.
-   No source-native arithmetic vector is inserted in this release.
-   `ARITHMETIC_ADVANCE=NO`, `FIXED_POWER_CREDIT=0`, `FULL_GATE_B=OPEN`, and `TWIN_PRIME_RESULT=NONE` are locked claim ceilings.
