# TPC-348 claim firewall

-   `W_I(D)` is a maximum over the predeclared mask-hit coordinate set; it is not an eigenvector fit and does not create an asymptotic theorem.
-   The column-norm inequality is exact finite-dimensional linear algebra. The reported ratios are numerical observations on the frozen 192-row grid.
-   The position formula retains both left and right projection defects; omitting either term is a tested mutation and is rejected.
-   No source-native arithmetic vector, cancellation estimate, fixed-power saving, Route-B reassembly, or twin-prime conclusion is introduced.
-   `ARITHMETIC_ADVANCE=NO`, `FIXED_POWER_CREDIT=0`, `FULL_GATE_B=OPEN`, and `TWIN_PRIME_RESULT=NONE` are locked ceilings.
