# TPC-383 proof package

## Finite proposition

For the predeclared affine panel, the local-diagonal and pooled-scalar constructions use one common raw geometry per origin/Q and one common c=1 mask. The certificate contains the complete 72-row Cartesian panel. Its reported spectra, pooled scalars, and origin-spread cells are reproduced by a direct reverse-shell checker.

## Checks

The producer and independent checker pass in normal and optimized modes. A 25-mutation adversarial suite rejects altered protocol, row, phase, firewall, and clue fields. The exact q=8 anchor is checked with rational arithmetic.

## Boundary

The proposition is finite and model-relative. It does not prove that a pooled or local normalization is source-valid, does not establish growing origin or scale uniformity, and does not close either route to the twin-prime endpoint.
