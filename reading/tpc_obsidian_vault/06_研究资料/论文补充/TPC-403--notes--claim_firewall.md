# Claim firewall

```text
CRT_PROXY_OBSTRUCTION = PROVED_EXACT_FINITE
CRT_ORIGIN_EXISTENCE_ABOVE_BOUND = PROVED_FOR_DECLARED_FINITE_PRIME_SET
ARITHMETIC_SIGN_IDENTIFICATION = OPEN
NORMALIZED_GROWING_OBSTRUCTION = OPEN
ARITHMETIC_ADVANCE = NO
FIXED_POWER_CREDIT = 0
FULL_GATE_B = OPEN
TWIN_PRIME_RESULT = NONE
```

The four cases are a finite exact certificate. They are not evidence that a predeclared bounded origin interval contains a CRT origin, and they do not transfer a synthetic sign law to the arithmetic source.
