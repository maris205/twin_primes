# TPC-411 derivation package

The two previously audited odd shells can be pooled because their prime sets are disjoint and every prime remains larger than `N=264`. The pooled cardinality is even, restoring equal parity counts `8229/8229`. Amplitudes retain their shell-local normalization `Q_i`; the proof only uses positivity and `a_i>1`, so the Cauchy–Schwarz cancellation is unchanged.
