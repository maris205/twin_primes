# TPC-416 derivation package

The fourth complete shell is disjoint and every retained prime is larger than `N=264`. Pooling preserves the exact local identities. Its odd total count is handled explicitly by the two parity sizes `37741/37742`; the Cauchy–Schwarz proof only needs the minus count and therefore still yields `z<=4/H`.
