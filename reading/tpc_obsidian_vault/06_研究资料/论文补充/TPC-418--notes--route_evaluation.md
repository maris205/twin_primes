# Route evaluation

TPC-418 is a finite-family extension of the TPC417 full finite matrix bound. The corrected shell sign `sigma_j` closes the scalar envelope needed by the endpoint-star/interior-bulk proof. It does not create a growing theorem: control of `K/m_-`, physical coefficient identification, arithmetic sign/L2 credit, Route-B, and twin-prime implications remain absent.
