The logarithmic two-point Chowla conjecture was proved by Tao and improved by Helfgott-Radziwill and Pilatte through a dilation identity and a spectral bound for a divisibility operator. On the generalised Elliott-Halberstam conjecture (GEH), Bombieri's asymptotic sieve turns the twin prime conjecture into a Chowla-type statement on almost primes, and Tao remarked that this restriction destroys the multiplicativity the Chowla arguments use. We make this a theorem: for a class of dilation-spectral certificates containing those of Helfgott-Radziwill and Pilatte adapted to the shift 2, with arbitrary dilators up to x^kappa, the resolution on twin-type parity is >> eta min(1, eta/kappa) log x, where x^eta is the roughness level. To state what such a certificate must resolve, we write the Bombieri-Tao reformulation as an exact identity in terms of a logarithmic parity defect of the Liouville function on almost-prime pairs, and show on GEH that the joint parity of rough pairs is governed by the twin parameter alone, so a single class of rough pairs suffices. Along the family g_t interpolating between Chowla and twin parity, these certificates cannot reach damping |t|<0.676, with any weights and in any l^p norm. Reading the Polymath8b proof of H_1<=6 in this language, GEH plus one comparison between the masses of twin, gap-4 and gap-6 pairs implies the twin prime conjecture. Finally we locate the barrier along the shift coordinate: on GEH the gaps occurring infinitely often have density at least 1/6, which is sharp for sieve methods. A census of parity-breaking results shows each ends in a bilinear form whose cancellation comes from structure in its coefficient sequence; for twins this is lambda(n+2), and we prove that every argument using only absolute values of Dirichlet polynomials stays a constant factor from the trivial bound. Nothing here proves or disproves the twin prime conjecture.
