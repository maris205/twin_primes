## What this edition specifies, rather than claims as deployed

- A unified claim record combining source identity/span/digest, evidence category, exact object/hypotheses, transfer requirements, and unresolved obligation. Existing cards contain many of these elements in metadata and prose, but the complete schema is not machine-enforced throughout the archive.
- A bounded AI task package, evidence-bearing output, scoped review, disagreement retention, and human-owned handoff.
- Controlled AI evaluation and selective formalization: [plan](refine-logs/EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md), [tracker](refine-logs/EXPERIMENT_TRACKER.md). All runs remain NOT_RUN.
- No fully automated research manager, formal proof graph, demonstrated hallucination reduction, evaluated autonomous prover, or whole-archive mathematical certification.
