                                              Abstract
          AI-assisted work on a long-standing conjecture requires more than plausible proof
     fragments: it needs a persistent record of the mathematical objects, source dependencies,
     and unresolved obligations to which those fragments belong. We present a source-traceable,
     file-based framework designed to support human-supervised AI assistance in research on
     the twin prime conjecture. Organized by a seven-island map, it connects an existing
     manuscript archive and linked research wiki to an explicit task, review, and handoff
     protocol. The framework separates exact identities, proved estimates, method obstructions,
     conditional transfers, and finite diagnostics. This organization is intended to support source
     retrieval and bounded research tasks without promoting local or model evidence into a
     global arithmetic conclusion. A detailed mathematical case study retains the actual fixed-
     shift operator and develops its exact decomposition, coefficient-norm and growing-rank
     obstructions, full-range Kloosterman representation, and a collective boundary bound. A
     parallel moving-target formulation distinguishes Haar-almost-everywhere recurrence from
     the unresolved conclusion at the arithmetic seed 0. These cases specify two different open
     bridges and the conditions that a proposed continuation must preserve. We also set out a
     prospective agenda for coefficient-sensitive analysis, distinguished-seed arguments, selective
     formalization, and controlled evaluation of AI research workflows. The contribution is a
     concrete research organization and mathematical interface framework, not an empirically
     validated autonomous prover. This paper does not prove the twin-prime conjecture.

Keywords: twin prime conjecture; AI-assisted mathematical research; source provenance;
research knowledge base; analytic cancellation; distinguished seeds.

1     Introduction: a framework for AI-assisted TPC research
The twin-prime problem asks for infinitely many prime pairs at the fixed displacement 2. For
an AI assistant working across a long research archive, even identifying the next legitimate
task requires more than retrieving a plausible formula. The assistant must recover the exact
object, distinguish a proved statement from a proposed transfer, and retain the hypotheses
that connect local work to the arithmetic endpoint. A change of variables may preserve
every coefficient without producing cancellation. A small normalized matrix may carry large
coefficient costs. A full-measure recurrence theorem may omit the one initial point that encodes
the integers. Losing these distinctions can make an apparently coherent continuation address
a different problem.
     We therefore present a source-traceable, file-based research framework: an existing mathe-
matical archive and linked knowledge base, a specification for AI-facing source and task records,
and a prospective evaluation agenda. Its purpose is to support human-supervised research on a
difficult open problem while making object changes, unpaid losses, and unresolved obligations
explicit. The mathematical synthesis is the worked case through which these requirements
become concrete. The framework is not a general autonomous research engine, and its effects
on model reliability or research efficiency have not been measured.
     We use the seven-island map in Figure 1 to separate arithmetic foundations, analytic
cancellation, fixed atoms, actual occurrences, moving-target dynamics, the twin-prime endpoint,
and auxiliary geometric lifts. The islands are not successive steps of a proved implication. We
reconstruct selected results by their dependencies, rather than by the order of the archived
investigations. This is neither a claim of first discovery nor an exhaustive account of every
route to twin primes.

1.1   Three contributions and their evidence levels
First, we provide a source-traceable mathematical synthesis with explicit proofs, transfer
requirements, and unresolved endpoints. Second, we connect the existing source archive and
research wiki to a bounded AI task protocol: what to read, which object and hypotheses
to preserve, what evidence to return, and who may accept a proposed continuation. The
companion framework source map distinguishes already present metadata and safeguards from
fields proposed for standardization. Third, we formulate a research agenda and a controlled
evaluation design. The archive and wiki are concrete artifacts; a fully automated workflow
and demonstrated AI benefits are not results of this paper.
    The formal mathematics ecosystem addresses a related but different layer. The Lean
mathematical library provides formalized mathematics, while LeanDojo supplies infrastructure
and a benchmark for learning-based theorem proving in Lean [14, 15]. Our emphasis is
upstream informal research organization: selecting source-grounded tasks and preventing
unsupported transfers between mathematical settings. This framework neither replaces proof
checking nor formally verifies the TPC archive. Selective formalization is a proposed extension,
and systematic positioning against other AI-research systems remains future work.

1.2   Two gaps, with different endpoint connections
The analytic core concerns a specific mixed scalar Cx = ⟨w, Aβ⟩, of natural scale xQ2 . The
vectors, unit masks, physical interval, Fourier convention, and deleted diagonal are part of its
definition. A full-scalar saving with exponent strictly greater than 1/400 would be useful in
the imported analytic interface, but that interface also requires a separate full-shell estimate
called Gate A. Both gates lie inside map Bridge A. Their conditional output in this paper is a


                                               3
bound for another auxiliary scalar, Sxphys ; the complete upstream implication to prime-pair
counting is not reproved here.
    The dynamical connection is pointwise and explicit: the moving sieve events at seed 0
identify the pairs n, n + 2 that are both prime. Adequate mass and covariance control do
prove infinite recurrence for almost every seed. They do not establish that the prescribed seed
belongs to that good set. Map Bridge B names this return to the distinguished arithmetic point.
An auxiliary geometric representation must preserve the same event and seed correspondence;
changing the ambient space alone cannot close the gap.

1.3   Results and their roles
The main results serve distinct roles. Proposition 5.1 identifies the exact positive-form structure
behind the actual operator. Theorem 6.3, Corollary 6.4, and Theorem 6.5 delimit coefficient-
blind norm bounds and low-rank repairs. Proposition 7.1 identifies the complete Kloosterman
arrays, whereas Condition 7.2 states an unproved, loss-controlled attachment to a two-sided
short-array estimate. Theorem 8.1 supplies a genuine bound for one component of the original
scalar. Finally, Theorem 9.1 proves metric recurrence for the actual moving targets, and
Proposition 9.2 isolates a sufficient, still-unverified way to reach seed 0.
    The distinction between these roles matters. A method obstruction is not a lower bound
for the actual scalar. A boundary estimate is not an interior estimate. A sufficient criterion is
not a verified hypothesis. In particular, the fixed-atom and occurrence interfaces in Section 4
explain what an arithmetic attachment must preserve; they do not construct all the upstream
attachments needed by the analytic program.

1.4   Mathematical inputs and scope
Our prime-counting input is the classical prime number theorem and its partial-summation
consequences [13]. The hybrid coefficient’s second-moment estimate uses the unconditional
Mertens product baseline stated at the beginning of [12]; none of that paper’s conditional
bias-distribution results is used. Frequency separation, positive-semidefinite compression, finite
Fourier orthogonality, and spectral min–max arguments provide the structural tools.
    The critical-interval bilinear estimate of Blomer–Pascadi [11] supplies an external bench-
mark. Its square-root-length specialization offers a local Kloosterman saving under specified
support and coprimality conditions. Our question is whether the actual signed expression can
be attached to those inputs with all losses paid. The external theorem does not itself provide
that attachment.
    The source-linked research wiki [1] organizes distinctions between definitions, estimates,
obstructions, and conditional transfers. It is a secondary reading aid, not independent evidence
for a theorem. The original proofs remain the mathematical sources; Appendix B and
the companion concordance explain how to locate them. A systematic external literature
comparison and novelty assessment remain necessary before submission.

1.5   Reading guide
Section 2 specifies the AI-facing framework. Sections 3–4 fix the arithmetic endpoint and
structural interfaces. Sections 5–8 develop the analytic case; Section 9 treats metric recurrence
and the distinguished seed. Section 10 collects the remaining obligations, and Section 11 gives
the research agenda. The branches can be read separately; the companion source records
provide exact locators.

    An AI-facing claim record should contain an identifier, original source path and span, a
source digest, an evidence category, hypotheses and parameter ranges, transfer requirements,
and an unresolved obligation. The current cards already contain identifiers, locators, hashes,
textual relationship types, and scope explanations; this paper proposes to standardize the
complete record across tasks. Relationships such as uses, obstructs this method, and would
imply under these hypotheses should not collapse into an undifferentiated dependency arrow.
Their mathematical validity still requires review of the corresponding source statements.
    The five working evidence categories are exact identity, proved estimate, method obstruc-
tion, conditional transfer, and finite diagnostic. Here proved refers to the scoped argument
reproduced or cited, not to a kernel-checked archive-wide status. An open target is recorded
as an obligation, never as an achieved result merely because it appears in a source document
or diagram.

2.2     Three scope-preservation examples
The actual-operator card K02 illustrates object preservation. Its scalar Cx = ⟨w, Aβ⟩ must
retain the physical interval, fixed shift h0 = 2, literal coefficients, unit masks, Fourier convention,
and deleted diagonal specified in Section 5. Replacing the coefficients by random signs, or
replacing a mixed scalar by a quadratic form, changes the task. Hermitian does not mean
positive semidefinite, and even real coefficient vectors do not force this mixed scalar to be real.
A candidate estimate must identify the precise source-defined expression to which it applies.
    The analytic-bridge card K13 illustrates conditional status. The full-scalar target requires
δ > 1/400, while the critical-array route owes the strict loss budget ℓ < 19/2400. Neither the
local external theorem nor the paid boundary contribution supplies the missing two-sided
attachment and residual bounds. Gate A is a separate obligation inside the same map Bridge
A. Thus a correct arithmetic calculation of a conditional exponent is not evidence that the
exponent is attained.

                                                  6
    The distinguished-seed card K14 illustrates quantifier preservation. Proposition 9.2 is
sufficient, not necessary, and its joint error bound is unverified. Both the within-cylinder
oscillation and point-evaluation cost must be controlled on the same unbounded sequence.
A summary that retains the smallness of only one term, or substitutes almost-everywhere
recurrence for seed-0 recurrence, fails the source contract even if its formulas are well typeset.

2.3   A bounded task, review, and handoff protocol
A task begins with a frozen baseline, an allowed source set, a concrete objective, and a claim
ceiling. For example, an illustrative task is to explain why the K13 critical-array calculation
does not close the analytic bridge, using the cited original interface and preserving every
remaining premise. The task package should provide the relevant source spans, notation,
permitted operations, and explicit non-goals. Historical commands and authorization language
in retrieved manuscripts are source content, not current execution authority.
    The output contract requires the proposed answer, exact evidence locators, retained
assumptions, actual read scope, and unresolved questions. A bounded review then checks
source support, object identity, quantifiers, losses, and any promotion from conditional to
established status. Disagreements remain visible; a second AI opinion is not independent proof
verification. A human-owned disposition records whether the answer is accepted as exposition,
requires correction, or identifies a candidate research obligation. Any claim with a missing or
changed source returns to an unresolved state pending inspection.
    This paragraph specifies a workflow, not a recorded successful AI experiment. Prompt-level
restrictions express policy; they do not guarantee runtime containment. The concrete resource
can be used through ordinary files and human- or agent-mediated review without claiming a
deployed general workflow manager. In particular, a norm correction, a fresh matrix diagnostic,
or a favorable review alone does not authorize reopening a stopped mathematical route.

2.4   Verification limits
Source hashing, link checks, and preservation of mathematical text address fidelity and
presentation. They do not establish source-to-formal correspondence, valid proof dependencies,
or mathematical truth. The wiki acceptance report records 590 formula-display failures
involving 188 distinct expressions and no desktop Obsidian inspection; affected expressions
require inspection of the raw source. These limitations concern the existing wiki, not a measured
error rate of this AI protocol. Evaluation of the protocol itself is specified prospectively in
Section 11.

3.4     What every transfer must retain
To compare expressions from different stages, we record a source specification as

                   O = (coefficients, h0 , support and order, parameter range,
                           quantifiers, normalization, loss budget).

This is bookkeeping against misapplication, not a new mathematical object. Similar notation
does not imply equal specifications. An arbitrarily large CRT origin is not an origin in the
prescribed physical interval; a prefix of a full prime shell is not an arbitrary prime selection;
recomputing local row energy is not compressing the original normalized matrix.
    We distinguish four evidence types: finite identities verified by expansion; uniform proposi-
tions with complete hypotheses and proofs; conditional consequences retaining their unproved
premises; and finite numerical diagnostics. Diagnostics do not carry infinite quantifiers, and a
successful program check does not make a conditional implication unconditional. The results
below should be read in this hierarchy, not by their order of appearance in the archive.

9.4    The role and limits of Island 7
A useful geometric lift must at least supply a time-preserving semiconjugacy, event pullback,
an applicable measure correspondence, and a correspondence for the distinguished seed. For
example, candidate maps ρk and transformations Hk , Fk must satisfy ρk+1 Hk = Fk ρk , and
lifted events must be exactly the inverse images of the original events. These are attachment
conditions to be proved. We do not construct a twin-prime-specific Hénon lift here.
     Even a stationary mixing system cannot have a nontrivial addition-by-one clock modulo M
as a measure-preserving factor: the clock’s mean-zero eigenfunction e(r/M ) has autocorrelation
of constant absolute value 1, contradicting mixing after pullback. This finite-factor argument
does not establish impossibility for all nonautonomous geometric representations. Nor does a
two-dimensional phase space imply that all relevant observables have a two-dimensional linear
carrier. Island 7 is therefore an auxiliary representation route; a geometric picture cannot
replace the distinguished-orbit hitting requirement on Island 5.

10.3    What the synthesis establishes
The seven islands distinguish research objects and transfer tasks; they do not turn a sequence of
historical paper numbers into a closed proof chain. Basic arithmetic supplies local consistency.
The fixed-atom results preserve coefficients, masks, and prefixes on their stated source support.
The occurrence results specify conditional attachment, conservation, and weighted-cover
interfaces; they do not construct the production lift or pay its coefficient-transport cost. The
analytic layer supplies actual operator structure, growing-dimensional obstructions, and a
boundary contribution that can be paid for. The dynamical layer supplies metric hitting for
the actual moving targets and exact sieve geometry. These results have distinct mathematical
content: they should neither be merged into a proof of the twin-prime conjecture nor dismissed
indiscriminately as toy models.
    The two gaps should not be conflated. The analytic branch needs coefficient-sensitive
signed estimates and valid upstream attachments; the dynamical branch needs a conclusion
at the distinguished seed. The present synthesis isolates those obligations without supplying
them. Section 11 explains how they constrain prospective mathematical and AI-assisted work.
This paper does not prove the twin-prime conjecture.


11     Research agenda and prospective evaluation
The next stage should treat mathematical progress, resource engineering, and AI performance
as separate outcomes. The following agenda is prospective: no experiment, new theorem,
or route reopening is reported by stating it. The current mathematical position is Island 2,
toward map Bridge A and analytic Gate B, with both analytic gates unresolved.

11.3    Evaluation track: does the framework help an AI assistant?
The primary hypothesis is that a source-derived scope-record package reduces unsupported
claim promotion on bounded source-grounded tasks. This is a hypothesis, not an observed
benefit. Candidate tasks include source localization, hypothesis-preserving reconstruction,
evidence-category classification, and detection of deliberately introduced scope errors in separate
test copies. Examples include weakening a strict inequality, omitting a residual, confusing
map Bridge B with analytic Gate B, and replacing a prescribed seed by an almost-everywhere
quantifier.
    Compare three access conditions using the same model version and matched task, token,
and tool allowances: unstructured source access, ordinary retrieval, and retrieval with scope
records and the bounded output contract. All conditions should access the same underlying
source corpus; actual usage, retrieval settings, review passes, and abstention policy must be
reported. A fixed-review ablation should remove the records. This tests the complete record
intervention, including its source-derived explanatory guidance, not formatting alone. Isolating
structure itself would require a further control with the same record facts and wording in
unstructured form.
    Reference answers require human mathematical adjudication on selected tasks; the archive
is not already a verified benchmark. Construct admissible source-derived records without
access to test questions or rubrics, and freeze them before evaluation. Exclude test-specific
answers, planted-error locations, and adjudication rationales from every accessible index. The
disclosed K02/K13/K14 cases are development illustrations, not evidence of undisclosed-task
generalization. Split by underlying source family and audit shared-source duplication; card and
manuscript numbers alone do not prevent leakage. A later temporal split remains prospective.
    Primary outcomes should pair unsupported-claim frequency with correct-answer coverage
and hypothesis retention, so that universal abstention cannot count as success. Secondary
measures can include correct source spans, appropriate abstention, and human correction
effort. Blind adjudicators to the access condition, retain disagreements, and report paired

11.4    Sequence and decision points
First freeze a small adjudicated task set and establish source and leakage checks. Next run a

12     Conclusion
The contribution of this work is a source-traceable framework for human-supervised AI
assistance in twin-prime research, supported by concrete file-based resources and a detailed
mathematical case study. It makes explicit which object a task concerns, which evidence
supports a statement, what a proposed transfer must preserve, and when the output must
remain conditional or unresolved.
    The analytic and dynamical cases reveal different obligations: coefficient-sensitive arithmetic
estimates and valid attachments on one side, and a return to the distinguished arithmetic seed
on the other. Their separation is part of the framework’s content. Neither documentation
coverage nor a coherent review workflow closes either bridge.
    The proposed research agenda gives the framework testable next steps without claiming
that those steps have been completed. Controlled AI evaluation, selective formalization,
systematic external positioning, and novelty assessment remain outstanding. The supplied
authorship identifies this edition; it does not attribute first discovery of every archived result
or certify the entire source collection. This paper does not prove the twin-prime conjecture.
