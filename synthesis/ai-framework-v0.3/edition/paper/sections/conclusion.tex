\section{Conclusion}
The contribution of this work is a source-traceable framework for human-supervised AI assistance in twin-prime research, supported by concrete file-based resources and a detailed mathematical case study. It makes explicit which object a task concerns, which evidence supports a statement, what a proposed transfer must preserve, and when the output must remain conditional or unresolved.

The analytic and dynamical cases reveal different obligations: coefficient-sensitive arithmetic estimates and valid attachments on one side, and a return to the distinguished arithmetic seed on the other. Their separation is part of the framework's content. Neither documentation coverage nor a coherent review workflow closes either bridge.

The proposed research agenda gives the framework testable next steps without claiming that those steps have been completed. Controlled AI evaluation, selective formalization, systematic external positioning, and novelty assessment remain outstanding. The supplied authorship identifies this edition; it does not attribute first discovery of every archived result or certify the entire source collection. This paper does not prove the twin-prime conjecture.
