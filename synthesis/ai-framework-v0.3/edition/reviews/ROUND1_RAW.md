## R1 baseline audit

The proposed title—**“A Source-Traceable Framework for AI-Assisted Research on the Twin Prime Conjecture”**—is supportable if “framework” means an existing file-based research resource plus an explicitly specified, human-supervised AI workflow. The inspected evidence does **not** establish an evaluated AI research system, automated mathematical verification, or improved research outcomes.

The substantive contribution should be the connection between source provenance, mathematical scope preservation, and bounded research tasks—not merely adding AI terminology to the existing synthesis.

### Six actionable recommendations

1. **Make the title, abstract, and introduction promise the same bounded contribution.**

   A suitable abstract opening is:

   > We present a source-traceable, file-based framework designed to support human-supervised AI assistance in research on the twin prime conjecture. The framework connects an existing mathematical synthesis and source-linked knowledge base to a bounded task, review, and handoff protocol.

   Follow this with concrete mathematical examples and an explicit separation between existing resources and prospective evaluation. Retain “This paper does not prove the twin-prime conjecture.”

   In the introduction, identify three deliverables: the source-traceable mathematical synthesis, the AI-facing information/task contract, and the prospective evaluation agenda. Describe the framework as **designed to support** source-grounded work, not demonstrated to make AI reliable or accelerate discovery. Preserve the baseline’s acknowledgment that systematic external positioning and novelty assessment remain outstanding. [Baseline introduction]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/tpc_english_revision/paper/sections/introduction.tex:16)

2. **Use K02, K13, and K14 as worked examples of scope preservation—not just screenshots or inventory counts.**

   These cards already supply an unusually concrete basis for the AI-facing section:

   - **K02:** the same operator definition must retain the physical interval, fixed \(h_0=2\), coefficients, masks, Fourier convention, and deleted diagonal. Hermitian does not mean positive semidefinite, and a mixed scalar between real vectors can still be complex.
   - **K13:** a source-linked summary must preserve both analytic gates and strict inequalities; it cannot promote a conditional loss calculation into an established saving.
   - **K14:** a sufficient return criterion must remain sufficient and unverified, with both error terms retained.

   Present a compact record specification: claim/object ID, source path and span, digest, evidence status, required hypotheses, transfer requirements, and unresolved obligation. **Distinguish fields already present from fields the new framework proposes to standardize.** The inspected cards contain metadata, textual relationship types, scope warnings, and source hashes; they do not establish a fully machine-enforced theorem dependency graph.

   These three examples would make the approximately two-page framework section substantive without adding mathematics. [K02]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/tpc_obsidian_vault/04_知识卡片/K02-实际物理标量与算子.md:17), [K13]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/tpc_obsidian_vault/04_知识卡片/K13-解析桥的两道Gate与严格损失账本.md:17), [K14]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/tpc_obsidian_vault/04_知识卡片/K14-指定种子0的柱集回接准则.md:17)

3. **Specify the bounded AI protocol operationally, while distinguishing policy from enforcement.**

   Give one illustrative task package with:

   - An exact baseline, read allowlist, source spans, objective, and claim ceiling.
   - An output contract requiring evidence locators, retained hypotheses, actual read scope, and unresolved questions.
   - A review step checking source support and scope changes, with disagreements retained.
   - A human-owned disposition and handoff; missing or changed sources prevent claim promotion.

   A useful worked task is: “Explain why the K13 critical-array calculation does not close the analytic bridge, citing the original interface and every remaining premise.” Label any displayed answer as an **illustrative specification**, not a recorded successful experiment.

   Preserve the existing distinction between navigation links and mathematical dependencies. Also carry forward the archive rule that historical commands and authorization language are source content, not current execution authority. Do not describe prompt instructions as guaranteed runtime containment, or a second AI review as independent proof verification. [Repository protocol]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/twin_primes/AGENTS.md), [Vault usage boundaries]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/tpc_obsidian_vault/99_维护/使用与更新说明.md:8)

4. **Organize the mathematical agenda by the seven-island map and exact open obligations.**

   For **Island 2 / Bridge A**, state the signed interior estimate or paid two-sided attachment/residual problem, preserving
   \(\delta>1/400\), and, for the critical-array route specifically, \(\ell<19/2400\). Gate A remains separate. The boundary contribution is already bounded, but does not supply the interior estimate or the compression residual. Likewise, the structural interfaces on Islands 3–4 do not establish the missing production attachment.

   Keep the conditional analytic output distinct from prime-pair counting: the baseline explicitly says \(S_x^{\rm phys}\neq S_2(x)\) and does not reconstruct the complete upstream implication to TPC. Avoid “only one estimate remains to prove TPC.” [Analytic bridge]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/tpc_english_revision/paper/sections/bridges.tex:21)

   For **Island 5 / Bridge B**, preserve the joint cylinder bound on the **same unbounded sequence**, with fixed positive margin. Small point-evaluation cost alone, or zero oscillation on an excessively fine cylinder, is insufficient. The criterion is one sufficient route, not a necessary characterization. Island 7 remains an auxiliary representation route requiring exact event/time/seed attachment. [Dynamics]( /root/autodl-tmp/math_research3/tpc_english_revision/paper/sections/dynamics.tex:63)

   Present these as prospective obligations, not permission to restart stopped methods.

5. **Make the AI evaluation agenda testable without treating the archive as a verified benchmark.**

   Propose bounded tasks such as source localization, hypothesis retention, conditional-versus-established classification, and detection of deliberately introduced scope errors in separate test copies. Useful errors come directly from the examples above: changing a strict inequality, dropping a residual, confusing the two bridge names, or replacing almost-everywhere recurrence by seed-\(0\) recurrence.

   Define prospective comparisons under matched model/access budgets—for example, unstructured source access, ordinary retrieval, and retrieval with structured scope records. Possible outcomes include source-span correctness, unsupported-claim rate, hypothesis preservation, appropriate abstention, and human correction effort. These are **measurements to conduct**, not benefits already obtained.

   Split tasks by underlying source family, not merely card or paper number: cards and the synthesis repeat shared sources. Human-adjudicated reference answers should cover selected tasks; 419 mechanically converted manuscripts are not 419 independently verified benchmark items.

   Treat formal proof checking as a separate future extension requiring selected formal statements, explicit assumptions, a checked source-to-formal correspondence, and kernel-checked proofs. Hash matching, TeX preservation, and formula rendering do not establish any of these. The vault report itself records 590 formula-display failures involving 188 distinct expressions and no desktop Obsidian test.
