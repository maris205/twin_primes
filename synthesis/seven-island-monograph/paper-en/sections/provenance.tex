\section{Sources, deduplication, and the scope of verification}\label{sec:provenance}
This paper selects core original sources from a standalone repository containing 420 TPC directories and rewrites them by mathematical dependency rather than paper number.
Every proposition proved in the body remains subject to its stated hypotheses; classical inputs such as the Chinese remainder theorem, the prime number theorem, and Mertens' theorem are not new discoveries here.
The number of historical manuscripts is not the number of independent contributions.

The accompanying \texttt{SOURCE\_CATALOG.md/json} enumerates all 420 directories, retaining original names, primary-source hashes, and candidate island labels obtained by text matching.
These labels aid retrieval; they do not represent a manual semantic audit of all 420 full texts.
The \texttt{CORE\_SOURCE\_MATRIX.md/json} records the core files actually used here, their source line ranges, SHA-256 hashes, and claim boundaries.
Source proofs, historical interfaces, current handoff state, and independent reviews are listed separately; status files do not replace proofs.

The early structural interfaces in Section~\ref{sec:structure} come from original TeX sources.
The main operator results in Sections~\ref{sec:operator}--\ref{sec:boundary} come from the unnumbered research cycle of 9 September 2026.
The CRT--Abel proof in Section~\ref{sec:dynamics} comes from the original moving-target proof, and the replication-and-deletion structure comes from the exact physical-intertwining document.
Original papers, research manuscripts, handoff files, and maps remain unchanged. The new synthesis is stored separately and adds no TPC paper number.

The verifiable core consists of complete definitions of shared objects, step-by-step proofs, and source hashes.
Successful compilation, resolved citations, embedded fonts, and page-by-page visual inspection verify presentation, not theorems.
Independent reviews apply only to their explicitly read scope; they certify neither the entire 420-directory collection nor novelty.

This full English translation preserves the Chinese edition's proofs and claim boundaries and reproduces the supplied English map unchanged. The Chinese sources remain the provenance baseline; no research route is reopened.
