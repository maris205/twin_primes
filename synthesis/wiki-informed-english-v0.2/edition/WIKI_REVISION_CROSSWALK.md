## Editorial consequences

The revised narrative groups these connections into three questions: is the original object preserved; is the proposed estimate valid on that object at the required scale; and does the resulting conclusion return to the endpoint with the correct quantifier? This is a reading framework, not a new theorem or a necessary architecture for every possible twin-prime proof.

The introduction and conclusion should make the two bridge gaps precise. Structural conservation in Section 3 does not construct the production occurrence attachment; the standalone operator in Section 4 is defined from its own literal source. The analytic ledger conditionally controls `S_x^phys`, not directly the prime-pair count. The dynamical sieve readout does directly identify prime pairs at seed 0, but the available metric theorem does not establish recurrence at that seed.

The source-frozen theorem and proof blocks are retained. Revision validation checks their exact text, not merely counts. This detects accidental editorial drift but is not an independent proof audit.
